How Well Does GTA 6 Run On Xbox Series S

📅 Nov 19, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

It’s GTA 6 release day, and millions of people will finally be stepping into Leonida for the first time on PS5, Xbox Series X, and yes, even Xbox Series S. We weren’t shown how GTA 6 runs on any sort of Xbox console until the day before it was released, which is why fans can’t quite believe just how good the game looks on the lesser Xbox.

GTA 6 is almost certainly the most visually stunning and graphically demanding game ever created, hence the skepticism about how well it might look and run on Series S. The console has given developers headaches in the past. It was one of the leading reasons why Baldur’s Gate 3 took so long to come to Xbox, and Tekken creator Katsuhiro Harada wished it didn’t exist.

Rockstar leadership vowed in a new interview with Bloomberg that the studio's next game won't take another 8 years to come out.

Somehow, though, Rockstar has managed to harness every last drop of the Series S’s limited power to make sure those playing it on the Xbox console have the best time possible. In Digital Foundry’s breakdown of how GTA 6 compares on each console, while it notes minor visual downgrades such as softer reflections and less detailed shadows, Rockstar has ensured the Series S version of GTA 6 is “surprisingly feature complete”.

Clips of how GTA 6 looks on Series S, including some comparing it to how it runs on Series X, were flooding social media before the game had even been released in most countries. A clip of a truck driving along the road with buildings whizzing past, demonstrating that the Series S FPS drops are minimal, already has 1.2 million views.

“GTA 6 running on Xbox Series S has made a lot of people look dumb af today,” another tweet reads, paired with a clip of Jason walking alongside some rather good-looking video game water. Mert Kirimgeri, a graphics programmer at Rockstar, has even weighed in, confirming that they worked on GTA 6 Series S optimization alongside dynamic signs, and glass and vehicle rendering.

The video game magic at play here to make all this happen has some believing that perhaps we will see GTA 6 on Switch 2 one day after all. While the Series S is slightly more powerful than Nintendo’s latest console, it now seems possible that Rockstar might eventually be able to figure out how to put the game on Switch 2.

The only real problem you’ll face if you plan on playing GTA 6 on Series S is its limited storage. The base model of the Xbox console has less than 400 GB of storage for games and apps. GTA 6 is going to occupy close to 60% of that. If you’ve been using it to play Modern Warfare 4, you might need to temporarily retire from Call of Duty if you want to spend the next few weeks in Vice City so there’s enough room for GTA 6 on your console.

We know it's been a long wait for GTA 6, but when you think about it, we've all lived so many lives since GTA 5.

European Commission hit by cyberattack on staff systems, Politico reports

📅 Nov 19, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The European Commission is investigating a cyberattack that may have exposed a large amount of personal data, Politico reported on Thursday.

An EU official told the publication that the Commission believes it has stopped most of the attack, but it is still unclear how much data may have been exposed. Moreover, the Commission had not issued a public statement at the time of writing.

Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier told Politico that “individual systems were targeted” and that the Commission had taken the necessary steps to deal with the attack, and the people who were affected had been informed.

The attack has been going on for around ten days, according to the report. During that time, the Commission took several systems offline, including tools used by staff to check payslips, manage working hours and request leave. A system for processing medical reimbursements was also affected. The Commission’s technology chief, Henna Virkkunen, briefed the other commissioners on Wednesday.

Politico also reported that CERT-EU, the cybersecurity team responsible for protecting EU institutions, found that the attackers had used a freely available AI tool to look for weaknesses in digital systems, and the tool was identified as ARTEX.

This is the Commission’s second major cyber incident this year, as in March, attackers used a stolen API key to access the Amazon Web Services account that supports the Europa.eu website. CERT-EU later said that around 92GB of compressed data had been stolen. The data included names, email addresses and the contents of emails.

The March attack may also have exposed data from at least 29 other EU institutions and bodies, according to CERT-EU. Another incident over the winter raised concerns about staff information. An attack on the systems used to manage the Commission’s mobile phones may have exposed employees’ names and phone numbers.

The latest attack comes as the Commission is trying to strengthen cybersecurity across the EU. In January, it introduced a package of new measures to protect against cyberattacks. Henna Virkkunen also said in July that the Commission itself had become a target because of the current geopolitical situation.

Manchester United's connections mean they cannot be ruled out in the race for RB Leipzig sensation Antonio Nusa.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout and expert on Norwegian football, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Several Premier League giants are thought to be in the mix for the 21-year-old, who has been in stunning form for the German club this season.

Nusa has scored four goals and provided seven assists in just nine Bundesliga games, and found the net in a Norway shirt brilliantly when he scored against Wales last week.

The former Club Brugge forward is under contract at Leipzig until 2029, and is expected to depart within the next two transfer windows if they receive a sizable fee.

Man United are expected to be busy in the January window following the appointment of Mauricio Pochettino, and are certainly in need of an addition on the wing.

If Man United are to sign Nusa, the move will almost certainly have to be made in the January window.

Several more clubs will join the race in the summer and will be able to offer a far more stable environment for development than the one at Old Trafford.

Nusa's former teammate, Benjamin Sesko, has struggled following his £74million move to Man United in 2025.

Pochettino can offer Nusa the privilege of being the face of the project that attempts to revive the Red Devils, and that will be no easy offer to turn down.
