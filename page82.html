Emergency Broadcast System Test Still On For Today, Homeland Security Says

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Politics  |  Read Source →

Executive Editor, Legal, Labor & Politics

Despite a series of technology outages the past week on email systems, aviation communications, airlines, banks, national security networks, and more, the Federal Emergency Management Agency says the long scheduled test today of the Emergency Broadcast System will go ahead.

“This is a nationwide test of the Emergency Alert System, issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency,” the one-minute long message will states, FEMA said. “This is only a test. No action is required by the public.”

Set to hit at 11:20 a.m. PT/2:30 p.m. ET, the test will appear on “radio and television broadcasters, cable systems, satellite radio and television providers and wireline video,” notes the Department of Homeland Security. According to FEMA, there will be a brief interruption, but “No blackouts, no secret emergencies” connected to the test. Watch on Deadline

FYI – FEMA falls under the jurisdiction of the DHS.

We’ve heard a lot of rumors about tomorrow’s EAS test. 📺📻 Let’s clear things up. It lasts 1 minute, and no action is required. No blackouts, no secret emergencies. Just our legally mandated, routine test on TV and radio at approximately 2:20 PM ET: https://t.co/WGD7xCuRDB pic.twitter.com/HEErLQVKga

“During an emergency, every second is crucial,” FEMA Administrator Cameron Hamilton said in a statement Monday. “FEMA’s alert and warning systems ensure that no second is wasted getting you the critical information needed to keep you safe. Operational tests, like the one to be conducted tomorrow, allow us to identify and fix potential issues before an actual emergency occurs.”

As you might recall from 2023, the feds are obliged by the IPAWS Modernization Act of 2015 to conduct a EAS test every three years. A more localized test of today’s test took place in Alaska with no problems.

A further safeguard is in place that if today’s test encountered difficulties. FEMA explained back in September: “If an actual emergency or significant weather event occurs on test day, FEMA and the FCC may postpone the test. The backup date for the nationwide test is December 3, 2026.”

8 Biggest Snubs in the 2026 Game Awards Nominations

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The Game Awards has announced its official list of nominees for this year’s prizes. Among the nominees are plenty of obvious contenders, such as Grand Theft Auto 6, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. There was stiff competition across several categories, meaning that some worthy games — and Polygon favorites — didn’t make the shortlist for certain prizes. These are the biggest TGA nomination snubs of 2026.

As one eagle-eyed Resetera user pointed out, Sony just broke a streak that dates all the way back to 2015. This marks the first time in 11 years that a first-party PlayStation release hasn't received a Game of the Year nomination. During that 11-year streak, Sony took home three GOTY trophies: God of War (2018), The Last of Us Part 2 (2020), and Astro Bot (2024). This year's big first-party game from PlayStation was Marvel's Wolverine, which is currently sitting at a 75 on Metacritic and incited a whole lot of debate about difficulty and UI design. The game was nominated in two categories: Best Performance for Liam McIntyre and Innovation in Accessibility.

This outcome won't come as a surprise to Polygon's Oli Welsh, who predicted Marvel's Wolverine missing out on a GOTY nomination and snapping Sony's impressive streak all the way back in September.

There were a ton of fantastic video game performances this year, and somebody’s favorite was bound to get left out. Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave fan favorite Jimmie Yamaguchi gave an outstanding performance as the oddball scholar Fabio, and by every measure, it deserved a nomination. In less capable hands, Fabio’s starry-eyed devotion to Fortune’s Weave co-protagonist Dietrich could have been played for laughs, but Yamaguchi makes the budding romance between these men believable and heartfelt.

Manni L. Perez was nominated for GTA 6, presumably for portraying Lucia Caminos. (Funnily enough, developer Rockstar Games has not yet officially confirmed Perez is actually playing Lucia, despite longstanding online rumors.) Apparently, the actor who played co-lead Jason Duval didn’t make enough of an impact with players to score a nomination.

Blizzard’s World of Warcraft has had a massive year, with the launch of its reimagined Classic experience, World of Warcraft: Forever. The team also released major updates for the modern version of the beloved MMORPG, like March’s Midnight expansion. Those changes weren’t enough to move the needle with voters, and WoW was also left out of the Best Community Support category.

The popular farming sim, which entered early access in 2024 before its full release this year, may have more than 26,000 “overwhelmingly positive” reviews on Steam, but that wasn’t enough for it to snag a nomination in any category. Best Debut Indie might have been Fields of Mistria’s best shot at a prize, but it wouldn’t have been out of place in Best Sim/Strategy or the broader Best Indie category, either.

Despite a wobbly third act, Fortune’s Weave is one of our favorite games of 2026, one of the best games available for the Nintendo Switch 2, and one of the best-reviewed games of the year. Surprisingly, developer Intelligent Systems’ epic strategy RPG only managed to pick up one nomination, for Best Role-Playing Game. It stands a decent chance of taking this one home: other nominees include Final Fantasy Resonance, Monster Hunter Stories 3, The Blood of Dawnwalker, and Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter.

Pearl Abyss’s open-world adventure game was an early-year frontrunner for a GOTY nom, even after middling reviews and a rocky launch. While it wasn’t a critical darling, it did have a pretty massive online following that made the prospect of several nominations seem likely. Surprisingly, Crimson Desert will compete in just one category: Best Community Support.

Bungie’s extraction shooter managed to snag a nomination for Best Multiplayer Game, which it frankly stands little chance of winning against a field that includes two indie chart-toppers and a well-regarded Call of Duty. However, the stylish game was overlooked in the Best Art Direction category, perhaps partly due to the shadow cast by a 2025 plagiarism scandal where Bungie admitted to using an artist’s designs without proper credit. (Bungie later credited her as a Visual Design Consultant.) Marathon’s retro-future meets Y2K cyberpunk aesthetic truly stands out in a sea of Unreal Engine 5 homogeneity, and it’s a shame to see it overlooked here.

Both the Best Indie and Best Debut Indie categories have some stiff competition this year. However, no independent game managed to get a nomination for Game of the Year in 2026. While there weren’t a ton of indie contenders for the top category this year, Polygon’s Oli Welsh had the co-op adventure Big Walk pegged as a dark horse, though developer House House’s recent AI controversy likely didn’t help matters.

The lack of an indie GOTY nominee represents a pretty massive shift from the 2025 crop, which included a record-setting three games developed by independent studios: Hades 2, Hollow Knight: Silksong, and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Sandfall Interactive’s much-loved RPG managed to take home the top prize and a total of nine Game Awards, the most wins by a single game. 2024 also saw one indie crack the category, Balatro, though the top honors went to Astro Bot.

The Game Awards 2026 will stream live on Disney+, YouTube, and Twitch, on Thursday, Dec. 10, starting at 7:30 p.m. EST / 4:30 p.m. PST.

Christian Bale’s New R-Rated Prime Video Movie Breaks 25-Year-Old Rotten Tomatoes Record

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Prime Video's Madden gets flagged with a record penalty.

In the new sports biopic, Christian Bale and Nicolas Cage will portray NFL legends Al Davis and John Madden, respectively. The story follows the titular real-life legend who gets involved in developing the now-famous video game franchise, Madden NFL. Now, ahead of its release, the first reactions are in.

With 13 reviews, Madden debuts with an abysmal 15% on Rotten Tomatoes, currently setting a record as Bale's lowest-rated movie. The previous low was a 25-year-old movie, Captain Corell's Mandolin, which also featured his co-star Cage in the lead role. However, the score will still fluctuate as more reviews become public.

Additionally, it's the second-lowest Rotten Tomatoes score for controversial director David O. Russell, who reunites with Bale for the fourth time. This would also be their second straight dud together after Amsterdam received a poor reception in 2022.

Unfortunately, the approach to Madden didn't bode well with critics, who found the biopic tonally inconsistent, lacking dramatic stakes, and one-dimensional. Even the star-studded ensemble couldn't salvage much of the movie's substantial flaws, as Cage's fat suit and prosthetics were deemed too cartoonish rather than sophisticated. However, Bale and Kathryn Hahn's sharper performances are the few bright spots.

In ScreenRant's review of Madden, Matthew Rudoy was one of the few critics who enjoyed the sports biopic, praising Cage for capturing the legend's "high energy, verbose passion, and many eccentricities." More importantly, he and Bale have "fantastic chemistry that successfully makes Davis and Madden's friendship the heart of the movie." Although the movie "struggles more in the second half," it earned a 6 out of 10 stars.

Written by Cambron Clark and Russell, Madden isn't without controversy. When the movie was filming in May 2025, there was a report that an actor had walked off set after refusing to do a nude scene and that the filmmaker allegedly used a racial slur during a monologue. This incident apparently caused other crew members to exit the set. However, those reports have been disputed.

Unfortunately, 2026 just hasn't been a great year for the Oscar winner. On top of Madden's poor reviews, his Frankenstein adaptation, The Bride!, also polarized critics, though it landed a higher 57%. Nevertheless, Bale hasn't had his best filmography in the last few years, with his projects earning mixed reviews.

However, that could turn with his upcoming appearance as LAPD Detective Vincent Hanna in Michael Mann's sequel Heat 2. Filming is expected to start this month, and the film will feature a star-studded cast of Leonardo DiCaprio, Ebon Moss-Bachrach, and Jason Clarke. Bale also takes over the role from Al Pacino, who previously portrayed the character in the original 1995 movie.

On the other hand, Cage also has a packed filmography for the next few years, including Best Pancakes in the Country, Lords of War, and Fortitude.

Madden will be released on Prime Video on November 18, 2026.

Jensen Huang says he would be ‘honored’ to return as Nvidia’s CEO after resigning

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang signaled interest Tuesday in returning to lead the computer chip company in his first public statement since he resigned last week following revelations of an extramarital affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

Some tech industry figures, including SpaceX and Tesla CEO Elon Musk, have called for the reinstatement of Huang, a high-profile booster for the artificial intelligence industry who oversaw Nvidia’s development as the key provider of chips driving advanced AI models. President Donald Trump, a Huang ally who has spoken with him frequently, has also endorsed the comeback bid.

“If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO,” Huang said in a social media post.

Huang acknowledged making “serious mistakes” in his personal life but said that he’s received an “outpouring” of support from employees, investors, business partners and customers for his return.

Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment on Huang’s statement. On Monday, a person familiar with the board’s thinking who insisted on anonymity to discuss it told The Associated Press that the board was not currently considering Huang’s return.

The Santa Clara, California-based company was scheduled to release quarterly earnings later Tuesday. Nvidia Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is expected to speak with Wall Street analysts on the earnings call for the first time since being named interim CEO.

A petition drive seeks Huang’s return

A “Stand with Jensen” petition set up last week has gathered momentum, gaining signatures from current and former Nvidia staff, investors and other Silicon Valley supporters. The petition calls on the board to bring back Huang while an independent investigation proceeds or until there’s “compelling evidence” that his departure is in the company’s best interests.

“We are concerned that Jensen was removed without meaningful due process,” the organizers said in an open letter. “We have not been shown evidence substantiating the allegation that the woman at the center of this controversy was a spy.”

Trump suggested Monday that the ex-CEO could return, saying Huang “learned a big lesson” and that he “would certainly not mind” if he was reinstated.

Huang resigned last week from Nvidia, apparently ousted by the board after revelations that he had extramarital affairs, both with a Chinese businesswoman suspected of being an intelligence asset for Beijing and with a senior executive who reported to him.

The businesswoman Huang was seeing who reportedly had links to Chinese intelligence was an executive at Singapore-based cloud computing firm Megaspeed International, a company being investigated by the U.S. for allegedly violating U.S. export controls by illegally diverting advanced Nvidia AI chips to Chinese companies.

Huang faces questioning by Congress

Also Tuesday, key House lawmakers said that Huang has voluntarily agreed to appear for transcribed interviews as the company faces intensifying national scrutiny over sales of its advanced computer chips.

Huang will appear before congressional committees on Dec. 1, followed by the company’s general counsel, Tim Teter, on Dec. 2.

“We look forward to hearing from Jensen Huang and Tim Teter on their roles in a potential breach of our national security,” said a statement from the chairmen of three House committees with oversight of China and intelligence issues.

“The American people need answers on Mr. Huang and Nvidia’s role in allowing dangerous PRC actors to access our most advanced chips, as well as the role of a potential Chinese intelligence asset in this scandal,” the lawmakers said, referring to the People’s Republic of China.

Meanwhile, Nvidia said it’s creating a new board committee to oversee national security and global trade compliance following the revelations about Huang.

Nvidia’s advanced chips are in high demand because they’re essential for cloud computing and data centers that underpin the AI boom, but they face restrictions on their sale to China. Trump said it was “good” that Nvidia had created the new oversight committee.

“Yes, that’s a good idea. It’s a great company,” Trump said shortly after arriving Tuesday evening in the Philippine capital of Manila, where he participated in a photo opportunity with the leaders of Australia, India and Japan. “It’s a great idea.”

Star Wars’ Thrawn Creator Is Bringing a Murder Mystery to Star Trek

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Timothy Zahn built one of Star Wars’ most enduring villains around the pleasure of watching someone notice what everybody else has missed. Thrawn can make a conversation about culture feel like the beginning of a military disaster, especially when the people across the table haven’t realized how much they’ve already told him. That makes his creator an intriguing person to put in charge of a murder mystery.

Zahn introduced Thrawn in Heir to the Empire in 1991, giving Luke, Han, and Leia a formidable new opponent after Return of the Jedi. Thirty-five years later, he’s taking that interest in secrets and competing agendas to another corner of science fiction. His destination is Deep Space Nine, a station where a valuable object changing hands is usually enough to get several people interested in who’s buying, who’s selling, and what neither side wants anyone else to know.

Zahn is writing Star Trek: Roc’s Egg, an original graphic novel releasing from IDW Publishing in October 2027, with art by Ángel Hernández. The story begins when an artifact arrives aboard Deep Space Nine and appears to be destroyed in transit, and a Federation JAG investigation follows, uncovering a secret auction, wealthy collectors, dangerous criminals, and a murder waiting to happen. Hernández’s previous Trek work includes Voyager — Homecoming and Deep Space Nine — The Dog of War.

Zahn’s relationship with Star Trek actually predates his work in Star Wars. In IDW’s announcement, he traced it back to watching the original series in 1966, describing the opportunity to work on the graphic novel now as “a huge honor and privilege,” making this a chance to contribute to a franchise he’s followed since its beginning.

The station is a promising place to start. A mystery aboard a starship puts suspects among people who generally have a reason to trust one another. On Deep Space Nine, the investigators have to contend with visitors whose business may already depend on keeping Starfleet at a distance. Add a private auction, and an apparently destroyed artifact becomes a much more interesting problem. For readers who know Zahn through Thrawn, having multiple angles to look at this story from is the draw. His most famous character wins by understanding what other people value and how far they’ll go to protect it, while Roc’s Egg gives him a station full of people with something to hide, and an investigation that could make those secrets expensive.

Star Trek: Roc’s Egg releases in October 2027.

Anthropic lets apps bill Claude costs to their customers

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Linked billing lets developers choose who pays for each request, but not every customer can use it.

Anthropic has launched Linked billing in beta, letting apps built on Claude bill API usage to a customer’s own Claude organisation. Customers can set a spend cap or unlink the app, but only organisations that pay Anthropic directly qualify, and their own data protections do not carry over.

Apps built on Claude can now charge their AI usage to their customers’ own Claude accounts. The feature, called Linked billing, launched in beta on Tuesday, according to Anthropic’s release notes.

A customer first links its Claude organisation to the app and approves it. The developer then chooses, on each request, whether the customer or the developer pays. The request still runs on the developer’s account, with its usage limits and model access.

Customers can set a monthly spending cap for each app in the Claude Console, or unlink it at any time. If Anthropic cannot bill the customer, the request is refused rather than charged to the developer. Anthropic is strict on limits elsewhere too: it ends enterprise discounts once customers use up their tokens.

Not every customer qualifies. It only works for organisations that pay Anthropic directly, not those billed through AWS, Google Cloud or Azure, or with only a Claude subscription. Credits from Claude plans and Anthropic’s startup programmes cannot be used either.

There is also a privacy catch. A customer’s own data protections, such as zero data retention and HIPAA agreements, do not apply to these requests.
