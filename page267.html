Roma in Serie A title mix, says Gasperini after 3-0 win over Sassuolo

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Gian Piero Gasperini believes his Roma team are firmly in the mix for the Serie A title after they dispatched Sassuolo 3-0.

Roma bounced back from their capitulation against Manchester United by swatting aside Sassuolo at Stadio Olimpico on Saturday.

Mario Hermoso and Rodrigo Mora were on target for Roma, who also benefited from Sebastian Walukiewicz's own goal.

Roma posted 3.73 expected goals in a dominant performance, as they made it 18 home league games unbeaten, which is their longest such run under a single manager since a 22-game streak between January 2016 and February 2017 under Luciano Spalletti (W19 D3).

They also became only the fourth team in the last 65 years to score more than 30 goals after 11 Serie A matches in a season, following Juventus (33), Napoli (32) and Lazio (31), all of whom achieved this in 2017-18. Roma have netted 31 league goals already this season.

Roma moved up to second, at least temporarily, with Inter in action on Sunday. Lazio are top.

— AS Roma English (@ASRomaEN) November 7, 2026

And Gasperini feels his team can now be labelled as title challengers.

"It [is] an incredible run of games, we lost only to Real Madrid and Manchester United, but also had a run of victories, drew with Como and Napoli," Gasperini said.

"Those two defeats hurt because they were late and we were leading, but they did come at the end of strong performances and against top-level opponents.

"Undoubtedly, this season we improved a lot in attack, so now we score lots of goals in different ways and with different players. Even when we lost, we scored lots of goals.

"In fact, I don't believe this is a response to a defeat, it's just more confirmation from this team of the track they are on.

"It's fun to play this season. We must not delude ourselves or disappoint ourselves, so there's no harm in dreaming, especially with the journey that we are on. I don't know what we could be capable of achieving.

"Inter are the best placed to win. For the rest, all of us are in the mix, including us, and can have our chances."

Mora had already hit the crossbar before Hermoso put Roma ahead in the 27th minute, firing home after initially trying to set up Donyell Malen.

Walukiewicz inadvertently turned in from Malen's shot, and Roma grabbed their third in the second half when Mora picked out the bottom corner.

Mikel Arteta has praised Noni Madueke’s response to a difficult start to the season after the winger played another decisive role in Arsenal’s 1-0 victory over Hull City.

Martin Odegaard scored the only goal at the Emirates Stadium after 72 minutes, reacting quickest when Kostas Tzolakis saved Madueke’s effort.

Arsenal were far from their fluent best, but the victory moved the defending champions six points clear at the top of the Premier League.

As reported by Arsenal News, Arteta believes Madueke has transformed his performances after responding positively to limited playing time earlier in the campaign.

Arteta said of Madueke: “That’s the second part of the story – the first is that you need to earn the right to be able to do that.

“Noni did that, he reacted to not playing often, went away, started to train and was a different Noni.

“He wasn’t going to accept that situation, he meant business, and since then he has been incredible.

“Now you are in that position, he needs to hone and improve it, so let’s see if he can do that.”

Madueke was Arsenal’s most dangerous attacker against Hull, registering five successful dribbles and creating several opportunities.

Sky Sports also reports that he is now averaging 3.57 chances created per 90 minutes, the highest rate of any Premier League player this season.

Madueke’s improvement could be particularly important while Bukayo Saka is unavailable.

Earlier in the season, a January exit was even being discussed. CaughtOffside reported in September that Atletico Madrid and Juventus were monitoring his situation after his limited Premier League involvement.

That possibility now feels considerably less likely.

Madueke has responded exactly how Arteta would have wanted: rather than becoming frustrated, he worked independently during the international break and returned sharper. His passing accuracy, crossing and dribble success have all improved compared with last season.

There is still one obvious area to improve. Madueke had chances to score against Hull and could have made the victory far more comfortable.

But Arsenal now have a winger who is creating chances at an elite rate and increasingly influencing important matches. If he can add more ruthless finishing, Arteta may soon face a genuine selection dilemma when Saka returns.

New England Patriots tight end Hunter Henry is officially listed as questionable to play in Sunday's game against the Green Bay Packers.

The Patriots didn't elevate another tight end to the active roster on Saturday, which could be a good sign that Henry is going to try to play through the injury. However, the offense could look improved in Week 9 if Henry does play. Patriots veteran wide receiver Mack Hollins is returning from injured reserve, which means Maye could have two of his most reliable receiving targets back on the field at the same time for this matchup.

West Ham fans’ furious reaction raises an uncomfortable question

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

As the Hammers subsided to their first away defeat in the Championship, long suffering supporters, who’d undertaken the 480 mile round trip to be served up a pretty spineless team performance, decided to let their team have it with both barrels.

After last week’s displeasure culminating an Hammers fans singing ‘sacked in the morning’ to their own manager, this weekend brought the players into the crosshairs as the next logical target for their ire.

..With Nuno Espirito Santo out of the job and with Scott Parker just 72 hours in, the 7,500+ travelling fans turned on their stars.

Under no illusions after the result – and the away fans’ response – yesterday: Scott Parker

..As well as booing their own goal keeper pretty much throughout, if the reports on the radio were correct.

Traditionalists will likely be aghast at the thought of fans undermining their own team in this manner, whilst those who participated no doubt feel it was the only way to show their contempt for the perceived lack of commitment and effort which those eleven representatives  of the Claret and Blue heritage displayed.

Not to mention the hundreds of pounds it costs each supporter, every game, merely to arrive at the turnstiles in support of our club. But is it the ‘right’ thing to do?

For me – it’s a no. I started attending games with my son when he was ten years old and however bad it got (it was pretty dire in that season of 2003-4) we never left early, never booed our own players (Luis Boa Morte excepted, I must confess to once letting my frustration with his indolence bubble over) and always, always applauded those who’d put in a shift, regardless of outcome. And I’d not even heard ‘you’re not fit to wear the shirt‘ sung by West Ham fans in my couple of decades’ worth of attending games.

I just don’t see it helping team morale or positively influencing the final outcome. Which is, after all, the point of ‘supporters’.

Giving ‘The Sun’ another gleeful headline to point out our sufferings sticks in my throat too.

But don’t get me wrong, each has absolutely the right to their own response. Just don’t see it helping is all.

From the old Bobby Moore Upper to the Billy Bonds' stand these days - I've been watching since '03 and a supporter since about 1970.. Favourite player - Dean Ashton: Curse you Shaun Wright-Phillips. I still watch YouTube repeats of the Cup Final of 2006 hoping in vain that Shaka Hislop grows another six inches and stops Steven Gerrard's injury time equaliser. Can tell I'm getting old knowing I watched both Mark Noble's debut and his last game at West Ham. Pulling on a Claret and Blue replica shirt still makes me feel the same butterflies as when I was seven years old. Magic.

Blizzard pulled out all the stops to ensure World of Warcraft: Forever survived a chaotic launch night. What was the company's secret weapon? Splurging an absurd amount of money on infrastructure made up of 3,924 virtual machines with 70,480 CPU cores and a whopping 251,136 GB, or 251 TB, of memory.

Praised by media and players as one of the smoothest launches in Blizzard's history, World of Warcraft: Forever requires significant computing firepower. 3,924 virtual machines work out to 18 cores and 64GB of memory per instance. Or, the company could be using a mixture of 16-core and 32-core nodes with slightly less memory.

To put the infrastructure's scale in consumer terms, we are looking at about 8,810 mainstream processors, or 7,848 high-end gaming systems with 32GB of memory each. In any event, the hardware is greatly improved compared to what World of Warcraft used to run on back in 2009.

In 2009, Blizzard said it used 10 data centers worldwide to host World of Warcraft. At the time, the infrastructure included 13,250 server blades, totaling 75,000 CPU cores and 112.5 TB of memory. By comparison, World of Warcraft: Forever leverages 6% fewer CPU cores but rocks 2.2X more memory. Modern processors are leagues faster than chips from 17 years ago. By today's standards, the bottleneck is memory capacity and bandwidth, which is why the infrastructure can run on fewer cores but has more than double the memory to compensate.

Blizzard does not specify the exact hardware that World of Warcraft: Forever runs on. However, if we were to translate the core specifications to, for example, AMD's EPYC 128-core Bergamo or 96-core Genoa chips, it could be anywhere between 275 and 365 2U server nodes, depending on which processor it uses. Therefore, World of Warcraft: Forever could be running on just eight to ten 42U server racks, a noteworthy improvement over 17 years ago.

Blizzard should have no trouble building the infrastructure for World of Warcraft: Forever. A ballpark estimate for about ten 42U server racks with EPYC chips is at least $15 million, even with today's memory pricing. But then again, since Microsoft owns Blizzard now, the company could just be tapping its parent's existing Azure platform.
