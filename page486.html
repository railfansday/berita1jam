The easy answer—happily!—is yes. And celebrity lymphatic specialist Rebecca Faria (also known as @DetoxbyRebecca on Instagram) is leading the charge. Based in Beverly Hills, Faria specializes in Brazilian lymphatic drainage (a rhythmic massage, which combines pressure and long strokes), and has a roster of A-list clients who swear by her techniques (including Jennifer Aniston, Hailey Bieber, Ariana Grande, and Anne Hathaway). While a treatment with one of Faria’s team starts from $350, she’s shared some of her favorite (easy) exercises that are designed to stimulate your lymphatic system at home. And they’re free.

Done while either laying down, seated, or standing, knee hugs aren’t just good to get the lymphatic system going, but also help release back tension. Hug one knee into your chest, and hold for 20 seconds, then do the other side.

Lie on your back and place the soles of your feet together, letting your knees fall out in a butterfly formation. This stretch is also a fantastic way to stretch your hips, as well as release your pelvic floor.

While lying down, hold your legs straight up, then lift one as you lower the other. These are called flutter kicks–do 20 of them. (Incidentally, these are also great for your core, a muscle group that has long term benefits to help with bloating.)

These aren’t just for your Pilates class. Remain lying down. With one leg on the floor, lift the other leg and move in a circular motion 20 times. Repeat on the other leg.

No bike needed. With your legs in a table-top position, straighten one leg, then the other, 20 times.

Another Pilates movement to try out. Lying on your left-hand side and keeping the legs relatively straight, circle your right leg 20 times. Do the same on the other leg.

Stand with your feet flat on the floor, then raise up onto your toes, lifting your heels off the ground. Do this 20 times. This is also a favorite movement of Jesse Inchauspé (aka the Glucose Goddess) to help with balancing blood sugar.

20 Strongest One Piece Characters

📅 Dec 21, 2023  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

One Piece's world and map aren't the only things that have broadened their scope over the last few decades. Luffy has continued to grow stronger and stronger with every new arc, which in turn means every new opponent he faces continues to do the same as well. As such, One Piece's power scaling has come a very long way since the humble East Blue.

From fighting an evil clown terrorizing a small town to taking down a centuries-old demon secretly ruling the world, One Piece's story and stakes have escalated to sky-high levels in very classic shonen fashion. And, as a result, One Piece now boasts numerous powerful characters, both enemies and allies alike. There are many, like Joyboy, who could be the strongest beings in the entire series, but One Piece has yet to reveal their full skill set properly. Luckily, there are countless others with tangible feats and demonstrated power and skill that immediately distinguish them as One Piece's strongest.

The current Fleet Admiral of the Navy, Sakazuki, is as twisted as he is powerful. His devil fruit gives him the scary power of becoming lava. Although he was no match for an angry Whitebeard, he dealt serious damage to him and contributed to his death.

Akainu's endurance is off the charts, as he battled Aokiji for ten days straight on Punk Hazard and came out on top. His body is extremely strong, withstanding two consecutive full-power punches imbued with both haki and devil fruit powers from Whitebeard and still getting up to fight. Given that his devil fruit power is heat-based, Akainu might also be Luffy's ultimate enemy, with his magma able to seriously wound the Straw Hat captain no matter how far he stretches.

Known as One Piece's strongest swordsman, Mihawk is a lone wolf who managed to become a Warlord despite having no crew. Many journey far and wide to challenge him, including Zoro, and they've all lost. He doesn't have devil fruit powers, as his sword skills and haki are more than enough to defend himself.

With one slash of his huge cross-shaped sword, Mihawk managed to cut a huge iceberg in half and destroy a huge ship. With his incredible agility, observant eyes that gave him the nickname Hawk-eye, and his mastery of haki, he came to be known as Shanks' rival, who is an Emperor of the Sea. Currently, Mihawk, Crocodile, and Buggy are leading the Cross Guild, and though the latter two members are powerful in their own right, there's no doubt that it's Mihawk who is the real muscle of this powerful pirate team.

King "The Conflagration," real name Alber, is one of the All-Stars of the Beast Pirates and Kaido’s second-in-command. Kaido freed King from imprisonment when they were both being experimented on at Punk Hazard, and ever since then, King has been Kaido’s most loyal confidant and the one who most supported Kaido’s quest to become the Pirate King.

One Piece has a surprisingly fiendish rogues gallery, but the worst of its worst have reached nightmarish proportions in strength.

King’s mastery of the Dragon-Dragon Fruit, Model: Pteranodon, gives him a lot of versatility with his attacks, but the biggest source of King’s strength comes from his being a Lunarian, a secret race of winged people with powerful fire abilities and immense speed and durability. Zoro only barely defeated King in their fight, and the fact that King’s Lunarian lineage factor was used as the basis for the powerful Seraphim only further cements his status as an incredibly powerful fighter.

Sanji is the chef of the Straw Hat Pirates and the last person to join the crew before they entered the Grand Line. Before coming to work at the Baratie, Sanji was the outcast of the Vinsmoke family, a notorious group of warmongering mercenaries from the North Blue, and it wasn't until the Whole Cake Island arc that Sanji was forced to confront them with help from Luffy.

Sanji fights using powerful kicks further strengthened by Armament Haki and, most recently, his cybernetic enhancements courtesy of his father, which even let him set his legs on fire to further increase his power and set his opponents ablaze. Sanji's biggest and most recent feat of strength is defeating Queen, one of the strongest members of the Beast Pirates, and there was no better way of solidifying his place within the "Monster Trio" of the Straw Hat Pirates.

Along with Luffy, Zoro has considerably increased his skills and power after setting out to sea only a few years ago in One Piece's timeline. Before he met his future captain in Shells Town, Zoro was already one of the most renowned and feared swordsmen in the entire East Blue. Still, as Mihawk demonstrated to him during the Baratie Arc, he still had a way to go before he'd earn the title of the World's Greatest Swordsman.

Zoro has a habit of pulling off unbelievable feats of strength, like cutting through solid steel and cleaving the Enies Lobby tower in two, and that was before he endured intense training during One Piece's time skip. During his time in Dressrosa and Wano, Zoro cut apart the mountain-sized Pica and deflected Kaido and Big Mom's combo attack, Ocean Sovereignty, which might be the most vigorous attack in the series yet.

Fans would be devastated if Zoro's rematch against Mihawk did not happen because it would show Zoro undoubtedly surpassing his previous feats yet again. And on Elbaf, Zoro is set to surpass his limits once again by finally mastering Conqueror's Haki in his fight against the Holy Knights.

The Holy Knights are One Piece's newest group of antagonists who have only just made their debut in the Elbaf Arc. This legion, which directly presides over the World Nobles, also possesses a contract with Imu in the form of the Depths Covenant. While they may not enjoy the immortality that the Five Elders do as part of the Abyssal Covenant, the Holy Knights of God are still blessed with advanced regeneration that makes them quite tiresome opponents for those unfamiliar with infusing their attacks with Conqueror's Haki.

In fact, the Holy Knights are pretty much unbeatable without Conqueror's Haki. In addition to this, the Holy Knights also possess fearsome devil fruit abilities, be it Gunko's Arrow-Arrow powers, St. Sommers' piercing thorns, or St. Killingham's broken ability to materialize nightmares and dreams. Even those without a devil fruit, like Shamrock, possess powerful devil fruit-infused weapons. The Holy Knights' abilities can also be buffed by Imu's Omen, which also grants them access to their family's weapon.

After nearly fourteen years of being missing in action and fans constantly asking about his whereabouts, Scopper Gaban, Roger's left-hand man, finally made his modern-day debut on Elbaf. Much like Rayleigh, Gaban completely lives up to his reputation as the "Mountain Eater" anda former member of the Roger Pirates with his overwhelming strength.

Gaban has mastered all three forms of haki, with his Observation Haki in particular being so advanced that he was able to sense the Straw Hats approaching Elbaf even when they were still miles away from the shore. Even though he may not be in his prime anymore, Gaban was still able to go up against Imu and the Holy Knights, wielding his twin axes in a most fearsome manner.

As Roger's first mate, Rayleigh's strength is all but given. Even though he may not possess a devil fruit like many of the other strong characters in One Piece, Rayleigh more than makes up for it with his powerful haki. Having taught Luffy everything he knows about haki, Rayleigh himself is a master of all three types of haki, which is quite a rare occurrence in One Piece.

One Piece has revealed a myriad of Devil Fruits thus far, but these 30 are stronger than the rest by a pretty wide margin.

Rayleigh's Armament Haki, in particular, is incredibly powerful and was able to block an attack by Kizaru with relative ease at Marineford. His advanced Observation Haki can instantly perceive every living thing across an entire island with no effort. Rayleigh still retains a lot of physical strength and vigor, as seen from his ability to swim across the Calm Belt and fight Sea Kings with his bare hands. However, he is not immune to the effects of age and even suggests that he no longer has the strength to defeat newer-generation powerhouses like Blackbeard.

Garp is a Vice Admiral but could've easily been a Fleet Admiral if he hadn't refused the multiple offers to be promoted. He frequently fought Gold D. Roger and the latter even said that they almost killed each other many times. Together with Sengoku, they were at the forefront of the fight against the pirates in their younger years. He earned the respect of Roger and even raised his son Ace together with Luffy at Roger's request.

A master of haki and the strength of a superhuman, Garp is one of the strongest, and barely anybody could go toe-to-toe with him in his prime. Though devil fruits might give out some of One Piece's flashiest powers, Garp and others like him prove that the mysterious fruits aren't the only way or the best way to truly obtain strength. He dramatically proved this point when he arrived at Blackbeard's base to rescue Koby, making quite the entrance with one of One Piece's strongest attacks that the anime has adapted so far.

A member of the Worst Generation, Emperor of the Sea and a Warlord, Blackbeard did the impossible by wielding two devil fruits at once. His thirst for power and strength is incredible — killing his own crew member, capturing Ace, and stealing Whitebeard's powers in a plan that finished off the strongest Emperor of the Sea. His pursuit of strength propelled him to monstrous levels.

With One Piece nearing its end, Teach will play a key role as one of One Piece's final villains, as Luffy's brother and adoptive father were killed by him. Blackbeard has been set up as Luffy's ideological opposite in many ways, with the villain using piracy as an excuse to further his own selfish goals, as opposed to Luffy, who is constantly freeing the oppressed. With Blackbeard's ability to wield two devil fruits, it will truly be a blowout when he and Luffy finally get their long-awaited true fight.

Obviously, the cheese has to be top-notch, but the real secret to the best version is the perfect crunchy topping. My recipe delivers on all of that and more, so you won't even think about reaching for another mac & cheese recipe for the rest of your gatherings. First, you’ll boil the pasta. Start with a big pot of salted water (and I mean super salty, it should taste like the ocean), then add your pasta and cook it until before al dente—I’m talking still-has-a-ring-of-hard-white-pasta-in-the-middle-when-you-bite-in uncooked. You want it to meld and absorb with your cheese sauce in the oven, not turn into mush. Undercooking now guarantees the perfect bite when you pull it out of the oven.

Then it’s cheese sauce time, and that means making a béchamel. You’ll start by melting your butter in a pan over medium heat, then sprinkling the flour over the top and stirring everything together until it starts pulling away from the pan slightly. You’re looking for what’s called a “blonde roux,” which has a slightly yellow tint and should smell a bit like baked goods. You don’t want it super dark (so keep an eye on the heat!), but you definitely don’t want it as light as it started.

Next, add the milk in slowly, stirring constantly so you don’t get thick lumps. Want to shortcut this process a bit? Heat your milk ahead of time to room temperature or even slightly warm. You’re going to cook it until it’s thick and bubbly (about 2 minutes), so having your milk warm will help you get there faster. It’s going to get a LOT thicker when you add the cheese, so if it’s still a little thin but you want to get on with it, that’s okay too.

Once you’re ready to add the cheese, it’s very important to take it off the heat before so that the sauce doesn’t start to separate. That said, if you’ve been at it for a minute and are still having trouble getting your cheese to melt, pop your pot back on the heat for just a few seconds to bring the temperature up and help the process along.

Once it comes together, you can stir in the macaroni until every noodle is coated.

At this point, you could eat this mac & cheese right now, and it would taste wonderful. But! If you want exceptional mac & cheese, you’ve gotta bake it. So pour it into a pan, then get ready for the best part—the topping. To make it, you’ll combine some breadcrumbs and the remaining half cup of Parmesan in a bowl, then add the olive oil and mix it all up. Sprinkle it on top of the mac and cheese in the casserole dish.

All you have to do is pop it into your preheated 375° oven and bake it until it's golden brown on top. Go ahead, snag a bite before it's fully cooled—you'll really be able to taste all your extra effort.

Do all carbon-fiber plates behave the same way? Sort of. An important lesson to learn here is that the plate isn’t the secret sauce of super shoes—it doesn’t cause propulsion. Instead, the plate, sandwiched between the highly responsive foams, acts as a stabilizer to the super foam. This is why super shoes have such tall stack heights, and why World Athletics caps racing shoe stack height at 40 mm. The super foam—and amount underfoot—is a major advantage.

When choosing a carbon-plate shoe, you’ll want to consider what you’re using it for—racing on the road or trail—and, if possible, have your gait evaluated to determine the best plate angle if you’re looking at shoes specific to running type.

Some plates are full-length or have cutouts. In shoes like the Asics Metaspeed Edge and Metaspeed Sky, the plate’s angle differentiates running types. The Edge’s plate dips lower in the forefoot so cadence runners can roll off their toes faster. The Sky’s plate sits flat in the forefoot, allowing stride runners to leverage bounce on longer steps.

The secret sauce in super shoes is the foam. PEBA (polyether block amide) was once considered the holy grail of all foams and you can still find it in most super shoes, but newer models are using TPEE (thermoplastic polyester elastomer) and A-TPU (aliphatic thermoplastic polyurethane) because they’re more resistant to bottoming out, or going flat. The ride is more durable—and can be quite aggressively bouncy and squishy.

To truly gauge which foam is best for you, trying on the shoe and giving it a run (preferably with some cornering) will help you determine if you want a firmer or squishier ride.

A carbon-fiber plate shoe is an investment, costing anywhere from $240 upward of $500. There are a couple of things to take into account on a personal level before buying: Can you afford a trainer that’s less durable compared to the standard workhorse shoe, which lasts between 450 to 500 miles?

While these super shoes are more durable than the ones made eight years ago, they still have a shorter shelf life due to high-performance foams—PEBA and supercritical blends—wearing down faster. Lastly, is the return there? While brands claim these shoes can improve performance, it’s not guaranteed your running economy will improve by around 4 percent.

I spoke with Ted Fitzpatrick, Saucony’s VP of product management, about the differences. He made these key points between carbon and nylon plates:

A nylon plate is semirigid with some flexibility. A carbon plate is rigid with little or no flexibility. Popsicle analogy—Imagine the plate as the stick that holds the soft ice cream together. The fundamental use of the plates is to keep the dimensional stability of the foam. With each type of plate, each offers different levels of compliance. The plate maintains the forward rolling effect of super shoes. Carbon is the stiffest, which offers the most concentrated effect of speed roll technology. Nylon still adds stiffness, however, it offers better compliance in how the foot transitions in the gait cycle. Some individuals can respond better to different plates. It is down to what works best for the user.

Saucony offers the nylon-plated Endorphin Speed 6, a training shoe that complements its racer, the Endorphin Pro. The shoe can also serve as a more affordable racing shoe, at $180. (The Endorphin Elite 3 and Endorphin Pro 5 cost $290 and $240.)

“The foam is what does the heavy lifting of a super shoe, however it’s the plate that guides how each foam behaves to the user,” says Fitzpatrick. “It is best to train in both to strengthen and build ‘muscle memory’ into different muscle groups.”

However, we wouldn’t recommend solely training in super shoes. For one thing, it would be extremely expensive to use these shoes as daily trainers when they have a shorter shelf life compared to standard everyday shoes. We advise going on a couple of runs before racing in the shoes, to get used to running in them and check for hot spots. While running constantly in these shoes won’t necessarily lead to injury, overuse and the desire to run faster in carbon shoes will likely lead to getting sidelined if you’re not careful.

Naruto's 4 New Episodes: Everything We Know So Far

📅 Mar 10, 2023  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Vanessa Piña has been writing as a journalist for over two years. As a senior writer, she focuses on timely coverage of the latest anime releases and popular comic franchises.

While the Naruto franchise has provided fans with a significant amount of content, setting aside the Boruto anime, it has been nearly a decade since the last release featuring a brand-new adventure centered on Naruto Uzumaki. Although the possibility of receiving new anime episodes once seemed remote, Masashi Kishimoto’s story is returning to the screen in the form of a limited series, giving fans plenty to get excited about.

Originally announced as part of the 20th-anniversary celebration of Naruto's anime adaptation in 2023, the project faced a difficult path and significant delays, leading fans to believe it had been canceled entirely. Fortunately, new information has been revealed regarding the four-episode Naruto anime special, confirming that the project is well underway. From where it fits into the franchise's timeline and which characters will appear, to what to expect from the story and the release date, here's everything known about Naruto's major anime comeback.

In a post on the official Naruto website in March 2023, four new anime episodes of Naruto were revealed to be in the works. On August 29, 2023, less than a week before the first episode was supposed to begin airing, Naruto's anime return was postponed. The sudden decision by the production committee was made in order to "create a work that meets everyone's expectations."

The post on X from the official Naruto account was made with an apology to fans, stating, "We have made the decision to postpone the broadcast due to the further improvement of the quality of this work." At the time, the Naruto anime special wasn't officially canceled; however, in the years that followed, there were no further updates from Studio Pierrot, which created a great deal of uncertainty among fans about whether the project would ever be released. Fortunately, this changed at New York Comic Con 2026, where more details about the anime special were finally revealed.

After three years without any updates, a major announcement regarding the Naruto anime special was made on October 10, 2026, during the franchise’s panel at New York Comic Con. Instead of a conventional anniversary project, Studio Pierrot is preparing a four-episode limited series titled Naruto x Ninku, confirming a crossover with Koji Kiriyama’s legendary anime franchise.

Ninku is not only one of the major ninja-themed manga ever published in Shonen Jump, but it's also a work that Masashi Kishimoto has acknowledged as a major influence on Naruto. In a message shared with fans, Kishimoto expressed his joy about the crossover, revealing that he was the one who proposed the project.

"I created NARUTO because I personally wanted to see the continuation of NINKU. I think many of you have already noticed, but NARUTO was immensely influenced by NINKU. Without NINKU, NARUTO would not exist.

Simply put, Fusuke is Naruto’s secret mentor.

Eventually, I began dreaming of bringing this master-and-student relationship out of the shadows, and into an actual storyline.

Kiriyama Sensei, who is as much a mentor to me as Fusuke is to Naruto, graciously agreed to my selfish request.

By meeting with me in person and with his extensive cooperation, he helped make my long-standing dream a reality." - Masashi Kishimoto

The Ninku anime, which aired between 1995 and 1996, was also produced by Studio Pierrot. While this might have made it easier for the studio to handle this crossover at the request of the creator of Naruto, the creative process and logistics could have taken longer than anticipated, which would explain why the anime special was postponed. Furthermore, Naruto x Ninku is being developed by Pierrot Films, a sub-studio that wasn't established until February 2024.

Although the plot details for the new Naruto episodes were initially a mystery, it was known that the special would be set during the period when Naruto Uzumaki and Team 7 were genin, with official art showing Sasuke Uchiha, Sakura Haruno, and Kakashi Hatake. However, now as a crossover bringing together two classic ninja franchises, Team 7 will cross paths with Fusuke and his friends, Aicho, Toji, Hiroyuki, and Rihoko. Along with the announcement, a teaser was presented in which characters from both series can be seen meeting. The official Naruto website also revealed the synopsis for the limited series.

Team 7 members Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura are given a mission by Kakashi. Since Kakashi cannot participate himself, Naruto is appointed acting leader.

As a result of this decision, Naruto is fully motivated to make the mission a success. However, on the way, Team 7 finds themselves lost in another world. What appears before the bewildered trio is a boy with a face resembling a frog's, named Fusuke. What awaits them in this strange new world?

The crossover is directed by Noriyuki Abe, who worked with both franchises in the past, and the creators of Naruto and Ninku helped oversee the story, while Pierrot Films is handling the production. They were also responsible for Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War, a project that marked a significant leap in the studio's animation quality, so fans can look forward to plenty of ninja action. The official Naruto website features visual material related to the crossover, as well as detailed summaries of each series and its characters, which helps to fuel fans' interest in the new series.

Although it lacks a specific date, unlike the Naruto Card Game, which is scheduled for a global launch in June 2027, its placement on the roadmap suggests that this special could be released during the first half of 2027.

Luckily, fans won't have to wait too long for further updates since the release date for Naruto x Ninku is expected to be confirmed during the Naruto panel at Jump Festa 2027. The panel will take place on Saturday, December 19, 2026.

However, now that Bleach's anime has concluded, the studio’s most prominent series is Black Clover, which is also nearing its end. Given the popularity of Naruto, it is highly likely that fans will see its anime return soon.

40 Greatest Football Players Of All Time (2026)

📅 Dec 21, 2022  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

One of the great football debates will continue to rage on so long as the sport exists. Indeed, deciding who is the greatest footballer of all time is never easy, particularly as fans, wearing their bias boldly, claim their club legend is the best.

It tends to be a common theme that a lot of people consider Cristiano Ronaldo, Lionel Messi, Diego Maradona and Pele to be the most prominent names in the hat for being the best player to have ever played the game, but always in different orders.

Unsurprisingly, all four of those names make it onto our list, but who else joins them in a ranking of the 40 greatest footballers of all-time?

Factors taken into consideration when ranking these players are as follows:

For Liverpool fans, Kenny Dalglish is the club's greatest ever footballer. He scored the winning goal in the 1978 European Cup final, going on to win that competition three more times, while also delivering six league titles to Anfield, not to mention a whole host of other honours. Celtic fans also hold him in similar esteem after he won four league titles, four Scottish Cups, and a Scottish League Cup with the Bhoys, too.

'King Kenny' also made 102 appearances for Scotland, their most ever, and is the joint-leading goalscorer (on 30, with Denis Law). His influence on British football cannot be overstated, as he went on to win the Premier League as Blackburn Rovers manager, having already coached Liverpool to three First Division titles. He truly dominated football throughout the 1970s and 80s.

Michael Laudrup was well and truly one of the most graceful midfielders to ever play the sport. 'I've been trying to be like him since I was a kid," Andres Iniesta once said of his childhood idol. That should tell you all you need to know about the Danish maestro.

Laudrup won La Liga titles with both Barcelona and Real Madrid, and also won Serie A in Italy with Juventus and Eredivisie with Ajax. Despite playing with some of the greatest footballers of all time, both Romario and Raul have described the Dane as the best player they ever played with. Michel Platini once said, “Michael had everything except for one thing: he wasn’t selfish enough.” Perhaps he would rank a little higher on this list if he was, but his unselfishness certainly didn't hold him back much.

Luis Suarez (not to be mistaken with the Uruguayan striker of the same name) was a wonderfully talented Spanish midfielder. Had he been born four or five decades later, he would have slotted seamlessly alongside the likes of Xavi, Iniesta and Sergio Busquets. Instead, he simply led the way for them as a pioneer of the game.

Indeed, Suarez won La Liga twice with Barcelona, which helped him seal the 1960 Ballon d'Or. He would then deliver three Serie A titles for Inter Milan, while also winning two European Cups in the same decade. He played more like an attacking midfielder (bit like Iniesta) in his youth, but then became a deep-lying playmaker (more akin to Xavi) as he aged.

Bobby Moore is one of the true iconic footballing captains of the sport, famously leading England to their only ever World Cup triumph in 1966. The legendary defender was best known at club level for his time with West Ham, leading them to FA Cup glory in 1964, while also winning the Charity Shield and European Cup Winners' Cup with the Hammers.

Pele spent his career making a mockery of centre-backs across the globe, more on that later, but Moore was one of just two men who truly gave the Brazilian a tough time. The late striker recalled: "You see, whoever I played against, I had the best defender marking me, but the best were Bobby Moore and Franz Beckenbauer."

Modern football fans will best know Giuseppe Meazza as the alternate title to the San Siro, after it was renamed in his honour back in March 1980. Spending the best years of his career with Inter, and returning there at the end of his career, winning three Serie A titles and later managing them, it's no wonder he's so loved in those parts. He also briefly played for AC Milan.

His greatest success came at the international level, though, as he led Italy to win two consecutive World Cups – in 1934, and then as captain in 1938. Despite playing so many years ago, only one man has more than his 33 goals for Italy (Luigi Riva, 42). Nicknamed Il genio (The Genius) by the Italian press, Meazza was a true joy to watch and will forever be one of the greatest strikers in football history.

Sir Stanley Matthews is one of the best English players ever. He was knighted in 1965 for his achievements in football while he was still at Stoke City. He remains the only footballer to be knighted during his playing career. Although he came from Stoke, his best achievement came at Blackpool, where he won the FA Cup in 1953.

Three years later, he won the inaugural Ballon d'Or, beating the likes of Alfredo Di Stefano and Ferenc Puskas to first place. He also remains in the English record books for making an appearance in a top-flight match at the age of 50.

Gareth Bale being on this list might surprise some, particularly when someone like Eden Hazard isn't. However, he was consistently world-class and epitomised the idea of 'hard work'. He made a name for himself at Tottenham Hotspur. The Welshman won just one trophy there - the 2008 Carling Cup - but he carried the team on his back.Bale famously broke onto the scene with a hat-trick away to European champions Inter Milan in 2010.

He eventually moved to Real Madrid for a world-record fee where he won everything he could. Bale's overhead kick to win the 2018 Champions League against Liverpool remains one of the greatest goals in the competition's history.

Manuel Neuer is renowned for being one of the first 'modern' goalkeepers, who was extremely skillful with the ball at his feet and has adopted the role of sweeper-keeper for many years. In fact, in 2020, he was awarded the International Federation of Football History & Statistics (IFFHS) goalkeeper of the decade award for his performances between 2011 and 2020.

He has won an abundance of team trophies as well. Most notably, he won the World Cup with Germany in 2014. Domestically, he has won the Bundesliga 13 times and the Champions League twice, with the most recent of these triumphs coming in the 2019/20 campaign.

Carlos Alberto is most well-known for captaining the Brazil team in the 1970 World Cup final. The Selecao beat Italy 4-1 in the final, with Alberto scoring the last goal, which is considered one of the best goals in football history. After a period of swift passes between the men in yellow, Pele laid the ball off to the right-back who smashed the ball into the bottom corner in front of over 107,000 people at Estadio Azteca.

Alberto was recognised for his achievements for club and country in 1998 when he was selected for the World Team of the 20th century. Domestically, he won 16 trophies for Fluminense, Santos and New York Cosmos before eventually retiring in 1982.

Kaka won the Ballon d'Or in 2007 after winning the Champions League with AC Milan. The team and individual achievements solidified his status as one of the greatest players of all time. He also won the World Cup in 2002 in his first year representing his country, highlighting his legendary status at an early stage. For AC Milan, he excelled over six years from 2003-2009, scoring 75 goals in 270 matches.

His spell at Real Madrid in the years was not as impressive, but he still managed to score 29 goals in 120 matches while winning La Liga and the Copa del Rey. The Brazilian will always be remembered as one of the sport's best entertainers — and one of the best Brazilians.
