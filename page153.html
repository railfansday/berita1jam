High-Gloss Furniture Will Be Everywhere in 2027—Here's How Designers Make It Feel Fresh

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

A single lacquered piece can make a neutral room feel instantly more interesting.

Every item on this page was hand-picked by a House Beautiful editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

After years of quiet luxury, soft neutrals, and interiors that favor understated details, a little drama feels like a very welcome change. High-gloss furniture is making a comeback, bringing shine, color, and a bit of personality back into our homes. Whether it's a lacquered accent table, a sculptural console, or a statement cabinet with a reflective finish, these pieces have a way of catching your eye and making even the simplest room feel more interesting. And honestly, who says every piece of furniture has to blend into the background?

The look also ties into Neo Deco, a modern take on Art Deco that brings together glamorous materials, bold silhouettes, and a fresh approach to decorating. But you don't need to turn your living room into a scene from The Great Gatsby to get in on it—a single glossy piece can add just the right amount of contrast to a neutral space, while mixing lacquer with natural wood, textured upholstery, or warm metallic accents keeps things feeling layered and livable.

Ahead, designers share how to bring high-gloss furniture into your home and embrace a little more shine without going over the top.

Not ready to go all-in on the high-gloss trend? An accent table is your low-commitment, high-impact way in. Tucked beside your favorite armchair or pulled up next to the sofa, a lacquered side table brings just enough shine to make the whole corner feel a little more interesting. The best part is the contrast, so have fun mixing finishes. Carol Alda, interior designer at Z Gallerie, loves pairing a glossy table with a “velvet or textured woven chair.” Just make sure the table sits at a comfortable height next to the chair so you can actually use it for your coffee (or cocktail).

Color is another way to make the piece feel right at home. Ali Esmaili, design expert and owner of Esmaili Rugs, suggests pulling the shade of your accent table from a patterned rug, which creates an easy connection between the two. An oxblood, emerald, or classic black table brings plenty of Neo Deco attitude, especially in a sculptural shape. It's a small swap, but one that can give even the most neutral living room a welcome dose of personality.

A little high-gloss feels right at home in a bar or entertaining area, where a touch of drama is practically part of the assignment. A lacquered bar credenza or sideboard in a saturated shade can turn your glassware and favorite bottles into a whole display moment.

Jennifer Fitzpatrick, founder of Studio J & Co., explains that high-gloss finishes bring extra energy to a room by reflecting daylight and catching the flicker of candles and lamps in the evening. In a bar area, that means your decanters, glassware, and cocktail accessories get their own little spotlight. Fitzpatrick recommends pairing high-gloss pieces with unlacquered brass, fluted glass, and textured textiles.

The coffee table is already the unofficial centerpiece of the living room, so why not give it a little extra attention? A lacquered version in a rich color or an unexpected shape can instantly make the space feel more interesting. And don't worry about all that shine feeling too formal. Fitzpatrick says today's high-gloss pieces are all about sculptural shapes, from curved profiles to clean geometric forms. A lacquered cocktail table can also catch the glow of your lamps in the evening, adding a little atmosphere without visually crowding your seating arrangement.

To keep the look feeling relaxed, mix that reflective surface with pieces that have plenty of texture. Esmaili loves pairing high-gloss furniture with vintage rugs, noting that “the gloss adds depth, movement and a touch of theatre.” The contrast between a shiny coffee table and a well-loved rug with plenty of pattern and texture gives the room a more layered, lived-in feel. Add a soft sectional and a few simple accessories, and you've got a statement piece that still feels right at home.

Google confirms huge list of new Home features, including three Nest Thermostat upgrades

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Google Home is constantly rolling out new updates and, today, the company is detailing some of its latest updates including a faster Gemini for Home experience and an upcoming Nest Thermostat upgrade.

In a post today, Google details several Google Home updates that are widely rolling out to users.

The first was announced last week – home keys in Google Wallet. This leverages compatible NFC and UWB (Aliro) locks on newer Pixel and select Samsung Galaxy smartphones, letting you unlock your door by simply tapping your phone.

Cameras are also getting an upgrade for Activity Zones:

…we’ve tightened Activity Zone boundary precision on our newest wired cameras to significantly reduce false alerts from passing cars, animals, and motion just outside your zone borders. We also redesigned the camera settings page to put Activity Zones front and center, and added a confirmation prompt on iOS when exiting an edited zone so you never accidentally lose your custom boundaries.

The Google Home website also gets faster scrubbing and new keyboard shortcuts – Left or Right arrow keys skip back or forward in 5-second increments, rapidly to jump 30 seconds or more.

Gemini for Home continues to get faster, with Google saying that the latest updates have resulted in an overall speed improvement of 20% on “everyday requests.” Gemini for Home has also expanded to six new countries including India, Taiwan, Singapore, Colombia, South Korea, and Brazil starting “next month.”

Finally, Google is also preparing some useful upgrades for Nest Thermostat.

The first is a dashboard for Nest Thermostat users in the US and Canada that shows “estimated cumulative savings” spurred by their thermostat. You’ll be able to see these savings in “the Climate space, thermostat controller, and Favorites widget.” The second is the abiltiy to use third-party room temperature sensors with the 4th-Gen Nest Learning Thermostat. Google has its own sensor for this that sells for $40, but there are more affordable alternatives that often bring more functionality too, so this is a really nice addition. Compatible brands will include IKEA, Eve, and Aqara.

Starting in December, Nest thermostat owners in the U.S. and Canada can check the app to see estimated cumulative savings. And they can also balance hot and cold spots with compatible third-party room sensors with their 4th-gen Nest Learning Thermostat.

A third update is to the Dynamic Farsight faces on the latest Nest Learning Thermostat. “New” faces are coming with “designs to match your aesthetics,” while a new Lunar face will track moon phases.

Dynamic Farsight already lets you see the time, temperature, weather, and air quality from across the room, and starting this December, we’re rolling out new Dynamic Farsight faces with designs that match your aesthetics. Keep an eye out for seasonal animations on the Temperature face, or switch to our new Lunar face to track the phases of the moon right on your wall.

Can you use a keyboard and mouse with an Xbox Series X?

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

There's no question the PS5 has handed the Xbox Series X and S their collective rear ends from a sales perspective this console generation. That's a shame for Microsoft fans. Even though there are drawbacks you should understand before buying an Xbox Series X, for my money, it's still an excellent console. It's also one that supports a rich suite of accessibility options, as does its little sibling. One of the biggest? Fairly robust keyboard and mouse support.

Using traditional PC peripherals on a console might seem a bit outfield, but hey, the Dreamcast was doing it a quarter of a century ago, so there's definite precedent. There are actually a ton of Xbox games that offer at least partial mouse and keyboard support. Let's get into the mouse and keyboard options that should work with the Xbox Series X/S, and also discuss how to set them up. Plus, we'll examine just how many games support non-gamepad control schemes on Microsoft's current-gen consoles.

You sure can... but there are caveats. While Microsoft has done a commendable job at providing accessibility features for Xbox fans, mouse and keyboard support on Xbox Series X/S isn't universal across all games. Additionally, of the titles that do work with PC-based peripherals, not all of them offer native support. That means certain actions in non-native titles will require you to have a controller nearby to progress.

There are more restrictions, too. There's currently no way to connect a Bluetooth mouse or keyboard to either Xbox Series console. For those looking to hook up their favorite PC gizmos, you're looking at either wired peripherals or ones that come with 2.4GHz dongles.

That's slightly annoying. The best Xbox Series X/S accessories are generally wireless because you're likely sitting a decent distance away from your TV while playing your favorite games. Unless you keep your Xbox at a desk like a PC, wired mice and keyboards are probably out of the question for everyday living room play. Dongles it is, then.

Oh, and there's one more catch. You can't use a mouse to navigate around the Xbox dashboard, which is a bit of a bummer.

Setting up a keyboard and mouse on an Xbox Series X/S is relatively simple, but functionality beyond that is going to vary based on the specific game. The easiest and most practical way to get started with PC peripherals on your Xbox is to plug in a wireless keyboard dongle and a mouse dongle: You can use the two USB-A slots on the back of either console, or the one at the front.

From there, you can utilize the keyboard for typing on the Xbox dashboard (though as previously mentioned, mouse navigation is a no-go). Once you launch a game, things are a tad up in the air. The title may recognize your PC-style peripherals immediately, and you can start pointing and clicking. Or, you may well need your trusty Xbox controller to navigate into the settings menu, where you should be able to select mouse and keyboard inputs, if the title supports them. Microsoft provides the platform, but accessibility options are up to the individual developer, so this workflow will vary game to game.

Basically, prepare to always have your gamepad in reach if you want to use typical PC peripherals with an Xbox Series console. Just in case.

A surprising amount. We're not quite talking PC levels here, but the list of Xbox games with current mouse and keyboard support (as reported by Pure Xbox) shouldn't be sniffed at by WASD fans. Well over 100 titles feature levels of mouse and keyboard functionality at present, though native support isn't guaranteed in all of them. That means you'll occasionally have to break out your Xbox controller to navigate menus. If you hop on the Xbox Store, a game's listing page should tell you what level of mouse and keyboard support a title offers. Those that don't natively offer traditional PC controls will be marked as "Limited."

Looking for a few big hitters that support native mouse and keyboard controls on Xbox Series X/S? You'll find Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, Halo Infinite and Gears of War: E-Day. Considering one of those games demands complex simulation controls that never quite felt at home on a pad, and the other two involve landing headshots on alien beasties from afar, mouse and keyboard sounds like a great way to experience all three. I always thought Master Chief's 2021 open world was overly criticized, and now I'm eager to replay it on Xbox, sniping hordes of Brutes with my favorite mouse, the Inphic F9 Mecha.

There are also competitive shooters like Overwatch that support cross-platform play, and will automatically pair you with fellow mouse and keyboard users if the game detects a PC-style control scheme on console. After all, in a fast-paced online shooter, bringing a gamepad to a mouse-and-keyboard match is like wielding a pillow in a lightsaber battle.

There are a number of benefits to using desktop peripherals with an Xbox Series X/S. Some aren't all that sexy — oooh, I can type my custom license plate in Forza Horizon 6 a teeny bit quicker — but others have far more practical use cases.

For simulation or strategy games with multiple mechanical layers and rich inventory systems, mouse and keyboard inputs are simply superior. I've had great experiences with these devices on my Xbox Series X playing Jurassic World Evolution 3, even though the dino-park management title does work pretty well with a controller. Keyboard support also gives you more quick buttons to assign should an Xbox title support it, which can definitely be useful in games that involve heavy inventory management, like Cyberpunk 2077.

The main reason you want keys and a mouse, though? For fans of first-person shooters, gamepads can never come close to the accuracy of blasting bullets with the utter precision of a mouse. Halo Infinite, Overwatch, the Call of Duty series and S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl are all well-situated for mouse and keyboard play, among many other headshot-heavy games.

Is time starting to catch up with Manuel Neuer? At 40, the legendary German goalkeeper is going through a delicate period, regularly disturbed by physical problems which keep him away from the field and weaken his availability. Affected in the right foot for several weeks, the Bavarian goalkeeper recently suffered a partial tear of a tendon during the match against Mainz, won 2-1 by the Munich team. Already forced to miss a few matches due to persistent pain, the 2014 world champion is unavailable for an indefinite period. A situation which reinforces the questions surrounding his succession, while the goalkeeper himself suggested that he could end his career at the end of the 2026-2027 season.

In this context, Bayern Munich would have ticked the name of Robin Risser. According to information from Gianluca Di Marziothe German club has, in fact, made initial contacts to try to attract the RC Lens goalkeeper. Aged 21, the Frenchman has established himself as a starter in the Artesian cages after his arrival in the summer of 2025. His performances and his progress have not gone unnoticed, since he has also attracted the interest of several Premier League teams.

Bayern will therefore have to face foreign competition to hope to complete this operation. The idea of ​​the Munich leaders would be to take action from the winter transfer window of January 2027. The scenario envisaged would consist of recruiting Risser this winter, then leaving him on loan at RC Lens until the end of the season, before integrating him into the Bavarian squad during the next financial year. A strategy that would allow the young goalkeeper to continue his progress in an environment he knows perfectly, while gradually preparing for his arrival in Germany.

Bayern would therefore not necessarily seek to shake up their hierarchy immediately, but rather to secure a future profile. Trained at RC Strasbourg, Robin Risser has experienced several stages in his career, with stints at Dijon and Red Star, before joining Lens during the 2025-2026 season. Now established in the landscape of French and international football, Risser could finally take a new step in his young career. It now remains to be seen whether the first exchanges will turn into concrete negotiations and whether the Bavarian club will manage to convince Lens, but also the player, despite the growing interest of the Premier League.
