40-year-old Josh Johnson making historic Cardinals start not seen since Jim Thorpe

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

It is rarely a positive development for a team that it must start a fourth different quarterback in four weeks.

For the Cardinals, it is somewhat historic.

The quarterback-desperate Cardinals will turn to the ageless but also aged Josh Johnson on Sunday in Kansas City, where the longtime backup will play a game at 40 years and 191 days old.

Arizona Cardinals quarterback Josh Johnson leaves the field after an NFL football game against the Los Angeles Rams in Glendale, Ariz., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Ross D. Franklin

According to Mark Dalton, the organization's senior vice president of media relations, Johnson will become the second-oldest non-specialist to play in a game for the Cardinals. To find the oldest, go all the way to Jim Thorpe in 1928, when the legend was 41 years and 185 days old in a Thanksgiving Day game against the Bears (which did not go well, with the Chicago Cardinals losing 34-0).

Johnson is getting a chance because Jacoby Brissett, who started in Week 8's win in Dallas, was traded to the Ravens; Gardner Minshew II, who started in Week 9's loss in Seattle, required season-ending elbow surgery; and rookie Carson Beck, who started in the Week 10 loss to the Rams, suffered back spasms that will not allow him to play Sunday.

So it is Johnson, who was signed off the Bengals' practice squad earlier this month, getting an opportunity to play a game for an eighth different franchise in a career that began as a fifth-round pick in 2008.

Football player Jim Thorpe poses for a photo between 1910 and 1920. HUM Images/Universal Images Grou

"There is no next step to the playbook, this is going back to the starting table of contents, and that's OK," head coach Mike LaFleur said, according to the team's website. "For a three-and-a-half-hour window, it's my job to figure out as you know whatever we can to get the ball moving forward."

Johnson will not become the oldest active player in the NFL. That title belongs to Aaron Rodgers, who turns 43 on Dec. 2 and will start for the Steelers in Philadelphia on Sunday.

‘His talent is bigger than this performance’ – De Zerbi hails goalscorer Williams-Barnett

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Roberto De Zerbi believes Luca Williams-Barnett's "talent is bigger than this performance" after the 18-year-old impressed on his first start for Tottenham against Ipswich Town.

Williams-Barnett tucked away Pedro Porro's cross in the 15th minute to net his first goal for Spurs, sending the home fans into raptures in north London.

Further chances fell to Mohammed Kudus and Jan Paul van Hecke, before Dominic Solanke struck in the 51st minute to cap off a move that once again involved Williams-Barnett, sealing a 2-0 victory for Spurs.

At 18 years and 51 days old, Williams-Barnett became the youngest player to score for Tottenham in the Premier League since Gareth Bale struck against Fulham in September 2007 (18y 47d).

And his performance has certainly left a big impression on De Zerbi, who had previously labelled the starlet "one of the best young players I have ever worked with."

"He's played very well, but his talent is bigger than this performance," De Zerbi told BBC Match of the Day.

"It was the first game, and I am very happy. I am very delighted with his performance, but he is a big, big, big talent.

"The management of the younger talented players is different because you can't take risks to lose their enthusiasm and their positive mentality.

"But I think Tottenham's dressing room now is a good environment for him.

"I put James [Maddison] to play very close to him to speak the same language but also to help him and put him in the best conditions to play. To play with freedom, to play with his qualities because his qualities are very big.

"He's a mature guy. He is smart and understands the situation. He has great behaviour inside the group and the group is good enough to help him stay calm and to play without pressure, because he has to enjoy it on the pitch.

"He has to work hard but also he has to keep the passion to keep the love of football."

“I am happy because he played with enjoyment, with passion, without pressure.”  Roberto De Zerbi was full of praise for Luca Williams-Barnett after today's win!

Williams-Barnett (18y 51d) became the sixth-youngest player to start a Premier League game for Tottenham, after Stephen Carr (17y 28d), Mikey Moore (17y 77d), Andy Turner (17y 145d), Philip Ifil (17y 270d) and Gareth Bale (18y 41d).

The youngster also became the fourth-youngest player to score on his first Premier League start, behind Daniel Jebbison in May 2021 (17y 276d), Danny Cadamarteri in September 1997 (17y 343d), and Mark Cullen in May 2010 (18y 12d).

Ipswich boss Gary O'Neil, meanwhile, admitted that his side were not at their best at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Indeed, Ipswich are now winless across their last five top-flight games, and they have conceded in each of their last 30 Premier League matches.

"We gave them a really soft first goal, which obviously gives them some comfort," O'Neil said.

"And then we didn't get to our level enough today. We got into some good attacking situations, but we didn't show enough quality.

"We should have dealt with both goals better. So all in all, we can't argue with the result. They were better than us which we have to accept."

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Hansi Flick has been left with a defensive headache ahead of this weekend's clash with Villarreal, with both Andreas Christensen and Pau Cubarsi suffering from injuries.

Flick confirmed Christensen would miss the next two matches after sustaining a calf issue in training, having only just returned from an injury lay-off earlier in the month.

The Danish defender played 30 minutes of Barcelona's 2-1 win over Atletico Madrid before the international break, but his absence offers Flick a problem he must try and tackle.

Cubarsi is another player likely to miss out on Sunday's clash after he was injured during Spain's 3-2 defeat to England in the Nations League, with the 19-year-old replaced at half-time having given away a penalty on Harry Kane in the first half.

"Andreas was really in a good moment, so this is why the decision was made not to go with the national team so he could train here," Flick said of Christensen's injury.

"I think it was the right decision, but unfortunately he's out for maybe two games. He is really disappointed.

"For me, when fit, he's one of the best central defenders. We give him our support and also we have to help him to maybe improve a little physically."

When asked about Cubarsi's issue, Flick added: "It's not a big thing, but we have to take care. He could be on the bench [vs. Villarreal] and we will see if he travels to Baku.

"With Cuba, he played amazingly last season, but with the World Cup he had a lot of minutes, and he's only 19 years old. So, it's now about taking care of him."

These reports have since been debunked, and not only has MobLand already been renewed for a third season, but Hardy is also confirmed to be back in the leading role for the next batch of episodes. In the last few years, Hardy has focused so much on his role as Eddie Brock in the Venom movies that he hasn't had as much time to star in other projects, but his future is more open now that his time as Eddie Brock is seemingly behind him.

Before starring as Venom in the SSU (Sony's Spider-Man Universe), though, Hardy had experience in some of the most epic sci-fi thrillers of the 2010s. One of the first projects that comes to mind is Mad Max: Fury Road, the 2015 sci-fi masterpiece co-starring Charlize Theron that was written and directed by George Miller.

However, several years before the release of Mad Max: Fury Road, Hardy teamed up with acclaimed filmmaker Christopher Nolan for one of the most mind-bending sci-fi epics of all time, Inception, which also stars Leonardo DiCaprio and Cillian Murphy. It's been over 15 years since Inception grossed over $830 million at the box office, and although the film doesn't have a streaming home in America, it's been in the VOD top 10 for over 300 days this year.

Inception follows a thief named Cobb (played by Leonardo DiCaprio) who has a unique skill set: He can infiltrate someone's dream and get into their head to steal corporate secrets and other valuable information. Your PC's USB port can charge your phone, even when it's turned off — Here's how

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Your USB ports might have a hidden "Always On" mode that charges your phone with the PC shut down.

Almost all PC USB ports supply enough current to charge a device, but not all continue to do so when the PC is shut down. This convenient feature can also be confusing, since each brand tends to have its own terminology. For example, Lenovo calls it an "Always On" port while Dell uses the term PowerShare. Others stick with icon labels, like a battery symbol or a lightning bolt. The best way to know for sure is to dig into your PC's manual, confirm the markings and learn how to enable it.

Depending on the system, USB standby power might be on by default or be part of an energy-saving setting that cuts power when the PC is off. Your desktop or motherboard documentation should have this information. Fortunately, testing the feature is fairly simple: plug your phone in, turn the PC off and see if your phone continues charging.

If it stops charging, it's safe to assume the setting is off, which you can rectify in the BIOS or UEFI on most Windows PCs. (If your PC never supported the feature, changing settings won't help.) Restart the PC and press the key the startup screen tells you to (usually F2, Del or F10). Look under Advanced, Power or Chipset for a USB power delivery or Always On charging option and enable it. If you see ErP (Energy-related Products) or Deep Sleep settings, make sure they're disabled. Save and exit.

You don't need a computer science or software engineering degree to do this, just a little patience as you wait for the PC to boot up and press the right key. The menu can be confusing at times, but you're unlikely to brick your PC.

For starters, energy-saving features may get in the way. Anything that uses standby power will conflict with firmware settings that cut power to reduce consumption. For instance, if ErP Ready is enabled in select ASUS NUC PCs, USB standby power during shutdown or hibernation is disabled. Check your system's instructions before changing anything.

You also may not have to turn off every single energy-saving feature just to charge your phone when your PC is off. Keep in mind that changing the energy-saving settings won't add post-shutdown charging if the system never supported it to begin with.

Laptops are an entirely different case, since they often come with battery reserve limitations. Depending on the model, USB charging may stop when the laptop's battery hits a reserve threshold. Laptop charging features may depend on an AC adapter after shutdown, and that's a potential restriction that shouldn't be confused with a desktop's standby power settings. Make sure your USB plug is completely in the port and that the cable is reliable, not one that's been sitting in an attic box for the last quarter century.

In this context, "Always On" refers to power availability, but that's not the same thing as "charging only." A supported port can continue to supply power while the computer is off, though the wording shouldn't be taken literally. Firmware settings can disable that power, and the behavior may be different between ports on the same system.

Power and data transfers are also different capabilities. A phone charging while the PC is shut down doesn't mean you can transfer photos from your phone or copy files to it. The specific feature here is all about electricity available via the USB connection.

Another problem is the lack of consistency in names, setup processes and USB port symbols. One system may offer a dedicated USB power setting while another's ports depend on power-saving configurations. Even within a single brand, capabilities and terminology can differ, and manufacturers implement "Always On" USB in drastically different ways.

For example, "Always On" could mean the feature is active after shutdown, during hibernation or while your PC is asleep. It could mean two of them, just one or all three. The feature may come with symbols, color coding, terminology or fine print in a manual, but not necessarily all of the above.
