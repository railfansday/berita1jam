England have made medical checks on Cole Palmer as Thomas Tuchel considers whether to select the Chelsea forward in his next squad.

The 24-year-old missed all four games during the previous international window with a minor injury.

He completed 90 minutes for Chelsea against Brentford before those matches, and started for the Blues in the first game afterwards.

Tuchel, who names his squad to face Croatia and Spain on Friday, said at the time that Palmer was "playing with discomfort and pain" but was "missing too many opportunities" with England.

Whether he is recalled will be a key theme on Friday. While it is normal for England medical staff to check on players under consideration, it also underlines that Palmer remains on Tuchel's radar despite his stark comments in September.

In addition to deciding whether to select Palmer, head coach Tuchel has a number of other dilemmas to resolve.

Bukayo Saka's injury absence opens the door to another attacker, with his Arsenal team-mate Noni Madueke pushing for a recall following a string of impressive performances.

Manchester United forward Marcus Rashford has not played since mid-October and is not fit for selection, while Chelsea defender Trevoh Chalobah is a major injury doubt.

Defenders Ben White and Djed Spence are pushing for recalls after missing out on the previous squad through injury.

The likes of Harry Kane, Jude Bellingham, Declan Rice and many others are now stonewall selections - but the intrigue is around the remaining few spots still seemingly up for grabs.

49ers linked to Jonathan Taylor in trade rumor that makes zero sense originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

San Francisco 49ers fans should expect the team to scour the NFL trade deadline market this season in search of more help in pursuit of a Super Bowl run.

After adding DeForest Buckner in a big trade with the Indianapolis Colts, they could double-dip and make another trade with the Colts this season. But a recent rumor pointed towards someone totally unexpected: Colts' running back Jonathan Taylor.

Mike Florio of Pro Football Talk shared a wild rumor that some in league circles are wondering if the 49ers will make a big splash and land Taylor in a stunning trade. This, simply put, would make zero sense for the 49ers.

"Chris Simms mentioned on Thursday's PFT Live that some in league circles are wondering whether the 49ers could make a play for Colts running back Jonathan Taylor," Florio writes.

This would be a stunning trade for the 49ers to make for so many reasons. One of which is the massive price tag it'd take to land Taylor, likely well north of a second-round pick, and close to what they spent on Christian McCaffrey back in 2022.

While McCaffrey might not be with the team long-term, as he's getting older and has dealt with injury issues in years past, trading for Taylor now wouldn't make sense.

MORE: NFL insiders reveal four landing spots for Colts' Jonathan Taylor

Why would the 49ers trade for a second star running back? Those picks would be a lot more useful if they were spent to acquire a wide receiver or more defensive help.

Adding Taylor would easily make for the best RB duo in the league, but it would be unnecessary, as both Taylor and McCaffrey are bell-cow backs. Not to mention there is optimism about Kaelon Black.

This rumor from Florio makes absolutely no sense. There is no scenario in which adding Taylor would help the 49ers enough to justify the cost, unless McCaffrey were to be ruled out for the remainder of the year.

This is one of those trade rumors that will be forgotten about in due time, as there is effectively no chance the 49ers land Taylor. It's purely speculation, and it's the kind that makes zero sense.

Russell Brand Trial Reveals Worst & Best Of TV Industry That Protected, Then Exposed A Predator

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Russell Brand has been convicted of rape and sexual assault after spending the best part of a month on trial. In many ways, the business that propelled Brand to fame and power was also in the dock.

In the first major #MeToo court case for the British television industry, London’s Southwark Crown Court heard from multiple complainants and witnesses about a culture that empowered Brand’s voracious sexual appetite — and did little to stop him crossing boundaries of consent.

As his former assistant put it in court: “I have worked with celebrities before and have seen a lot of people normalizing bad behavior, but this was on another level.” Related Stories News Russell Brand's Lawyer Rounds On Journalist Who Exposed Brand's Behavior News Russell Brand Found Guilty Of Rape & Sexual Assault

Four of the six women who made police complaints about Brand worked in the television and radio industry. Three of those four convinced a jury that they were the survivors of brazen sexual assault when Brand was at the peak of his powers. Watch on Deadline

Brand was convicted of grabbing the breasts of an ex-MTV employee at an industry party in 2004. He was found guilty of pushing a woman against a wall and molesting her at the offices of a major radio company in 2004 or 2005. Brand was also convicted of burying his head in the genitals of a former Channel 4 employee during the recording of a DVD in 2009.

In each case, the consequences for Brand were not obvious, despite the women raising concerns about his behaviour shortly after they were assaulted.

The ex-MTV employee was introduced to Brand by a well-known TV producer who was hosting the party at London’s Nordic Bar. In Brand’s words, he was told by the producer, “There’s a bird here who wants to meet you.” Within minutes, Brand had sexually assaulted her. It’s no wonder the prosecution argued that Brand treated women in the industry as a “disposable commodity.”

The ex-MTV employee raised concerns with the producer after the party and the court heard how he confronted Brand’s associates and told them the behavior was “not acceptable.” It was not enough to stall Brand’s rising star. Asked by Brand’s defense why she didn’t initially report the allegation to the police, the woman said she feared being “seen as trouble” and “the media industry protects its own.”

The former Channel 4 employee was sexually assaulted in front of two producers, one of whom continues to work on prominent British shows. In evidence, she said the producers shrugged as Brand demanded a massage and put his head between her legs, before laughing. She reported the incident to her Channel 4 line manager at the time, but it’s taken 17 years for her complaint to be fully heard.

In a statement after Brand’s conviction, a Channel 4 spokesperson said: “Our thoughts are first and foremost with the six women involved in the case against Russell Brand, including those affected by the crimes for which he has been convicted. We recognise the profound impact of these crimes and commend the extraordinary courage of all those who gave evidence as prosecution witnesses at trial.”

While Channel 4 did not do enough to protect its former employee in 2009, the British broadcaster can legitimately claim credit for eventually bringing Brand to justice. The network partnered with The Times and The Sunday Times on an investigation into Brand’s conduct, resulting in the 2023 documentary Russell Brand: In Plain Sight.

The film was referenced repeatedly during the Southwark Crown Court trial. It did not feature any of the women at the heart of criminal proceedings but was the catalyst for their police complaints. It was the reason why, as the prosecution explained, the women “realized they are not alone and have bravely come before you to tell the truth.”

Louisa Compton, Channel 4’s director of news, digital and audience trust, put it like this: “The outcome of the Russell Brand trial belongs to all of the women who had the courage to speak out … The trial heard that women involved in the criminal case came forward after seeing the Channel 4 Dispatches film. It is a powerful demonstration of why fearless, independent, investigative journalism in the public interest matters.”

Russell Brand may have been on trial, but the court proceedings revealed the worst and best of the British TV industry.

2028 QB Tristin Gaines sets commitment date, Oregon among finalists

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Not long ago, the Oregon Ducks were well-positioned to land one of the top quarterbacks in the 2028 recruiting cycle as their first commitment in the class. Now, their chances are less likely, but a top target is set to come off the board very soon.

2028 quarterback Tristin Gaines, the No. 9 quarterback and No. 85 player overall in the class, is set to announce his commitment on Nov. 14, with his group of finalists including Oregon, SMU, Houston, LSU and Florida. He will announce his decision live on the Rivals YouTube channel.

The Ducks have been closely linked to Gaines for some time, and even started to grow closer to him than fellow quarterback recruit Josiah Boyd, who ultimately committed to UCLA. Gaines has been to Eugene for games twice this fall after picking up an offer in the summer, and it looked like just a matter of time before the Texas native landed in Oregon's class.

However, as things often do in recruiting, the race has taken a turn. SMU, almost four hours north of Gaines' home in Waller, has been "setting the pace" in the recruitment, according to Rivals' Sam Spiegelman, and is considered the favorite right now. Gaines will visit LSU this weekend, for the second time in a month, as the Tigers host Alabama.

Gaines is a dual-threat under center, passing for 2,016 yards and 22 touchdowns as a sophomore while rushing for 542 yards and nine more scores. In nine games this season, Gaines has completed 56% of his passes for 2,223 yards, 24 touchdowns and three interceptions while rushing for 580 yards and two scores. Gaines is also a star on the baseball diamond, winning a pair of gold medals with USA Baseball.

Oregon has continued to make Gaines a priority in their class, but the Ducks appear to have lost some steam in recent weeks, and the team's disappointing season and firing of offensive coordinator Drew Mehringer could have some impact. Quarterbacks coach Koa Ka'ai has been leading the recruitment for Gaines and even went to visit him last month.

The Ducks are still searching for their first commitment in the 2028 class. Oregon has some ground to make up, but no matter what direction the commitment goes, Gaines will likely still be recruited by Oregon heading into the offseason.
