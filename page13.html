Former NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang described Collette Kress, the company’s CFO who has now replaced him as interim CEO, as “a deer in the headlights” during a presentation earlier in the summer, the Wall Street Journal reported, citing an anonymous source.

Huang made the comment during a meeting with Nvidia’s finance team about the company’s plans to provide guarantees worth billions of dollars to data center operators that couldn’t afford to buy its chips upfront, according to the report.

He reportedly also said Kress was a person who couldn’t help him steer strategy or decision-making.

Kress has been with Nvidia for over 13 years now, after serving as SVP and CFO of Cisco’s Business Technology Operations Finance. The report said she has questioned Nvidia’s program-focused, credit-based deals involving Huang’s son-in-law Nico Caprez, and wanted to know how to decide which deals are beneficial, how to value guarantees, and determine returns.

Earlier in the year, she had scaled back on Nvidia’s commitment for OpenAI’s ambitious Ohio data center project. The company was in talks to provide $250 billion in credit to OpenAI, but ended up committing $105 billion.

Huang resigned Thursday after reports of two extramarital affairs, including one with the company’s VP for healthcare, Kimberly Powell. The company is also investigating a possible Chinese espionage plot.

After his resignation, Reuters reported that he was having a third affair with another employee. Nvidia is currently searching for a permanent CEO to replace Huang, who had the top job for over 33 years.

How Manchester United could line up in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game vs Liverpool

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Mauricio Pochettino is officially set to become the next Manchester United manager, and his first game will come just days after his start date.

The Red Devils return from the final international break of 2026 to face the daunting prospect of visiting arch-rivals Liverpool in the Premier League on Sunday, November 22.

Pochettino inherits a side with just one win in 10 top-flight games this term, a string of results that have left Man Utd in the relegation zone.

The Argentine’s style of play centres around a high-press and an ability to build from the back - two things United have struggled with so far this season.

Injuries across the pitch will impact Pochettino’s selection, with Carlos Baleba and Benjamin Sesko definitely ruled out for the trip to Anfield.

In goal, Senne Lammens is established first-choice and that is unlikely to change with Pochettino’s arrival.

Interestingly, Pochettino might be tempted to copy Carrick and play Leny Yoro at right-back. Out of possession the right-back tucks in to form a three-man defence in Pochettino’s favoured system, but with an actual left-back fit, there is little reason to play defenders out of position.

For that reason, Diogo Dalot will likely start at right-back, with a much more simple defensive approach. Yoro and Ayden Heaven in the heart of defence could give United a bit more pace than a backline featuring Harry Maguire, but the experience of Lisandro Martinez may be hard to ignore as Pochettino balances a squad lacking in confidence.

Patrick Dorgu seems a strong fit at left-back. Antonee Robinson loved to get forward for Pochettino while on USA duty, and the wing-back is a proven attacking threat.

With the USA, Pochettino has settled on a 4-3-3 formation, with the midfield featuring a sitting player behind two more proactive team-mates.

Pochettino’s immediate issue with the United squad will be the fact that there is no clear candidate as a holding midfielder. The injured Baleba does well at screening the backline, but his strengths rely on driving forward with the ball.

It is a similar story for Andrey Santos - a midfielder that is still unsure whether his best position is as a No6, 8, or 10.

The deep-lying playmaker role is a battle between Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans. Pochettino has shown a tendency to trust young, talented players, so it would not be a surprise to see the former Tottenham manager try and work his magic with Mainoo.

Tielemans could play the box-to-box role alongside Mainoo, offering experience during the transition to Pochettino’s ideal approach.

While Pochettino has utilised a deeper player in the middle of the park with the USA, it is likely that he will instead have to build a more attacking midfield at United.

That is because club captain Bruno Fernandes is clearly the Red Devils’ best player, which merits his inclusion.

Matheus Cunha looks a good fit on the left wing as a creative spark, while Bryan Mbeumo’s pace down the right wing would allow Pochettino to create a similar attacking shape to his USA side.

However, with United’s striking options limited, it would not be a surprise to see Mbeumo lead the line, with Amad Diallo playing instead on the right.

Bastoni avoids suspension despite yellow card confusion during Italy 3-1 France

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Alessandro Bastoni will be available for selection when Italy take on Belgium in their final Nations League group stage fixture on Sunday evening as he did not pick up a yellow card during Thursday’s 3-1 victory over France, despite reports from UEFA suggesting that he had.

In UEFA Nations League matches, players earn one-match suspensions for every even number of yellow cards they receive, i.e. after every second, fourth and sixth booking.

Bastoni was one of five players in Italy’s matchday squad against France who had been carrying a yellow card, along with Gianluigi Donnarumma, Nicolo Fagioli, Antonio Vergara and Gianluca Scamacca. Moise Kean, Daniel Maldini, Gianluca Mancini and Matteo Ruggeri had also been booked before the France game, but were not in the squad on Thursday night.

There were momentary fears on Thursday night when UEFA reported that Bastoni had been shown a yellow card during the second half of the France victory, which would have earned him a suspension for Sunday’s test away against Belgium.

However, it has since been confirmed by the FIGC that this was an error in reporting and that the yellow card had actually been shown to Davide Frattesi. Neither Bastoni nor Frattesi will be suspended against Belgium.

Pio Esposito was also booked against France, but this was his first yellow card of the Nations League campaign and he also remains eligible to face Belgium on Sunday.

UK, Singapore, and Thailand central banks complete foreign exchange settlement trial linking traditional and tokenized systems

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The Reserve Bank of Australia and Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas participated as observers, while the Bank of England plans to launch a live synchronisation interface in 2028.

Three central banks have completed tests of a cross-border foreign exchange settlement system connecting traditional payment infrastructure with distributed ledger technology.

In a joint statement on Friday, the Bank of England, Monetary Authority of Singapore, and Bank of Thailand said they conducted the experiments using a prototype developed by Partior, which coordinated payment-versus-payment (PvP) settlement and multilateral net FX transactions across their test environments.

The three central banks are working under Project Meridian FX APAC, announced last November. The project encompassed multiple currencies, technologies, settlement infrastructures, and time zones, with the Reserve Bank of Australia and Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas also participating as observers, the banks said.

According to the statement, Partio's system matched settlement instructions, coordinated the reservation of funds, monitored payment readiness, and initiated settlement.

The experiment also examined multilateral netting, which allows banks to offset payments against one another to reduce the liquidity needed for settlement.

However, the accompanying policy report identified questions around operational resilience, settlement finality, governance, liability, and cross-border data sharing. It also warned that additional functions, including holding funds or calculating net positions, could change an operator's regulatory responsibilities.

Tom Mutton, the BOE's executive director of payments, innovation and fintech, said the project provided "practical evidence of how synchronized settlement could help reduce risk and improve efficiency in cross-border FX transactions."

The BOE plans to launch a live synchronization interface in 2028. Findings from Project Meridian FX APAC will inform further work on synchronized settlement.

Il fondo americano guidato da Gerry Cardinale ha annunciato il lancio di Milan Sports & Entertainment (MSE), una holding destinata a riunire i propri investimenti a Milano nei settori dello sport, delle infrastrutture e dell'intrattenimento. Tra i progetti contenuti nel comunicato di presentazione compare espressamente lo sviluppo di "una nuova destinazione per lo sport e l'intrattenimento dal vivo a San Donato". Una conferma importante, dopo che il club rossonero aveva accantonato il progetto di realizzare il proprio stadio nell'area a sud di Milano, scegliendo invece di procedere insieme all'Inter sul futuro di San Siro.

Al momento RedBird non ha fornito dettagli sul tipo di struttura che intende realizzare a San Donato, né sui tempi o sull'entità dell'investimento. Il comunicato si limita a indicare la volontà di sviluppare una nuova destinazione dedicata allo sport e agli eventi dal vivo, senza precisare quali attività potrebbe ospitare. È comunque un'indicazione significativa sulla strategia del fondo americano, che evidentemente continua a considerare San Donato un'area di interesse per i propri investimenti. Non è stato chiarito se e in che misura queste attività potranno riguardare anche San Donato. Più in generale, MSE valuterà una possibile franchigia milanese nella futura NBA Europe e opportunità nel tennis internazionale. Per Cardinale si tratta di declinare in una dimensione cittadina l'esperienza maturata nei settori dello sport e dell'intrattenimento: "Oggi applichiamo questa esperienza su una scala diversa: non a un solo club, a un solo sport o a un solo impianto, ma a un'intera città e a un portfolio che comprende sport, lifestyle e intrattenimento".

Mauricio Pochettino will leave USMNT for Manchester United after Haiti matches

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Mauricio Pochettino will officially join Manchester United after the current United States men's national team camp concludes, bringing an end to his two-year tenure in the job that was headlined by this year's World Cup on home soil.His final games in charge will be the two-legged Concacaf Nations League semifinals against Haiti, the first of which will take place on Saturday at Miami's Nu Stadium while the second will be on Tuesday at Cincinnati's TQL Stadium.For the USMNT, the timing is awkward. Though the next World Cup is still four years away, Pochettino signed a new four-year deal in August and is currently with the team at U.S. Soccer's national training center in the suburbs of Atlanta. He spent the week with the USMNT while his representatives worked out a deal for him to join United.

"That news kind of got sprung on us, like the rest of the world, I'd say," Robinson said. "Similar things happened in the past, happened at the World Cup where huge teams lose their manager and they start looking at possible candidates and for us to have a manager who's linked with those sorts of jobs means we're very lucky to work with a top-level manager but it never seems to leak into the work we do, whether it's the World Cup or whether it's going to be this camp."

Pochettino was the first USMNT head coach with genuine international appeal, U.S. Soccer more often than not picking from a domestic talent pool in the team's modern history. His tenure has reflected just that – he became the federation's highest-paid coach ever when he initially joined in 2024, the salary and the scope of his role only increasing in his recent contract extension. U.S. Soccer surprisingly plucked him out of the European club game, where he had spent the entirety of his coaching career beforehand, making a name for himself at Tottenham Hotspur before later stops at Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea. Naturally, clubs of that ilk never lost interest and over the course of his time with the USMNT, he was linked to a handful of jobs. The most notable approach came from AC Milan, who were considering the move in the build-up to the World Cup but ultimately hired ex-Manchester United coach Ruben Amorim instead.

"Even when we first met a couple summers ago, there were lots of other people interested in having Mauricio and team join," U.S. Soccer CEO J.T. Batson said in May. "He had standing offers from other places to come. He wanted to be here. He's a big believer in what we're doing at U.S. Soccer. He's a big believer in soccer in America and he's a big believer in this men's team and so, throughout this entire process, when you have top-class talent, whether it's salespeople, whether it's marketing people or whether it's coaches, other organizations want them and throughout this, Mauricio and team have been incredibly transparent. They've been great partners and are, of course, excited about the summer … There's been a longer list of outreach than what has even been reported and so they've been great about that."

Pochettino has also been open about his desire to return to the Premier League during his stint with the USMNT, something the move to United will accomplish.

United are currently in the relegation zone with one win in 10 games, the least of any team in the Premier League this season.

Pochettino's track record with the USMNT is not exactly notable – he has 21 wins in 45 games, nearly all of them against teams below them in the FIFA rankings. They stumbled in a variety of ways when they attempted to punch above their weight, most notably in the World Cup round of 16 loss to Belgium that was tinged by the Folarin Balogun affair. His attempts to lead the USMNT to a statement-making run at a World Cup on home soil were mixed at best, since the team could not pass the last 16 hurdle they have been stuck at since 2010, but Pochettino still found a way to recalibrate the U.S. team in his two years in charge.

The specifics of the round of 16 elimination were a surprise, even if the exit was not. The U.S. team's player pool, talented as it was at the World Cup, was not considered amongst the world's elites so a genuinely deep run over the summer felt improbable. Pochettino, though, managed to get the best out of the group and had the USMNT playing in a way it rarely had previously – in an entertaining, high-intensity, attack-minded system. The style is a hallmark of Pochettino's coaching career but has been historically difficult to integrate at the international level, with teams usually gathering for 10 days at a time, weeks or months apart, and has historically not been the USMNT's strategy. Pochettino's predecessor Gregg Berhalter, for example, was more reserved in his tactical approach and before him, the U.S. team were known more for grit than style.

Slowly but surely, Pochettino's approach worked with the generation of players Berhalter brought in. He correctly identified that the player pool's strengths were in attack, forwards Balogun and Christian Pulisic continuing to serve as central figures for the team. More notably, though, Pochettino found use for players Berhalter had previously cast aside – midfielder Malik Tillman became a key player, his versatility something Pochettino counted as an asset. Forward Ricardo Pepi also rose up the ranks in the build-up to the World Cup, his first after being one of the last omissions from Berhalter's 2022 team.

Pochettino's legacy might actually be the player pool shakeup he oversaw on the road to the World Cup, which felt rushed because of the short timeline but was ultimately useful. Defender Alex Freeman was the breakout of the Pochettino years, going from relative obscurity at Orlando City to a World Cup starter and goalscorer in less than 500 days. Midfielder Sebastian Berhalter, the son of Pochettino's predecessor, was not on the national team's radar until his first call-up in 2025 and looks like a national team fixture for years to come. The player pool expansion project will only get more exciting from here with the USMNT's up-and-comers, with some like Cavan Sullivan and Adri Mehmeti poised to be some of the best players in the history of the men's program.

The coach's departure will sting in some ways, but his tenure ensured that the USMNT would be in a position of strength, which was hardly the case when he arrived. He inherited a team that had lost its way after a group stage exit in the Copa America and will leave behind a team with a clear enough roadmap to the 2030 World Cup. The Pochettino years prove that the next head coach should primarily be a strong tactical fit, no matter the candidate's global appeal, because he was right to identify that an attack-minded approach would suit the player pool. That's especially true with the up-and-comers since many of the brightest prospects are attackers.

There is no reason U.S. Soccer should not hire the best fit for the job but for perhaps the first time in the program's history, the USMNT's prospects might truly rest with the players at their disposal. Pochettino's successor will be chiefly tasked with getting the best out of them, much as he did with the U.S. team's core. As an exciting generation of talent finds their footing with the senior team, that's exactly how it should be.

Manchester United have confirmed an agreement with Mauricio Pochettino to become their next head coach, with the Argentine set to take charge after the international break.

The 54-year-old will replace Michael Carrick, who was dismissed on Thursday following United’s disastrous start to the Premier League season. Pochettino is set to sign a two-and-a-half-year contract that will keep him at Old Trafford until the summer of 2029.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss is currently managing the United States national team but is expected to fly straight to Manchester after completing his USA international games.

Pochettino will first oversee the USA’s CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final ties against Haiti on Saturday and next Tuesday. He is expected to arrive in Manchester on Wednesday before taking charge of the club on Thursday.

Pochettino’s first taste of action as a United manager will be against Liverpool at Anfield on November 22.

Pochettino brings considerable Premier League experience after previous spells with Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea. He has also managed Paris Saint-Germain before the USA job.

His appointment makes him Manchester United’s eighth permanent manager since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013.

Carrick’s departure came less than six months after he signed a two-year contract. His second spell in charge ended after United managed just one win in their opening 10 league matches, leaving them in the relegation zone.

Carrick’s coaching staff, including Jonny Evans, Jonathan Woodgate, Steve Holland, Travis Binnion and goalkeeping coach Craig Mawson, have all left alongside Carrick.

Major crypto exchange Coinbase is set to suspend trading in 12 crypto trading pairs as part of an effort to improve overall market health and consolidate liquidity on the platform. This decision affects two trading pairs involving Cardano (ADA) and Stellar (XLM): ADA/ETH and XLM/BTC.

Coinbase said it regularly monitors the markets on its exchange and, in line with this, trading will be suspended on the named markets on November 25, 2026. In an effort to improve overall market health and consolidate liquidity, Coinbase will be removing a number of non-USD trading pairs for supported assets.

According to an announcement, Coinbase will suspend trading on the following markets on November 25, 2026, at or around 12 PM ET: APE-USDT, ATOM-USDT, ALGO-BTC, CRV-BTC, ETC-BTC, ICP-BTC, XLM-BTC, APE-EUR, ADA-ETH, 1INCH-GBP, CHZ-GBP, SNX-GBP.

However, Coinbase Advanced Trade users in eligible regions can still trade these assets in the USD order books.

In light of this, APE-USDT, ATOM-USDT, ALGO-BTC, CRV-BTC, ETC-BTC, ICP-BTC, XLM-BTC, APE-EUR, ADA-ETH, 1INCH-GBP, CHZ-GBP have been moved to limit-only mode on Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Advanced.

In recent delistings, Coinbase has disabled trading for Balancer (BAL) and Tensor (TNSR), but users will continue to have the ability to withdraw their funds at any time.

The suspension of trading for Stargate Finance (STG) and Vulcan Forged (PYR) has also been postponed to November 20, 2026, at or around 2 PM ET. Based on recent reviews, Coinbase will suspend trading for Ampleforth Governance Token (FORTH) on November 23, 2026, at or around 2 PM ET. Trading for Neon EVM (NEON) and Syndicate (SYND) will be suspended on November 16, 2026, at or around 2 PM ET.

Spot trading for Pearl (PEARL) will go live on November 13, 2026. The opening of the PEARL-USD trading pair will begin at or after 9 AM PT, if liquidity conditions are met, in regions where trading is supported.

Perpetual futures trading for GRAM, GRASS, QNT and ZRO will go live on November 13, 2026, at or after 12:00 PM UTC.
