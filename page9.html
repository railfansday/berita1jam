📅 Nov 06, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Joanna Gaines detailed how she and husband Chip Gaines “create magic” in their 23-year marriage, sharing what makes their relationship even "stronger" over time.

If your relationship is in need of a fixer upper, Joanna Gaines has the blueprint for you.

The HGTV star shared insight into her long-lasting marriage with Chip Gaines, revealing what she believes is the secret to their strong bond after over 25 years together.

“I love just how opposite we are,” Joanna shared on the Nov. 4 episode of the Not Gonna Lie podcast. “I do feel like once you understand that Chip’s strengths and my strengths are very different, but together, it really does create magic. There’s a tension there, but then there’s power there that really happens when you embrace it.”

“For both of us, we’re OK evolving,” she continued to host Kylie Kelce. “It’s not like, ‘This is who I am, and that’s who you are.’”

The Fixer Upper star—who shares kids Drake, 21, Ella, 19, Duke, 18, Emmie, 16, and Crew, 8, with Chip—emphasized that her husband of 23 years is “challenging me every day.”

As Joanna put it, the couple holds “each other’s differences with this respect and almost this awe.”

And the pair—who tied the knot in May 2003—lean into the less comfortable moments, even if they aren’t easy.

“Even though I call it chaos,” Joanna explained, “but finding that discomfort in every season and working through that together really does make you stronger.”

“There’s always a challenge so that we’re never just comfortable with each other,” she added. “And we’re always learning something new together.”

As for what following this mindset can look like? The Magnolia founder shared that she’s seen a “shift happening,” in which she’s becoming “more extroverted” like her husband and vice versa.

“It’s like dating all over again,” she noted, “getting to know each other.”

Ultimately, Joanna and Chip are always encouraging each other to be their best selves.

“So enjoying the unfolding, enjoying the evolution,” she shared, “but also always pushing each other to a way that’s good.”

“He really is my best friend,” the 48-year-old added. “I love it.”

Five9 buys contact center intelligence startup Level AI after posting narrow earnings beat

📅 Nov 06, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Contact center company Five9 Inc. flexed its acquisitive muscles today as it announced a $325 million all-cash deal to buy an artificial intelligence-native rival called Level AI as it posted its third-quarter financial results. The deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter, subject to customary closing conditions, Five9 said.

Five9 is a leading player in the cloud contact center software industry, selling tools and services that enterprises use to manage their customer service interactions. Its web-based software spans traditional phone calls, video calls, emails, messaging, social media and other channels.

As for Level AI, it’s the developer of a contact center and customer experience intelligence platform that uses artificial intelligence to support human agents on calls and analyze, score and augment their interactions with the callers they serve. Its platform is designed to listen into customer service agent’s calls, so it can provide real-time guidance throughout and support workflow automation. The insights it generates can also be used to assist in agent coaching.

Level AI also develops autonomous AI agents that can automate some simpler customer inquiries and background operational tasks, freeing up human agents to focus on more complex customer issues. “Level AI’s AI applications help organizations improve agent performance, automate more customer interactions across voice and digital channels, and turn conversations into insights the whole organization can use,” Five9 said in a blog post announcing the deal. “Its applications are in production with enterprise customers today and handle more than one billion customer interactions a year.”

Five9 said it intends to continue offering Level AI’s applications as standalone tools for organizations that don’t want to shift their on-premises contact center operations to the cloud, also those who are using other cloud contact center platforms. However, it also plans to integrate the startup’s tools with its Genius CX Platform. “As AI reshapes the customer experience landscape, we believe adding Level AI strengthens our competitive position, expands the customers we can serve, and deepens the AI talent behind our continuous innovation,” said Five9 chief executive officer Amit Mathradas.

Level AI founder and CEO Ashish Nagar said that by joining Five9, his company will be able to bring its applications and customer service-native AI models to thousands of new contact centers around the world, while allowing it to continue supporting its existing customers.

Investors don’t seem to have shared the CEOs’ enthusiasm for the deal, however, for Five9’s stock fell more than 23% in late trading after the deal was announced. That may be because of the cost of the acquisition. The $325 million cash outlay amounts to almost half of Five9’s current cash and marketable investments, which stood at $738 million at the end of the third quarter.

Five9’s results weren’t too bad. The company reported adjusted earnings of 82 cents per share on revenue of $323.3 million, surpassing Wall Street’s guidance of 80 cents per share in earnings and $318.3 million in sales. But it’s notable that the company’s full-year guidance excludes any contributions from the pending deal, and so its value hinges on Five9’s ability to rapidly integrate Level AI’s technology.

For the current quarter, Five9 said it’s expecting earnings of between 88 cents and 92 cents per share on sales of $328 million to $334 million. But that guidance was mixed. Wall Street is looking for significantly higher earnings of 99 cents per share and $330.7 million in revenue.

For the full year, Five9 said it’s looking for earnings of $3.17 to $3.21 per share on total sales of $1.27 billion. Wall Street wants to see higher earnings of $3.26 per share on similar revenue.

Ramayana: Rise of a Legend ends on a big cliffhanger, with Rama set to venture across the realms to confront Ravana in a bid to save his wife Sita. For much of Ramayana, Rama and Ravana operate outside of one another's lives. The film concludes with a deliberate tease of the next movie, with Rama learning a bit about Ravana's champions and family members that he'll have to overcome to reach the enmy king. Coupled with a furious Rama's final vow to kill Ravana and save Sita, the finale of the film is a very overt tease for the sequel, which is poised to be a far more direct and action-packed affair.

One of the interesting wrinkles of the film is the way Ramayana doesn't simply make Ravana a villain for Rama to overcome. The film takes pains to portray the character as a multilayered person, whose origin story centers around his efforts to life his people out of exile and back to the surface world. He is, in many ways, the protagonist of his own story instead of just an antagonist.

This plays into the movie's focus on the balance between order and chaos, but quietly inverts them in effective ways. Rama, as the mortal avatar of Vishnu, is tied inherently to order. However, he's also a chaotic whirlwind in battle and resists expectations to stay true to his heart. Conversely, the chaos-bringing Ravana is shown as a king, commanding a system built up in the wake of his victories.

The film isn't necessarily about good vs. evil, even when Ravana begins crossing serious moral lines within the narrative. It's a story of two forces that circle one another and contrast against each other. While Rama is content to abdicate his throne and is quick to rage when Sita is threatened, Ravana proves deceptively cruel and patient when it comes to ensuring his rule.

The thing to remember about Ramayana is that to millions of people around the world, the tale of the Ramayana is just as much a religious text as it is a mythic epic. The central story of the film is rooted in the morals and messages laid out in the original tale. Ramayana has inspired people for ages, underlining the importance of honor, loyalty, and humility through Rama's arc.

Ravana, by contrast, showcases the power that can be amassed, while laying the groundwork for his defeat. His overwhelming confidence leaves him open to being defeated in the follow-up. Ramayana: Rise of a Legend is still about those things, reinforcing its perspective on the danger of pride and hostility by having other characters like Shurpanakha and her husband Vidyutjihva suffer for theirs.

Ramayana is in many ways a morality play, highlighting the inner strength that comes from Rama and Sita that provides them with a happiness and peace that Ravana and his people have never known. Despite the power they've gained, there's still an aura of chaos in their perspective on the world that will invite chaos to their door, with a vengeful Rama in the follow-up.
