Alex Consani Defies Gravity in a Twisted Gucci Gown at the LACMA Gala

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Alex Consani is no stranger to a gala, and tonight, she brought her signature glamour to the Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s annual fundraiser. The event—the LACMA Art+Film Gala—celebrated its 15th edition this evening, supporting its ongoing efforts to expand its film programming. And like every year, a whole lineup of familiar faces, from models to actors to philanthropists to musicians to athletes, showed up to participate.

Joining fellow attendees such as Cardi B, Nicole Kidman, and Paris Hilton, Consani arrived in a theatrical, gravity-defying gown. Courtesy of Gucci, the gala’s sponsor, the look twisted around her body, with an exaggerated, asymmetrical strapless neckline that extended far past her right shoulder. Its long, voluminous pleated skirt was finished with a zig-zag hem that gave the whole thing a witchy touch, and she wore it with pointed-toe heels.

Before heading inside, the model stopped for photos by Chris Burden’s Urban Light—a public art installation-turned-red carpet composed of oversize street lamps.

The 2026 gala, co-hosted by Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow for the 15th year, recognized the work of painter Vija Celmins, known for her photo-realistic work that explores the complexity of nature, as well as Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve. For live music, Blackpink’s Rosé joined a historic roster of past musical guests, including Stevie Wonder, Florence and the Machine, Elton John, and Charli XCX.

Consani first attended LACMA’s event last year, wearing another Gucci masterpiece. This one was a sheer, shimmering champagne gown, with a plunging halter neckline and ultra-high slit that exposed her entire left leg. The floor-length piece was covered in sequins, and she paired it with matching nude heels.

Steelers Hall of Fame linebacker Jack Ham hospitalized

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Pittsburgh Steelers legendary linebacker Jack Ham has been hospitalized in Washington. The Hall of Fame linebacker was getting set for Penn State vs. Washington, as he serves as the commentator for Penn State's radio broadcast. Penn State head coach Matt Campbell says Ham, 77, is in stable condition, and Ham's radio partner, Steve Jones, for playing a big part in Ham being okay.

"First of all, I think we should all know Steve Jones and the heroic effort that he did for somebody he loves dearly," Campbell said. "And if it's not for Steve and what he did, I don't know if we would have as good of news as we do right now. And then I want to also thank the University of Washington, their athletic director, their emergency team were incredible. They were outstanding. I think, hopefully, as all of you know, Jack is in stable condition right now, and obviously, the emotion of that."

Ham was part of all four Super Bowl-winning teams of the 1970s in Pittsburgh. He was a six-time All-Pro and was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1988.

Jennifer Welch and Angie Sullivan Apologize for Laughing at “Insensitive Joke” About Charlie Kirk on ‘I’ve Had It’ Podcast

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

"We regret it, and we apologize. Whatever our political differences, there is nothing funny about gun violence," they wrote in a joint statement on Saturday.

I’ve Had It podcast hosts Jennifer Welch and Angie “Pumps” Sullivan have released an apology statement for laughing at a joke about Charlie Kirk on the Thursday episode of their show.

“On Thursday’s show, we laughed at a joke about Charlie Kirk. It was an insensitive joke, and we should not have laughed. We regret it, and we apologize,” they wrote in a statement shared on the I’ve Had It podcast Instagram Saturday. “Whatever our political differences, there is nothing funny about gun violence. We’ve long supported common-sense gun safety reform. To that end, we’ve made a donation to Everytown for Gun Safety, the largest gun violence prevention organization in America.” Related Stories TV Pete Davidson Makes Shocking Charlie Kirk Joke at Kevin Hart Roast News The Widow and the Firebrand: How Candace Owens Turned a Conservative Succession Into a Crusade Against Erika Kirk

During the Thursday episode of the podcast, the hosts read posts made by online users amid the 2026 midterm results. They read one that said, “We’re digging up Charlie Kirk and doing it again,” and they both laughed. (That segment of the Thursday episode has since been cut out, but was reshared online.)

“We’ll keep using our platforms to fight for the safety of all Americans,” Welch and Sullivan’s joint statement concluded.

Kirk died in September 2025 after being fatally shot while speaking at a college event in Utah County, Utah.

On Thursday, in response to a video of the cut clip from I’ve Had It on X, Cash App announced they were no longer sponsors of the podcast. “We do not condone violence of any kind, and we are no longer sponsors of this podcast,” Cash App wrote.

Boll & Branch, a luxury bedding company, also said they would not “advertise with the I’ve Had It podcast and will not in the future.” Chewy additionally put out a statement noting they “do not sponsor or support the I’ve Had It podcast. We unequivocally condemn violence in all forms. The comments made by the hosts do not reflect Chewy’s values or what we stand for as a company.”

Personal Investments • Re: Trust at Merrill Lynch. Beneficiary passed. How to get it out of ML

📅 Nov 07, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

If the father's Will says to distribute the balance at the wife's death to the children (that was common in 1990 since from 1979 to 1995 if you provided for the children in further trust, you couldn't give each child control over his/her trust), then the trustee should do that and distribute the trust assets to the children (after paying or providing for any expenses, and getting releases from the children).

Whether to distribute the assets in kind or sell them and distribute the proceeds is up to the trustee. However, given the substantial appreciation in the assets, most likely the trustee would distribute the assets in kind and let each child decide what to keep and what to sell.

The trustees could either work for the lawyer handling the wife's estate or a different trusts and estates lawyer of their choice.

Most likely if the father died in 1990, his estate was settled long ago, but if it wasn't, the executor should do so now.
The intent is to distribute the trust assets in kind to the adult children of the father. That estate was settled in 1990.

The wife's estate is being settled by someone else...and we are not involved in that estate and her offspring will not get trust assets from the father who passed in 1990. He was not their father, and they will have a considerable inheritance from their mother. No one is contesting anything and we are all on good terms.
