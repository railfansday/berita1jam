Pirates Of The Caribbean 6 Return Addressed By Star After Johnny Depp Update

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

After Johnny Depp's latest comments, another franchise star addresses returning for Pirates of the Caribbean 6.

During the press tour for his new movie, Ebenezer, Depp shared that he doesn't "know if you'll see us set sail. We might do that privately." He then revealed his Pirates of the Caribbean 6 return condition is that "if things are exactly where they need to be with regard to how they're going to top off the Pirates thing, the only involvement I really want to have truly is just to send them off on a better note."

In an interview with The Daily Beast, Pirates of the Caribbean star Mackenzie Crook, who plays Ragetti, was asked if he is open to returning after Depp's recent remarks. He replied, "Yes, I think I would for the hell of it. It would be so interesting – obviously it would depend on the script – to even just have a cameo to see what happened to those characters, Pintel and Ragetti, what happened to them after we last saw them. Again, I was really fond of those characters."

Crook also looked back on the making of the movies, admitting "at times it was grueling" while also emphasizing that "I was aware that this was sort of the end of an era. In one of the movies, a ship had to blow up, and so we got a ship and packed it with explosives and blew it up. And I don't think you'd do that now. It would all be CGI, but this sort of felt like the last remnants of old-fashioned movie making. It was special."

The bumbling, comedic, and fan-favorite pirate duo Ragetti and Pintel (Lee Arenberg) are in the first three Pirates of the Caribbean movies, in which they serve on the crews of both Captain Hector Barbossa (Geoffrey Rush) and Captain Jack Sparrow (Depp). They are last seen in Pirates of the Caribbean: At World's End with Barbossa aboard The Black Pearl after stealing the ship from Jack.

There were plans for Ragetti and Pintel to return in the fourth and fifth installments, On Stranger Tides and Dead Men Tell No Tales, but their appearances were cut from the former, and scheduling conflicts got in the way of coming back in the latter sequel. Now that Crook confirms he is open to reprising his role in Pirates of the Caribbean 6, Ragetti could appear for the first time since At World's End was released in 2007, both continuing his story and potentially providing closure for it.

Depp's return condition makes it clear that closure is what he is most interested in when it comes to the long-gestating movie. Producer Jerry Bruckheimer has spoken on numerous occasions about the project being in development and that there have been ongoing talks with Depp. Along with bringing back prior actors from the franchise, Margot Robbie has been reported as being attached to the film at various points, in what previously was being described as more of a reboot, while discussions in more recent years have been more indicative of a sequel featuring Jack.

Amy's Original Doc Story Continuation Is Dead For Now

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Doc is finding fascinating new ways to evolve beyond its original premise. After a car accident left Molly Parker’s Dr. Amy Larsen unable to recall the last eight years of her life, she spent season 1 trying to remember who she was. Doc season 2 shifted from rediscovering Amy's identity to confronting the consequences of her past.

Initially, Amy couldn't remember the tragedies that led to her divorce from Dr. Michael Hamda (Omar Metwally), or that she had fallen in love with Dr. Jake Heller (Jon Ecker), the Chief Resident working directly under her. However, Amy's amnesia effectively reversed their professional and personal circumstances, stripping her of both her authority and her memories of their relationship.

Doc's messy love triangle rivals the romantic drama of Grey's Anatomy, with Michael remarried and expecting a child while pining for the new old Amy. Meanwhile, in the Doc season 2 finale, Amy realizes Jake still has feelings for his ex-wife. Recognizing that both men are holding on to the past, Amy decides to move forward on her own.

Doc season 3’s flashbacks have introduced another romantic entanglement: Blair Underwood’s Dr. Ben Grant, with whom Amy had an affair during those forgotten eight years. Entertainment journalist Matt Webb Mitovich stated in his newsletter, Matt's Inside Line, what Doc fans had already begun to suspect: Amy and Michael aren't likely to rekindle their romance anytime soon.

With Jake also moving on (or moving backward), the series appears ready to leave its original love triangle behind. However, Amy's forgotten past keeps creating new complications, ensuring Doc isn't running out of romantic drama anytime soon.

Despite their complicated history, Michael has remained one of Amy's strongest supporters, using his managerial position at Westside Hospital to help her rebuild her career. His second marriage even ended because of his lingering feelings for Amy, seemingly clearing the way for a romantic reconciliation. However, Doc season 3 has shown little interest in bringing the former spouses back together.

Instead, the series is doubling down on Amy's forgotten relationship with Dr. Grant, which was far more complicated than a simple affair. During their relationship, Amy consulted on Ben's medical research and agreed to overlook unfavorable data to strengthen his study. Now that the research is moving forward, Amy is gradually recovering her memories of the project.

Meanwhile, Michael may have a new romantic prospect too. As Mitovich noted in Matt's Inside Line, Sasha (Nazanin Boniadi), one of Ben's research partners, appears to have taken a "shine" to Michael. Although it's unclear whether their connection will develop into a full-fledged romance, Sasha's introduction further complicates the possibility of Michael reuniting with Amy.

More intriguingly, Sasha's professional connection to Ben places both Amy and Michael within the orbit of a potentially unethical medical research project. Rather than simply introducing another love interest to keep the former spouses apart, Doc could be setting up a storyline that intertwines their romantic histories with a larger professional scandal.

December 2026 Is Officially The End Of An Era For Avatar: The Last Airbender

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Avatar: The Last Airbender kicked off in earnest in 2005, and over 20 years later, it's still actively delighting tons of fans with new projects. In addition to a sweeping novel series featuring multiple Avatars throughout the series' history, there's also a live-action Netflix adaptation that just shipped its second season, and a full sequel film featuring Aang and Team Avatar was recently released on Paramount+.

Then there's Avatar: Seven Havens, which just wrapped its inaugural season and takes place 225 years after The Legend of Korra, the original Avatar sequel series. With Seven Havensrenewed, it's looking good for fans of the series.

Sadly, not everything lasts forever, and that includes some elements of the Avatar franchise. One project in particular is ending in December.

After three years in operation, developer Bamtang Games and publisher GameMill Entertainment have announced that Avatar: The Last Airbender - Quest for Balance will be removed from sale due to licensing agreements.

The announcement came directly through the game's Steam page:

"Hi everyone, we wanted to inform you that Avatar: The Last Airbender - Quest for Balance is soon to be leaving Steam at the end of December 2026 as our licensing agreement for the title has concluded. If you already own the game, there’s no need to worry, it will remain in your library and will continue to be fully playable. Thank you for your continued support! GameMill Entertainment"

Originally released in 2023, Quest for Balance is a puzzle-based game involving multiple characters from the Avatar universe, set to the tune of 18 chapters and one-to-two-player action.

"Team Avatar is back! Relive the epic adventure of the original Avatar: The Last Airbender series, as you play as Aang and his 8 friends to explore the most unique locations from the series, solve challenging puzzles and experience the most heart touching moments of Aang’s story.

Explore the World of Avatar and travel across the four nations through a 1-2 player co-op 18 chapter adventure. Play as 9 iconic characters including Aang, Toph, Sokka, and Katara. Unlock the Mysteries and navigate challenging puzzles using bending and the unique abilities of water, earth, fire and air; upgrade your abilities throughout the story to unleash the full potential of Avatar!

Reviews are decidedly "mostly negative" according to Steam, in case you're wondering what you're missing. Users report it takes roughly 10 hours to clear the game.

Avatar: Seven Havens is truly the future of the franchise right now, given the strong reception and renewal. Book One dropped on October 9 of this year, and it's already been a resounding success. Paramount ordered three more seasons, potentially telling a full story and ending with season four.

It's a massive win for Avatar fans everywhere, despite the complete erasure of Quest for Balance. If you really want to get in on this oddball piece of Avatar history, however, Quest for Balance is available to download and keep before that December cutoff.

This Playful Florida Raised Ranch Is Proof That Bold Style Can Be Incredibly Timeless

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Classic materials and a modern layout read as "traditional with a twist."

How do you turn a straightforward ranch-style home into something exciting and unexpected yet timeless? When a young family of four asked designer James Farmer to do just that for their central Florida abode, he opted for a gut renovation, using his homegrown knowledge of Southern style, years of interior expertise, and pointed enthusiasm to renovate ranches to create a highly functional and striking escape for his clients.

Located in an old, established neighborhood on a lake in Winter Park, Florida, the home was surrounded by mature live oaks and beautiful landscaping. “I would describe the area as old-school Florida with a touch of Mayberry,” says Farmer, referencing the fictional town from The Andy Griffith Show. The home itself was humble and honest—“no hiding its hand at the poker table,” says Farmer.

Alongside Skolfield Homes, Farmer kept the architectural style generally the same, with the most impactful changes being a complete overhaul of the galley kitchen and a relocation of the staircase from the side of the house to the middle, putting it in its deserved place as a focal point of the living room. The new kitchen is what he calls a “glorified galley,” with a sunny breakfast room off to the side that used to be a screened-in porch. He then reinforced the home as a "front door house," ensuring the formal entry space would actually be used rather than passed through by adding a bar perfect for fancy cocktail parties or a casual post-work martini.

For the actual interior, the designer layered various textures and bold patterns, combining them with hallmarks of grand Floridian style, like floral wallpapers, gold details, intricate trim, palm motifs, pecky cypress wallcoverings, and a coastal color palette. The line between indoor and outdoor living is further blurred with trellis details and a selection of timeworn brick and wood flooring. "We really leaned into this in a new way with this project because of its location," he says. "True, classic Florida can be so fun but also timeless, and that was our goal with this project."

Farmer encouraged his clients to splurge only on materials and pieces that'll truly stand the test of time, opting for classic finishes and prints that will age with the young family—an approach he details intimately in his newest book, Home Again: A Return to Gracious Interiors. Ultimately, the decadent interior paired with the modern structural changes naturally created what ended up being Farmer's motto for the home: "Traditional with a twist!"

FAST FACTSDesigner: James Farmer of James Farmer DesignsLocation: Winter Park, FloridaThe Space: A ranch-style home with 4 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms

Fabulous cocktail parties start in this formal space before migrating to other parts of the house. With no TV in the space, all eyes are drawn to the bar set behind a custom set of accent chairs by Thibaut, which is where Farmer's clients shake up (not stir) "the best martinis in Florida."

A color palette of peach, robin's egg blue, and butterscotch is celebrated in this room, the designer explains. By painting the ceiling in a blue tone—a trick commonly seen on Southern porches—he gave the room a subtle indoor-outdoor touch commonly seen on Southern porches.

Borrowed light from the kitchen and stairwell shines on the rich velvets and great patterns in this family room. “The color combo of terracotta and emerald with creamy white millwork creates a handsome and elegant palette,” Farmer adds.

Once a screened-in porch, Farmer kept the alfresco feel by covering the walls and ceiling with trelliage panels and outfitting the room with timeless white wicker furniture by Memoire Design (once known as Mainly Baskets Home). Vintage elephant garden stools now stand as whimsical side tables.

“We kept the original corner cabinets and wainscoting but gave it a bit of glam with a custom Gracie Studio wallpaper and lacquered ceiling,” Farmer says of the dining room, which kept its original footprint. The console tables are painted in a lemony yellow hue to match the window treatments and the room's other cheerful elements.

Farmer broke up this completely new, double galley kitchen into sections to make hosting a breeze. On one side of the counter is the seating and hangout zone, while the other side plays host to a functional working kitchen, outfitted with a Wolf range and Sub-Zero fridge. Think of it like traditional with a twist; "These clients are fabulous entertainers, and the island can also make a great buffet for parties," he says. "We like to think that The Golden Girls would thrive in this kitchen… Mid-century meets Maw-Maw!"

Old Chicago brick and palm motifs in the wallpaper make this breakfast hub feel more like a terrace than an interior enclave. Farmer created this room as part of the kitchen expansion—its bay window only enhances the porch-like vibe he wanted. "Sometimes geometry is your best friend," Farmer says, noting that the rug, table, and even shape of the room are octagonal. "You might not notice it, but it makes a moment."

“We were able to move the staircase in this house, which allowed for a more efficient floor plan for this young family,” he explains. “When you are under a major renovation, you can think outside of the box, or sometimes inside the box, just as easily.” Now the stairwell can live as a beautiful moment all its own at the center of the home, rather than pushed to the side.

The daughter was sleeping in a crib when Farmer first started working on his clients' house, so he wanted to design a room that could grow with her (by the time the project wrapped, she was in her big girl bed). The pink trellis wallpaper by Schumacher perfectly rides the line between feminine and elevated.

Touches of bamboo, palm, and pecky cypress introduce that old Florida feel, reminiscent of family vacations to the family's favorite spots in the Sunshine State, without being too on the nose.

“An interior design trick that is so old-school, but still so fun, is using a drapery fabric for wall covering and vice versa,” Farmer explains. “We were able to bring that from the primary bedroom to the primary bathroom.”

James Farmer is a Southern author, interior designer, and speaker known for his ability to create beautifully familiar and welcoming homes. To James, a home engages all of the senses—the pleasantries of colors and materials, the feel of the doorknob warmed by the sun, the smell of fresh laundry, the sound of the closing door, and the taste of supper on the table. Through his design firm, James works with clients to truly create a home.

Projects by James Farmer Designs can be found from the countryside of Connecticut to James's home state of Georgia. Though his work can be found throughout the country, James proudly has built his business in his hometown of Perry, Georgia.

Gears of War: E-Day delivers an impressive visual and technical showcase. The game brings together several advanced graphics technologies, including Unreal Engine 5’s Nanite and Lumen, MegaLights, and RTX Mega Geometry for more accurate ray-traced shadows and reflections, alongside a range of upscaling and frame generation technologies.

However, today we’re focusing on a less-discussed technology that plays an equally important role in bringing this visually rich, densely detailed world to systems with more limited hardware resources: storage technology designed to reduce RAM usage.

We’ll begin by examining its behavior on a high-end system with plenty of resources to spare, before moving to a more modest configuration that better represents the hardware available to the average PC gamer. This will allow us to see how much of a difference the technology makes, particularly on lower-end systems.

In an interview with Digital Foundry, The Coalition explained how they leveraged the Xbox Series S’s fast SSD to bring the dense world of Gears of War: E-Day to a memory-limited system. By effectively using the NVMe SSD as an extension of system memory, they can dynamically load and offload complex city data in real time. This approach allows the game to stream the necessary assets for each scene just in time, overcoming the console’s limited RAM.

Digital Foundry notes that the true “special sauce” lies in The Coalition’s smart data management. The team employs advanced heuristics to determine which assets are fetched from the SSD and when, carefully balancing performance. Overusing this technique could harm frame rates, so the system is tuned to swap data only when it benefits the experience.

Gears of War: E-Day also supports DirectStorage, although based on our previous testing, this alone does not always result in high SSD activity or read rates.

YouTuber Mostly Positive Reviews shared his findings in a recent post on X, where he found higher-than-usual SSD activity in Task Manager when playing the game.

Based on his findings, we decided to investigate further.

Task Manager does not use a low enough polling period to measure SSD activity accurately, so we used HWInfo with the polling period set to 20 milliseconds. We tested SSD activity across all of the game’s graphics presets at 4K using DLSS Quality.

In our previous tests of games that relied heavily on the SSD, none came close to sustaining 1 GB/s of average bandwidth. The highest we recorded was 366 MB/s in Forspoken at 4K with maximum settings, while many titles stayed well under 100 MB/s. Some games did show higher peak bandwidths, but these spikes lasted only a few milliseconds. For our purposes, it’s the sustained throughput – reflected in the average bandwidth – that truly matters.

In Gears of War: E-Day at higher settings, we essentially have a steady stream of a little over 1 GB/s at all times, indicating that the disk is indeed being utilized as an extension of system memory. As you can see, peaks hit up to 3.7 GB/s of throughput.

We measured RAM usage on our high-end system to see how much the game will use across each generation of SSD tested.

The game uses a significant amount of system memory, but having the game installed on an NVMe SSD – regardless of generation – saves about 2GB of RAM. The storage technology seems to be working as expected.

Since the game’s average read bandwidth far exceeds the limits of a SATA SSD, we expect that installing the game on one would lead to visual issues – and that is exactly what we observe.

In the first comparison, textures are slow to load, which noticeably reduces image quality. In the second comparison, lighting and shadows take some time to appear. While everything eventually loads correctly, running the game on a SATA SSD does hurt immersion.

We tested the game across four generations of SSDs to evaluate any impact on performance. Using a high-end system with ample memory, we aim to determine the behavior of the storage technology on a system that is not resource-constrained. We will then compare the performance of our laptop’s internal PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD with that of a SATA SSD.

On a system with sufficient memory, installing the game on a SATA SSD has no measurable performance impact. The only drawback is the aforementioned visual degradation.

Now, we focused on our lower-end system with only 16GB of DDR5.

Now we start to see the benefit of installing the game on an NVMe SSD, especially on higher settings.

Although the average frame rate remains virtually identical on both the NVMe and SATA SSDs, increasing the graphics preset impacts the 1% lows. The NVMe SSD delivers smoother frametimes, resulting in a more responsive and fluid gaming experience.

While the concept isn’t entirely new – Windows already uses virtual memory to page data to disk when physical memory is exhausted – The Coalition has fine-tuned this technology specifically for their game. Their approach ensures that the data being paged out won’t noticeably slow down gameplay as it’s read from disk. In our testing, this technology successfully reduces RAM usage when the game is installed on an NVMe SSD.

Placing the game on an NVMe SSD also enables near-instant asset loading. For systems with 16GB of RAM or less, an NVMe SSD can deliver higher 1% lows, resulting in smoother performance. With RAM prices climbing and many players using machines with 16GB – or even 8GB – of memory, it’s reasonable to wonder if other developers will adopt similar techniques. For titles striving to create visually dense, richly detailed worlds, leveraging fast storage could be a practical way to preserve their vision on lower-end systems.

‘Magical’ Tree House With 30-Foot Glass Living Room Returns to the Market

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

A “magical” four-bedroom retreat encased in glass has returned to the market in Austin, TX, for $1,095,000.

The Glass Tree House debuted on the market for $1.5 million in August 2024 and has received several price cuts since then.

Custom slate stairs lead to a jaw-dropping living room with 30-foot-tall windows and custom rock walls carved into a cliffside, with tree-lined views that make you feel at one with nature.

“When you walk in, it feels like wherever you go, there is so much privacy and the view is very unique,” says listing agent Jenny Law of Kuper Sotheby’s International Realty. “It reminds me of a modern tree house that takes you far away from city life.”

Built in 2008, the 2,180-square-foot metal-clad contemporary was designed by architect John Allen to blur the boundaries between the indoor living spaces and the outdoor setting.

“The sellers use it as a short-term rental property,” Law notes. “It’s a nice retreat and would be a popular second home for someone because of its views and location, or it could be used as a primary residence for its school system.”

The sleek kitchen has custom black cabinetry, honey onyx countertops, built-in seating, and stainless-steel appliances.

The glass-surrounded primary suite has a soaking tub, a moss rock shower, Calacatta marble countertops, marble flooring, and access to a private terrace.

“It’s located in Apache Shores, where there are not a lot of restrictions in that area,” Law says. “It’s about 15 to 20 minutes from downtown Austin, but you feel like you are out in nature with these nice hill country views.”

A second lot, which could allow for a future pool, workshop, or guesthouse with applicable approvals, is available for separate purchase.

The property is also minutes from Lake Travis and Lake Austin, a residents-only boat launch, an 18-hole disc golf course, a soccer field, a community hall, a swimming pool, and sports courts.

“In Austin, there are so many homes that look the same, but this one is unique and beautiful,” Law says. “You truly feel like you are living outside.”

Portugal ‘Retire’ Cristiano Ronaldo’s 7 Shirt Despite FIFA Rule

Portugal's post-Cristiano Ronaldo era is set to commence on Saturday night when Jorge Jesus' side face Denmark in the Nations League, with a new number seven set to don the famous red shirt for the first time in over 20 years.

Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football earlier this month, bringing an end to his 23-year spell as the talisman and figurehead for the Selecao as Quinas. He bows out with 146 goals and 234 caps for his nation, alongside a Euro 2016 winners' medal and two Nations League titles.

Historically, players with such legacies who become synonymous with a specific shirt number often have that shirt number retired in their honour.

However, a FIFA rule stipulates that for international tournaments organised by the global footballing body, teams are required to designate consecutive numbers for every player in the squad from 1 to 26. This regulation essentially prevents national teams from retiring a shirt, meaning players will wear number seven for Portugal after Ronaldo — Rafael Leao or Carlos Forbs is expected to wear it during this international break.

However, this hasn't prevented the European national team from concocting an inventive way around this, as they look to preserve the iconic shirt number in a tribute to their greatest-ever player.

In the purest sense of the word, Portugal can't retire the number seven shirt. They cannot prevent players from wearing it in the future, but they can still do their best to ensure Ronaldo's image and brand are preserved.

Posting on their Instagram account on Saturday, the Portuguese Men's National Team shared a video revealing that from now on, they'll be printing their number seven shirts with a little cross through the middle of the number, distinguishing them from Ronaldo's crossless seven.

"Time to unveil a new 7. Because there is one that will be eternal," the caption read.

This mark of respect for Ronaldo's achievements is permitted by FIFA, as it's not eradicating the number seven from future use, but rather slightly modifying how it'll appear on the back of Portugal shirts moving forward.

Argentina reportedly had plans to retire the number ten shirt after Lionel Messi called time on his international career, but the South American nation ran into the same issue Portugal did.

Unfortunately for the 2026 World Cup finalists, there was little scope for ingenuity or invention regarding how they present the number ten shirt, so they've not been able to honour Messi in the same way. That said, Lionel Scaloni did refrain from selecting someone to wear ten during Argentina's first post-Messi match against Uruguay on Friday. Italy fines Meta €805m over Facebook’s access to Android photos

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Italy’s privacy watchdog says Facebook’s Android app read phone galleries without proper consent. Meta has 60 days to fix it.

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg at a dinner with tech leaders in the State Dining Room of the White House in Washington, Sept. 4, 2025.

Italy’s data protection authority, the Garante, has fined Meta €805m, or about $887m. It says the Facebook app for Android accessed photos, videos and their metadata on Italian users’ phones without specific consent or proper notice.

The decision, first reported in English by Reuters, targets a gallery suggestions feature available since 2023. While users scroll Facebook, it offers photos and videos from their phone as ideas for posts.

The Garante found that Facebook asked for gallery access with one permission for the whole app. That request followed a vague prompt to enable other features. Users could not tell they were also switching on gallery suggestions.

Meta’s EU base is in Ireland, and under GDPR the Irish regulator usually leads. Meta argued the case belonged there. The Garante disagreed, because it acted under ePrivacy rules, which have no one-stop shop.

The rule it applied, Article 122 of Italy’s privacy code, treats a smartphone as part of a person’s private sphere. Any access to what the phone holds needs free and informed consent. It applies even if no data leaves the phone and no harm is done.

The regulator said about 20 million Android users in Italy were affected. It also weighed a 2019 decision against Meta and what it called poor cooperation. Changes Meta made after the complaint counted in its favour, but the Garante called them late and limited to users who update the app.

The fine equals about 0.47% of Meta’s global turnover. Within 60 days, Meta must ask for separate, specific consent for the feature. It must also keep the feature off by default, even for users who already granted access.

The probe did not examine whether gallery content was used to train AI, which Meta denies. The Garante will look separately at a cloud processing feature launched in 2026.

The penalty is close to the €825m Dutch fine on Uber, though below Meta’s €1.2bn GDPR fine over data transfers. Meta is also tangling with Italy over a ruling on publisher payments.

Reuters said Meta was not immediately available for comment.

The 5 Healthiest Fast Food Salads, According To Nutritionists

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

A drive-thru doesn’t have to derail your diet.

Fast food chains aren’t exactly famous for their salads. They usually fall into two categories: a sad bowl of lettuce added to menus as an afterthought, or a vehicle for fried, calorie-dense ingredients that offset any nutritional upsides. But some chains do make an effort to sell salads worth ordering.

To identify the best better-for-you fast-food salads, we asked three nutrition experts to weigh in. Here are the menu items they recommend, along with some tips and tricks for maximizing their nutrition.

First, we need to debunk salad’s diet-friendly reputation. “A salad isn't automatically the healthiest choice on a fast food menu,” says Kathy Levin, RDN, CDCES, dipACLM, LDN. “Once you add fried chicken, bacon, cheese, candied nuts, crunchy toppings, and a creamy dressing, some salads land in the same calorie and fat range as a burger and fries.”

Taking all of those components away isn’t always a solution either. A sad salad with just lettuce and a smattering of vegetables is more like rabbit food than a satisfying meal for humans. According to Briana V. Reamer, MS, RDN, LDN, the secret is getting enough fiber, which she says “will keep you satisfied and full until your next meal.” Take advantage of the restaurant's add-ons—like beans, avocado, and more vegetables—to make your salad more substantial.

Experts also recommend watching your saturated fat intake, specifically with your protein and dressing. Grilled chicken generally has fewer calories and less saturated fat than deep-fried alternatives. Consider your dressing options, too. “If you’re not sure what to pick, look for a vinaigrette or olive-oil-based dressing,” says Tina Sergi, MS, RD, CSSD, CSCS, TSAC-F.

And, as a rule of thumb, order your dressing on the side. It keeps your salad from getting soggy on the drive home, for one, but it also gives you more portion control. “Using only part of the packet cuts saturated fat and sodium, especially when they're already on the higher end,” Sergi adds.

Of course, the dressing is only part of the equation. With those tips in mind, here are the five salads that made the cut.

This regional chain is known for its fire-grilled chicken, and this salad packs plenty of it. Each portion contains a double serving of citrus-marinated chicken breast, sliced avocado, cotija cheese, salsa fresca, and tortilla strips over a bed of greens. It's served with a creamy Mexican-inspired Caesar dressing.

El Pollo Loco strikes the right balance between substantial and light with this dish. Before you add dressing, the entire salad contains 420 calories and 52 grams of protein. “The fire-grilled chicken makes it very filling, the avocado adds heart-healthy fat and fiber, and the salsa fresca adds flavor without much sodium or sugar,” Levin says.

Like with most salads, the dressing significantly raises the calorie count—in this case, it adds 440 calories if you use the entire serving. If you're closely monitoring your daily intake, Levin advises going easy on the dressing.

Culver’s may be best known for its ButterBurgers, but experts say you shouldn’t sleep on its salads. The Chicken Cashew Salad in particular is a great choice. It starts with a bed of mixed greens, topped with cheddar, grape tomatoes, cucumbers, and roasted cashews. You can also choose from several dressings to finish it off.

With grilled chicken, the salad comes in at 430 calories and 41 grams of protein before adding dressing. Culver’s also offers six dressing varieties ranging from 50 to 260 calories.

“This is a great fiber- and protein-packed option,” says Sergi. “Plus, the cashews add healthy fats, copper to support energy production, and magnesium for optimal nerve and muscle function.”

This Lone Star staple sells a Cobb salad that’s a lighter alternative to its lineup of burgers and sandwiches. It comes loaded with all of the essentials: greens, bacon, a hard-boiled egg, grape tomatoes, carrots, and shredded cheese. You can also choose from three chicken options (the grilled chicken filet is, naturally, lighter than the fried options).

Without dressing, Whataburger's Cobb with grilled chicken has 430 calories and 44 grams of protein. It's also a source of fiber and is low in sugar, according to Reamer. “Salads high in fiber help with weight management and satisfy hunger.”

Some of Whataburger's creamier dressings add 200 to 280 calories, while the balsamic vinaigrette has 180.

For a fried chicken chain, Chick-fil-A makes some surprisingly stellar salads. The Market Salad starts with mixed greens and is topped with strawberries, blueberries, red and green apples, blue cheese, roasted almonds, and the chain’s Harvest Nut Granola. Chick-fil-A also has the most protein options on this list. The grilled chicken can come in nugget or filet form, warm or cold, and spicy or regular. Talk about variety!

The different grilled chicken options provide between 26 and 32 grams of protein, according to Chick-fil-A's nutrition guide. Levin also says you can forgo chicken entirely for a meatless meal that doesn't feel like an afterthought. “Berries and apples bring fiber, antioxidants and natural sweetness, which makes it one of the most plant-forward options on the list.”

The Market Salad is typically paired with Chick-fil-A's Zesty Apple Cider Vinaigrette Dressing, which contains 230 calories per packet. But the chain is known for its sauces, after all, so you can swap it out for another option. There's a Light Balsamic Vinaigrette with 80 calories, Fat-Free Honey Mustard with 90, and Light Italian Dressing with just 25.

Zaxby's is the third chicken chain on this list, and it stands out by offering a salad with Asian-inspired flavors. The Asian Zensation Zalad (not Salad) adds shredded carrots, cabbage, wonton strips, and Asian Slaw to mixed greens, along with a drizzle of teriyaki sauce. It also comes with a vegetable egg roll on the side.

This salad is loaded with plant-based toppings, which is why Levin also recommends the garden version as a vegetarian option. Zaxby's lists that version at 390 calories, while the grilled chicken version comes in at 530 calories and 39 grams of protein, before adding dressing.

Alongside the teriyaki drizzle, Zaxby's offers Citrus Vinaigrette with the salad, which has 110 calories per packet. It's one of the chain's lighter options. But if you're watching your calorie intake, you can swap in Lite Vinaigrette, which contains just 30 calories.

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

The Brooklyn Nets (3-8) are currently 1-2 on their four-game road trip after picking up an impressive 145-111 win over the New Orleans Pelicans on Wednesday. Brooklyn's road trip continues on Saturday as they visit Ty Jerome and the Memphis Grizzlies (8-4) and if you're wondering where you can watch all the action live, you've come to the right place!

The Nets come into this game following the aforementioned win over the Pelicans in which they began the game with a 14-point lead after the first quarter and never looked back. Forward Michael Porter Jr. led the way for the Nets with 22 points and six rebounds while forward Julius Randle had one of his better games of the season with 17 points and five assists.

The Grizzlies enter this matchup after a 142-111 NBA Cup win over New Orleans on Friday in which they led the Pelicans 70-58 heading into halftime and New Orleans was able to cut back into the deficit. Forward Jerami Grant had 22 points in 26 minutes off the bench while Jerome had 17 points and nine assists as he continues his breakout season leading Memphis.

Here is what you need to know to get ready for this matchup:

Nets: OUT: Tyler Bilodeau (G League), Joshua Jefferson (G League), Grant Nelson (G League), and Ben Saraf (G League). QUESTIONABLE: Mikel Brown Jr. (illness). PROBABLE: Nolan Traore (calf).
