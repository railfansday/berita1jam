South Korea: Bus hits 32 vehicles near Seoul, dozens injured

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

The bus plowed into over 30 vehicles, including other buses, during rush hour traffic in the South Korean city of Seongnam, just south of Seoul.

A bus hit dozens of passenger cars, a truck, and four other buses in Seongnam, South Korea on Wednesday morning, leaving many injured, according to South Korean authorities.

The fire department of Gyeonggi Province, were Seongnam is located, said 57 people were injured, with nine seriously hurt. None of them were in life-threatening condition. Yonhap News Agency had reported that 48 people were injured and six of them had serious injuries.

An investigation has been launched into the cause of the collision.

The driver told investigators the brakes on his bus had failed, according to a report by the Yonhap. The man, believed to be in his 60s, tested negative in a breathalyzer test.

The city of Seongnam, with its population of nearly 900,000, is located just south of the capital metropolis of Seoul. The crash occurred during rush hour traffic.

Dozens of fire personnel and vehicles were deployed to the scene of the crash and a emergency medical station was also set up there.

‘Beyond Anything I Could Have Imagined’: Carl Cox Presented With OBE By Prince William

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

The pioneering DJ and record producer was honored during a ceremony at Windsor Castle.

Oh yes, oh yes! Carl Cox has been presented with his OBE (Officer of the Order of the British Empire), with Prince William on royal duties.

The pioneering British DJ and record producer is recognized for his outstanding services to music, and was one of several music figures identified in the King’s Birthday Honours List back in June of this year.

King Charles’ first born, William, had the honor of presenting Cox with his award during a ceremony this week at Windsor Castle. “I’m not quite sure I can put into words what today means to me,” Cox writes on his social channels. “Music has been my whole life. From those early days of falling in love with records and DJing, to travelling the world and sharing that love with so many people, I’ve never stopped being grateful that I get to do what I do.”

To receive his OBE from Prince William at Windsor Castle, “recognising nearly 50 years of doing something I absolutely love,” Cox writes, “is beyond anything I could have imagined.”

Cox isn’t just a DJ. He’s an institution, an integral part of the house and techno electronic music story, one of the first “superstar” DJs and a vinyl technician who mastered the three-deck and four-deck mixing techniques. He founded his label Intec Records in 1999, and is a familiar face behind the decks at the biggest festivals and clubs, everywhere.

As clubland remained in lockdown during the pandemic, Cox used the time wisely to complete his autobiography, “Oh Yes, Oh Yes!,” a reference to his catchphrase. The book dropped in August 2021, published by White Rabbit.

“I’ve been thinking a lot about my mum and dad today,” he says of his OBE. “I know just how proud they would have been, and I wish they could have been here to share this moment with me.

“This honour means so much, not just to me personally, but because of where our music has come from and how far it has travelled. To see dance music recognised in this way is something I’m immensely proud of.”

Signing off, he gives a shout out to “family, friends, everyone who has believed in me, supported me and danced with me over the years, thank you. You’ve all been part of this. My heart is very full today. I’m proud, humbled and deeply honoured. Thank you.”

PHILADELPHIA — Jayson Tatum scored 21 points and outplayed former Boston teammate Jaylen Brown, and Paul George hit six 3-pointers and scored 36 points in his return to Philadelphia, leading the Celtics past the 76ers 123-101 on Tuesday night.

Brown ran into early foul trouble and scored only 13 points in his first game against Boston since the 2024 NBA Finals MVP was traded over the summer to the Sixers. His head-to-head showdown with Tatum was one of the more anticipated early-season matchups with both players refusing to say much about their frosty relationship.

Tatum tried to dap up Brown before the opening tip with a tap on the shoulder and thump on the chest that his former teammate ignored. Tatum shook hands and exchanged a big hug with LeBron James.

Brown picked up two quick fouls in the first quarter and was whistled for his fourth early in the second half.

George, traded for Brown after two seasons with the 76ers, enjoyed the homecoming. He appeared to give the finger in a bowling motion near the visitor's bench after he sank his fourth 3 of the game for a 100-82 lead that pushed the Celtics to their ninth win in 10 games.

The Sixers survived a scare late in the second half when Joel Embiid landed awkwardly and hit the court on a missed layup attempt. The oft-injured big man curled up on the court while VJ Edgecombe dunked over him.

Embiid gingerly ran down the court and hunched over as the halftime horn sounded with the Celtics up 62-51.

Embiid's health has been a career-long issue for him, and the Sixers have declined to play him in back-to-back games. A two-time NBA scoring champion, Embiid has slumped with his shot this season, with no game worse than last Friday in Cleveland when missed all nine shots from the floor and scored only two points.

He had missed 27 of 30 (and 20 consecutive) 3-pointers entering the game. Embiid missed two 3-point attempts in the first half but opened the second with his first 3 since the second game of the season.

Tyrese Maxey led the Sixers with 24 points.

There where big changes to HSA eligibility in the OBBB Act.

Currently any Bronze or Catastrophic plan is HSA eligible, even if it has copays or other benefits before the deductible. This might make such plans more attractive than a traditional "HSA" plan (which still exist), but in some cases the deductible and OOP can be even higher than what would qualify as a HDHP, because HDHP plan's deductibles are limited by statue, while Bronze plans (ex-HDHP) are only limited by actuarial value and not by deductible directly.

So, long story short, it might be worth looking into this plan a bit more.

U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was reportedly moved from his Washington, D.C., home to a military base due to Iranian threats on his life.

The New York Times, citing officials familiar with the matter, said that U.S. officials had concluded Iranian plotting against current and former American officials had reached a more advanced stage.

Separately, Reuters reported that the severity of the threats against Bessent was not immediately clear.

Bessent has led "Operation Economic Outcast," a Treasury campaign launched in August to isolate Iran economically through sanctions and other financial measures, in addition to the U.S. military actions.

The Times reported that Bessent's role in that campaign has made him one of the Trump administration officials Iran most wanted to target.

The move comes as the Department of Justice Department said late Tuesday that three Iranian nationals had been indicted on terrorism-related charges.

The department said the three were allegedly involved in nearly 20 attacks and attempted attacks across Europe and the United States.

The alleged plots included a September 2026 attack targeting RAF Fairford in the U.K., where U.S. military personnel were stationed.

"Anyone who plans a terrorist plot on a military base housing American servicemembers will be prosecuted by the United States government," Attorney General Todd Blanche said.

Earlier in October, British Prime Minister Andy Burnham said Iran was involved in the incident at RAF Fairford that led to the arrest of five men on suspicion of explosives and terrorism offences.

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi then rejected the allegation, writing on X that Burnham was "barking up the wrong tree."

RAF Fairford, in Gloucestershire, is a key U.S. Air Force operating location in Europe and has supported deployments of American strategic bombers and other aircrafts.

"We are seeing positive momentum toward an end to this Conflict," Trump added.

Asked Tuesday why he would not simply declare an end to the Iran war, Trump said he wanted to ensure U.S. allies in the region were "not hurt" and that there was a "long term solution.

However, Iranian officials have denied that negotiations are underway. Security chief Mohsen Rezaei said claims that talks were continuing were aimed at "easing economic pressures and reducing oil prices."
