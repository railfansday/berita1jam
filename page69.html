Consob e Commissione Atelli firmano un protocollo: più controlli sui conti dei club di Serie A e B

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

La Consob e la Commissione indipendente per la verifica dell’equilibrio economico e finanziario delle società sportive professionistiche hanno sottoscritto oggi un Protocollo d’intesa con l’obiettivo di sviluppare, sulla base delle rispettive competenze, una collaborazione attiva nei settori di comune interesse.

Le due Autorità si coordineranno attraverso un tavolo tecnico al quale è affidato il compito di valutare le questioni che richiedano una trattazione congiunta, condividere le informazioni sulle rispettive iniziative, segnalare i casi in cui emergano ipotesi di anomalie. Il tavolo tecnico, che si riunirà almeno una volta l’anno e ogni qual volta sia ritenuto opportuno, è composto dai responsabili delle unità organizzative competenti in relazione alle materie trattate. Il Protocollo prevede inoltre la collaborazione nell’ambito di indagini conoscitive e proposte normative su materie di interesse comune.

“Una vigilanza efficace nello sport è indispensabile per garantire la sostenibilità a lungo termine di ogni disciplina”, ha detto il Presidente della Consob, Guido Stazi, sottolineando come “senza un giusto equilibrio tra partecipazione di base, corretta gestione delle risorse economiche e benessere degli atleti, lo sport rischia di smarrire il suo insostituibile valore sociale ed educativo. Questo Protocollo ci consentirà di attivare le più ampie sinergie nell'azione di controllo sulle società sportive quotate e sui revisori delle squadre di calcio di Serie A e B, rafforzando la trasparenza e la solidità dell'intero sistema. I miei più sentiti auguri di buon lavoro a questa nuova Autorità, chiamata a svolgere un ruolo fondamentale per la tutela di un ecosistema sportivo sano”.

Per il Presidente della Commissione indipendente per la verifica dell’equilibrio economico e finanziario delle società sportive professionistiche, Massimiliano Atelli, “il Protocollo firmato oggi, oltre a dare attuazione ad una specifica disposizione della legge istitutiva della nostra Commissione, rappresenta un passaggio essenziale nella definizione del nuovo modello italiano di vigilanza economico-finanziaria sulle società sportive professionistiche. Un modello basato sul coordinamento tra i diversi attori istituzionali, e con specifico riferimento al segmento dei club quotati, a quello tra le risalenti attribuzioni della Consob e quelle della nuova Autorità indipendente che rappresentiamo".

World of Warcraft: Forever's in-game economy has been ravaged by an exploit that allowed players to farm thousands of gold

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

World of Warcraft: Forever has suffered its first economic nightmare, as a dungeon farm exploit has left some players with thousands of gold in their bags while the vast majority of players are barely above their first hundred.

This exploit was quickly and quietly hotfixed by Blizzard, but not before a currently-unknown number of players were able to make out like bandits. In basic terms, the new Blackmaw Hold dungeon includes several packs of non-elite, easy to kill enemies right at the start of the dungeon. Prior to the hotfix, the loot drop tables for these enemies were incorrect, and worth vastly more in terms of gold than they should have. Keen-eyed players spotted this but kept it quiet, running into the dungeon and quickly killing off these early mobs and resetting the dungeon every hour.

This was possible on every class, and according to Twitch streamer Ziqo, allowed players to farm out hundreds of gold in a few hours. He is seen streaming with over 4,000 gold - and claims other streamers made out with more than that.

For an idea of how much money that is, one of the most expensive things a player will likely buy on their character throughout the original Classic World of Warcraft is the expert riding skill. It takes a long time to save up for, and stands as a major accomplishment in the life of a character. It costs around 900 gold. These players, in just a few days of dedicated grinding, will have been able to pay this price several times over.

Looking at community hubs for the game (Reddit or the official World of Warcraft forums), regular players are demanding Blizzard take action - going further than just the already-deployed hotfix. Maya on the WoW forum wants the ill-gotten gold to be removed and for players to be banned who made use of the Blackmaw Hold exploit, writing: "Remove the gold or I and many others will just quit. This is worse than the bots at this point."

Flnrodi on Reddit, referring to streamers who took advantage of this exploit, wrote: "Not sure what to say. The fact that he did this all offstream says enough that [Ziqo] (and others) knew that this is not intended and 100 percent considered an exploit. Personally, I think there should be some kind of punishment and definitely all the gold [should be] revoked."

You may be wondering why this is such a big deal? So what if a few players can get fancy mounts faster, or snatch up some really good gear on the player auction house? Well, exorbitant sums of money in the hands of a few players can have a massive impact on a server's economy. You could, for example, use your newfound wealth to flip every listing of a certain rare resource on the auction house and mark the price up significantly for profit, which obviously inflates the price for regular players. Likewise, you can out-bid other players for rare and powerful BOE items, which in turn raises the prices of those as sellers may find they can make more cash than they could off such drops prior to the exploit.

This all has an inflationary effect, and could push a server's economy to a more expensive level faster than it otherwise would. Even if this effect isn't so drastic, these players can essentially gear out multiple characters in fantastic gear without putting in the hours, or out-bid the rest of the community on the Black Market Auction House for rare and largely unparalleled special loot.

The counter argument against punitive measures is that these players didn't cheat. They didn't buy gold from real-money-traders, nor did they plug in some external tool to give them an unfair advantage over others. They simply put in the time to get to around level 55, ran a dungeon, and killed mobs repeatedly for valuable drops. Would it have been a good thing to flag the exploit the moment they found out about it? Sure. But once other players at your level take advantage of it, and no strict ruling around whether doing so is punishable, it's arguably in a player's best interest to join in while the going is good.

As of writing, there's been no official word from Blizzard on what it intends to do beyond fixing the exploit. Given the required level to take on Blackmaw Hold, the number of exploiters cannot be too large, and the sheer amount of gold they gained in a small amount of time should make them easy to flag. Rolling back their accounts and reverting the gold they gained back to a pre-exploit amount certainly appears to be a popular option. Whether Blizzard actually does so, or enforces other punishments, is the big question.

Meta says it tuned Instagram to show less explicit content in India

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The change limits sexually suggestive and violent posts from accounts users don’t follow, after a government advisory in October.

Mark Zuckerberg on stage at Meta Connect 2026

Meta has changed how Instagram picks posts to show users in India. The aim is to show less sexually suggestive and violent content. The company made the change public on Tuesday, Reuters reported.

Meta now uses better AI models to check posts against its rules, Bloomberg reported. TNW has not seen Meta’s own statement and relies on both reports.

The change covers posts from accounts that users do not follow, according to Meta. It limits what Instagram pushes to users. It does not ban the posts.

Meta said users in India now flag suggested posts about half as often as a year ago. It did not share the raw figures, Reuters noted.

Users can also ask for fewer political posts and less sensitive content. In India, the most mature posts are not suggested by default, according to Bloomberg.

The update follows an October 10 notice from India’s tech ministry. It asked Meta to review its recommendation systems, so false or misleading posts do not spread too far.

Meta has faced months of pressure in India, its biggest market by users. In July, the ministry summoned Meta executives after the BBC found Instagram ads that pushed child abuse material. It then ordered the ads removed.

Instagram’s design is also being probed in Europe. In July, the EU told Meta to change Facebook and Instagram, such as autoplay and endless scroll.

Bayer Leverkusen beat Stuttgart 3-2 in the Frauen Bundesliga on Monday 16 November, with Caroline Victoria Kehrer scoring the winner in the second minute of stoppage time. Stuttgart had just drawn level through Mira Arouna in the 87th minute. Julia Boike refereed the matchday 10 fixture.

Leverkusen led at the break. Loreen Bender opened the scoring in the 20th minute, set up by Cornelia Kramer, and the visitors took a 1-0 lead into half-time.

Stuttgart answered immediately after the restart. Dafina Redzepi equalised in the 46th minute, and the Stuttgart forward went on to have the biggest influence on the match. She was rated 8.86, the highest on the pitch, with a goal, an assist, 3 shots and 5 key passes across the full 90 minutes.

Leverkusen restored their advantage in the 59th minute when Maja Sternad finished from a Valentina Mädl pass. Sternad was booked 3 minutes later, after Luzie Zähringer had been cautioned for Stuttgart in the 54th minute. Those were the only cards of the night.

Redzepi then turned provider, laying on the 87th-minute equaliser for Arouna, who scored with her only shot in 21 minutes on the pitch. The draw lasted barely 5 minutes. Mädl delivered again, and Kehrer converted in the second minute of stoppage time, having been on the field for just 2 minutes. Mädl finished with 2 assists in a 33-minute appearance.

The numbers favoured the winners without being one-sided. Leverkusen had 54% of the ball, completed 366 of 483 passes against Stuttgart’s 266 of 387, and put 8 shots on target. Stuttgart goalkeeper L. Giuliani’s side were kept in the game by 6 saves. Stuttgart actually shot more often, 15 times to 13, but only 4 of those efforts tested the Leverkusen goal and 8 went wide. Expected goals finished 1.43 to 1.2 in Leverkusen’s favour.

Leverkusen were caught offside 4 times. Stuttgart were not flagged once.

Pochettino says he would tell Guardiola to take USA job

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Mauricio Pochettino was appointed United States head coach in 2024

Mauricio Pochettino says he would tell Pep Guardiola to take the United States job if the former Manchester City manager was offered it.

Pochettino, 54, is leaving his role as the US men's team head coach to become Manchester United's new manager from 18 November, after signing a two-and-a-half-year contract until June 2029.

He takes charge of his final game with the US on Tuesday when they face Haiti in the second leg of the Concacaf Nations League quarter-finals in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Guardiola, 55, is taking a one-year sabbatical after stepping down as City boss in May.

Reports have suggested Guardiola could be a contender to succeed Pochettino in the US, although there has been no formal contact between the Spaniard and the United States Soccer Federation.

Pochettino told reporters on Monday his advice to Guardiola would be to take the role, "because it's a perfect moment to arrive at the federation."

The Argentine added: "Everything is on the right path, in the right direction.

"Of course, take it. Of course, you are going to find really good players, good people - the players can fit his style."

Former Tottenham and Chelsea boss Pochettino is replacing Michael Carrick at United. Carrick was sacked as head coach last week with the club in the relegation zone in 18th place.

His long-time assistant Jesus Perez, goalkeeping coach Toni Jimenez and athletic coach, 31-year-old son Sebastiano Pochettino, arrived in England following the US's 6-0 first-leg victory over Haiti in Miami last weekend.

Pochettino's first test back in the Premier League is an away trip to Anfield to face fourth-placed Liverpool on Sunday.

"They are in advance, they are preparing all there, because Wednesday when we will arrive, it is going to be non-stop," he said.

"For me, it's not difficult, I think we are well prepared to switch quick."

Prediction: The Stock Market Will Make a Big Move on Nov. 18 (Hint: Nvidia Will Be the Catalyst)

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Nvidia's third-quarter earnings report could send shockwaves through the stock market.

Nvidia (NVDA -4.12%) has been one of the market's most influential companies during the artificial intelligence boom, but the stock has struggled in 2026. Shares have declined 16% year to date, while the S&P 500 (^GSPC -1.92%) has added 8% and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -2.49%) has added 5%.

Investor uncertainty intensified last week when Nvidia announced the sudden resignation of CEO Jensen Huang. The company will announce its third-quarter fiscal 2027 financial results after the market closes on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Those results could trigger substantial volatility across the stock market the next day.

Nvidia's revenue growth has accelerated in five consecutive quarters amid strong demand for artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure, and the company beat Wall Street's consensus earnings estimate in each of those quarters. But the bar is especially high for the upcoming report, given growing skepticism about the durability of the AI boom.

Here's what Wall Street expects when Nvidia announces its financial results for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 after the market closes on Tuesday, Nov. 17:

Beyond the headline numbers, investors should pay attention to management's guidance for the fourth quarter. Nvidia's outlook will provide important clues about demand for AI infrastructure, particularly as technology companies continue to invest enormous sums of money in data centers.

Investors should also listen for commentary concerning the next-generation Vera Rubin compute platform (which started shipping in the last few months), competition from custom AI chips, and whether Nvidia customers are generating sufficient returns to justify their capital expenditures.

Finally, this will be the first earnings call since former CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang resigned. Interim CEO Colette Kress will likely face questions about the board's search for a permanent successor and whether the leadership change will disrupt Nvidia's technology roadmap or relationship with major suppliers and customers.

Nvidia is one of the largest companies in the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite, meaning the stock has a disproportionate impact on the performance of both indexes. Nvidia's earnings often influence market sentiment toward other semiconductor companies and the broader AI industry.

As shown above, Nvidia stock usually declined on the day following its last four quarterly earnings reports, dropping by an average of 0.5%. Meanwhile, the broader S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite also typically suffered losses on those days.

This time, strong results and optimistic guidance could reassure Wall Street that massive investments in AI infrastructure remain sustainable, potentially sparking a rally across the semiconductor industry and technology sector, or even the broader stock market. Having said that, history makes it clear that solid financial results do not necessarily translate into share price appreciation.

Conversely, disappointing guidance or cautious commentary about customer spending could increase anxiety about an AI bubble, especially given that Jensen Huang recently left the company. That could easily lead to a sell-off, and the drawdown could be severe. That's why I think Nov. 18 will be an unusually consequential day for the stock market.
