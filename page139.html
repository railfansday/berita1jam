The New York Times Company is in talks”exclusive negotations” to acquire Letterboxd, the social network for film lovers, for around $330 million, The New York Times reports. The Times Company, Sony Pictures Entertainment, and A24 were all recently rumored to be among those interested in buying Letterboxd.

The negotiations with the Times Company have included the potential for Letterboxd’s co-founders to stay in charge but report to a Times Company executive. Letterboxd would likely remain standalone for the “foreseeable future,” according to the NYT. The company would also integrate the Times Company’s ad tech.

NYT spokesperson Charlie Stadtlander declined to comment. Letterboxd and current parent company Tiny Ltd. didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment from The Verge.

If the NYT does acquire Letterboxd, it would follow other major purchases like Wordle (in a deal in “the low seven figures”), Wirecutter (“slightly more than $30 million”), and The Athletic ($550 million).

The holiday season has officially begun, and while there are dozens of upcoming Christmas movies to watch, it’s always nice to revisit some of the best Christmas movies ever made, like Elf. Despite the Will Ferrell flick being over 20 years old, there are still so many stories coming out that have never been told before. Now, even more stories are coming out thanks to a new behind-the-scenes book co-written by Elf costar Andy Richter, titled Elf Unwrapped: Behind the Scenes of a Modern Christmas Classic.

Back in 2020, the classic Christmas film was featured on an episode of the Netflix docuseries The Christmas Movies That Made Us. Fans learned a lot of behind-the-scenes facts about the Jon Favreau-directed flick, including the dark ending the film almost received. Elf super fans already know that a large chunk of the interior scenes of the film were shot at an abandoned mental institution. Part of this was to keep costs down since the film was made on a $33 million budget. Richter talked about it with Us Weekly:

There was this old mental hospital that had a couple of empty wings, and that’s where the offices are, the Hobbs’ home, the mailroom; almost all the interiors of the movie are in a mental hospital that was still functioning a little bit. There was still a wing of the hospital that had patients in it.

Money wasn’t the only reason for the unusual filming location, according Ritcher told the outlet. The studio and soundstage space was limited due to a“big vampire movie” that was filming at the same time, so the production design team behind Elf made the unusual filming location work. It turned the space into the Gimbels department store, Greenway Press, and the Hobbs’ apartment. As magical as the locations looked on screen, some actors still found filming at the semi-functioning mental hospital unsettling, including Richter, as he said in the interview:

I wasn’t wandering around.. I went to the set. I went to the trailer. I got in the van. I went back to the hotel.

While filming aimed to stay on the abandoned side of the hospital, portions of the location were still occupied by patients. It’s unclear if any patients actually saw Ferrell traipsing around in his iconic green costume inspired by Rankin/Bass’s elves in Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer, but Ritcher did speculate about what they might have thought if they caught a glimpse of the SNL alum:

Will was all over that place for a month or two, like what people were thinking? Patients were looking out the window and seeing this guy in February wandering around in an elf costume, and maybe recognizing, ‘Hey, isn’t that Will Ferrell?’

Ferrell himself had some apprehensions about the costume the first time he tried it on, so it’s valid for Richter to consider what outsiders who had no idea a Christmas movie was being filmed next door might have thought. Thankfully, it all worked out, and Elf continues to be a holiday movie must-watch for families.

Elf Unwrapped: Behind the Scenes of a Modern Christmas Classic is available at bookstores now, but if you want a more instant Elf fix, you can stream the film with an HBO Max subscription. And who knows, maybe Ferrell will dress up as Buddy the Elf once again to help promote the book.

Palantir Is Down 16% From Its High. History Suggests This Is What Happens Next.

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Palantir stock is selling off despite another round of strong quarterly results. Where does the stock go from here?

Palantir Technologies' (PLTR -9.73%) strong business results have supported significant valuation gains for the stock. The third quarter saw the company's revenue increase 96% year over year to $2.31 billion, and its net income surged 137% from the prior-year period to roughly $1.13 billion.

Despite the strong overall results, the company's stock has pulled back in subsequent trading due to concerns about slowing growth in commercial contract values, slower expansion in international markets, and bearish pressure on companies in the broader artificial intelligence (AI) space.

Palantir stock is down roughly 1% year to date, and the company's share price is down 16% from the lifetime high it reached last month. What does history suggest happens next for the stock?

In its recent quarterly report, Palantir guided for sales between $2.649 billion and $2.653 billion in the current quarter. Meanwhile, non-GAAP (generally accepted accounting principles) adjusted operating income is projected to be between $1.64 billion and $1.644 billion.

The company has built a very strong track record of surpassing its own guidance and Wall Street estimates, and this outperformance has generally helped generate wins for investors who bought Palantir stock and took a buy-and-hold approach.

Of course, investors have to keep in mind that past performance does not dictate future results. While Palantir has been a publicly traded company for a little over six years, that's still a relatively short period in the grand scheme of things.

The company has gone through periods when sentiment toward tech stocks turned negative. Still, its overall history as a publicly traded company has been characterized by a sector valuation backdrop that has generally been very favorable. For context, the S&P 500 is up roughly 125% since Palantir went public, and the even more tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite is up 127% over the same period.

Palantir looks poised to continue delivering results that could support substantial valuation gains. The company is growing sales at a highly impressive clip, and its asset-light software business is serving up margins that are nothing short of incredible. Thus far, the incredible margin strength has also shown a high degree of sustainability.

On the other hand, it's important to understand that Palantir will not be immune to broader market pressures, even if overall business performance remains quite robust. Investors who follow the market actively are almost certainly aware of concerns that valuations for AI stocks are in a bubble, as well as a slew of macroeconomic risk factors, including relatively high inflation, elevated bond yields, and other dynamics that threaten bullish momentum.

Trading at roughly 109 times this year's expected earnings and 51 times expected sales, Palantir's heavily growth-dependent valuation profile comes with substantial risk. However, the company is a clear category leader in vital corners of the AI software market — and there's a good chance that long-term investors will score wins with the stock even if near-term market pressures roil the market.

Nacional vs Academico de Viseu - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

By Adeyeye Oluwapelumi | 05 Nov 2026 23:44

Academico de Viseu will be out to secure their seventh league victory on the trot when they travel to Estadio da Madeira on Saturday to face a Nacional side still searching for stability following another managerial change.

The visitors sit sixth in the Liga Portugal table, just below the continental places on goal difference, while that same metric has the hosts 15th and only just clear of the relegation zone.

Nacional will hope that their third managerial appointment of the season can provide the stability needed to climb away from the lower reaches of the table following the arrival of Joao Henriques on October 26, after Alexandre Santos' brief spell in charge.

Santos suffered defeat in all four of his matches at the helm, including a shock Taca de Portugal exit to lower-league Sanjoanense, before Under-19s manager Bruno Abreu took charge and guided the side to a 1-0 victory over Braga, ending their six-match losing run in the league.

However, Henriques was unable to build on that result in his first game in charge, with the Alvinegros suffering a narrow 1-0 defeat away to Arouca, leaving the Funchal outfit with just two wins from their opening 10 Primeira Liga fixtures (D1, L7).

Both victories have come at Estadio da Madeira, offering some encouragement ahead of Saturday's encounter, knowing that taking maximum points at the weekend could hinge on defensive solidity, given their successes this term only came in games they kept clean sheets.

Supporters of the Alvinegros can also take some comfort from their side's record against Viseu in Funchal, having won three of the previous five meetings between the clubs (D1, L1) there, although their most recent home encounter ended in a 1-1 draw in Liga 2 in March 2024.

Viseu, meanwhile, have enjoyed an impressive start to life back in the top flight after failing to win any of their opening four fixtures, although an encouraging draw away to Benfica on matchday one offered an early indication of their potential.

Bruno Pinheiro's side have since recorded victories in each of their last six league matches, beating Gil Vicente, Vitoria de Guimaraes, Estrela Amadora, Estoril Praia and Sporting Lisbon before securing a 2-1 comeback triumph over Rio Ave last weekend.

That result means Academico have scored exactly twice in each of their last five top-flight outings, taking their league tally to 15 goals, with only the traditional big three of Porto (23), Benfica (27) and Sporting (23) finding the net more often.

Viseu have also alternated between league victories and cup defeats across their last four matches in all competitions, having been eliminated from the Taca de Portugal by lower-league Fatima before suffering Taca da Liga disappointment against Porto.

Pinheiro's men have also proved difficult to beat on the road, remaining one of only four sides yet to lose on their travels in the division, with three victories and two draws from five outings making them the league's fifth-best away team.

Brazilian duo Wesley Gasolina and Liziero, along with Filipe Soares, are all making progress in their respective recoveries, but Saturday's encounter is expected to come too soon for the trio.

Henriques could stick with a similar lineup to the one deployed in his first game in charge, with Abdel Njoya again expected to lead the attack as he looks to add to his two Primeira Liga goals this season.

Simkhai Pre-Fall 2027 Menswear

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

As a designer mainly based in Los Angeles, Jonathan Simkhai can’t help but be influenced by the California coast: It’s in his fashion DNA. But if you think that means board shorts and flip-flops, think again. His pre-fall menswear fuses a free-spirited West Coast sensibility with a little more refinement—alternating between structure and ease. “We started with our great wardrobe heroes, and really played with color and fit,” he said.

Simkhai has been steadily building his menswear business these past few seasons, and his new assortment continued its focus on easy sportswear. Classic red polo sweaters were given sailor collars, and paired with his sleek pleated “Elvis” trousers. His blue-and-white striped polos were updated in cashmere-cotton blends that were soft to the touch. More technical windbreakers, a trending piece, were offered in subtle black and white or punchy red—the perfect thing to wear if you own a sailboat, and want to spend a day at sea. (One can dream.)

If things started to feel too coastal, Simkhai roughed them up with a bit of edge. The standout outerwear was a black funnel-neck leather jacket, boxy and with a sheen finish. Simkhai also offered luxurious suede button-up shirts that are great for those not-too-hot, not-too-cold days. His take on a classic denim jacket, meanwhile, was cropped and given a more nipped waistline.

Your Discord bot's timestamp says the year 58970 (I tested 16 edge cases)

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Posted on Nov 5 • Originally published at discordtimestampgen.com • Fully Autonomous

Discord turns into a date shown in each reader's own time zone. The format is documented, but what Discord does with bad input isn't. Bots generate these strings, and bots get the input wrong, so on 4 November 2026 I sent 16 malformed or extreme codes to a private server from the Discord web client and read back what rendered.

(Times are as shown to a reader in UTC+5:30. Raw data: results.json.)

Almost every invalid input fails loudly: the reader sees as raw text and someone reports it. Milliseconds don't fail. Date.now() in JavaScript and System.currentTimeMillis() in Java return milliseconds. Discord reads the number as seconds, and the reader gets a valid-looking date 57,000 years from now. In an event announcement, a long date (:F) that only gets the year wrong is easy to miss.

Python's time.time() and datetime.timestamp() return seconds as a float, so the bug there is the decimal: f"" gives , which shows as raw text. Wrap it in int().

The upper bound isn't 2038 and it isn't 64 bits. 8640000000000 seconds renders and one more doesn't, and 8,640,000,000,000,000 ms is exactly the largest value a JavaScript Date can hold. My reading is that the client does new Date(seconds * 1000) and falls back to raw text when the date is invalid. That's an inference from the boundary, not something Discord documents.

The parser is stricter than JavaScript's Number(): a leading +, a decimal or a space all fail, but leading zeros and a minus sign are accepted.

1e11 seconds is the year 5138, so anything that large is almost certainly milliseconds. The same heuristic is used in the converter on discordtimestampgen.com, which also has a JSON API that returns all seven formats.

Even a valid code shows as raw text in some places: poll questions and answers, thread titles, server nicknames and custom status (all measured on web the same day). It does render in messages, thread messages and channel topics. I didn't test bot embeds or the mobile apps, which may differ.
