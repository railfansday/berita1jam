Denver-based Frontier Airlines has rapidly cut a total of 16 international routes from its network this year, with Atlanta (ATL), one of the airline's major bases, losing a total of eight routes overall. These network changes have taken place between March and August 2026.

Data from Cirium, an aviation analytics company, shows that the budget airline has reconfigured its international operations. It is redeploying aircraft on more lucrative domestic or other international routes following a difficult year for airlines in the face of high fuel prices and other operational demands. This data has been reviewed against 2026 and 2027 flight schedules.

Frontier is the second-largest operator at Atlanta. However, it is still just a marginal player when compared to Delta, which calls Atlanta home. Frontier operates around 6% of all flights from Atlanta, according to the airport's website. The airline trimmed eight nonstop international routes from the airport in April of this year.

These affected services to Puerto Vallarta (PVR), San José del Cabo (SJD), Santo Domingo (SDQ), Nassau (NAS), Aruba (AUA), Providenciales (PLS), St Maarten (SXM), and San Pedro Sula (SAP). This leaves Frontier with a network of 40 domestic and six year-round international destinations from Atlanta.

The remaining five year-round services from Atlanta to Cancún (CUN), Guatemala City (GUA), Montego Bay (MBJ), San José, Costa Rica (SJO), and San Salvador (SAL). There is also a seasonal link to Punta Cana (PUJ).

Earlier this year, Denver (DEN) lost two international services served by Frontier. The airline withdrew its operations to San José del Cabo (SJD) from the Colorado airport in March, while Denver to Puerto Vallarta (PVR) lost its international link in April. This was followed by Charlotte (CLT) also losing its international route to Cancún (CUN) after the airline pulled service on the city pair in April.

This leaves the airline with no international routes from Charlotte. Now, it only has domestic flights to Baltimore (BWI), Chicago–O'Hare (ORD), Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW), Denver, Detroit (DTW), Fort Lauderdale (FLL), Houston–Intercontinental (IAH), Miami (MIA), New York–LaGuardia (LGA), Orlando (MCO), Philadelphia (PHL), San Juan (SJU), and Tampa (TPA).

Frontier's international route from Raleigh/Durham (RDU) to Cancún was also lost in May. Meanwhile, Orlando's link to St Maarten (SXM) was axed in summer, after the airline pulled the pin in August.

Atlanta loses multiple leisure routes while competitors like Delta expand to absorb sunseeking travelers from the hub.

The decision to consolidate a number of international routes was also seen in both Chicago and Miami, with Frontier terminating two services from each city. Chicago Midway (MDW) lost its service to Cancún in April, while O'Hare (ORD) lost its link to Punta Cana (PUJ) in the Dominican Republic.

This leaves Frontier with 23 domestic routes from O'Hare, and a further nine domestic routes from Midway. Frontier is the second-largest airline at Chicago Midway, the city's second busiest airport after O'Hare. While Southwest operates more than 91% of all flights at Midway, Frontier served over half a million passengers between June 2025 and May 2026, accounting for 3% of all traffic.

Packers fan kicked out of Lambeau Field for spreading loved one’s ashes after loss

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

A Green Bay Packers fan was ejected from Lambeau Field after spreading a loved one’s ashes onto the field following the team’s loss to the Minnesota Vikings.

The incident occurred on Sunday, November 15, after Green Bay suffered a 30-10 defeat at home. Police were called to the stadium after a spectator was spotted dispersing what authorities later confirmed to be cremated human remains.

According to local news station WBAY, officers spoke with the individual and confirmed that the ashes belonged to a loved one. The fan was subsequently removed from the stadium for violating local regulations.

Under Green Bay Municipal Code 24-117, spreading human ashes onto the playing field is considered unlawful conduct at a public event.

The Packers addressed the incident in a statement to WBAY, acknowledging that some supporters want to honor deceased friends and family members at the stadium while emphasizing that the practice is prohibited.

“While we understand the desire of some fans to have their ashes, or those of friends or family, spread at Lambeau Field, it is against the law and prohibited at the stadium,” the team said.

Rather than scattering cremated remains, the organization encouraged supporters to consider alternative ways of remembering loved ones, including its commemorative brick program.

“To honor a loved one’s legacy appropriately at Lambeau Field, we refer fans to the commemorative brick program. We also suggest they can coordinate tributes in other appropriate ways with their funeral home,” the Packers continued.

The program allows supporters to purchase personalized bricks honoring individuals, creating a permanent tribute at the historic stadium without violating its regulations.

It’s been nearly three years since the sci-fi adaptation first premiered.

The second season of Netflix's 3 Body Problem finally arrives on January 14, which will place it nearly three years after the series first premiered. The streamer has dropped an action-packed trailer that's filled to the brim with sci-fi setpieces and it looks pretty nifty.

The second season will continue to adapt Cixin Liu's Remembrance of Earth's Past novel trilogy.A third (and final season) has already been greenlit. The books have been an international phenomenon for decades and the first season was a modest hit for the platform.

9 Modern Spy Thriller Shows To Watch To Prepare For Robert Pattinson’s Bond Era

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

The next era of James Bond is closer than ever, and with Robert Pattinson officially cast as James Bond, there's a lot to look forward to now that Amazon MGM is behind the wheel.

With Denis Villeneuve directing and a script from Steven Knight, the creative team behind the next James Bond film is now complete, with Pattinson tapped as the future 007. Expectations and stakes are high for the coming take on the franchise. As James Bond continues to push boundaries in the espionage genre, it would be smart to look to the best spy thriller TV shows that are capturing the attention of audiences everywhere.

Tom Hiddleston, the star of The Night Manager​​​​​​, has a James Bond energy himself, and he brings this gravitas to the role of Jonathan Pine in the critically acclaimed series. Set in Cairo, The Night Manager follows Jonathan, the titular evening manager of a high-end hotel, as he becomes involved with a dangerous ring of arms dealers and the agents trying to bring them down.

Also featuring Hugh Laurie, Olivia Colman, and Noah Jupe, The Night Manager's sleek style and complex storylines are elevated by the skill of the central cast. Laurie plays a villain worthy of any James Bond movie, and the dynamic between him and Hiddleston throughout the episodes will keep audiences on the edge of their seats.

Enormously popular, sharp, and proof that Apple TV can tackle thriller TV shows as well as science fiction, Slow Horses has been setting the tone for contemporary spy series since 2022. The announcement of Slow Horses' season 9 and 10 renewal doesn't come as a surprise, as the show has found a dedicated audience base thanks to the beloved cast led by Gary Oldman and Jack Lowden.

Also based on a book series, like James Bond, Slow Horses follows a group of disgruntled and low-achieving MI5 agents sent to Slough House to do the drudgery of the agency. However, somehow, the characters find themselves caught up in thrilling investigations. Though they lack the smooth grace of James Bond characters, this makes them even more interesting to watch.

A period drama, neo-noir, and a must-watch non-English-language series for audiences who haven't yet discovered it, Babylon Berlin is set in Berlin beginning in 1929. Following the rise to power of the Nazi Party and the fallout of World War I, Babylon Berlin is a fresh take on this period of history, which has been brought to life onscreen many times before.

Volker Bruch leads the cast as Inspector Gereon Rath, a tortured police officer caught up in a deeper conspiracy than he could ever imagine when he enters the seedy underbelly of the city. Though James Bond has always appealed to international audiences, the warm critical and audience reception of Babylon Berlin makes it clear that viewers are ready to see their heroes in espionage stories complicated and full of anguish.

Zoe Saldaña has made her mark on most of the biggest movie franchises of the 21st century, from The Avengers to Avatar​​​​​​. However, her recent work in the thriller series Lioness shows Saldaña giving one of the best performances of her career. She plays Joe McNamara, the CIA agent heading up the titular Lioness program by recruiting female agents.

One of the most exciting aspects of modern thriller TV shows is how ready they are to put women in leading roles and give them juicy espionage storylines up to par with the legacy of James Bond and other classic spy narratives. As the series has progressed, Lioness has only gotten better, crafting complex storylines and giving Saldaña and her co-stars plenty of opportunities to shine in action-oriented and nuanced emotional moments.

Black Doves season 2 recently dropped on Netflix, and the series' second outing has proven to be just as exciting, cementing it as one of the streamer's best thriller shows of the decade. With Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw leading the cast as Helen Webb and Sam Young, a pair of spies-for-hire, Black Doves is a twisting narrative of secrets, betrayals, and moral questions that the lead characters are forced to face throughout the seasons.

It's fun to watch Whishaw's performance in Black Doves, as it differs sharply from his work as Q in James Bond. In Black Doves, he gets the chance to step into the action-oriented side of spy work. Black Doves leans into the fun tropes of spy thrillers while pushing boundaries in ways that major franchises often struggle to do. Focusing on Helen as a female spy and Sam as a gay man, Black Doves expands the definition of a traditional espionage series to great effect.

Netflix's spy series Black Doves has cast Daniel Craig's Q, Ben Whishaw, as a James Bond-style cold-blooded assassin with a license to kill.

Based on the French series The Bureau, Paramount+'s The Agency boasts a star-studded cast, featuring some James Bond veterans of its own. Led by Michael Fassbender as Brandon Colby AKA "Martian," The Agency sees Brandon adjust to new assignments and politics after being pulled from undercover work. Jeffrey Wright co-stars as Henry Ogletree​​​​​​, Brandon's supervisor and confidant. Wright clearly draws upon his work in the Craig James Bond films as Felix Leiter in the series.

Though The Agency season 3 hasn't been officially confirmed for renewal, hopes are high for fans of the espionage show. Dealing with the relationship between dueling intelligence agencies and the intensity of being immersed in complex webs of secrecy, The Agency is reminiscent of later James Bond movies, which see Bond reflect on the toll his life as an agent has taken.

It's difficult to imagine anyone other than Sandra Oh and Jodie Comer playing Eve and Villanelle, the MI6 agent and brutal assassin who embark upon a twisted, intense obsession with each other in Killing Eve. The chemistry between Oh and Comer makes the intensity and depth of the love-hate relationship between Eve and Villanelle so compelling, and makes it impossible not to become obsessed with how their doomed romance will end.

Though Killing Eve received mixed reviews in the series' later seasons, it's worth sticking with the show until the end to discover what happens between Eve and Villanelle. James Bond has often faced criticism for its depiction of female characters, and while the Craig era corrected many of these dated choices, Pattinson's turn as Bond could learn from Killing Eve's complex depiction of female spies and assassins.

Eddie Redmayne is unrecognizable in The Day of the Jackal, Peacock's recent reimagining of the classic 1973 thriller film. As the Jackal, or Alexander, Redmayne is a ruthless, cold-blooded killer that the audience can't help rooting for. The Day of the Jackal chronicles the cat-and-mouse game between the Jackal, a world-renowned assassin, and the MI6 agent Bianca (Lashana Lynch), who is hot on his trail.

Peacock has now finally confirmed when Eddie Redmayne's TV assassin will be returning to the small screen for The Day of the Jackal season 2.

The Day of the Jackal has a lot in common with a traditional Bond narrative. Globe-trotting, glamorous, and full of beautifully designed weapons that make the Jackal nearly untraceable, the series doesn't skimp on tense action sequences or gorgeous set pieces. The Jackal is the kind of villain that Bond himself might come up against, and one that would make the viewer question who they want to win in a battle between the two.

Arguably the best spy thriller of the 21st century, The Americans is a dark, brilliantly-paced take on the Reagan era of the Cold War. It stars Keri Russell and Matthew Rhys as Elizabeth and Phillip Jennings, two Soviet KGB officers posing as American citizens and raising a family outside Washington, D.C. Grappling with the psychic toll of their double life and the life-and-death stakes of their work, The Americans only gets better as the seasons progress.

Though The Americans isn't as flashy or gadget-oriented as some other espionage thrillers, its realism and the gritty depictions of violence and manipulation make it stand apart from other additions to the genre. As James Bond enters a new era, the popularity of series like The Americans will influence how the franchise evolves, and any audience member who appreciates a smart spy series will fall in love with The Americans.

This article contains discussion of sexual assault.

Netflix's soapy drama Heartland takes pages out of Nashville's playbook with a twisty plot, star-studded cast, and killer original music. And like Nashville, Heartland is divisive among fans and critics. In the vein of a more beleaguered Rayna James (Connie Britton), Jessica Chastain plays a country-legend-turned-recluse, Misty Jones, who is sucked back into Nashville when her niece goes missing.

It's not the first time that Charlie Heart (Carter Faith) hasn't shown up for rehearsals or left calls unanswered. That's just how she is. But something about this disappearance feels different, and the cops won't do anything beyond a wellness check.

Charlie's father Donny (Paul Sparks) turns to his sister for help, and Misty begrudgingly steps back into the world that spurned her 20 years ago. Heartland peels back the layers of Music Row, exposing the exploitation of young women through brutally honest storytelling. In its heightened state, Shana Feste's crime/drama, described as "watchable" by ScreenRant critic Alex Harrison, aims to burn down the house built on the backs of artists such as Loretta Lynn, Dolly Parton, and Kitty Wells. Feste is best known for directing Country Song, as well as the criminally underrated horror film Run Sweetheart Run.

Heartland explodes with two big twists. Once Misty Jones realizes the cops won't do their jobs, she exposes a family secret to get the industry's attention. She's not Charlie Heart's aunt; she's her biological mother. The bombshell comes when she pulls out Charlie's birth certificate and appears on the popular TV talk show, The Roger Lane Show. Police investigators have no choice but to take notice, but Misty's crusade comes to a screeching halt when Charlie reveals herself to be very much alive.

In a stunning revelation, Charlie felt she had no other choice but to fake her own death. Live Forever Records owner Roy Combs (Garrett Hedlund) proved to be a slimy label executive who gave Charlie her career after demanding sexual favors in exchange for undeniable success, such as touring slots and radio hits. Roy possesses photos and videos from when she first arrived in Nashville at 16 years old, and it weighs heavily on her heart. Also, Roy took out a life insurance policy on Charlie, and it was a matter of time before he would plot her death, anyway.

In that way, Heartland carries a similar tone to Nashville, the hit TV show which ran from 2012 to 2018. Nashville's Juliette Barnes (Hayden Panettiere) explored Music Row backroom dealings, love affairs, scandals, and the intricacies of contractual obligations for recording artists. Heartland takes it one step further with its fake death scenario, making the stark reality that much harder to swallow when it comes to young women in Music City. Women aren't believed unless there's a dead body. While Rayna James and Misty Jones differ wildly, they are connected by the feeling of loss—of faded dreams and desperation.

Once the dust settles, Charlie Heart, along with several other women, come forward with their allegations against Roy Combs and other industry movers and shakers. The truth forces seven men within the country music scene and the police department out into the open. Charlie finds liberation in confronting her pain, and she eventually plays the iconic venue, the Bluebird Café, in promotion of her new album. With Misty smiling in the crowd, the triumphant moment rings like an alarm bell on an industry that's been long overdue for a real reckoning. Heartland picks up where Nashville left off.

When a flabbergasted Misty Jones learns the truth, she plots with her old nemesis and fellow country legend, Winnie Jo Lean (Jennifer Nettles), to trap Roy Combs. To lure Roy, Winnie invites Misty out to sing on a high-profile stage (think: the Ryman Auditorium) and reveals that her husband's domestic violence was the cause of her busted lip back in the day. Misty took the fall, and now, Winnie hopes to make it up to her. After performing together, Roy becomes enmeshed in his own performance, giving Misty a chance to steal his laptop.

Misty takes all the photo and video evidence back to Charlie. It's up to her and how/when she decides to expose the truth. Roy eventually shows up to Winnie's mansion, where they are hiding out. Roy attempts to run Misty over in the driveway, but she shoots out his tire, flipping the truck. She's sent to prison for several months for attempted murder, but it could honestly be way worse. After suffering some near-fatal injuries, Roy Combs comes under the microscope for a new batch of sexual assault charges from Charlie Heart and numerous other women.

In the post-Me Too era, it's still remarkably refreshing when popular media continues to give women and sexual assault victims a voice. Even now, it can be difficult territory to navigate, and a streaming hit like Heartland basking in actual justice can still prove instrumental in empowering other women to come forward with their harrowing stories. It might appear that Heartland missed the boat on the popularity cemented by Nashville, but considering artists like Ella Langley dominate sales and industry accolades these days, country music remains as popular as ever. And it's never too late to share one's truth.

Barnsley are staring down the barrel of a first fourth-tier campaign since the 1978/79 season, and things may be made even worse in January, with EFL content creator and Reds fan Callum Fowler predicting that both Reyes Cleary and Patrick Kelly could be sold.

Daniel Stendel's side were unable to capitalise on some momentum and 12 goals in a couple of cup wins in the week leading up to their trip to fellow bottom-four side Wigan Athletic on Saturday.

A 2-1 defeat at the Brick Community Stadium saw the Tics climb out of relegation danger, leaving the Tykes winless in nine league outings, picking up just two points in that time. They're only off the bottom thanks to a superior goal difference compared to Bromley, who have the same record as them after 13 games.

It's looking pretty bleak at Oakwell right now, and the prospect of losing their best players in January is looking even more likely at this moment.

Despite Barnsley's dire situation, there's no doubt that they currently employ two of the brighter young stars in the division. Both Cleary and Kelly have a ceiling that surpasses the third tier, and perhaps could see them playing top-flight football in their careers.

Cleary burst onto the scene last season with 20 goal involvements in 30 league starts, and while he hasn't been as influential so far this year, his quality is still there, as seen with the cross he delivered for the Reds' equaliser against Wigan on Saturday.

Arguably even more concerning is the potential loss of Kelly, though. Only Derensili Sanches Fernandes and Oliver Norwood have created more open-play chances than the Northern Irish international in League One this season, and he's been the main source of everything good at the club.

If there's one thing that the current regime at Barnsley under chairman Neerav Parekh can attest to doing well, it's ensuring that the club squeezes as much as they can out of buyers when they approach the Tykes for their players.

The likes of Liam Kitching, Mads Andersen, Michal Helik and Carlton Morris have all sold for seven-figure fees since the club came down to League One in 2022, and while undisclosed, it's likely that the late winter sale of Davis Keillo-Dunn to Wrexham eclipsed that, too. Some suspected above £2 million.

If anything, though, that ability to sell well just stokes the frustration surrounding the current hierarchy, as not a lot of that money, if any of it, is reinvested into the playing squad. Barnsley continue to take chances on young players, and it's led to this steady decline down the League One table.

Fowler doesn't think that, if Cleary and/or Kelly are sold this January, they will be replaced adequately, and then a Barnsley squad which is already in trouble will be even worse.

"It's heart over head at times, because I'm thinking, 'surely they'll chuck some money at this in January,' but they never have, not once," he continued.

It's close to universally agreed that Daniel Stendel has Barnsley coached well — at least, better than their league position would suggest — but the general naivety of the squad and the youthful nature often leads them to fade away in games.

The Tykes have led in seven of their 13 games so far this season, but have won just two of those. They've conceded 11 times in the final half hour of games, and four times in the final 10 minutes.

Photographer Captures Eerie Images of Looming Data Centers in Virginia

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Photographer Captures Eerie Images of Looming Data Centers in Virginia

A photographer has set about documenting data centers in northern Virginia — an area that has the highest concentration of data centers in the world.

Stephen Voss’s unnerving photographs show giant box buildings looming over housing developments, baseball fields, and cemeteries.

“Three years ago, I was driving back from a photo shoot in Virginia when I noticed a data center for the first time, in all its drab, boxy glory. Here was the physical reality of something we’ve somehow agreed to call ‘the cloud’,” Voss tells PetaPixel.

The disconnect, Voss says, stuck with him. Over the next three years, he made over 100 trips to data centers in northern Virginia, exploring how these facilities intersect with the communities and places where people live their lives.

“Where does a data center end and someone’s neighborhood begin? What does it look like when the infrastructure behind our digital lives becomes the view from someone’s backyard?” Voss asks.

The project became Every Past and Tomorrow, a book Voss intends to self-publish via Kickstarter.

“Most of us rarely think about the infrastructure behind our online lives — where our photographs are stored, our music is streamed, or an AI prompt gets answered,” Voss says.

“For people living near data centers, those buildings are part of their everyday lives and I don’t think we should discount what it means to live right near one.

“In some neighborhoods, the sounds of cooling fans and natural gas turbines can be heard inside of their homes. People watch their kids play baseball at fields with 75-foot data center walls just past the outfield fence.”

During the course of his project, Voss says he’s witnessed forests cut down, empty fields become enormous buildings, and places where he once made photographs closed off behind fences.

“Every time I return, I see a landscape changing and felt this urgency to document as much of it as possible,” he adds.

Voss says the landscape is changing so much that Google Maps can’t keep up.

“Street View and satellite imagery often show an empty field where there’s already a building,” he says. “So, I spend hours driving around, searching through online permits and county records, and returning to places again and again to see how they’ve changed over time.”

Over the course of the project, Voss gained a drone license so he could take to the air to better show the relationships between data centers and the neighborhood.

However, bringing massive nondescript buildings to life is no easy task, and he started to see the giant blank walls as a canvas.

“I dug through AI research papers, terms of service, and old science fiction,” Voss explains. “At night, I projected words from those texts onto the data centers themselves — returning the language of this technology to the structures that make it possible.”

Voss says that more than once security guards came and spoke to him as he projected text onto the buildings, making him hard to miss.

To support Voss’s book Every Past and Tomorrow: The Physical Landscape of AI, head to his Kickstarter page. More of his work can be found on his Instagram and website.

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

A single lacquered piece can make a neutral room feel instantly more interesting.

After years of quiet luxury, soft neutrals, and interiors that favor understated details, a little drama feels like a very welcome change. High-gloss furniture is making a comeback, bringing shine, color, and a bit of personality back into our homes. Whether it's a lacquered accent table, a sculptural console, or a statement cabinet with a reflective finish, these pieces have a way of catching your eye and making even the simplest room feel more interesting. And honestly, who says every piece of furniture has to blend into the background?

The look also ties into Neo Deco, a modern take on Art Deco that brings together glamorous materials, bold silhouettes, and a fresh approach to decorating. But you don't need to turn your living room into a scene from The Great Gatsby to get in on it—a single glossy piece can add just the right amount of contrast to a neutral space, while mixing lacquer with natural wood, textured upholstery, or warm metallic accents keeps things feeling layered and livable.

Ahead, designers share how to bring high-gloss furniture into your home and embrace a little more shine without going over the top.

Not ready to go all-in on the high-gloss trend? An accent table is your low-commitment, high-impact way in. Tucked beside your favorite armchair or pulled up next to the sofa, a lacquered side table brings just enough shine to make the whole corner feel a little more interesting. The best part is the contrast, so have fun mixing finishes. Carol Alda, interior designer at Z Gallerie, loves pairing a glossy table with a “velvet or textured woven chair.” Just make sure the table sits at a comfortable height next to the chair so you can actually use it for your coffee (or cocktail).

Color is another way to make the piece feel right at home. Ali Esmaili, design expert and owner of Esmaili Rugs, suggests pulling the shade of your accent table from a patterned rug, which creates an easy connection between the two. An oxblood, emerald, or classic black table brings plenty of Neo Deco attitude, especially in a sculptural shape. It's a small swap, but one that can give even the most neutral living room a welcome dose of personality.

A little high-gloss feels right at home in a bar or entertaining area, where a touch of drama is practically part of the assignment. A lacquered bar credenza or sideboard in a saturated shade can turn your glassware and favorite bottles into a whole display moment.

Jennifer Fitzpatrick, founder of Studio J & Co., explains that high-gloss finishes bring extra energy to a room by reflecting daylight and catching the flicker of candles and lamps in the evening. In a bar area, that means your decanters, glassware, and cocktail accessories get their own little spotlight. Fitzpatrick recommends pairing high-gloss pieces with unlacquered brass, fluted glass, and textured textiles.

The coffee table is already the unofficial centerpiece of the living room, so why not give it a little extra attention? A lacquered version in a rich color or an unexpected shape can instantly make the space feel more interesting. And don't worry about all that shine feeling too formal. Fitzpatrick says today's high-gloss pieces are all about sculptural shapes, from curved profiles to clean geometric forms. A lacquered cocktail table can also catch the glow of your lamps in the evening, adding a little atmosphere without visually crowding your seating arrangement.

To keep the look feeling relaxed, mix that reflective surface with pieces that have plenty of texture. Esmaili loves pairing high-gloss furniture with vintage rugs, noting that “the gloss adds depth, movement and a touch of theatre.” The contrast between a shiny coffee table and a well-loved rug with plenty of pattern and texture gives the room a more layered, lived-in feel. Add a soft sectional and a few simple accessories, and you've got a statement piece that still feels right at home.

Can you use a keyboard and mouse with an Xbox Series X?

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

There's no question the PS5 has handed the Xbox Series X and S their collective rear ends from a sales perspective this console generation. That's a shame for Microsoft fans. Even though there are drawbacks you should understand before buying an Xbox Series X, for my money, it's still an excellent console. It's also one that supports a rich suite of accessibility options, as does its little sibling. One of the biggest? Fairly robust keyboard and mouse support.

Using traditional PC peripherals on a console might seem a bit outfield, but hey, the Dreamcast was doing it a quarter of a century ago, so there's definite precedent. There are actually a ton of Xbox games that offer at least partial mouse and keyboard support. Let's get into the mouse and keyboard options that should work with the Xbox Series X/S, and also discuss how to set them up. Plus, we'll examine just how many games support non-gamepad control schemes on Microsoft's current-gen consoles.

You sure can... but there are caveats. While Microsoft has done a commendable job at providing accessibility features for Xbox fans, mouse and keyboard support on Xbox Series X/S isn't universal across all games. Additionally, of the titles that do work with PC-based peripherals, not all of them offer native support. That means certain actions in non-native titles will require you to have a controller nearby to progress.

There are more restrictions, too. There's currently no way to connect a Bluetooth mouse or keyboard to either Xbox Series console. For those looking to hook up their favorite PC gizmos, you're looking at either wired peripherals or ones that come with 2.4GHz dongles.

That's slightly annoying. The best Xbox Series X/S accessories are generally wireless because you're likely sitting a decent distance away from your TV while playing your favorite games. Unless you keep your Xbox at a desk like a PC, wired mice and keyboards are probably out of the question for everyday living room play. Dongles it is, then.

Oh, and there's one more catch. You can't use a mouse to navigate around the Xbox dashboard, which is a bit of a bummer.

Setting up a keyboard and mouse on an Xbox Series X/S is relatively simple, but functionality beyond that is going to vary based on the specific game. The easiest and most practical way to get started with PC peripherals on your Xbox is to plug in a wireless keyboard dongle and a mouse dongle: You can use the two USB-A slots on the back of either console, or the one at the front.

From there, you can utilize the keyboard for typing on the Xbox dashboard (though as previously mentioned, mouse navigation is a no-go). Once you launch a game, things are a tad up in the air. The title may recognize your PC-style peripherals immediately, and you can start pointing and clicking. Or, you may well need your trusty Xbox controller to navigate into the settings menu, where you should be able to select mouse and keyboard inputs, if the title supports them. Microsoft provides the platform, but accessibility options are up to the individual developer, so this workflow will vary game to game.

Basically, prepare to always have your gamepad in reach if you want to use typical PC peripherals with an Xbox Series console. Just in case.
