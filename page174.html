All About Saoirse Ronan’s Husband, Jack Lowden

📅 Jul 28, 2024  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Saoirse Ronan and Jack Lowden have been together for over five years. They tend to keep their lives together very private, but have posted some shots on social media as their relationship progressed.

In July 2024, it was reported that the couple had gotten secretly married. With just a few friends and family members present, they had a ceremony in Edinburgh, Scotland, around the weekend of July 20, 2024. In spring 2025, Ronan revealed her first pregnancy, and the couple was seen pushing a stroller that September.

Here’s everything to know about Lowden and his relationship with Ronan so far.

Lowden is also an actor from Scotland, with a long career onstage. In 2013, he won both an Olivier Award and the Ian Charleson Award for his role as Oswald in Ibsen’s Ghosts. He’s also had some significant roles in film and TV, including Christopher Nolan’s Dunkirk.

In 2020, he bought a home in west Cork with Ronan, but they also live in London and have a terrier named Fran. The couple shares a production company with producer Dominic Norris. Their film The Outrun stars Ronan.

In December 2018, reports surfaced that they were dating after they were seen together at an after-party at Central Park’s Tavern on the Green. The pair played husband and wife in the film Mary Queen of Scots. Sightings of them over the next few years were sparse, and they infrequently appear together on social media. In 2020, they were photographed out in London. In 2022, they were spotted at the Australian Open together. On New Year’s Eve 2023, Lowden shared a mirror selfie of them to celebrate.

In 2019, a reporter for The Telegraph asked Ronan, “Am I allowed to ask if you’re going out with the person that everyone thinks you’re going out with?”

Earlier that year, Lowden was asked about Ronan by ES Magazine and answered the same way, but with more words.

“Oh I’m not going to talk about that. I don’t like talking about that side of life,” he said. “Most of the actors I know live pretty normal lives, it’s just every now and again they have to do something ridiculous or extraordinary. My life hasn’t changed. People seem more interested—I guess that’s changed, for sure.”

Lowden has gushed about Ronan when talking about her acting, including in an interview with i newspaper.

“The woman is a force of nature, on- and offscreen, and utterly fearless and a brilliant leader as well,” he said. “My first love is actors, and when you’re given a Ferrari like her, it’s all about how you make everything good for the Ferrari to show off. Don’t make a Ferrari do what a Renault Clio does. It’s a dreadful analogy, but they’re like gold dust, good actors. When there’s someone like that, you just have to facilitate them and you will get gold. And my God, did we.”

He praised her again in a March 2025 interview with The Times, saying that seeing her perform in Mary Queen of Scots inspired him to keep acting and not quit. “There was me at 27 thinking about stopping because I felt self-conscious or like I didn’t know how to do it,” he said. “And then watching this 23-year-old carry the weight of a huge film like that with ease and grace and majesty. It was remarkable to watch. She makes it look so easy, like breathing. It was totally inspiring.”

The pair made their debut on the carpet as a married couple at the 76th Primetime Emmy Awards at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on Sept. 15, 2024. Lowden wore a classic black tux with satin lapels. Ronan was wearing a custom Louis Vuitton two-piece with a sleeveless top and trailing skirt. Around her neck, she wore a stunning diamond necklace by Bulgari.

In an October 2024 interview with USA Today, Ronan shared that she once thought she’d “never” have a partner or a “beautiful network” of people because her career kept her so busy.

“My life has become so much more than just work,” Ronan said. “For a long time, that’s all I did; that’s how I met people and made sense of the world.”

She continued, “I feel like I’m part of a group, which I never thought I would have. I used to think, ‘You’ll never have friends. You’ll never have a partner.’ I was so in my own world, in a lot of ways. So now, to feel like I’ve got a very rich personal and social life—it’s so precious to me.”

In March 2025, Lowden offered rare comments on his relationship with Ronan in his The Times interview. When asked how they keep their marriage strong as two actors whose jobs sometimes require travel, he said, “[We] just have to check in with each other a lot. Obviously it made it a lot easier when we worked on the same project.”

They plan to work together again, he added, “but the question is, how do we do it, because Saoirse, she has ambitions as well to direct.”

On May 22, 2025, Ronan made an appearance at the Louis Vuitton Cruise at Palais des Papes in Avignon, France, wearing a semi-sheer dress by the fashion house that revealed she and Lowden are expecting their first child. The silky black gown was covered in matching black lace trim and folded around her body, showing off her black sandals under the asymmetrical skirt.

On May 31, the couple was seen in London. Ronan wore a form-fitting black bodysuit that appeared to show off her pregnant figure, under a puffer jacket and a sweater tied around her waist. She had on a baseball cap and sunglasses. Lowden was dressed in black sweatpants and an Adidas hoodie and was carrying a backpack.

On Sept. 11, 2025, Hello! reported that the couple had quietly welcomed their first baby. According to the outlet, Ronan and Lowden were seen earlier this month in London pushing a stroller. Lowden later spoke about becoming a father in his November 2026 GQ cover story.

Speaking to People, Ronan revealed that she hopes to act alongside her husband in the future. “I mean, I would love for us to work together again. I’ve said to him I really want him to do a play with me because he’s so incredible onstage, and that’s where he really comes to life,” she said.

“I think that’s where he feels at his best, and it’s not necessarily where I feel at my best, so I would really like to do it with him so he can hold my hand a little bit,” Ronan continued. “I mean, listen, we met through work and we’ve produced stuff together, and we’re very much a part of one another’s work life as well as personal life.”

Still, Ronan said Lowden needs a little more convincing. “It just feels like when the right thing comes along, it would be the obvious choice for us to do it together. But I think he’s more hesitant to work with me than I am with him,” she said. “I don’t know. I think he just finds it weird. He finds it weird working with any of his friends. He gets embarrassed.”

Lowden praised his wife’s acting and opened up about being a father for the first time in his GQ profile.

He reflected on watching her shoot a film in Italy that summer. “I’m going to sound a bit soppy, but getting to watch her act, in the flesh—that’s how we met—is really wonderful,” he said. “She’s so at home doing it.”

He also spoke about welcoming their first child in 2025. “Since I was a kid, I always knew I wanted to be a dad, and now I am,” he admitted. “You become aware that you’ll watch your kid grow up—sounds morbid—and you think, ‘Oh my God, I’m in charge of a kid, and I’m still a kid myself.’”

The Best Exercises for Lymphatic Drainage, Courtesy Of A Detox Expert

📅 Mar 28, 2024  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

We are a world obsessed with the lymphatic system right now and the best exercise for lymphatic drainage. Google searches for lymphatic drainage have gone up by 60% across America year over year, while on TikTok there are over 79 million views on the term and growing.

Lymphatic drainage massage (and other techniques, like dry body brushing), can help to reduce water retention, puffiness and inflammation by flushing out toxins and excess liquids. Celebrities love it, but is it accessible to the rest of us?

The easy answer—happily!—is yes. And celebrity lymphatic specialist Rebecca Faria (also known as @DetoxbyRebecca on Instagram) is leading the charge. Based in Beverly Hills, Faria specializes in Brazilian lymphatic drainage (a rhythmic massage, which combines pressure and long strokes), and has a roster of A-list clients who swear by her techniques (including Jennifer Aniston, Hailey Bieber, Ariana Grande, and Anne Hathaway). While a treatment with one of Faria’s team starts from $350, she’s shared some of her favorite (easy) exercises that are designed to stimulate your lymphatic system at home. And they’re free.

Done while either laying down, seated, or standing, knee hugs aren’t just good to get the lymphatic system going, but also help release back tension. Hug one knee into your chest, and hold for 20 seconds, then do the other side.

Lie on your back and place the soles of your feet together, letting your knees fall out in a butterfly formation. This stretch is also a fantastic way to stretch your hips, as well as release your pelvic floor.

While lying down, hold your legs straight up, then lift one as you lower the other. These are called flutter kicks–do 20 of them. (Incidentally, these are also great for your core, a muscle group that has long term benefits to help with bloating.)

These aren’t just for your Pilates class. Remain lying down. With one leg on the floor, lift the other leg and move in a circular motion 20 times. Repeat on the other leg.

No bike needed. With your legs in a table-top position, straighten one leg, then the other, 20 times.

Another Pilates movement to try out. Lying on your left-hand side and keeping the legs relatively straight, circle your right leg 20 times. Do the same on the other leg.

Stand with your feet flat on the floor, then raise up onto your toes, lifting your heels off the ground. Do this 20 times. This is also a favorite movement of Jesse Inchauspé (aka the Glucose Goddess) to help with balancing blood sugar.

Ballon d'Or 2027 Power Rankings

📅 Oct 31, 2023  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Harry Kane recently won the 2026 Ballon d'Or, but it's already time to look forward to what the next year of football will bring.

More importantly, who will be in contention for the prestigious individual prize in 12 months' time?

Much of that will hinge on which teams claim the biggest trophies, including league titles and the Champions League.

In the past, the prestigious individual accolade was based on the performance of players across a calendar year. However, the votes are now based on the results of the season.

Despite being a full year away from the 2027 ceremony, we've listed – and ranked – the players who are best placed to win the Ballon d'Or based on their respective teams' chances of success as well as their early-season form.

We've determined the order based on the following:

Desire Doue already looks like he's stepping up and becoming one of the most important players Luis Enrique has at PSG. With Bradley Barcola gone and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia absent for several months, the 21-year-old will be crucial.

He finished 14th in the 2025 Ballon d'Or and will be looking to improve on that next year. Having missed out on the 30 nominees in 2026, Doue has started the new season with eight goal contributions in 11 appearances for the French champions.

Will Ousmane Dembele regain the Ballon d'Or and join the exclusive club of players who have won the award more than once? If he can get back to the form that saw him crowned in 2025, there's every chance.

PSG are aiming for three Champions League titles in a row, and Dembele will now be vital to those plans with Khvicha Kvaratskhelia sidelined long-term. He's never been the most clinical in front of goal and only has four goals this season so far, but the France international brings pace, skill and technical ability to any team he plays for.

Is there a better all-action midfielder or set-piece maestro in world football right now? Except for his compatriot Jude Bellingham, the Arsenal and England star has no equal as things stand. 40 Greatest Football Players Of All Time (2026)

📅 Dec 21, 2022  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

One of the great football debates will continue to rage on so long as the sport exists. Indeed, deciding who is the greatest footballer of all time is never easy, particularly as fans, wearing their bias boldly, claim their club legend is the best.

It tends to be a common theme that a lot of people consider Cristiano Ronaldo, Lionel Messi, Diego Maradona and Pele to be the most prominent names in the hat for being the best player to have ever played the game, but always in different orders.

Unsurprisingly, all four of those names make it onto our list, but who else joins them in a ranking of the 40 greatest footballers of all-time?

Factors taken into consideration when ranking these players are as follows:

For Liverpool fans, Kenny Dalglish is the club's greatest ever footballer. He scored the winning goal in the 1978 European Cup final, going on to win that competition three more times, while also delivering six league titles to Anfield, not to mention a whole host of other honours. Celtic fans also hold him in similar esteem after he won four league titles, four Scottish Cups, and a Scottish League Cup with the Bhoys, too.

'King Kenny' also made 102 appearances for Scotland, their most ever, and is the joint-leading goalscorer (on 30, with Denis Law). His influence on British football cannot be overstated, as he went on to win the Premier League as Blackburn Rovers manager, having already coached Liverpool to three First Division titles. He truly dominated football throughout the 1970s and 80s.

Michael Laudrup was well and truly one of the most graceful midfielders to ever play the sport. 'I've been trying to be like him since I was a kid," Andres Iniesta once said of his childhood idol. That should tell you all you need to know about the Danish maestro.

Laudrup won La Liga titles with both Barcelona and Real Madrid, and also won Serie A in Italy with Juventus and Eredivisie with Ajax. Despite playing with some of the greatest footballers of all time, both Romario and Raul have described the Dane as the best player they ever played with. Michel Platini once said, “Michael had everything except for one thing: he wasn’t selfish enough.” Perhaps he would rank a little higher on this list if he was, but his unselfishness certainly didn't hold him back much.

Luis Suarez (not to be mistaken with the Uruguayan striker of the same name) was a wonderfully talented Spanish midfielder. Had he been born four or five decades later, he would have slotted seamlessly alongside the likes of Xavi, Iniesta and Sergio Busquets. Instead, he simply led the way for them as a pioneer of the game.

Indeed, Suarez won La Liga twice with Barcelona, which helped him seal the 1960 Ballon d'Or. He would then deliver three Serie A titles for Inter Milan, while also winning two European Cups in the same decade. He played more like an attacking midfielder (bit like Iniesta) in his youth, but then became a deep-lying playmaker (more akin to Xavi) as he aged.

Bobby Moore is one of the true iconic footballing captains of the sport, famously leading England to their only ever World Cup triumph in 1966. The legendary defender was best known at club level for his time with West Ham, leading them to FA Cup glory in 1964, while also winning the Charity Shield and European Cup Winners' Cup with the Hammers.

Pele spent his career making a mockery of centre-backs across the globe, more on that later, but Moore was one of just two men who truly gave the Brazilian a tough time. The late striker recalled: "You see, whoever I played against, I had the best defender marking me, but the best were Bobby Moore and Franz Beckenbauer."

Modern football fans will best know Giuseppe Meazza as the alternate title to the San Siro, after it was renamed in his honour back in March 1980. Spending the best years of his career with Inter, and returning there at the end of his career, winning three Serie A titles and later managing them, it's no wonder he's so loved in those parts. He also briefly played for AC Milan.

His greatest success came at the international level, though, as he led Italy to win two consecutive World Cups – in 1934, and then as captain in 1938. Despite playing so many years ago, only one man has more than his 33 goals for Italy (Luigi Riva, 42). Nicknamed Il genio (The Genius) by the Italian press, Meazza was a true joy to watch and will forever be one of the greatest strikers in football history.

Sir Stanley Matthews is one of the best English players ever. He was knighted in 1965 for his achievements in football while he was still at Stoke City. He remains the only footballer to be knighted during his playing career. Although he came from Stoke, his best achievement came at Blackpool, where he won the FA Cup in 1953.

Three years later, he won the inaugural Ballon d'Or, beating the likes of Alfredo Di Stefano and Ferenc Puskas to first place. He also remains in the English record books for making an appearance in a top-flight match at the age of 50.

Gareth Bale being on this list might surprise some, particularly when someone like Eden Hazard isn't. However, he was consistently world-class and epitomised the idea of 'hard work'. He made a name for himself at Tottenham Hotspur. The Welshman won just one trophy there - the 2008 Carling Cup - but he carried the team on his back.Bale famously broke onto the scene with a hat-trick away to European champions Inter Milan in 2010.

He eventually moved to Real Madrid for a world-record fee where he won everything he could. Bale's overhead kick to win the 2018 Champions League against Liverpool remains one of the greatest goals in the competition's history.

Manuel Neuer is renowned for being one of the first 'modern' goalkeepers, who was extremely skillful with the ball at his feet and has adopted the role of sweeper-keeper for many years. In fact, in 2020, he was awarded the International Federation of Football History & Statistics (IFFHS) goalkeeper of the decade award for his performances between 2011 and 2020.

He has won an abundance of team trophies as well. Most notably, he won the World Cup with Germany in 2014. Domestically, he has won the Bundesliga 13 times and the Champions League twice, with the most recent of these triumphs coming in the 2019/20 campaign.

Carlos Alberto is most well-known for captaining the Brazil team in the 1970 World Cup final. The Selecao beat Italy 4-1 in the final, with Alberto scoring the last goal, which is considered one of the best goals in football history. After a period of swift passes between the men in yellow, Pele laid the ball off to the right-back who smashed the ball into the bottom corner in front of over 107,000 people at Estadio Azteca.

Alberto was recognised for his achievements for club and country in 1998 when he was selected for the World Team of the 20th century. Domestically, he won 16 trophies for Fluminense, Santos and New York Cosmos before eventually retiring in 1982.

Kaka won the Ballon d'Or in 2007 after winning the Champions League with AC Milan. The team and individual achievements solidified his status as one of the greatest players of all time. He also won the World Cup in 2002 in his first year representing his country, highlighting his legendary status at an early stage. For AC Milan, he excelled over six years from 2003-2009, scoring 75 goals in 270 matches.

His spell at Real Madrid in the years was not as impressive, but he still managed to score 29 goals in 120 matches while winning La Liga and the Copa del Rey. The Brazilian will always be remembered as one of the sport's best entertainers — and one of the best Brazilians.

How The Southlands Turns Into Mordor (LOTR Map & Full Explanation)

📅 Oct 05, 2022  |  Category: Science  |  Read Source →

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power showed how the peaceful, sleepy region of Middle-earth known as the Southlands became Mordor, the seat of power for the Great Evil that defined the Third Age. J.R.R. Tolkien never offered much insight into Mordor before Sauron turned the region into a smoking hub of villainy, and while The Rings of Power tried valiantly to hide its big twist, plenty of viewers quickly deduced the Southlands was actually Second Age Mordor.

Essentially, an ancient key switches on a dormant Southlands volcano. The resulting cataclysm brings forth ash, steam, fire, rock, water, and, most of all, misery, and by the end of The Rings of Power season 2, the transformation of the Southlands is well underway. However, even for a being as powerful as Sauron, morphing a relatively pleasant land of plains and mountains into hell on Middle-earth is no simple feat. One does not simply turn the Southlands into Mordor — it's a process of epic proportions that begins with a disgruntled tavern owner and ends with a permanent scar upon the map.

The — quite literal — key to turning the Southlands into Mordor proves to be the broken black sword kept hidden by Morgoth loyalist Waldreg. The weapon slots into an ornate hole at the Elven watchtower of Ostirith and begins a chain of events that give rise to Mordor — but who constructed this mechanism, and for what purpose?

Although Ostirith is occupied by Elves when The Rings of Power begins, the magical black sword and skull mechanism obviously aren't their doing. The Southlands fought for Morgoth during the War of Wrath, so Ostirith was once under the control of the dark forces of Middle-earth, meaning the sinister key and its corresponding doom-switch were likely forged during this period.

It can also be assumed this dark machinery built into the mountain wasn't just for some mundane purpose like opening the dam or changing water levels. Not even a showman like Sauron would craft a magic black sword with a giant skull lock for such an ordinary reason. Whoever made this lock-and-key combo apparently had the formation of Mordor in mind from the beginning.

The Rings of Power hasn't yet offered enough information to say whose mind the Mordor master plan originated from. The black sword dates back to Morgoth, but Morgoth wasn't the type to plan for his own downfall, which leans more toward Sauron as the chief architect. The Southlands-shaped sigil on Finrod's corpse proves Sauron held an interest in the area for centuries, and the Lord of the Rings villain could've carried out his designs in secret.

Going into Rings of Power season 3, Sauron's evil kingdom is fully established, and he is set to forge the One Ring in the fires of Mount Doom. As this story continues, the show may provide further details regarding Sauron's overarching plan for the Southlands, including when he first set to work on the idea and just how much came from his own mind.

The Rings of Power's Ostirith tower is located on the eastern side of the Ephel Dúath mountain range (red circle on the map above), meaning the offending waterway could be either the Poros or some tributary of Middle-earth's huge River Anduin. Regardless, turning the black sword lowers the wall holding this water back, and the cascade courses down the rocky cliff, destroying part of Ostirith's battlements along the way (further proof that the release system wasn't designed for regular dam control).

Once the water hits the bottom level, it flows into a cavern and through a system of tunnels and trenches. These are the very same tunnels and trenches that Adar's Orcs have been digging ever since their Southlands invasion began. Their purpose, as confirmed in The Rings of Power season 1, episode 6, was to direct the flow of river water along a specific course through the Southlands.

Thanks to the scenes of destruction witnessed by Galadriel and the other victorious warriors, it's known that the water passes through the village Tirharad (yellow circle), as well as Hordern (green circle), which was excavated by Orcs earlier in The Rings of Power. All the various underground tunnels feed into a central overground trench dug by Arondir and the other prisoners, which carries the water north to an inactive volcano — the future Mount Doom.

Weirdly for a world that boasts immortal wizards and ghost armies, The Rings of Power's volcanic eruption is more or less plausible, scientifically speaking. The meeting of cool water released from Ostirith with the magma bubbling peacefully beneath Halbrand's Southlands kingdom creates a massive amount of steam within a confined space, which in turn leads to a violent phreatomagmatic eruption churning out magma, ash, and pyroclastic fragments, as well as generating extraordinary heat.

Even the thunderstorms triggered within the eruption's ash cloud are semi-based in scientific fact, since such weather typically occurs due to warm air and water particles rising. Warm air and water are exactly what's rising in The Rings of Power's explosive season 1, episode 6 ending, so while the thunderstorms may happen very quickly, they're not just present for dramatic flair.

It's also important to remember that this particular eruption was triggered by a magic black sword, so there's very likely an element of sorcery to The Rings of Power's volcano compared against real-world phreatomagmatic eruptions. Mount Doom is, after all, where Sauron's One Ring will be crafted, making it special even within the context of Middle-earth. The eruption itself may be triggered by science, but one cannot rule out Morgoth or Sauron weaving a touch of dark magic into the process.

The main purpose of The Rings of Power's eruption — at least for Adar — is generating a massive plume of steam and ash that covers the sky, thus blocking out all sunlight, which Orcs have a bad allergic reaction to. Even during Frodo's journey, Mordor is renowned as a realm cloaked in darkness, and those days begin when Waldreg turns the sword-key in The Rings of Power.

Darkness isn't the only outcome of Adar's Southlands transformation project, however. Where Orcs tunneled upwards into settlements such as Tirharad and Hordern, water now bursts through the cavities, turning the Southlands' smooth plains into Mordor's craggy landscape. The trench carved out by Adar's prisoners isn't vanishing anytime soon either, leaving a huge permanent gash all the way from Ostirith to Mount Doom. Any Southlanders who think they could endure a few potholes and a surprise river should think again.

The flying rocks and clouds of steam emanating from Mount Doom's eruption will destroy settlements, while the oppressive heat will make living and farming in the area all but impossible. Even during the War of the Ring, Mordor is largely inhospitable for any race but Orcs, and intense heat isn't the only factor behind the Southlands' newly-barren landscape — the volcanic activity could produce sulfur dioxide, stunting nearby plant growth.

In many ways it's obvious why the Southlands becoming Mordor in The Rings of Power is important. Mordor is, after all, the final destination of Frodo's quest in The Lord of the Rings, and where the final battle against Sauron takes place in both of the wars against him. However, there are some important points to consider when it comes to the impact the change had in-universe for the citizens of Middle-earth who existed beyond the Southlands themselves.

Thanks to Morgoth, the races of Middle-earth were no strangers to threat. However, once Mordor was created, the danger existed right on their doorstep in a way it never had before. For those alive during the creation of Mordor in The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, the time prior to the eruption of Mount Doom would likely come to be known as an era of, if not literal peace, but peace of mind at the very least, and one that wouldn't be seen again for many, many millennia.

The best shredder maintenance is self-restraint. Don’t continually stuff the cutters to their rated maximum, allow the bin overflow, or feed the machine materials it wasn’t designed to digest.

Despite the name, most modern “shredders” no longer shred paper into long strips. While cross cut and micro cut are clues for how small the ultimate shred pieces will be, P-ratings, part of the DIN 66399 security standard, put an actual number on “small.”

P-ratings run from P-1 through P-7; the higher the number, the smaller the pieces produced. For ordinary household paperwork, P-3 is already plenty secure. A cross-cut P-3 machine reduces a document to hundreds of pieces, which is more than enough discouragement for anyone contemplating reconstructing your bills, junk mail, receipts, or other everyday paperwork. After all, a document pieced back together like a taped mosaic isn’t likely to fool many people.

P-4 is the sweet spot for buyers who want their paperwork diced considerably finer without spending much more, and P-4 is common among consumer micro-cut shredders. P-5 turns documents into even tinier bits and makes sense for especially sensitive financial, legal, medical, or professional paperwork. P-6 and P-7 go further still, into security levels intended for documents whose contents matter considerably more than last month’s Visa bill.

For most of us, then, P-3 is plenty, P-4 buys extra peace of mind, and P-5 is for paperwork you’d really rather nobody put back together.

Sex And The City: How Old Carrie Was At The Beginning & End

📅 Jul 17, 2021  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

The Sex and the City franchise follows Carrie Bradshaw through decades of her life, from a single woman in her 30s to a widow in her 50s. Considered one of HBO's best series, Sex and the City was revolutionary in its depiction of sexually active, unmarried women in their thirties, as Carrie taught generations of women what it meant to be "single and fabulous."

The life of Carrie Bradshaw was so beloved that Sex and the City spawned two movies, a prequel series, and a sequel series. And Just Like That ended in 2025, and the polarizing show sadly didn't quite capture the experience of being a 50-something woman the original series did with Carrie's 30s. Still, our Carrie has learned, grown, and loved a lot in the 27 years that we've known her.

The timeline of the original Sex and the City series makes it fairly easy to determine Carrie's age while also setting up a useful bit of information to determine it in the rest of the franchise. She states that she is 32 years old at the beginning of season 1, which takes place in 1998, so her birth year is 1966.

Carrie's Age In The Sex And The City Franchise

Her birthday is also established in the series as October 10. The show's timeline typically follows the passage of time in real life, so Carrie should age one year in each Sex and the City season.

She then turns 35 in season 4, meaning that when Sex and the City comes to an end in 2004, Carrie is 38 years old, which is also confirmed by the title of the season 6 episode, "Catch-38". Once the movies come into the picture, the timeline and the corresponding characters’ ages get fuzzier.

The first Sex and the City movie premiered in 2008, seemingly four years after the events of the series. The film centers on Carrie and Mr. Big's impending wedding, which provides some context for her age. A magazine editor asks Carrie to pose as the “40-year-old bride,” and if the film takes place in the year it premiered, she would be about 41-42.

The timeline is a bit confusing, considering the film is supposed to take place over two years, so you can assume it begins in 2007, or at least before Carrie’s 41st birthday, and finishes in 2008 when she’s 41-42.

It's also clear that the magazine article is about 'the 40s' as a decade of age, so the line could mean a 40-something bride, rather than Carrie being 40 exactly. She also says to her assistant, "your 40s are to pay for the drinks," confirming that she is in her 40s at this time.

Sex and the City 2 is the next movie in the franchise and continues to show what Carrie Bradshaw's life is like, now that she and Big are finally married. The movie takes a look at Carrie settling into married life with Mr. Big, missing some of the excitement of her younger years.

Released in 2010, Sex and the City 2 picks up two years after the first movie, likely putting Carrie Bradshaw's age at 43-44 years old. However, her age is not a big plot point in this film.

Though short-lived, there was a Sex and the City prequel series that looked at Carrie's teen years. Played by AnnaSophia Robb, Carrie Bradshaw's age in The Carrie Diaries is revealed to be 16 years old, and she's a junior in high school at the start of the series.

Carrie lives in Castlebury, Connecticut, and the prequel series shows her secretly working at a magazine company while interning at a New York City law firm. In season 2, Carrie celebrates her 17th birthday and enters her senior year of high school.

Following the cancellation of the third Sex and the City movie, the franchise returned to the small screen for a sequel series, And Just Like That. The show explored how times have changed and what life is like for Carrie and her friends as middle-aged women, with it taking place 11 years after the second movie and seeing Carrie as a 55-year-old woman.

Carrie is shown to be more conservative when talking about sex compared to the younger generation, and seems to have settled into a quieter life than she had in Sex and the City. Before Big's tragic death in the And Just Like That pilot, she's even shown to (somewhat) enjoy cooking with him. This is a far cry from the 30-something Carrie who used her stove for shoe storage.

The depiction of Carrie's aging in the sequel series didn't go over well with viewers, many of whom felt frustrated with storylines like Carrie undergoing hip surgery. Still, there was some excitement that we might get to see Carrie celebrate her 60th birthday, should the show have continued into 2026.

Alas, that's not what happened, as it was abruptly announced that And Just Like That season 3 would be its last, a mere two weeks before the finale. And Just Like That's final episode takes place over the American Thanksgiving holiday, which is celebrated in November, so it can be assumed that Carrie had recently celebrated her 59th birthday the month before.

Sex and the City follows Carrie and her friends across just over half a decade of their lives, with the movies covering the interim years between the original show and the spinoff, And Just Like That, which picks up decades later.

Given this vast timeframe, it's no surprise that Carrie's age factors into many storylines in Sex and the City, And Just Like That, and even The Carrie Diaries. The Carrie Diaries makes obvious use of Carrie's age, since it's a spinoff set during her younger years, but the other two shows also weave age into the exploits they portray.

Sex and the City is primarily about two things — dating and growing up. Carrie starts the show in her early 30s, and many episodes have her wrestling with figuring out how she wants to spend the rest of her life against the backdrop of most of her friends settling down and starting families of their own.

As an experience, dating also changes as one grows older. The wild and spontaneous relationships of Carrie's youth weren't appealing to her as 40 approached, and she wanted a stable life with Mr. Big.

Age also became important in And Just Like That. Following Carrie in her mid-50s created yet another unique variety of storylines that were only possible due to her age. However, despite And Just Like That's backlash, none of the shows have ever preached any toxic messages about aging or have claimed that age is important when finding a romantic partner.

With Carrie's age comes wisdom, and in the And Just Like That finale, she realizes that she spent most of her life either looking for or with a partner, and that this may no longer be her path anymore. The very last scene sees her dancing around her beautiful home, happy to be in her own company. This never would have happened in Sex and the City, when Carrie was in her 30s.

It was indeed a surprise to learn that And Just Like That was seemingly closing the book on Carrie Bradshaw, but the ending, as controversial as it was, seemed definitive. In the original Sex and the City finale, Carrie said, "The most fabulous relationship of all is the one you have with yourself," and that's a lesson she wholly embodies in And Just Like That.

There are currently no known plans to continue the story of Carrie Bradshaw, but that doesn't mean we'll never see her again. When addressing the potential for a return to TVLine, And Just Like That writer and executive producer Elisa Zuritsky said, "She's alive, so it could happen."

"Sarah Jessica Parker is [And Just Like That showrunner] Michael Patrick King’s muse," Zuritsky continued, "and the reason he says ‘I’ve said goodbye before and I’ve come back’ is because it’s true." Parker also noted that she was saying "farewell" to the character, not "goodbye." (via People)

So, while we likely won't be seeing Carrie Bradshaw on our TV screens anytime soon, anything is possible, and in 20 years, we very well may be catching up with the Sex and the City heroine in her 70s. Now that would be fabulous.

A camping chair’s durability is evaluated based on the frame, the material of the seat and back, and overall weather-resistance. Most of the camping chairs we write about are made with steel tubing that’s powder-coated or treated to resist rust and corrosion. Similarly, seat durability relies on sturdy materials. Woven polyester is one of the most common options. Camping chair materials are often combined with a water-resistant coating so any spilled drinks or rain drops don't damage the fabric. This also protects any interior cushioning from absorbing water.

Great camping chairs are also treated to resist UV light. This helps prevent the fabric from fading over time. Similar to the metal frame, the fabric should also be strong enough to offer a weight capacity of 300 pounds at minimum.

While the most durable options (like the Yeti Trailhead) tend to be more expensive, think about upfront cost in relation to long-term use. Camping chairs are specifically designed to be portable. That said, the relative portability between them can range drastically.

Small, lightweight camping chairs pack down for easy transport and work well for backpackers or anyone traveling by foot. These typically come with carrying straps or collapse small enough to latch onto or pack into a large backpack. Ideally, they should weigh less than eight or nine pounds.

Larger chairs can weigh more than 10 pounds, which makes them far more cumbersome to carry and better suited to car camping and backyard lounging. What you get with that added size and weight, though, significantly more comfort afforded by a wider seat with more cushioning and a potentially higher weight limit.

Regardless of size, many camping chairs come with shoulder straps or carrying cases to make travel easier.
