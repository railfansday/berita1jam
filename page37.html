Cowboys vs 49ers: 2 players to buy, 2 to sell against San Francisco

📅 Nov 15, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The Dallas Cowboys are coming off a narrow win over the Indianapolis Colts, and now the 7-1 San Francisco 49ers are coming to town, bringing one of the league's most complete rosters and a defense that has consistently given Dallas problems in their recent matchups.

There are some opportunities for the Cowboys, particularly with CeeDee Lamb playing at a high level, but there are also some difficult matchups that could expose weaknesses on Dallas' roster. Here are two players to buy and two to sell ahead of Sunday's game.

I mean, who else would this list start with? Lamb has been the best player for the Cowboys all season, and there's no reason to think that Dallas will stop feeding him the ball. Lamb is coming off a monster performance against Indianapolis, hauling in 10 catches for 195 yards and a touchdown on 16 targets. It was his second-highest receiving total in a game during his career.

Through nine games, Lamb has 70 receptions for 904 yards and seven touchdowns. He's already surpassed the 900-yard mark, and his 108 targets lead the Cowboys by a wide margin. The matchup against San Francisco is difficult, but Lamb is the kind of receiver who can change a game regardless of the opponent. With George Pickens possibly missing another week and Dallas dealing with inconsistencies elsewhere the offense, Dak Prescott should continue looking Lamb's way early and often.

The 49ers will undoubtedly have a plan for him, but Lamb's recent production makes him difficult to leave out of the buyers market. He's been the Cowboys' most reliable playmaker overall, and Dallas needs to find ways to get him involved.

At some point, the Cowboys have to figure out how to beat San Francisco, and Dak Prescott has to be a big part of that solution. Unfortunately, his history against the 49ers doesn't inspire much confidence. Prescott is 2-4 against San Francisco in his career, including an 0-2 record in the playoffs. In those two postseason games, he has thrown for 460 yards, two touchdowns and three interceptions. His most recent regular-season meetings haven't been much better. He threw three interceptions in the 2023 blowout loss and two more in the 2024 matchup.

Across his four regular-season games against San Francisco, Prescott has completed 65.5% of his passes for 875 yards, eight touchdowns and five interceptions. That's not necessarily disastrous production, but the turnovers have been costly, particularly against a team that knows how to capitalize on mistakes.

The 49ers have won four straight meetings against Dallas, and Prescott will have to overcome both that team history and his own struggles against this opponent. San Francisco's defense is disciplined, physical and capable of making quarterbacks pay for forcing throws.

With Fred Warner leading the unit, Prescott will have to be sharp with his reads and decision-making, especially against the blitz, where he has looked terrible this year. Dallas needs a big performance from its quarterback to pull off the upset. Given the history, though, this is a matchup where I'm selling Prescott until he proves otherwise.

With Jake Ferguson ruled out due to a knee injury, Brevyn Spann-Ford has an opportunity to take on an even bigger role in the Cowboys' offense. It might not be the most obvious matchup to exploit, but he could be a sneaky contributor against San Francisco.

Spann-Ford has caught 15 passes for 130 yards this season, and his role has been growing. Against Indianapolis, he played 55 offensive snaps compared to Ferguson's 27, catching two of three targets for 20 yards. That workload suggests the Cowboys are already comfortable giving him significant playing time.

The 49ers have plenty of defensive talent to account for, and their attention will naturally gravitate toward Lamb and the Cowboys' other primary playmakers. That could leave opportunities for Spann-Ford underneath or in the middle of the field, particularly if Dallas uses him to help with protection before releasing him into a route.

This isn't a prediction that Spann-Ford is suddenly going to become the focal point of the passing game. Rather, it's a bet that his increased playing time and the attention paid to Dallas' other weapons could create a few favorable opportunities. With Ferguson unavailable, the Cowboys need someone else to contribute from the tight end position. Spann-Ford is in a good position to do that.

The Cowboys have indicated that Jaishawn Barham could see an increased workload as injuries continue to pile up on the defensive side of the ball. That's understandable from a personnel standpoint, but it also creates a potential problem against an offense as versatile as San Francisco's.

Barham had some encouraging moments early in the season, but his limitations have become more apparent as the competition has increased. His playing time has also been limited recently, which makes the prospect of a larger role against the 49ers particularly concerning.

San Francisco is exactly the type of opponent that can put a young linebacker in difficult situations. Kyle Shanahan's offense uses motion, misdirection and mismatches to create space, and open up running lanes.

If Dallas gives him more responsibility, San Francisco will have plenty of opportunities to test him. The Cowboys need all the help they can get defensively, but this is a tough week to ask an inconsistent young player to take on a bigger role.

Central African Republic vs Benin - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

By Nsidibe Akpan | 14 Nov 2026 23:59

Central African Republic host Benin at the Japoma Stadium in Douala, Cameroon, on Monday in the fourth round of Group F in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying.

Benin won the first leg 4-1 in Abidjan on Thursday and lead Group F on goal difference, level on seven points with Burkina Faso, while Central African Republic remain bottom with no points and a goal difference of -6.

Central African Republic, known as Les Fauves, have lost all three qualifiers, scoring two goals and conceding eight, and have not won any match since October last year.

Mauritania won 3-1 in their opener on September 24, and Burkina Faso followed with a 1-0 victory over them on September 28.

Head coach Abdelaziz Kerkache, a Moroccan who has previously worked at Mouloudia d’Oujda, was appointed on November 6 to replace Eloge Enza Yamissi, only six days before the first leg.

Kerkache recalled Geoffrey Kondogbia of Olympique de Marseille and Le Mans forward Louis Mafouta, who both missed the opening two qualifiers, and Mafouta responded by equalising in the first leg.

Les Fauves had more of the ball in Abidjan, with 56% possession and eight corners to Benin’s five, yet goalkeeper Dominique Youfeigane faced eight shots on target and conceded four.

Central African Republic are still chasing a first Africa Cup of Nations finals appearance after narrowly missing out in 2021 and 2023, and their home leg is being staged in Douala rather than in Bangui.

Benin, nicknamed Les Guepards, have taken seven points from three Group F games under Gernot Rohr, beginning with a 1-1 draw at Burkina Faso on September 25.

A 2-1 win over Mauritania in Abidjan on September 29 followed, with Rodolfo Aloko scoring either side of half time, as Benin again used an Ivory Coast venue because the Stade de l’Amitie in Cotonou is not yet approved for qualifiers.

Hassane Imourane opened the scoring in the seventh minute of the first leg, Louis Mafouta equalised in the 19th, and Benin were 3-1 up at half time after Aloko struck in the 31st minute and Aiyegun Tosin in the 36th.

Tosin completed his brace in the 56th minute, and he and Aloko have now scored six of Benin’s seven goals in qualifying.

Benin won despite having only 44% of the possession, turning 18 shots into four goals, with eight efforts on target against Central African Republic’s 14 shots and five on target.

Rohr said afterwards that the scoreline flattered his side because Central African Republic attacked a great deal, and he expects a reaction in a rematch only four days later.

Germany vs Netherlands - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

By Anthony Nolan | 14 Nov 2026 23:59

Fighting for a place in the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals, Germany are set to host Group A2 leaders the Netherlands at Olympiastadion Berlin on Monday.

Die Mannschaft know that they likely need to win to progress, while Oranje through barring an unprecedented swing in goal-difference.

Jurgen Klopp's Germany are pushing to qualify for the Nations League quarter-finals for just the second time, but they face a tough test to reach the last eight.

Die Mannschaft are currently third in Group A2, level on eight points with Greece and three behind the Netherlands, and their chance to go through leans on the fact UEFA prioritises head-to-head points between tied teams.

Klopp's side were beaten 1-0 by the Azure and White on September 27 before drawing 0-0 with them on matchday four.

However, Ivan Jovanovic's men were hammered 7-0 by the Dutch on Friday, meaning that a German victory over the Netherlands on Monday would see them progress ahead of Greece regardless of their result against Serbia.

Die Mannschaft were held to a 1-1 stalemate by Oranje on matchday one, with Cody Gakpo equalising in second-half stoppage time to deny his former Liverpool manager maximum points.

However, Klopp's team have remained undefeated in the group since their loss to Greece, most recently beating Serbia 3-1 on Friday, when they raced into a 2-0 lead within the first 14 minutes thanks to an own-goal from Srdjan Babic and a follow-up by Florian Wirtz.

Meanwhile, Xavi's Netherlands have yet to taste defeat in the Nations League this term, and are in a commanding position at the top of their group.

Top of the pile with 11 points, Oranje's 7-0 demolition of Greece last time out - following a 2-2 draw on October 1 - means the only possibility of them finishing below the top two is if there is a dramatic 14-goal swing in the Azure and White's favour.

Xavi is without talismanic centre-back Virgil van Dijk this month, but his team were still able to keep their first clean sheet in the competition on Friday, while Tijani Reijnders's first-half hat-trick set the tone for his flourishing attack.

However, it remains to be seen whether the Netherlands can repeat that feat in Germany, considering that they have won just one of their last six clashes with Die Mannschaft as the away side.

On the other hand, Oranje are unbeaten in their last seven matches on the road, last suffered a penalty shootout defeat against Spain in March 2025.

Germany have no new injury issues to contend with, though they are missing Bayern Munich stars Jamal Musiala and Serge Gnabry due to respective muscle problems.

In their absence, Klopp could continue with Brajan Gruda, Florian Wirtz and Derry Scherhant behind Arsenal man Kai Havertz up top.

As for the Netherlands, they were forced to take off Frenkie De Jong at half time against Greece after he suffered back pain, and he has now left the national squad to return to Barcelona.

Teun Koopmeiners looks set to step in and fill the gap alongside Joey Veerman at the base of Xavi's midfield, while Tijani Reijnders operates in the number 10 role.

Further forward, Donyell Malen has been a standout for Roma this season with 12 goals across all competitions, and he should lead the line on Monday while Cody Gakpo and Crysencio Summerville provide width.

Captain Virgil van Dijk has sat out this international break with complaints of physical discomfort, so expect to see Tottenham Hotspur's Micky van de Ven and Jan Paul van Hecker start at centre-back.

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Prolific director, screenwriter and producer Peter Farrelly reflected on some of his most famous films Saturday at Deadline’s Contenders Film Los Angeles event.

Contenders Hall of Fame honoree Farrelly talked with Deadline’s Mike Fleming Jr about his most recent film I Play Rocky and the process of bringing it to the screen. Earlier, Farrelly had appeared on the film’s panel with star Anthony Ippolito, who plays Sylvester Stallone in the film, and talked about the uphill battle to making the crowd-pleasing underdog tale.

Farrelly revealed that it was his daughter’s ex-boyfriend who first read the script and told Farrelly it “was the best script he had ever read.” Farrelly agreed and pushed forward with the film, noting it was imperative that he had Stallone’s approval on the script, which came quickly.

He also shared his own “Rocky” moment: “The bravest thing I ever did in my life was decide to write when I was in my mid-20s,” he said.

Farrelly also talked about his years and years of getting turned down for scripts and having to push through the rejection and not give up. As for 1994’s Dumb & Dumber, “We wrote that script five years before it got made, and it was the exact same script,” he shared. “Every studio passed, every actor passed.” He acknowledged that he changed the title to A Power Tool Is Not a Toy and “everyone started reading it.” It was the eventual film’s star Jim Carrey who told him Dumb & Dumber is the title, “so we went back to that,” he said.

“You’ve got to believe,, that they’re wrong and you’re right,” he said regarding the rejections. “You know, year after year getting turned down, it’s hard. You just gotta stick out and hope that you’re right.”

One of the most memorable and hilarious scenes in Farrelly’s films was in There’s Something About Mary and Cameron Diaz’s “hair gel.” “I realized we were going to have to go to R [in that film] because we were right on the PG-13 line.

“There’s two things that the studio didn’t want was the zipper joke in the beginning and the hair gel thing of course, and we understood that, especially the hair gel.” But of course, the hair gel stayed in and the rest is history.

Common reasons your Roku TV can turn on by itself and how to fix it

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Some people have been experiencing random power-ons recently.

You're sound asleep in the middle of the night when, all of a sudden, your TV turns on. You know you turned off the TV before you went to bed, no one else is awake, and there's nothing near your remote to accidentally push the power button. What — or who — could have turned on the TV? A bored spirit haunting your home? A burglar looking to take a quick break?

Those scenarios are highly unlikely. But, unfortunately, some Roku TVs and Roku streaming device owners have been jolted out of their slumber by those products inexplicably powering on randomly over the past few months. And no one really knows why it's happening. There may be a few explanations, however, and a couple of potential fixes to try if your Roku device has gone rogue during the wee hours.

When Roku OS version 15.3 went live in August 2026, owners of Roku TVs and Roku streaming devices began reporting these middle-of-the-night disturbances. Not every Roku TV or streaming player has been impacted by this supposed 15.3 bug, but the timing of the reports with the software update seems more than coincidental.

What's odd about that, though, is that there was nothing in the 15.3 update that involved autoplay features or even power management. So, in theory, the changes that were made to the Roku OS shouldn't have spurred these instances of people's Roku TVs "waking up" without being powered on by anyone (or anything) and playing a TV show or movie. But we all know that software updates often introduce bugs with no real rhyme or reason, and that feels like the most logical explanation for these strange occurrences.

Having your Roku TV's HDMI-CEC feature enabled is another very plausible reason the TV powers on randomly. If you're not familiar with HDMI-CEC, it's basically a chain that links all your A/V components with built-in HDMI switching (like soundbars, gaming consoles, receivers and speakers) since they're all connected via HDMI cables. And this allows all of those devices to be controlled with one remote.

So, when you power on your Roku TV, for example, HDMI-CEC essentially notifies all the other connected components to turn on. All it takes is one glitchy signal to confuse the whole chain, which could lead to a random power-up. And there is a history of HDMI-CEC causing strange switching between inputs and components, as well as phantom power-ons. Samsung Anynet+, Sony BRAVIA Sync, LG SimpLink and Roku 1-Touch Play are all just different names for the same HDMI-CEC standard, but each has its own custom software added to it to create the proprietary HDMI-CEC feature for each brand of smart TV.

Unfortunately, compatibility sometimes becomes an issue unless all of your connected devices are made by the same company (though even that doesn't guarantee incident-free performance). This can cause signal miscommunication that results in the random behavior of your Roku TV or streaming device.

There are other possible explanations for your Roku TV turning on by itself. For example, the Fast TV Start feature is a proprietary power-management setting that keeps your Roku TV's OS on in the background even when you shut off the TV.

This allows the TV to turn on instantly without any boot time, and it keeps the TV on alert for voice commands to turn the TV back on. Fast TV Start has also seemingly caused phantom power-on problems in conjunction with the Roku OS 15.3 update, as the always-on network connection kept open the door for overnight glitches.

You may try all of these suggestions and still not have much luck because the root of the issue has not been "officially" identified by Roku, or anyone else, for that matter. And without the root cause, there remains no guaranteed fix. But these possible solutions are certainly worth trying and just might do the trick.

The first thing to try, if you haven't already, is updating your Roku TV's OS to version 16, which was released in October. Fixing the 15.3 bug wasn't part of the update, and no reports have surfaced that OS 16 patched the automatic power-on problem, but it's definitely worth trying. The worst-case scenario is that your Roku TV's OS is fully updated.

Next, disable 1-Touch Play (HDMI-CEC) not just on your Roku TV but on all the other components connected to the TV, regardless of brand. This includes your A/V equipment and gaming consoles. To do so on your Roku TV, go to Settings > System > Control other devices and uncheck 1-Touch Play. Also disable Fast TV Start, which you can find under Settings > System > Power > Fast TV Start, then either clear the Enable Fast TV Start option or check Off to disable it.

If these don't work, try unplugging the TV when you're done watching it each night (no, not ideal), using a smart plug to automatically cut the power for you or consulting Roku's tech support portal at support.roku.com for further assistance.
