2 Billionaire Funds Dumped AMD Last Quarter. Is It a Sell?

📅 Nov 25, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

AMD's stock is pricey compared to its peers.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 25, 2026 at 5:23PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Advanced Micro Devices ›

AMD (AMD -1.75%) has been a fantastic stock to own in 2026. The stock has risen around 100% so far, though it was up 200% at the start of October. AMD became a popular choice for many investors, including the smart money in hedge funds. However, now that the latest round of 13-F filings has come out, it's clear that selling AMD has become a trend among fund managers.

Two major funds sold all of their shares during the third quarter: Appaloosa Management, run by David Tepper, and Coatue Management, run by Philippe Laffont. When giant funds like this sell, it's normally time to start thinking about selling your shares as well. So, is it time to move on from AMD stock?

First, let's tackle the business. AMD has always played second fiddle to Nvidia (NVDA -3.91%) in the data center industry, as Nvidia's hardware and software have always been a step ahead of AMD's. However, AMD's products are becoming more popular among certain customers because they are cheaper. While they may not be as capable, power-efficient, or user-friendly, they can be quite useful if they’re not being tasked with a wide variety of operations.

This has led to more adoption of AMD's products, and AMD's Q3 results back that up. The company experienced 97% year-over-year revenue growth in its data center division to $8.5 billion during Q3. However, AMD is more than just a data center company. Its overall growth rate was 45% year-over-year to $11.5 billion, as its client and gaming segment decreased year-over-year, partially offset by increasing Embedded processors doing well. Overall, those are solid results for AMD, but there's just one problem: It's far smaller than Nvidia.

AMD already has a tough enough time being compared to Nvidia's products. For it to be a better investment than Nvidia, it should be showing stronger growth, but it's not. During its fiscal third quarter (ended in October), Nvidia's overall revenue rose 103% year over year to nearly $116 billion. So, Nvidia is 10 times larger than AMD. On the data center side, Nvidia's revenue rose 110% to $108 billion.

Nvidia has the technology edge and the growth edge. However, AMD has dramatically outperformed Nvidia this year, and there is one obvious reason for that: valuation.

Sometimes, a stock goes up not because the business is doing well, but because the market is willing to pay more for the stock. That's exactly what's going on here, and it has gotten a bit out of control with AMD's stock.

AMD is one of the pricier stocks on the market, trading at 81 times trailing earnings and 21 times next year’s earnings.

Because 13F filings don't reveal exact transaction dates, we don't know whether these funds exited early in Q3 or right before the quarter ended—when AMD traded at over 80 times forward earnings and nearly 40 times next year's earnings—though either way they missed the stock's early-October peak.

Still, when you compare price tags, AMD is far more expensive, because Nvidia trades at 15 times trailing earnings and 8.6 times next year's earnings. Nvidia is clearly the far better value, and with it growing faster at a much larger scale, Nvidia’s stock looks like a far better investment.

So, while now may not be the best time to sell AMD compared to a month and a half ago, I still think investors would be wise to move on to another AI stock. Many artificial intelligence investments are on huge sale right now, but AMD is not one of them.

King Crimson Co-Founder Michael Giles Dead at 84

📅 Nov 25, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Michael Giles, a drummer and singer who cofounded King Crimson, died Monday, according to King Crimson’s record label, DGM, which did not report a cause of death. Giles was 84.

“Michael’s originality, musical curiosity and uncompromising commitment to creative freedom challenged conventions and inspired generations of musicians around the world,” Crimson’s label said. “He leaves behind an extraordinary musical legacy, and his unique contribution to the evolution of progressive music will never be forgotten.”

Giles’ musical beginnings with Giles, Giles, and Fripp, a trio he cofounded in 1967 in Bournemouth — where was he was born on March 1, 1942 — with his brother Peter and guitarist Robert Fripp, were inauspicious, at least by King Crimson standards. The band’s only album, 1968’s The Cheerful Insanity of Giles, Giles and Fripp, was all psychedelia: shimmering organ, pensive vocals, and relatively straightforward, march-like drumming that only occasionally hinted at jazziness. It’s straightforward pop rock, but you can also hear how it provided the framework for King Crimson’s outré, ponderous adventures a year later.

When Peter quit to become a solicitor’s clerk in November 1968, Michael and Fripp wanted to continue making music, so they formed King Crimson with Greg Lake, who’d played bass with the Gods, and multi-instrumentalist Ian McDonald. The lineup included Peter Sinfield as lyricist and lighting director. Together, the conceived an album that would define the sound of prog rock in the early Seventies: In the Court of the Crimson King, which came out in 1969.

The album’s seven-minute opening cut, “21st Century Schizoid Man,” was an FM radio hit in spite of its turn-on-a-dime rhythm changes and extended bass and sax duets. The stylistic shift allowed Giles to challenge himself to make his drum parts more musical, whether mirroring McDonald’s sax or rolling rhythms like waves in the main riff. “The Court of the Crimson King,” a 10-minute musical journey, took the streamlined rock sound of Giles, Giles, and Fripp and augmented it with flute, Mellotron, and choral-like harmonies. Giles’ drumming on the song is the musical equivalent to expressionism as he brings out his bandmates’ drama. He latter cited Elvin Jones, who’d played with John Coltrane, as an inspiration. Sens. Warren, Van Hollen lead push to scrap SEC’s Regulation Crypto and innovation exemption

📅 Nov 25, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

In a letter sent to SEC Chair Atkins, Sens. Warren and Van Hollen said the agency's "Regulation Crypto Assets" would allow crypto firms to circumvent securities laws.

Top Democrats Sen. Elizabeth Warren and Chris Van Hollen are demanding that Securities and Exchange Commission Chair Paul Atkins take down its crypto rulemaking governing how crypto projects can raise capital and how tokenized securities can be traded.

In two separate letters sent to Atkins on Tuesday, Sens. Warren, Van Hollen and Tina Smith and other Senate Banking Committee members said the agency's "Regulation Crypto Assets" would allow crypto firms to circumvent securities laws, weaken state jurisdiction and could harm investors.

"The proposal would create a new 'tailored securities offering regime' for certain investment contracts involving crypto assets," the senators wrote. "However, the proposal only serves to help crypto assets escape from the SEC's regulatory regime and gives them special treatment—with less oversight—compared to other traditional securities."

In August, the SEC proposed crypto offering rules to offer startup and fundraising exemptions for digital asset offerings. It allows for a "startup exemption" that would exempt offerings of up to $5 million from registration rules under the Securities Act of 1933 for four years — a federal law focused on anti-fraud and requiring certain disclosures. The proposal also includes a "fundraising exemption" for offerings up to $75 million for a year.

Warren is poised to take on a more prominent role following Democrats’ victories in November's midterm elections, which handed the party control of both chambers of Congress. Warren has been critical of crypto in the past and has particularly brought up concerns around illicit finance and money laundering. Meanwhile, Van Hollen could be the next Senate Majority Leader. He has since challenged current Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer for that role.

Sens. Jack Reed and Tina Smith join the two in the second letter asking the SEC to withdraw its innovation exemption — a measure released in September to bring capital markets onchain through the trading of tokenized stocks. It is a five-year exemption that would exempt certain trading venues, called tokenized securities venues, from being defined as an exchange and also exempts specific liquidity providers from being defined as a dealer under securities law when they trade stocks or provide liquidity through automated market makers.

The senators said it would allow platforms to operate outside of certain safeguards, including involving fair pricing and market transparency.

"The order would allow TSV platforms to operate a shadow stock market outside longstanding regulatory protections designed to ensure fair and transparent markets, impose new burdens on publicly traded companies seeking to protect their shareholders, and create unnecessary risks of illicit financial activity," the senators wrote.

The three senators also said the innovation exemption could cause capital markets to splinter.

"As concerned stakeholders have pointed out, fragmenting our securities markets by creating a lightly regulated alternative marketplace for tokenized securities unnecessarily risks dividing liquidity into isolated pools and expanding opportunities for corrosive regulatory arbitrage," they said.

A separate group of Senate Democrats have also brought up concerns about the innovation exemption. Last week, Sens. Angela Alsobrooks, Ruben Gallego, Mark Warner and Catherine Cortez Masto said it could open the door for illicit finance actors and also had concerns about protections for retail investors around pricing.

The SEC did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

All Peewees Billboard locations in GTA 6

How to claim the Peewees Billboard grand prize

How to claim the Peewees Billboard grand prize

Peewees Billboards are one of the many scannable WAiNK collectibles in Grand Theft Auto 6, but they're also part of the Mr. Enigma X Peewees scavenger hunt. Mr. Enigma, a play on Dr. Pepper, and the Peewees, a play on Popeyes, have teamed up to create a challenge where you must find four Peewees billboards, and you'll earn a valuable reward.

All the info you're given is that there is a billboard in the following areas: Vice-Dale, Mariana County, Lummox County, and Leonard County. As these areas are fairly large, it can take a long time to find them.

To save you some time, we've listed where you can find every Peewees Billboard in GTA 6 below.

To start the Mr. Enigma X Peewees scavenger hunt, look out for a Peewees post on Snapmatic, or you can look for one of the four Peewees billboards. After you start, you'll get a text from Chicken Challenge, telling you that you've entered the scavenger hunt. They'll also send you a clue that points you toward another billboard when you start the challenge and every time you find one.

There are four Peewees Billboards in GTA 6, with one in Vice-Dale, Mariana County, Lummox County, and Leonard County. To mark them off the list, head to their location and scan them using WAiNK (hold Up on your D-pad).

Below, we explain where you can find each billboard in detail.

The Vice-Dale Peewees Billboard is on the west side of Balsero, which is the northwest corner of Vice-Dale County. The billboard itself is between Route US-10 and US-35, which is just northwest of Randy's shop.

The Mariana County Peewees Billboard is technically in Big Pine in Grassrivers, but it's very close to the border between Grassrivers and North Mariana County. Along the border, you'll find three dome-shaped buildings and Route US-82. Head northeast of the dome-shaped buildings, and look in the median strip to find this Peewee Billboard.

The Lummox County Peewees Billboard is located on the border between South West Lummox County and Mount Kalaga National Park. The billboard is just west of Route US-628. Head north from the junction that connects US-628 to SR-73, and you'll find the billboard on your left.

The Leonard County Peewees Billboard is located in the northwest corner of Waning Sands. The billboard is just next to SR-27, which is between Lake Leonida and Mount Hailes Club. Look northeast of Lake Leonida, and you'll find the golf course. Follow along the road until you find the Peewees Billboard.

After you find every Peewees Billboard, you'll receive a text from Chicken Challenge, telling you that you won... almost! You just need to solve one more puzzle, but they don't tell you what the puzzle is.

If you take a look back at the clues that Chicken Challenge has been sending you, you'll notice that a couple of letters in each photo are covered by the Peewees logo. The following letters are blocked off: V - L - I - L - L - E - B - E. Now, it looks like a bunch of gibberish, but if you scramble the letters, you'll get Belville, which is the southern region of Leonard County.

It just so happens that there's a Peewees in Belville.

To claim your grand prize, you need to head to the Peewees in Belville, which is just east of the hospital. Alternatively, you can open your map, scroll down to the "Fast Food" section of the "Food & Drink" tab, and select the Peewees in Belville to speed up the process.

Head inside and speak with the manager to claim your reward, which is a Peewees hat, a Peewees shirt, and free Peewees food!

Will Ferrell’s Netflix Golf Comedy ‘The Hawk’ Canceled After One Season

📅 Nov 25, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Will Ferrell is not going to be thankful for this Wednesday-before-Thanksgiving bad-news dump. Ferrell’s out-of-bounds golf comedy The Hawk has been canceled by Netflix after just one season, The Hollywood Reporter has learned.

Ferrell and Netflix are not done with each other, however: Ferrell is executive producing new Netflix series All the Rage, Prep, and Nikki Glaser’s first (unnamed) scripted series. (Part of her Netflix Is a Joke Studios deal.) Ferrell produces for his Gloria Sanchez banner.

The problem with The Hawk was, well, it was (very) subpar. The Hawk, co-created by Ferrell and Harper Steele, followed disgraced pro golfer Lonnie Hawkins (Ferrell) who seeks to regain his former glory. In his review, THR TV critic Daniel Fienberg called The Hawk “disposable,” writing that, “In general, The Hawk is a show that consistently operates at a barely minimal level, starting with the credits song, a slice of ’80s anthemic rock that contains the lyrics, ‘They call him the Hawk/ He’s a golfer/ The greatest golfer in the world.’ Wait. Sorry. The song doesn’t contain those lyrics — those are the only lyrics. It’s a piece of cheeky minimalism and a promise delivered.”

“There will be countless worse shows this year, but it’s doubtful that 2026 will offer a show as hollow and forgettable as The Hawk,” Fienberg scathed.

In addition to Ferrell and Steele, Jessica Elbaum, Alix Taylor, Rian Johnson, Ram Bergman, Nena Rodrigue, Chris Henchy, David Gordon Green and Andrew Guest also executive produced The Hawk.

The news comes as no surprise to the series’ creators. Earlier this month, Steele appeared on Late Night With Seth Meyers, calling The Hawk “short-lived” but also “probably the best show ever.”
