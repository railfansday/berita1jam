Birthday at Shaw’s! (Day 161)

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Politics  |  Read Source →

It’s my 29th birthday today! A golden one at that. It’s so cool to spend my birthday on the AT at Shaw’s Hiker Hostel with my dad, Dreamcatcher and Small Bites; these amazing people I love and have experienced so much with. As you might be able to tell, I’m quite sappy about it all. We’re in the last leg of this adventure and I’m soaking it all in. Also, I noticed I have not taken a lot of pictures today. Sorry about that!

It’s also our last zero-day of the entire trail, so we’re making it count! We all relaxed and had some fun. I even got some presents! Do you remember that I lost my cool Trek hat at the Lake of the Clouds hut in The White Mountains? Well, Dreamcatcher just bought me the exact same hat again! She also gave me a little stuffed porcupine to bring along with me. They’re my favorite quirky animals on trail. These gifts are so thoughtful and awesome! I truly appreciate it.

Dutch people have an unique way of doing birthdays. Guys congratulate guys with a handshake, girls with three kisses on the cheek (left, right, left). Then everyone congratulates everyone else with my birthday too. You sit in a circle, bring out some form of charcuterie board and snacks and talk about politics. That last part might just be at our birthdays, oh well.

Dreamcatcher prepared the charcuterie board and we all emulated the Dutch birthday party for a little bit, which was fun. If she hadn’t done enough already, Dreamcatcher had another surprise, but she needed time and I wasn’t allowed in the common area for a while. Okay.. interesting. I guess I’ll play some chess with my dad! This time I did win! That’s… suspicious..

While being way too curious about the surprise, I called my family, texted back some friends, relaxed and did 9 days worth of resupply. Shaw’s offers a food drop halfway in the 100-mile wilderness. We’re doing it. This way we don’t have to rush through it, can take our time and soak it all in before we’re done.

My dad took us all out to dinner. Monson is very tiny, so we didn’t have to think for too long where to eat. He even called ahead to check if they had lobster rolls for me, which they did! I can’t call myself a proper tourist if I didn’t try a lobster roll in Maine! It was delicious, a very much appreciated little bit of luxury before heading into the wilderness tomorrow. Take that Timmy!

Back at the hostel, Dreamcatcher revealed her surprise. It’s an apple pie! One she made herself all from scratch in the hostel kitchen. It’s the first pie she ever made and she did a great job! They decorated it with candles, lighted them on and asked me to do a wish. I hope it comes true. I’m so grateful for all the effort everyone made to make this birthday truly unforgettable.

Broncos beat Panthers 26-21 for fifth consecutive win

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

It is rarely pretty, but the Broncos keep finding ways to win.

The Broncos were outgained by the Panthers 353 to 303, went 2-for-4 in the red zone, including a red-zone interception. They still won their fifth consecutive game, beating Carolina 26-21 to move to 7-2. The Panthers are 3-5.

The Panthers led 14-13 into the fourth quarter, but the Broncos outscored them 13-0 before Carolina gave itself a chance for a comeback on Tetairoa McMillan’s 4-yard touchdown reception. The Broncos got the ball back with 2:59 left with a chance to run out the time, but on second down, Bo Nix’s pitch to Jonah Coleman was fumbled backward for a 12-yard loss.

That gave Carolina a chance to win it with 1:49 remaining.

The Panthers got 20 yards to the Denver 36 before Nik Bonitto had a strip-sack of Bryce Young that the edge rusher recovered with 1:18 left.

That gave Nix his 17th consecutive win in one-score games, the longest streak in the NFL since 1950.

Nix was 25-of-37 for 237 yards with two touchdowns and an interception. Courtland Sutton caught a 3-yard score and Pat Bryant had a 26-yard touchdown reception. Coleman ran for a 9-yard touchdown.

Bryant caught four passes for 56 yards and Coleman had 17 runs for 57 yards.

The Panthers had 13 penalties for 119 yards, had two turnovers and Ryan Fitzgerald missed a 43-yard field goal.

Young was 28-of-39 for 276 yards with three touchdowns and an interception. Chuba Hubbard had 16 carries for 79 yards and Darren Waller had eight catches for 90 yards and a touchdown.

A tutto Gasperini: "Dal Genoa all'Inter, la Juve e Roma". E per l'Atalanta si commuove

L'allenatore della Roma, Gian Piero Gasperini, al Charity Event 2006 di Stelle nello Sport - a sostegno della Fondazione Gigi Ghirotti - è stato insignito del premio "Un Cuore da Leone" (dedicato alla memoria di Gian Luigi Corti). Il tecnico ha parlato dal palco dell'evento, di seguito le sue dichiarazioni raccolte da TuttoMercatoWeb.com: "Io non sono mai mancato da Genova. Tutte le volte che posso a Genova ci vengo. E' un posto dove vivo molto bene. Quando è possibile ci vengo molto volentieri. Questa è stata un'occasione maggiore per una serata particolare. Questo binomio di sport e solidarietà è meraviglioso".

I ricordi con il Genoa. "Il Genoa era la piazza che si prestava ad avere coraggio. Giocare anche contro squadre forti. Il Genoa, quando sono arrivato io, aveva appena superato la Serie C. C'era una Serie B con Juventus e Napoli. Negli anni successivi in A, non sempre e solo con la speranza di non perdere, ma anche con la speranza di vincere".

Quel Genoa-Napoli che è valso la promozione di entrambe le squadre. "In quella giornata ci sono degli aneddoti incredibili. Soprattutto dalla parte del Napoli c'era il massaggiatore che diceva: 'A Piacenza è finita!' ma non era finita. C'è stata un'invasione di campo, sono sparite le magliette. E' stata però una giornata di sport pazzesca e il coronamento di una stagione veramente bella".

Cosa è stato il Genoa per Gasperini? "Coinvolgimento totale. Sono arrivato da semi-sconosciuto. Quando sono arrivato ero un po' la sorpresa ed ero un po' sotto giudizio di tanti. Abbiamo cambiato radicalmente quella squadra d'accordo con Preziosi, con tanta gente svincolata e prestiti. Con una certa: il Genoa non poteva non ambire a tornare in Serie A. E' stato un percorso esaltante dall'inizio. Il pubblico a Genova è stato la componente fondamentale, una spinta per la squadra notevole. Qualcuno all'inizio diceva che ero un visionario, che in B era difficile giocare in quel modo. Poi me l'hanno detto anche in A".

Le grandi squadre faticavano a Marassi come la Juventus? "C'erano Thiago Motta, Milito, Palladino, poi è arrivato Gilardino, c'era Juric. Era una squadra che aveva coraggio di affrontare le grandi squadre con voglia di vincere e regalare gioia ai tifosi".

Quell'anno il Genoa arrivò quarto. L'anno del derby di Milito. "Era forte anche la Sampdoria. Il derby di Genova era più straordinario. Noi li abbiamo vinti tutti e due e ha dato una spinta notevole a noi. E' stata la svolta. Se fossimo andati in Champions si sarebbe scritta un'altra storia perché la Champions avrebbe portato un potenziamento ancora maggiore della società. Il fatto di non aver potuto partecipare abbiamo dovuto abbassare un po' gli obiettivi".

La storia all'Inter? "E' durata veramente poco. Alla terza di campionato sono uscito. In quel momento non era l'ambiente ideale per quello che io cercavo di fare e avevo fatto prima nelle giovanili poi in C col Crotone ed infine col Genoa. Il Genoa è già una piazza molto improntante, per tifoseria e ambizioni. Io mi sentivo molto testato. Come in tutte le situazioni anche Milano mi ha dato qualcosa. Il 3-4-3 si è evoluto nel tempo".

Anche l'inizio con l'Atalanta non fu facile. Poi arriva la sfida con il Napoli. "Quando dico che il coraggio è un valore fondamentale. Io ritenevo l'Atalanta avesse tanti giocatori Under 21 che erano un po' accantonato come lo stesso Genoa. Io ho sempre lavorato con i giovani, ho sempre ritenuto che dovevamo dare loro un'opportunità. Bergamo ne aveva tanti non considerati. La settimana prima della gara con il Napoli sapevo che era decisiva per me ma mi sono detto: 'Ho trovato la svolta'. Non immaginavo di vincere però superata quella partita abbiamo fatto un pareggio e poi abbiamo vinto otto partite di fila".

Quando hai capito che il tuo calcio poteva diventare anche a livello europeo importante? "Prima lo facevo a livello giovanile e dava soddisfazioni. In C ha vinto. In B ha vinto e la conferma l'abbiamo avuto in Europa con l'Atalanta. In Inghilterra, Francia hanno iniziato a seguirci. L'Atalanta poi ha fatto risultati e poi sono stato più credibile".

Come riconosci il talento? "Te lo dimostra lui. A volte noi cerchiamo tante cose ma magari ha una qualità che emerge sulle altre. Quella qualità spicca, la vedi ed è già una componente di talento sulla quale lavorare. E' sopra la media. Non solo può essere tecnico-tattica ma anche caratteriale. E' importante che abbia un qualcosa che emerge. Avendo fatto tanto settore giovanile è una cosa che mi ha aiutato".

Nella società di oggi con i giocatori che sono delle vere e proprie aziende quanto è difficile gestire? "E' più difficile perché ci sono tante persone intorno a loro. I giovani adesso sono diversi. Hanno i social, si affidano a tante persone. Alcuni si prendono i personal trainer. Io lavoro sempre sulla squadra che ha delle esigenze fondamentale. Ci sono i ruoli, i comportamenti, gli atteggiamenti. Poi quando sono fuori da questo contesto sono liberi di agire come vogliono".

La vittoria in Europa League con l'Atalanta. "(Si commuove ndr) Noi eravamo arrivati terzi in campionato ed è stata la cosa più facile. Dopo di che una settimana prima giocavamo la finale di Coppa Italia contro la Juventus facendo una partita orribile. La finale di Europa League era la partita più difficile contro una squadra che era 51 partite che non perdeva. Questo per dire come le cose nella vita e nello sport le cose sono strane. Forse abbiamo vinto perché una settimana prima avevamo perso la Coppa Italia. A volte hai bisogno di perdere per reagire".

La vittoria di Verona con la Roma firmata El Shaarawy. "Dopo otto anni la Roma va in Champions League e Stephan, che aveva esordito in quel campo a Verona aveva esordito come giocatore più giovane del Genoa, fa quel gol. Come si ripetono alcune casualità siano bellissime".

L'avventura alla Roma. "E' stata un'altra scommessa fantastica. Quando è finita la storia con l'Atalanta e, come sempre, le storie lunghe non finiscono mai banalmente. Venire via da Bergamo è stato un salto, sono andato in una società e una piazza di cui ero un acerrimo nemico. Ero un avversario non proprio stimato perché l'Atalanta ha portato via spazio alla Roma. Però anche questa era una bella sfida perché ero convinto che quella piazza fosse la più entusiasta di calcio. E fare calcio in una piazza con così tanto entusiasmo era quello che desideravo. Sfida e coraggio sono due cose che si abbinano".

Come mai la Juventus non ti ha mai chiamato? "Alla Juventus sono cresciuto. Ci sono state tante situazioni e quando cresci in un ambiente hai il desiderio di tornare. Poi sono contento di come è andata".

De Rossi in panchina del Genoa? "Credo che c'è una nuova generazione di allenatori che sta crescendo e lui è uno di quelli che sta facendo vedere idee e capacità. Sicuramente farà bene".

Manchester United 4-4 Aston Villa: Carrick’s side twice fell apart, either side of another comeback, and will spend the international break 19th

Michael Carrick said he still expects to be working with Manchester United over the international break and insisted he will not walk away after his players scored two stoppage-time goals to rescue an extraordinary 4-4 draw against Aston Villa in the Premier League.

The visitors appeared on course for a comfortable win after restoring their two-goal advantage in a crazy game Old Trafford, but late goals from Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu saw United pull off their second great escape of the week after Tuesday’s incredible comeback victory against Roma.

The result still leaves United in the Premier League’s bottom three, however, and they will spend the international break 18th in the table before visiting rivals Liverpool. United have won just once in their first 10 in the Premier League, but Carrick was still applauded off the pitch by the United fans.

“We haven't won enough games, but I expect to move forward and keep working on things that we've been working on and showing a lot of the good in the game today,” Carrick said. “We need to tighten up on a few mistakes and win football matches.”

Villa boss Unai Emery was hugely frustrated to see his side twice throw away a two-goal lead, and stormed down the tunnel before full-time after Dorgu’s equaliser. Villa were unhappy that United goalkeeper Senne Lammens appeared to prevent Zion Suzuki from catching the ball before Dorgu headed in.

Follow reaction from Manchester United v Aston Villa in the Premier League, below

Roy Keane felt Manchester United deserved credit for fighting back to claim a point against Aston Villa but lambasted their defending.

Speaking on Sky Sports, former United midfielder Keane said: “I think we’ve said previously there have been question marks over this team but they’re showing a bit of fight, a bit of desire and, as bad as the game was from a footballing point of view, they kept going to the end.

“So, on the one hand, you say dreadful to give up four goals at home, but they kept going, so we have to give them a little bit of praise.

“In terms of the bigger picture, it’s that rollercoaster of watching United. Some bits were so, so bad. Defensively the way it started, the start of the second half, but they found a way to get a point in a game where they looked dead and buried.

“Still nowhere near good enough to try and get towards the middle of the table.”

Gary Neville has claimed the Manchester United “don't have a clue what to do” as they consider the future of Michael Carrick over the international break.

"Michael could leave, Michael might stay, we don't know, the ownership don't have a clue what to do,” Neville said on NBC.

Neville said he did not think sacking Carrick would change anything, highlighting that United have had seven permanent managers since Sir Alex Ferguson departed.

"None of it has worked because if you recruit badly, if you've got poor performance department, poor recruitment department, you've not got leadership that is strong right through the spine of the football club you will fail.

“Every organisation in the world will fail if you've not got an aligned leadership from top to bottom - your board and your ownership right the way through to the person who is stood on the pitch trying to play football.

"There are real issues within that football club, you've got people doing those jobs who have never done those jobs before in senior positions."

Michael Carrick endured an awkward end to a post-match interview as he had an excruciating exchange about his future as Manchester United manager.

Carrick firstly bristled slightly when asked if United are looking at a relegation fight, saying: “No, we’re in November. I don’t like this situation we’re in, I don’t like the results we’ve had but we can definitely do something about it.”

From there, things got worse, as reporter Patrick Davison asked a lengthy question suggesting there were clearly going to be conversations about his future during the international break over the next couple of weeks, to which Carrick simply responded: "Is there?"

The Aston Villa manager told Sky Sports: “They scored in minute 96 and there were six minutes added. I think the match was finishing. They scored the goal in minute 96. You were making the question [about why] I left before. Because the minute 96 and 30 seconds, was when we started the kick-off. My English is so poor, but it's clear or not?"

Matheus Cunha was in no mood to celebrate Manchester United snatching a 4-4 draw against Aston Villa.

“So tired. I feel so disappointed,” he told Sky Sports.

“After the other night we talked a lot about how to concede goals and then be there and then change our mentality to come into the game. And then we did it again, we conceded some goals. We can’t do it. We need to play like Manchester United and go forward.

“[We need] a little bit more courage, like how to play, and then always we do this after the concede goals. We show a character after concede goals, but if you do this a little bit before, I feel like we can win much more games.”

“Disappointed we haven't won the game. I thought we were the better team for large parts of the game. We've given them... we’ve made it difficult for ourselves with the goals that we've given away.

“Not just giving away one... but it's kind of two [games] in succession that put us in such a difficult position.

We did it the other night, We did it again today, and then come back into the game. We could have won it in the end, so I'm disappointed that we haven't won the game.”

There was a slightly tetchy end to Michael Carrick’s interview with Sky Sports, but the Manchester United said he expects to keep working with his players over the international break.

He said: “We haven't won enough games, but I expect to move forward and keep working on things that we've been working on and showing a lot of the good in the game today, tighten up on a few mistakes and win football matches.”

As the Manchester United players who had been told they were not fit to wear the shirt, in perhaps the earliest ever airing of that chant in footballing history, celebrated a second ridiculous comeback in five days, the Stretford End launched into a different chorus.

They had told the team they were unworthy of representing them after a mere five minutes, had cheered in damning fashion when the dreadful Diogo Dalot was substituted, had been vitriolic about the Glazer family, Sir Jim Ratcliffe and Jason Wilcox. But United left the pitch in the relegation zone while their supporters serenaded Michael Carrick.

“It’s hard to believe it’s not Scholes,” they sang. In one respect, it was hard to believe it was not Sir Alex Ferguson, plucking results from nowhere. In another, it was hard to believe this was not the prelude to a sacking.

It may still be. United have one win in 11 games, one point in three in the Premier League but Carrick is starting to specialise in escapology. Bournemouth had seemed the end for him, Roma looked it when his side were 2-0 down. Aston Villa seemed his valedictory game twice, before two two-goal comebacks.

Manchester United goalkeeper Senne Lammens was forward for the late corner, and appeared to make no attempt to win the ball from the Bruno Fernandes cross. He instead looked to block Zion Suzuki from gathering the cross, which left Patrick Dorgu free to score the equaliser.

According to Opta, Manchester United are the first team in Premier League history to trail by two goals and level twice in the same game.

It was that kind of night. No one is really sure what to make of it.

The star edge rusher returned from a knee procedure and showed the difference he can make, racking up 3.0 sacks in the Rams’ 20-13 victory over the Commanders on Sunday afternoon.

Garrett’s first sack also forced a fumble, as he poked the ball out from quarterback Jayden Daniels in the second quarter. That led to a field goal late in the half, which cut Washington’s lead to 10-6 at halftime.

Garrett then had two more in the fourth quarter, one of which came after he’d just been flagged for an offside penalty.

While Garrett starred on defense, receiver Puka Nacua paced the offense with 14 catches for 150 yards with a touchdown. His 14 receptions are the third-most he’s had in a single game in his career. He also had a pair of carries for 20 yards.

The Commanders had a 10-6 lead at halftime from a 37-yard field goal and a 7-yard touchdown pass from Jayden Daniels to Van Jefferson. The Rams got two field goals from Brandon McManus for their six points.

But the Rams were able to double up with a touchdown on their first drive of the second half. Blake Corum ran it in for a 1-yard score to cap an eight-play, 69-yard drive. And while a Matthew Stafford interception allowed the Commanders to tie the game at 13-13, Stanford then threw a touchdown to Nacua to put the Rams ahead for good.

Washington had a chance to at least tie the game late, but time ran out on their last drive.

Daniels finished the game 24-of-35 for 273 yards with a touchdown. He had four carries for 16 yards.

Stafford finished 26-of-43 for 228 yards with a TD and a pick.

At 5-4, the Rams are now above .500 for the first time this season and on a three-game winning streak. The team will be on the road to face division-rival Arizona next weekend before a Week 11 bye.

The Commanders have fallen to 2-5-1. They won’t have much time to think about their missed opportunities, as they’ll face the Giants on Thursday night in Week 10.

The CEO reportedly admitted to an affair after being linked to a woman suspected to be a spy.

Following a string of reports from The New York Times and Financial Times linking NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang with a woman suspected to be a Chinese spy, the company says it's launching an independent investigation into the claims that he "has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort."

In the statement released on Sunday, NVIDIA said, "A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside independent counsel, will oversee the investigation. The Board prioritizes US national and economic security, which is paramount to the interests of NVIDIA and its shareholders. While the Board has no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised, the Board's inquiry will examine all available information."

The New York Times reported at the end of October that Huang had "developed a close relationship" with a woman named Alice Huang (no relation to Jensen), who US intelligence agencies had determined to be a Chinese intelligence asset. In a followup, The New York Times reported that Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based company Megaspeed — a major buyer of NVIDIA chips — was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to gather intelligence on the CEO. The Financial Times later reported that US officials believe Jensen Huang was romantically involved with the alleged spy.

Jensen Huang reportedly admitted to an affair in a memo sent to staff on Sunday, per Bloomberg, as NVIDIA publicly confirmed its own investigation into the claims. "Jensen fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it," NVIDIA said. "The Board has confidence in Jensen and his leadership team and will provide further updates as appropriate."

Recasting Sabrina The Teenage Witch In 2026: All 9 Main Roles

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

In 1996, Melissa Joan Hart perfectly embodied the titular star of the magical coming-of-age sitcom, Sabrina the Teenage Witch, but 30 years on, a reboot could have a very different cast. The show was a massive success, and as such, it ended up running for seven seasons from 1996 until 2003.

Beyond that, the series also ended up with an animated spin-off and sequel, a reimagined revival in 2018 which took a much darker approach to the narrative, and a handful of feature films, including the 1996 movie starring Hart and Ryan Reynolds that kicked it all off.

But, if TV executives wanted to bring the series back today, and stick more closely to the light-hearted comedy roots of the 1996 series, there are only a handful of actors who could really excel at playing the parts that brought this series to life in the first place.

First up, we need a loud, boisterous, and versatile actor who can bring the humor, enthusiasm, and depth to the character of Sabrina that this role deserves. And with that in mind, there is no one better suited to playing this part than Ruby Stokes. Stokes has been steadily building out her credits since 2010, with a major turn in 2016 when she played a central role in the film Una.

Since then, Stokes has tackled a variety of performances, from the stage to the screen, and performed in period dramas, dark comedies, and captivating dramas, all of which have given her both the experience to play the role and more than qualified her to take the lead as a mischievous young witch discovering her powers.

While Gillian Jacobs played the uptight and anxious Britta in Community, the role that she is currently best known for, she also has the capacity to play much more laid-back and fun-loving roles. And considering Hilda Spellman, one of Sabrina's aunts who is also a full witch, is prone to act on impulse and take things less seriously than her sister Zelda, it would make a lot of sense to cast Jacobs as Hilda.

Hilda is the younger sister who loves to have fun and is willing to play along with Sabrina's ideas, often getting herself in trouble with her sister along with their niece. Jacobs brings humor, vibrancy, and warmth to her roles that allows her to deliver characters who may not be the most practical, but they are certainly endearing.

Meanwhile, Zelda Spellman is frequently the voice of reason, and the most level-headed witch in the room. In order to bring this part to life, someone like Ayo Edebiri, who recently came off her turn in the incredible series, The Bear, would be an ideal choice to play the person keeping everything together while everyone else is chasing their own directions.

In many ways, this is exactly how her character, Sydney, ended her run in The Bear, having learned valuable lessons through her challenging work with the team at The Bear restaurant, before taking the reins and becoming the Head Chef and co-owner. So, in a sense, playing Zelda could help to see that type of character evolve even further in Edebiri's capable hands.

Bill Hader is an accomplished comedy actor whose deadpan delivery style has made him a fan favorite throughout his career, from his time as a regular cast member on SNL to a series of roles in TV and film over the last decade or so. Hader has also taken to voice acting in recent years with a growing list of credits for major roles in projects such as The Cat in The Hat, Inside Out, and many more.

With all of that preparation, Hader is easily the strongest candidate to tackle the sarcastic and snappy wizard who was turned into a cat as a punishment for attempting to take over the world, Salem.

Harvey Winkle is the awkward but lovable, goofy and endearing love interest that Sabrina Spellman finds herself fawning over. While the character in the 1996 series was popular thanks to his status as an athlete, times have moved on, and there are many other qualities that earn people a stamp of popularity and approval in high school settings, which opens the door for an updated take on Harvey, played by Jack Martin.

Martin has only recently started to land credited roles on the screen with his career taking off in 2020. Since then, he has tackled a number of small parts in TV and film, before landing a central role in the 2026 comedy series, Not Suitable For Work. It's here that Martin proved himself as the perfect cast for a part like Harvey Kinkle, and with that series getting axed after its debut season, Martin has some space on his schedule.

Libby Chessler is the quintessentially popular girl in a high school setting who is the head of the cheerleading squad, and who is frequently seen belittling and mocking other girls for being weird, or as Libby liberally refers to her classmates such as Sabrina, "freaks."

As an athlete, it makes sense to cast a performer like Maddie Ziegler who can bring the physicality to the role to convincingly play this part, while also having a cold and intense outward face that could inspire fear in others and establish her as a dominant force.

On the back of his performance as Phil Dunphy in Modern Family, Ty Burrell is an outstanding actor who brings so much physicality, personality, and incredible humor to his roles. All of that would go a long way to depicting one of the most fascinating mainstays of the original show, Principal Willard Kraft.

Principal Kraft is a bit of a bumbling buffoon who has an intense crush on Sabrina's aunt Hilda, but throughout the show's run ends up sparking relationships with both aunts at different points. Burrell would be an incredible addition to the lineup, and one that could reasonably juggle all of the different facets of the character that becomes so integral throughout the show's run.

In season 1 of Sabrina the Teenage Witch, Sabrina has a best friend by the name of Jenny Kelley. Jenny is quirky, studious, and eccentric, which means she matches Sabrina's energy as a young witch without knowing her friend's newfound secret. Together, the pair get up to all sorts of misadventures, and they appear to have a powerful bond that brings them together.

However, the series mysteriously replaced the character from season 2 onward. Regardless, a reboot could bring Jenny Kelley back into the story with an actor like Anna Cathcart who delivered an outstanding performance in To All the Boys and XO, Kitty, making her a more central and enduring part of the narrative. Cathcart perfectly embodies the traits of this character, and would make a great contrast to the energy that Ruby Stokes could bring to Sabrina.

In seasons 2 and 3, Valerie Birkhead takes Jenny's place as Sabrina's best friend, and the show avoids referencing Jenny ever again. Valerie then asserts herself as the new best friend, though the energy she brings to the part is much more self-conscious, shy, and awkward. And in a reboot, it would be great to have Sabrina matched up with both Jenny and Valerie to flesh out the cast and give the character more to bounce off.

So, to tackle this role, an experienced comedy actor with a talent for playing more reserved and quiet roles makes the most sense, which is why Iris Apatow is a perfect fit. Apatow has perfected the portrayal of teenage neuroticism that is central to playing the role of Valerie, and her presence opposite Ruby Stokes would be a pleasure to watch unfold.

All in all, while an updated take on Sabrina the Teenage Witch would no doubt look very different to what came before, the talent to pull off such a reboot is perfectly positioned to make an updated version of the series that captures the magic of the original.

He's Wanted: Man Utd May Have Another JJ Gabriel Situation as 17 y/o Yet to Sign Deal

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Manchester United could have another JJ Gabriel situation on their hands, with another youngster yet to sign a new contract at Old Trafford, and he's attracting plenty of interest.

GIVEMESPORT understands that Gabriel is closing in on a move to Spanish giants Real Madrid after he previously made it clear that he wanted to have his registration terminated in order to leave the club.

Gabriel has been linked with almost every big club around Europe, but it appears that Madrid are close to winning the race for his signature.

United will be entitled to some compensation when it comes to Gabriel's departure, but it's certainly going to be minimal in comparison to the sort of fee they'd want for a player of his potential.

Unfortunately for United and many clubs, they are always left in difficult situations when it comes to young talents without professional contracts, and the Red Devils could have a similar situation with another one of their youngsters.

According to a report from Telegraph journalist Mike McGrath, Manchester United youngster Jariyah Shah is the latest academy product to be attracting interest from around Europe.

The 17-year-old talent has been watched by Juventus, Monaco, Sturm Graz, and Dutch clubs, and he too could move for a small fee due to the fact that he's yet to sign a professional contract with the Red Devils.

Unlike Gabriel, Shah hasn't indicated that he's unsettled, but with a host of different clubs showing an interest in him, there's every chance he follows in the footsteps of the Real Madrid-bound teenager.

It's believed that United are expected to offer professional terms to Shah, but he's set to play at the U17 World Cup, where even more clubs will likely be keeping a close eye on him if he performs.

Man Utd are eyeing a move for a new left-back target in January after an intense scouting mission.

Shah was born in and represents Australia at international youth level, and he made the move to United a few years ago.

He joined the club at U13 level and signed scholarship terms with United back in 2025. Shah joined United from Doncaster Rovers and made his U18 league debut in February 2025.

Shah has played nine times for United's U18 side in the U18 Premier League so far this season, scoring twice and providing an assist.

Shah is eligible to represent England, Australia, Pakistan, and Vietnam, but has only represented Australia at youth level so far in his career.

Of course, losing Shah wouldn't appear to be as big a blow as Gabriel, but he's clearly a highly rated talent given the number of clubs keeping a close eye on his development.
