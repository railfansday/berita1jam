Upgrade Your Next Grilled Cheese With A Rich & Creamy Store-Bought Spread

📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

We may receive a commission on purchases made from links.

The essence of the best grilled cheese lies in the delicious combination of melted cheese and crisped bread. There are many methods to accomplish such a pairing, with plenty of tips to give you the best results imaginable. If you're after a version that's especially ooey-gooey, try making the sandwich with cheddar cheese spread.

Available from several brands at widespread grocery stores like Walmart, cheddar cheese spread is essentially cheddar mixed with butter. The foodstuff is easily spreadable on bread and melts when it's heated, making a grilled cheese with a pleasantly runny interior. While it's creamier than classic cheddar, the flavor is no afterthought, with this cheese's sharpness still perceptible.

Using butter alongside cheese is common in grilled cheese recipes; the spread lets you conveniently lather on both components at once. The fat enhances the bread's crispiness, all while retaining a moist interior. As a sole filling, the spread turns the dish luxuriously decadent — but complemented with a few slices of cheese, it's a grilled cheese rendition to remember.

There are many international grilled cheese styles to explore; cheddar cheese spread fits into a wide range of renditions. Combined with a store-bought loaf of sourdough and sliced cheddar, the spread makes a mouth-watering classic. However, you can consider layering in other ingredients, too — thereby making a cheddar grilled cheese with a twist.

For example, the cheese's sharpness welcomes a sweet contrast. Spread a jam or jelly into the sandwich, whether a rich fig jam, tart cranberry preserves, or a sweet blackberry jam. For a sweet touch with less sugar, you could also use fresh fruits to upgrade any grilled cheese; the spread would go great with apples, for example, as apples and cheddar are a match made in sandwich heaven. And you could also consider leaning into the spice, smearing on spicy brown mustard or hot pepper jelly for a light dose of heat.

Consider trying out other ingredient add-ins, too. Caramelized onions will lend a grilled cheese a subtle nutty sweetness, while bacon bits bring a savory touch. Cheddar cheese spreads allow the many different flavors to come together, letting you easily craft a sandwich to your tastes.

Bitcoin Fell More Than 20% in November. History Says This Happens Next.

📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The world’s top cryptocurrency could face a lot more selling pressure.

Bitcoin's (BTC -4.84%) price has declined by over 20% in November. At just under $60,000, it's trading more than 50% below its all-time high of about $126,000 from last October. Let's see what caused that decline -- and what its historical performance suggests could happen next.

A year ago, many investors expected the Fed to cut interest rates in 2026. Lower rates would presumably weaken the U.S. dollar, making blue chip cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin more attractive, and spark a rotation from conservative, higher-yielding investments into riskier ones.

Long-term catalysts also supported Bitcoin. Its spot-price exchange-traded funds (ETFs), initially approved in early 2024, were attracting more retail and institutional investors. It was also attracting more attention from corporations and governments as a hedge against the debasement of fiat currencies. Investors also expected its next halving -- which halves the rewards for mining Bitcoin every four years -- to exacerbate its scarcity in 2028.

But in 2026, those tailwinds dissipated as the Iran war exacerbated inflation and prompted the Fed to raise its rates for the first time in three years in September. The Democratic sweep in the U.S. midterms also increased the likelihood of tighter restrictions on the crypto market. Those challenges curbed the market's appetite for cryptocurrencies, and Bitcoin's price plummeted.

Past performance never guarantees future gains, but Bitcoin has a solid record of bouncing from steep peak-to-trough declines. However, each drawdown shook out many investors.

As of this writing, Bitcoin has declined 53% from its all-time high. Therefore, investors shouldn't be surprised if it sinks even lower over the next year before stabilizing. An 80% drop from $126,000 would reduce its price to about $25,000.

That would be a painful pullback -- especially for investors who bought Bitcoin in late 2025 -- but its historical performance suggests it could bounce back sharply after it bottoms out. Every time Bitcoin crashed, it set new all-time highs within a few years.

I'm not saying that's guaranteed to happen this time, but plenty of catalysts -- including interest rate cuts and its next halving -- could drive the bulls back to Bitcoin. While it could certainly get worse before it gets better, I believe Bitcoin will be trading much higher in just a few years.

Ahead of the deliberations of the Globes’ eligibility committee, The Hollywood Reporter dissects a host of eyebrow-raising category submissions — ‘Club Kid’ as a drama, ‘Obsession’ as a comedy, and more.

The Drama is a comedy — or so say the backers of the film, about an engaged couple’s darkest secrets, in their submission for the Golden Globes, which requires all films be entered as either A) a drama or B) a musical or a comedy. And that’s not even one of this year’s more questionable submissions.

Other comedy entries include I Play Rocky, about the making of Rocky (which competed at the Globes as a drama); Obsession, about a young woman’s loss of agency; Tender Loving Care, about the frustrations of two young women, including the decline of one’s father; Artificial, about AI growing out of control; and Project Hail Mary, about a man sent into space against his will to try to stave off the end of the world. Meanwhile, Club Kid, about a man-child who ends up with a child — widely described as an elevated version of Adam Sandler’s comedy Big Daddy — has been entered as a drama. Yes, all of the aforementioned musical/comedy submissions have moments of laughter and all of the dramas have elements of drama — but still, these classifications are pretty eyebrow-raising, begging the questions: What is going on? And is there a better way?

The first Globes were held in 1944. There were no genre-specific Globes categories until the eighth, when the lead acting categories were split into two, one for dramas, the other for musicals/comedies. The following year, the same split was implemented for best picture. That category was reunified for the 11th ceremony, but the split was brought back for the 12th and every year since, helping to attract more stars to the ceremony.

Until 2023, Globes category submissions were made by studios and reviewed by the HFPA’s reminders list committee, which rarely overturned anything, even as dramas with music passed as musicals (Ray, Walk the Line and Rocketman), dramas with few laughs passed as comedies (Ghost, The Tourist and My Week With Marilyn) and comedies passed as dramas (All About Eve, Born Yesterday, Harvey and Sunset Boulevard — all in the same year). Once in a blue moon, the committee would reject a submission (Promising Young Woman as a musical/comedy), but it usually deferred to studios’ preferences.

Frustrations burst into the open in 2015 after The Martian, a film about an astronaut stranded on Mars, was submitted and accepted as a musical/comedy. Judd Apatow, whose comedy Trainwreck was in contention that year, tweeted, “Trying to dominate the comedy category when you are really a drama afraid of dramatic competition is a punk move.” The Martian ended up beating Trainwreck for best picture, something Apatow was still smarting over when he presented at this year’s Globes: “My film Trainwreck lost best comedy to Ridley Scott’s The Martian. You know Ridley Scott, America’s favorite comedy director — director of such hilarious comedies as Gladiator, Blade Runner and Alien? I can’t wait till later tonight when best comedy is won by Hamnet.”

But ownership of the Globes had changed in 2023 (the Globes now share the same owners as THR), resulting in widespread reforms. This year’s rules declare: “Motion pictures shall be entered in the category that best matches the overall intent, tone and content of the film. Therefore, a motion picture that is primarily intended to have a serious or dramatic tone but contains comedic overtones or elements shall be entered as a drama as opposed to a comedy. Likewise, a motion picture that is primarily intended to have a comedic or satirical tone but contains dramatic overtones or elements shall be entered as a comedy as opposed to a drama.”

Once the film submission period ends (Nov. 24 this year), an eligibility committee comprising current Globes voters (the quantity and names are kept private) evaluates every submission for “potential misclassification issues.” If found, they “may contact the studio … to discuss those issues and to resolve them informally.” Eventually, the eligibility committee meets with a review committee to discuss any outstanding concerns, and those committees — or at least members of them who have seen a film in question — vote to determine if a change is necessary; two-thirds must favor a change for one to occur.

Why do people try to game the Globes categories? Because the nationally telecast Globes ceremony provides the biggest spotlight for awards contenders before Oscar nominations voting (the 2027 Globes will take place Jan. 10, and Oscar nomination voting starts the next morning), making it highly valuable for a film or individual to be represented at the ceremony as a nominee or, better still, a winner.

There usually are fewer standout musicals and comedies than dramas, meaning it’s often easier to land a nom or win if a drama can pass as a musical/comedy. (This year’s aforementioned musical/comedy submissions surely wanted to get out of the way of The Odyssey.) Another motivation: Studios with multiple films prefer to not have them pitted against one another. (A24 probably wants Club Kid to compete as a drama because it already has The Invite and The Debut up for best musical/comedy, just as Neon probably wants Artificial to compete as a comedy because it already has All of a Sudden, Fjord and Paper Tiger up for best drama.)

It remains to be seen whether the Globes’ eligibility committee will overturn some of this year’s submissions. (Reports suggest it already rejected Michael as a musical/comedy.)

Moving forward, it would make everyone’s life easier if the Globes consolidated its genre-specific categories — picture, actor and actress — into single categories with 12 slots rather than divided categories with six each. Or, if the Globes prefers to continue to have multiple categories for each in order to yield twice as many winners, it might consider retaining the “drama” label for one but changing the other to “genre,” which would better describe recent musical/comedy nominees like Poor Things, The Substance and Bugonia.

Vitor Matos admits ‘anger’ expectation after leaving Swansea City to join Strasbourg

📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Swansea City confirmed on Wednesday that head coach Vitor Matos had left the club to take charge of Strasbourg, with the Portuguese manager’s appointment officially announced. The Swans received compensation of around £5m, with Leon Britton and Kris O’Leary taking charge on an interim basis, alongside former defender Ashley Williams.

Matos’ departure came as a shock and it has left sour feelings from Swansea City fans. Speaking ahead of his first game as Strasbourg boss, Matos has revealed his feelings towards his former side, and what he expects from the Swansea fans.

As reported by French news outlet ICI Alsace, Matos said: “It was a difficult decision to leave Swansea. I expect nothing from the fans but anger, and I understand it“.

He added:“It’s a really exciting project and I felt I could help move the team forward. It wasn’t an easy decision to leave Swansea, given the emotional dimension and emotional bonds I had forged there.”

It does not seem a replacement for Matos is close with Britton, O’Leary and Williams in charge for the clash against West Brom. Rob Edwards and Tommy Elphick are reportedly on the clubs radar as potentially replacements.

Swansea City will be frustrated to lose another manager just as they appeared to be building towards something special. The situation is reminiscent of Russell Martin’s departure, when he was poached by Southampton.

The Swans made significant progress under Matos, so finding a successor who can maintain a similar tactical approach should be a priority. Doing so would allow them to get the best out of the existing squad while limiting the disruption and adjustment period that inevitably comes with an unexpected change in the dugout.

Swansea City will be carefully weighing up a number of candidates, but there will be an urgency to appoint Matos’ replacement as quickly and efficiently as possible. The longer the vacancy remains, the greater the risk of disrupting the momentum they had begun to build.

Swansea City took a bold gamble when appointing Matos last season. He arrived with very little experience as a head coach, but the decision has proved to be an inspired one and could earn the club a substantial compensation fee at the very least.

With his departure looking increasingly likely amid his interest in the Strasbourg job, the Swans should have plenty of confidence in their ability to find a suitable replacement. The Matos appointment itself is evidence of their judgement, while their recent transfer windows have also been particularly impressive.

Overall, Swansea’s recruitment of both players and staff has been very strong and has helped transform them into a much more competitive side. There is little reason to doubt that their next managerial appointment could be another success.

Move Over, Balaclavas: The Snood Is Set to Be Winter's Biggest Fashion Trend

📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Alongside their cousin, the hooded scarf!

It was just a few years ago that the balaclava was everywhere. Fashion people referred to it as the winter headpiece you absolutely needed. But now, I’ve noticed a new star, and that’s the snood, alongside the hooded scarf. Most people over the years have blended these three headpieces, but I’m here to inform the masses: you’re probably wearing a snood or hooded scarf now, not a balaclava.

Balaclavas are a full-coverage piece of headgear that wraps around the entire head, neck, and face (leaving only the eyes, nose, or mouth exposed), whereas a snood is a continuous circular loop or tube of fabric designed primarily to insulate the neck and cover the top of the head.

Balaclavas tend to be closer to the face, similar in fit to a ski mask, while snoods are loose-fitting and have a sort of cowl design to them. According to the winter sports community, balaclavas are better for skiing and extreme cold, but for most of us, we’re just looking to warm up a little with our winter accessories.

If you’re ready to wear your own snood this winter season, I’ve gathered some stylish inspo below to help build out those mood boards.

This brown snood moment with a light beige trench coat is peak winter outfit. The headpiece pulls the whole cozy vibes together and gives ultimate cool girl.

As a Floridian girlie, shorts in the winter are my kind of speed. Throw on those uber warm tights I see scrolling down my TikTok ‘for you’ page and you’re in business. The hooded scarf just elevates the whole moment too and keeps your head warm, which is ultimately the goal.

Colorful snoods aren’t always top of the list, especially if you’re looking for a headpiece that can blend with all your outfits. But if you’re hoping to bring a bit more fun to your winter style, then grabbing a snood like this one above is the way to do it.

Monochromatic is always in, but getting super close to a full black look and throwing in a light-colored headpiece is my personal winter hack. It brightens up the whole outfit, but doesn’t a neutral color like the one above still keep the look uniform and stylish.

You can reveal the full GTA 6 map in minutes using this simple plane trick

📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

Clear up that fog in no time at all, but be careful.

GTA 6 has a huge map, which is around twice as big as the one found with GTA 5. That's a whole lot of Florida-inspired strip malls and alligator-soaked swamps. However, new players start the game in a fairly remote region, based on the Florida Keys, and the vast majority of the map is unavailable for perusal.

Fog is obscuring every part of the map that you haven't explored yet. New regions get uncovered as you travel through them. You could do this the long way, by following the story. You could also do this the slightly-shorter way, by grabbing a car and just driving north until the wheels fall off. GTA 6 doesn't have a system in place that automatically ups the wanted level as you enter new areas, so that's good.

However, the quickest way to unlock the full map is found in the skies, and not the roads. Long-time players know the drill. Jason needs to get an airplane.

Luckily, there's a plane just a few miles north of Jason's first house in Key Lento. Take a break from making home renovations and head to Southpoint Airfield, as seen in the map above. It's basically just north of the starting area.

Drive the western bridge that runs over the Shoeless Keys and then head west a little bit onto a runway. It's hard to miss. The area is marked as restricted, but it's mostly abandoned so there shouldn't be any armed onlookers.

Once at the airfield, look for a duster plane just waiting to be flown. Hop in and do your thing. Planes move incredibly fast and will quickly unlock all of those foggy areas. Just fly carefully and keep an eye on fuel.

You probably won't be able to get the whole map done on a single fly through, due to that fuel, but planes automatically regenerate at airfields as soon as one leaves. Fly back to Southpoint and start again, this time heading in another direction. You'll have the whole map uncovered in minutes.

Happy travels. The only real thing to worry about here is the Mile Zero Air Station at the bottom left of the map. Flying over that airspace will instantly generate a four-star wanted rating. This wanted level can bring police after you in helicopters, so airplanes aren't necessarily safe. Save this area for last and exit as soon as the fog lifts from the map. Mile Zero is home to some really fast aircraft, but it's likely not worth the trouble just yet.
