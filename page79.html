Thailand to block Bitget in December over unlicensed crypto trading

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Thailand’s SEC filed a criminal complaint against Bitget’s operator, with access to its website and app set to be blocked from Dec. 5.

Thailand’s securities regulator has filed a criminal complaint against BG Limited, the operator of cryptocurrency exchange Bitget, for allegedly operating without a license.

Thailand’s Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) said Thursday that it had notified the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society to block access to Bitget from Dec. 5. The restriction will cover Bitget’s website, other associated domains and its mobile app.

The regulator advised users to take action regarding assets held on the platform before the deadline.

According to the SEC, Bitget offered crypto trading services between 2022 and 2025, charged trading fees and promoted its services through Thai-language websites and social media channels. The regulator said these activities violated Thailand’s digital asset business licensing requirements.

The regulator filed the complaint with Thailand’s Economic Crime Suppression Division for investigation. Prosecutors will decide whether to bring charges, while courts will determine criminal liability.

Thailand’s Ministry of Digital Economy and Society has authority to restrict access to unlicensed crypto trading platforms under a 2025 decree aimed at protecting investors and combating financial crime.

Bitget did not immediately respond to Cointelegraph’s request for comment.

Portugal icon Cristiano Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football.

Ronaldo is the leading scorer and appearance-maker in the history of men's internationals, plundering 146 goals in 234 games since his Portugal debut in 2003.

He captained Portugal to their first-ever major trophy at Euro 2016, with Eder scoring the winning goal against hosts France in the final after he had gone off injured, while also lifting the Nations League trophy on two occasions, in 2019 and 2025.

Ronaldo considered retiring from Selecao duty after their round-of-16 exit at this year's World Cup, only to be talked into staying on by the FPF (Portuguese Football Federation).

However, Ronaldo then abruptly left their camp during the last international break, accusing new head coach Jorge Jesus of breaching his trust after he was left on the bench throughout a Nations League win over Norway.

Ronaldo, who also accused Jesus of failing to read a series of pre-agreed points regarding that decision to leave him on the bench, was subsequently given a 30-day suspension by the FPF on October 19.

With the Selecao facing Denmark and Wales in the Nations League on November 14 and 17, that would appear to mean there will be no farewell appearance for the five-time Ballon d'Or winner, who posted an emotional video statement on Instagram on Thursday.

Binance Revokes API Keys, Security Issue Found at SignalPlus

📅 2026-11-05  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Binance has revoked all API keys associated with SignalPlus after the digital asset trading infrastructure provider identified a potential security vulnerability, prompting the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange to take measures to protect users.

The exchange confirmed Thursday that the decision was intended to prevent possible unauthorized access through connections established between Binance accounts and SignalPlus. Binance emphasized that its internal systems had not been compromised and that customer funds remained safe.

The precise nature of the security issue remains unclear. Neither the extent of the potential vulnerability nor the number of users affected has been publicly established. There has also been no confirmation that attackers accessed customer accounts or that the incident resulted in financial losses.

Following a potential security issue reported by third-party platform SignalPlus, we have proactively removed all API keys linked to SignalPlus as an immediate precautionary measure. Binance's own systems are unaffected. User funds remain safe. If you used SignalPlus, please log into your Binance account directly to review your positions and open orders. We recommend waiting until SignalPlus confirms full resolution before rebinding any API keys, and always enabling IP whitelisting. We also encourage all third-party platforms integrated with Binance to review their security controls, monitor for unusual activity and contact us immediately if they detect any concern

The development underscores the security challenges associated with third-party infrastructure in cryptocurrency markets, where trading platforms increasingly depend on external applications for execution, analytics and automation.

API-related vulnerabilities have previously created risks for exchange users. In 2022, the exposure of API credentials associated with trading platform 3Commas triggered security warnings and key revocations across multiple cryptocurrency exchanges.

Binance's security documentation advises users to revoke potentially compromised credentials immediately and restrict API permissions to necessary functions.

For affected SignalPlus users, restoring automated trading functionality may require generating new credentials once the integration is deemed secure.

SignalPlus provides trading infrastructure and analytical tools for cryptocurrency derivatives markets, allowing traders to manage positions and execute strategies across exchanges through API integrations.

These connections enable third-party applications to interact with exchange accounts without requiring users to transfer their assets off-platform. However, depending on the permissions granted, compromised API credentials can potentially expose accounts to unauthorized trading activity.

Binance has effectively severed existing integrations between its platform and SignalPlus. The measure could potentially disrupt automated trading strategies that rely on those connections.
