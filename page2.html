Fast SSD leads to lower RAM requirements and smoother gameplay.

Gears of War: E-Day delivers an impressive visual and technical showcase. The game brings together several advanced graphics technologies, including Unreal Engine 5’s Nanite and Lumen, MegaLights, and RTX Mega Geometry for more accurate ray-traced shadows and reflections, alongside a range of upscaling and frame generation technologies.

However, today we’re focusing on a less-discussed technology that plays an equally important role in bringing this visually rich, densely detailed world to systems with more limited hardware resources: storage technology designed to reduce RAM usage.

We’ll begin by examining its behavior on a high-end system with plenty of resources to spare, before moving to a more modest configuration that better represents the hardware available to the average PC gamer. This will allow us to see how much of a difference the technology makes, particularly on lower-end systems.

In an interview with Digital Foundry, The Coalition explained how they leveraged the Xbox Series S’s fast SSD to bring the dense world of Gears of War: E-Day to a memory-limited system. By effectively using the NVMe SSD as an extension of system memory, they can dynamically load and offload complex city data in real time. This approach allows the game to stream the necessary assets for each scene just in time, overcoming the console’s limited RAM.

Digital Foundry notes that the true “special sauce” lies in The Coalition’s smart data management. The team employs advanced heuristics to determine which assets are fetched from the SSD and when, carefully balancing performance. Overusing this technique could harm frame rates, so the system is tuned to swap data only when it benefits the experience.

Gears of War: E-Day also supports DirectStorage, although based on our previous testing, this alone does not always result in high SSD activity or read rates.

YouTuber Mostly Positive Reviews shared his findings in a recent post on X, where he found higher-than-usual SSD activity in Task Manager when playing the game.

My 14400F is constantly sitting at 80% CPU usage, 16GB RAM is maxed out, and disk read is sitting at a constant 120 MBps, with spikes up to 500 MBps. Will test this with the 9060 XT as well. Based on his findings, we decided to investigate further.

Task Manager does not use a low enough polling period to measure SSD activity accurately, so we used HWInfo with the polling period set to 20 milliseconds. We tested SSD activity across all of the game’s graphics presets at 4K using DLSS Quality.

In our previous tests of games that relied heavily on the SSD, none came close to sustaining 1 GB/s of average bandwidth. The highest we recorded was 366 MB/s in Forspoken at 4K with maximum settings, while many titles stayed well under 100 MB/s. Some games did show higher peak bandwidths, but these spikes lasted only a few milliseconds. For our purposes, it’s the sustained throughput – reflected in the average bandwidth – that truly matters.

In Gears of War: E-Day at higher settings, we essentially have a steady stream of a little over 1 GB/s at all times, indicating that the disk is indeed being utilized as an extension of system memory. As you can see, peaks hit up to 3.7 GB/s of throughput.

We measured RAM usage on our high-end system to see how much the game will use across each generation of SSD tested.

The game uses a significant amount of system memory, but having the game installed on an NVMe SSD – regardless of generation – saves about 2GB of RAM. The storage technology seems to be working as expected.

Since the game’s average read bandwidth far exceeds the limits of a SATA SSD, we expect that installing the game on one would lead to visual issues – and that is exactly what we observe.

In the first comparison, textures are slow to load, which noticeably reduces image quality. In the second comparison, lighting and shadows take some time to appear. While everything eventually loads correctly, running the game on a SATA SSD does hurt immersion.

We tested the game across four generations of SSDs to evaluate any impact on performance. Using a high-end system with ample memory, we aim to determine the behavior of the storage technology on a system that is not resource-constrained. We will then compare the performance of our laptop’s internal PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD with that of a SATA SSD.

On a system with sufficient memory, installing the game on a SATA SSD has no measurable performance impact. The only drawback is the aforementioned visual degradation.

Now, we focused on our lower-end system with only 16GB of DDR5.

Now we start to see the benefit of installing the game on an NVMe SSD, especially on higher settings.

Although the average frame rate remains virtually identical on both the NVMe and SATA SSDs, increasing the graphics preset impacts the 1% lows. The NVMe SSD delivers smoother frametimes, resulting in a more responsive and fluid gaming experience.

While the concept isn’t entirely new – Windows already uses virtual memory to page data to disk when physical memory is exhausted – The Coalition has fine-tuned this technology specifically for their game. Their approach ensures that the data being paged out won’t noticeably slow down gameplay as it’s read from disk. In our testing, this technology successfully reduces RAM usage when the game is installed on an NVMe SSD.

Placing the game on an NVMe SSD also enables near-instant asset loading. For systems with 16GB of RAM or less, an NVMe SSD can deliver higher 1% lows, resulting in smoother performance. With RAM prices climbing and many players using machines with 16GB – or even 8GB – of memory, it’s reasonable to wonder if other developers will adopt similar techniques. For titles striving to create visually dense, richly detailed worlds, leveraging fast storage could be a practical way to preserve their vision on lower-end systems.

‘Magical’ Tree House With 30-Foot Glass Living Room Returns to the Market

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

A “magical” four-bedroom retreat encased in glass has returned to the market in Austin, TX, for $1,095,000.

The Glass Tree House debuted on the market for $1.5 million in August 2024 and has received several price cuts since then.

Custom slate stairs lead to a jaw-dropping living room with 30-foot-tall windows and custom rock walls carved into a cliffside, with tree-lined views that make you feel at one with nature.

“When you walk in, it feels like wherever you go, there is so much privacy and the view is very unique,” says listing agent Jenny Law of Kuper Sotheby’s International Realty. “It reminds me of a modern tree house that takes you far away from city life.”

Built in 2008, the 2,180-square-foot metal-clad contemporary was designed by architect John Allen to blur the boundaries between the indoor living spaces and the outdoor setting.

“The sellers use it as a short-term rental property,” Law notes. “It’s a nice retreat and would be a popular second home for someone because of its views and location, or it could be used as a primary residence for its school system.”

The sleek kitchen has custom black cabinetry, honey onyx countertops, built-in seating, and stainless-steel appliances.

The glass-surrounded primary suite has a soaking tub, a moss rock shower, Calacatta marble countertops, marble flooring, and access to a private terrace.

“It’s located in Apache Shores, where there are not a lot of restrictions in that area,” Law says. “It’s about 15 to 20 minutes from downtown Austin, but you feel like you are out in nature with these nice hill country views.”

A second lot, which could allow for a future pool, workshop, or guesthouse with applicable approvals, is available for separate purchase.

The property is also minutes from Lake Travis and Lake Austin, a residents-only boat launch, an 18-hole disc golf course, a soccer field, a community hall, a swimming pool, and sports courts.

“In Austin, there are so many homes that look the same, but this one is unique and beautiful,” Law says. “You truly feel like you are living outside.”

Portugal ‘Retire’ Cristiano Ronaldo’s 7 Shirt Despite FIFA Rule

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Portugal's post-Cristiano Ronaldo era is set to commence on Saturday night when Jorge Jesus' side face Denmark in the Nations League, with a new number seven set to don the famous red shirt for the first time in over 20 years.

Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football earlier this month, bringing an end to his 23-year spell as the talisman and figurehead for the Selecao as Quinas. He bows out with 146 goals and 234 caps for his nation, alongside a Euro 2016 winners' medal and two Nations League titles.

Historically, players with such legacies who become synonymous with a specific shirt number often have that shirt number retired in their honour.

However, a FIFA rule stipulates that for international tournaments organised by the global footballing body, teams are required to designate consecutive numbers for every player in the squad from 1 to 26. This regulation essentially prevents national teams from retiring a shirt, meaning players will wear number seven for Portugal after Ronaldo — Rafael Leao or Carlos Forbs is expected to wear it during this international break.

However, this hasn't prevented the European national team from concocting an inventive way around this, as they look to preserve the iconic shirt number in a tribute to their greatest-ever player.

In the purest sense of the word, Portugal can't retire the number seven shirt. They cannot prevent players from wearing it in the future, but they can still do their best to ensure Ronaldo's image and brand are preserved.

Posting on their Instagram account on Saturday, the Portuguese Men's National Team shared a video revealing that from now on, they'll be printing their number seven shirts with a little cross through the middle of the number, distinguishing them from Ronaldo's crossless seven.

"Time to unveil a new 7. Because there is one that will be eternal," the caption read.

This mark of respect for Ronaldo's achievements is permitted by FIFA, as it's not eradicating the number seven from future use, but rather slightly modifying how it'll appear on the back of Portugal shirts moving forward.

Argentina reportedly had plans to retire the number ten shirt after Lionel Messi called time on his international career, but the South American nation ran into the same issue Portugal did.

Unfortunately for the 2026 World Cup finalists, there was little scope for ingenuity or invention regarding how they present the number ten shirt, so they've not been able to honour Messi in the same way. That said, Lionel Scaloni did refrain from selecting someone to wear ten during Argentina's first post-Messi match against Uruguay on Friday. Theoretically, they can keep doing this until their next tournament.

Italy fines Meta €805m over Facebook’s access to Android photos

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Italy’s privacy watchdog says Facebook’s Android app read phone galleries without proper consent. Meta has 60 days to fix it.

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg at a dinner with tech leaders in the State Dining Room of the White House in Washington, Sept. 4, 2025.

Italy’s data protection authority, the Garante, has fined Meta €805m, or about $887m. It says the Facebook app for Android accessed photos, videos and their metadata on Italian users’ phones without specific consent or proper notice.

The decision, first reported in English by Reuters, targets a gallery suggestions feature available since 2023. While users scroll Facebook, it offers photos and videos from their phone as ideas for posts.

The Garante found that Facebook asked for gallery access with one permission for the whole app. That request followed a vague prompt to enable other features. Users could not tell they were also switching on gallery suggestions.

Meta’s EU base is in Ireland, and under GDPR the Irish regulator usually leads. Meta argued the case belonged there. The Garante disagreed, because it acted under ePrivacy rules, which have no one-stop shop.

The rule it applied, Article 122 of Italy’s privacy code, treats a smartphone as part of a person’s private sphere. Any access to what the phone holds needs free and informed consent. It applies even if no data leaves the phone and no harm is done.

The regulator said about 20 million Android users in Italy were affected. It also weighed a 2019 decision against Meta and what it called poor cooperation. Changes Meta made after the complaint counted in its favour, but the Garante called them late and limited to users who update the app.

The fine equals about 0.47% of Meta’s global turnover. Within 60 days, Meta must ask for separate, specific consent for the feature. It must also keep the feature off by default, even for users who already granted access.

The probe did not examine whether gallery content was used to train AI, which Meta denies. The Garante will look separately at a cloud processing feature launched in 2026.

The penalty is close to the €825m Dutch fine on Uber, though below Meta’s €1.2bn GDPR fine over data transfers. Meta is also tangling with Italy over a ruling on publisher payments.

Reuters said Meta was not immediately available for comment.

The 5 Healthiest Fast Food Salads, According To Nutritionists

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

A drive-thru doesn’t have to derail your diet.

Fast food chains aren’t exactly famous for their salads. They usually fall into two categories: a sad bowl of lettuce added to menus as an afterthought, or a vehicle for fried, calorie-dense ingredients that offset any nutritional upsides. But some chains do make an effort to sell salads worth ordering.

To identify the best better-for-you fast-food salads, we asked three nutrition experts to weigh in. Here are the menu items they recommend, along with some tips and tricks for maximizing their nutrition.

First, we need to debunk salad’s diet-friendly reputation. “A salad isn't automatically the healthiest choice on a fast food menu,” says Kathy Levin, RDN, CDCES, dipACLM, LDN. “Once you add fried chicken, bacon, cheese, candied nuts, crunchy toppings, and a creamy dressing, some salads land in the same calorie and fat range as a burger and fries.”

Taking all of those components away isn’t always a solution either. A sad salad with just lettuce and a smattering of vegetables is more like rabbit food than a satisfying meal for humans. According to Briana V. Reamer, MS, RDN, LDN, the secret is getting enough fiber, which she says “will keep you satisfied and full until your next meal.” Take advantage of the restaurant's add-ons—like beans, avocado, and more vegetables—to make your salad more substantial.

Experts also recommend watching your saturated fat intake, specifically with your protein and dressing. Grilled chicken generally has fewer calories and less saturated fat than deep-fried alternatives. Consider your dressing options, too. “If you’re not sure what to pick, look for a vinaigrette or olive-oil-based dressing,” says Tina Sergi, MS, RD, CSSD, CSCS, TSAC-F.

And, as a rule of thumb, order your dressing on the side. It keeps your salad from getting soggy on the drive home, for one, but it also gives you more portion control. “Using only part of the packet cuts saturated fat and sodium, especially when they're already on the higher end,” Sergi adds.

Of course, the dressing is only part of the equation. With those tips in mind, here are the five salads that made the cut.

This regional chain is known for its fire-grilled chicken, and this salad packs plenty of it. Each portion contains a double serving of citrus-marinated chicken breast, sliced avocado, cotija cheese, salsa fresca, and tortilla strips over a bed of greens. It's served with a creamy Mexican-inspired Caesar dressing.

El Pollo Loco strikes the right balance between substantial and light with this dish. Before you add dressing, the entire salad contains 420 calories and 52 grams of protein. “The fire-grilled chicken makes it very filling, the avocado adds heart-healthy fat and fiber, and the salsa fresca adds flavor without much sodium or sugar,” Levin says.

Like with most salads, the dressing significantly raises the calorie count—in this case, it adds 440 calories if you use the entire serving. If you're closely monitoring your daily intake, Levin advises going easy on the dressing.

Culver’s may be best known for its ButterBurgers, but experts say you shouldn’t sleep on its salads. The Chicken Cashew Salad in particular is a great choice. It starts with a bed of mixed greens, topped with cheddar, grape tomatoes, cucumbers, and roasted cashews. You can also choose from several dressings to finish it off.

With grilled chicken, the salad comes in at 430 calories and 41 grams of protein before adding dressing. Culver’s also offers six dressing varieties ranging from 50 to 260 calories.

“This is a great fiber- and protein-packed option,” says Sergi. “Plus, the cashews add healthy fats, copper to support energy production, and magnesium for optimal nerve and muscle function.”

This Lone Star staple sells a Cobb salad that’s a lighter alternative to its lineup of burgers and sandwiches. It comes loaded with all of the essentials: greens, bacon, a hard-boiled egg, grape tomatoes, carrots, and shredded cheese. You can also choose from three chicken options (the grilled chicken filet is, naturally, lighter than the fried options).

Without dressing, Whataburger's Cobb with grilled chicken has 430 calories and 44 grams of protein. It's also a source of fiber and is low in sugar, according to Reamer. “Salads high in fiber help with weight management and satisfy hunger.”

Some of Whataburger's creamier dressings add 200 to 280 calories, while the balsamic vinaigrette has 180.

For a fried chicken chain, Chick-fil-A makes some surprisingly stellar salads. The Market Salad starts with mixed greens and is topped with strawberries, blueberries, red and green apples, blue cheese, roasted almonds, and the chain’s Harvest Nut Granola. Chick-fil-A also has the most protein options on this list. The grilled chicken can come in nugget or filet form, warm or cold, and spicy or regular. Talk about variety!

The different grilled chicken options provide between 26 and 32 grams of protein, according to Chick-fil-A's nutrition guide. Levin also says you can forgo chicken entirely for a meatless meal that doesn't feel like an afterthought. “Berries and apples bring fiber, antioxidants and natural sweetness, which makes it one of the most plant-forward options on the list.”

But the chain is known for its sauces, after all, so you can swap it out for another option. 📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

DW journalist Jaafar Abdul Karim and Russia correspondent Katja Gloger have been awarded the prestigious Werner Holzer Prize for international journalism.

At a time when press freedom is under pressure and international newsrooms are shrinking, the Werner Holzer Prize celebrates a profession that many consider indispensable to democracy: foreign correspondence.

Presented in honor of Werner Holzer, the veteran reporter and former editor-in-chief of the German daily newspaper Frankfurter Rundschau, this year's award coincided with what would have been his 100th birthday. Holzer spent decades reporting from some of the world's most important hot spots and championing international journalism.

At the award ceremony in Frankfurt's Römer, the city's historic town hall, Mayor Mike Josef recalled that fundamental rights such as freedom of speech and freedom of the press were first enshrined for all Germans by the Frankfurt Parliament in the nearby Paulskirche in 1848.

The 2026 winners reflect the breadth and ambition of the award. First prize went to Katja Gloger, a veteran Russia correspondent who moved to Moscow in 1989 and accompanied Vladimir Putin during the early years of his presidency.

In her recent nonfiction book, "The Failure: An Investigative Report of Germany's Russia Policy," Gloger and co-author Georg Mascolo offer a searing critique of the misjudgments and illusions that shaped German policy toward Russia for decades.

Second prize was awarded to DW journalist and presenter Jaafar Abdul Karim. His talk show JaafarTalk reaches millions of viewers across the Arab world and in Germany. Whether in Tunis, Beirut or Cairo, Abdul Karim tackles subjects that are often considered taboo.

A research grant went to freelance journalist Luise Land, whose current project explores why her German-Namibian family still owns extensive landholdings in Namibia today. By tracing that history, she examines the enduring legacy of the genocide against the Herero and Nama peoples by German colonial forces in the early 20th century.

"Katja Gloger is one of the leading foreign policy reporters. For decades, she has been among the foremost experts on Russia and speaks out on uncomfortable truths," the jury stated on Friday, explaining its decision to honor Gloger. At the prize-giving ceremony, Gloger delivered a passionate plea for more journalism — and more international coverage — in the German press. "We must defend trustworthy, reliable and fact-based journalism with everything in our power," she said.

"Especially now, in times of polarization, when those who believe they're always right are often the loudest, we as journalists have a special responsibility. So far in 2026, JaafarTalk has already garnered more than 1.1 billion views across all platforms, and its community has grown to more than 9 million followers. Its motto: "Diversity fosters dialogue."

"We need fact-based journalism that promotes dialogue and reflects the diversity of our society," Abdul Karim stressed. "By that, I mean an honest and open dialogue in which we do not look away or remain silent. Lazio davvero da scudetto? Veron: "Perché, è vietato? Ditemi chi ha fatto meglio"

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

La Lazio può credere nello scudetto? "Perché è per caso vietato? Ditemi chi, in questo inizio di stagione, nel campionato di Serie A, ha mostrato di avere qualcosa in più della Lazio": questo il pensiero di Juan Sebastian Veron, leggenda del club biancoceleste - oggi presidente dell'Estudiantes de La Plata - che in un'intervista rilasciata a SportWeek assicura di credere al tricolore per la squadra di Gattuso.

Poi ancora: "Vi rispondo io: nessuno. La classifica attuale non è uno scherzo del destino, ma rispecchia i valori mostrati fino a questo momento. Poi qualcosa potrà cambiare, magari subentrerà un po' di stanchezza nelle gambe dei ragazzi di Gattuso, ma le basi su cui è stato costruito il primato sono solide, molto solide".

Parlando di singoli, poi, Veron indica in particolare Davide Frattesi fra i giocatori che più lo entusiasmano della Lazio di oggi: "Chi mi entusiasma di più? Se chiudo gli occhi e rifletto un attimo dico che Frattesi, che secondo me era un ottimo centrocampista anche prima di arrivare alla Lazio, sta facendo cose straordinarie. In Italia ci sono pochi elementi con le sue capacità d'inserimento. E poi fa gol, che nel calcio non è mai un dettaglio".
