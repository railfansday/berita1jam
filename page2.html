The Rings Of Power Season 3's Final Episodes Set All-Time Series IMDb Record

📅 Nov 26, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Warning: There are spoilers ahead for The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power season 3, episodes 7 and 8.The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power season 3 sets the one record to rule them all.

Season 3, episode 7, "Orodoruin" sees Sauron (Charlie Vickers) forging the One Ring, a long-awaited moment that the entire series has been building up to, and the episode was even screened in select theaters a day before it was released alongside the season 3 finale on Prime Video. Season 3, episode 8, "The Doom of Men" culminates in Sauron surrendering and kneeling before Ar-Pharazôn (Trystan Gravelle) as part of a larger scheme that will be revealed in the already renewed season 4.

On IMDb, season 3, episode 7 is now The Rings of Power's highest-rated episode of all time after receiving a rating of 8.5/10. Since the score is only based on 1,900 ratings as of this writing, it may fluctuate, but for now, it has surpassed the previous record-holder of season 1, episode 6, "Udûn." As for the season 3 finale, it is now in fourth place on the series' IMDb chart, having accrued a score of 7.8/10 from 1,300 reviews.

"Udûn" is an action-packed battle episode in which Adar (Joseph Mawle) and the Orcs attack the Southland villagers, who are saved by the arrival of Queen Míriel (Cynthia Addai-Robinson) and the Númenórean army, although their victory is short-lived due to the enemy engineering the eruption of Mount Doom and transforming the land into Mordor. Season 2, episode 7, "Doomed to Die," which is centered on the Siege of Eregion, used to be the second highest-rated episode, and is also centered on a battle unfolding.

By comparison, "Orodoruin" is building toward a battle between Sauron's army and the Númenóreans, but its main focus is the villain finally forging the One Ring. The anticipated battle between these two forces then does not end up happening and instead leads to Sauron's surrender. Despite featuring less action, though, season 3's final episodes are now considered among the best in the entire series.

Season 3, episode 4, "Gundabad" has a rating of 7.7/10, which means that half of the all-time top six on IMDb are installments from this season. This is indicative of the overall reception being more positive than ever, as Rings of Power season 3 reviews led to a "Certified Fresh" 87% Tomatometer score and 63% Popcornmeter score. The critics' score is a record-high and improved significantly after debuting at a series-low of 71%. Meanwhile, the audience score is "Fresh" for the first time in the show's history.

Prime Video has already confirmed that it will be building on this success with the announcement that The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power has been renewed for season 4, pre-production has begun, and filming is slated to begin in the spring. The streamer shared that season 3 reached 33 million global views in its first 12 days and is among their top 10 most-watched returning seasons of all time. It has also held onto the platform's number one spot on the worldwide chart and in more than 100 countries.

All episodes of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power seasons 1-3 are streaming on Prime Video.

The partners intend to start with practical uses in Japan before expanding across Asia and linking up with global markets.

Qualified investors first gained access through Singapore-based DigiFT on July 14, according to SBI Global Asset Management. On Nov. 5, SBI Global Asset Management announced a second distribution channel through HashKey Earn, a service of the licensed Hong Kong platform HashKey Exchange.

Clients must qualify as professional investors under HashKey and issuer rules and complete applicable onboarding and suitability checks.

Rams shut down Packers in second half for a 26-13 win in NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve game

📅 Nov 26, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

The Rams and Packers played to a tie in the first 30 minutes of Wednesday night’s game in Los Angeles, but the second half was all Rams.

Blake Corum ran for 151 yards and a score in final 30 minutes and the Rams defense shut out the Packers to pave the way to a 26-13 win. The victory is the fifth in a row for the Rams and they are now 7-4 with a Thursday night home game against the Chiefs on deck in Week 13.

The win moves the Rams ahead of the Packers in the race for NFC playoff spots and Thursday’s game between the Lions and Bears will have further impact on the chances for all four teams to advance to the postseason.

Corum’s touchdown came at the end of the Rams’ first possession of the second half and he finished the game with a career-high 32 carries for 184 yards.

That scoring drive ate up more than eight minutes. The Packers had a quick three-and-out, but briefly showed some life when linebacker Ty’Ron Hopper picked off Matthew Stafford to set the offense up in Los Angeles territory. Jordan Love turned the ball right back over when he hung a deep pass that safety Jaylen McCullough snagged out of the air. The Rams would then ride Corum and Kyren Williams on a pair of drives that covered more than 11 minutes.

The Packers’ last chance came with 6:13 left and they moved the ball to midfield before Love threw his second interception of the game. Cornerback Emmanuel Forbes got that one and the Packers were out of timeouts well before the two-minute warning.

Sticking with the ground game helped to limit Packers defensive end Micah Parsons’s impact in his return from last season’s torn ACL. He had a sack late in the first half, but Stafford only threw eight times after the break and the Packers’ run defense could not get their offense the ball often enough to get back into the game.

Love was 17-of-29 for 198 yards and a touchdown, but clearly missed wide receiver Christian Watson after he left the game with a concussion. They only picked up two first downs after halftime and they’ll hope that an extended break before their Week 13 trip to New Orleans is enough time for him to return to action.

The Rams have several injuries of their own to monitor as guard Bill Murray joined tight ends Davis Allen and Tyler Higbee in making early exits from the game. They’d like to have all those players back next week, but they showed on Wednesday night that Corum can sometimes be enough all by himself.

Top Boy, created by MobLand creator Ronan Bennett, is one of the best gangster series Netflix has ever delivered and can be called a masterpiece throughout its 32-episode run. Not only does Top Boy offer viewers thrilling gangster-fueled drama, but its lead characters, Dushane (Waters) and Sully (Kane "Kano" Robinson), are the backbone of the series with fierce ambition and fascinating moral complexity. Dushane and Sully are partners who constantly challenge each other while also working together to maintain dominance in the streets. They must deal with rival gangs while adhering to unspoken laws of the land, all while keeping their delicate power dynamic afloat. While Dushane is often calculated and highly strategic, Sully is impulsive and more prone to violence, which creates a compelling contrast between the two throughout the series.

Senate Democrats urge SEC to scrap crypto, tokenized stock exemptions

📅 Nov 26, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

US Senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Van Hollen, among others, have taken aim at two recent SEC efforts to ease regulatory requirements for the crypto industry.

US Senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Van Hollen have urged the US Securities and Exchange Commission to drop a proposal that would allow eligible crypto projects to raise funds from US investors without registering their offerings and revoke an exemption for tokenized stock platforms.

In two letters sent to SEC Chair Paul Atkins on Tuesday, the Democratic lawmakers argued that the agency had exceeded its statutory authority in both cases and that the moves will put investors at risk.

“Together, the letters raise concerns about the SEC’s efforts to carve out special treatment for crypto assets and tokenized securities at the expense of investor protections and market integrity,” they said.

Both SEC measures drew crypto industry support, as they came at a time when the CLARITY Act struggled to advance in Congress. The Regulation Crypto Assets was first introduced in August when the landmark crypto legislation was stalled, while the tokenized stock exemption came out in September, two days after the CLARITY Act fell short of the 60 votes needed to advance.

The letters also come just a day after OKXICE, a joint venture between crypto exchange OKX and New York Stock Exchange parent Intercontinental Exchange, launched a round-the-clock trading venue for tokenized US securities under the exemption on Monday.

The tokenized stock exemption allows qualifying platforms to offer blockchain-based trading of tokenized US stocks without registering as exchanges.

SEC lists four other entities that intend to use the exemption, including TSV LLC (which commenced operation in early November), Kephas Corporation d/b/a Perpetuals.com, Formation Securities LLC and Fairmint TSV LLC.

Van Hollen, Warren and two other senators urged the SEC to withdraw the order, warning the order could create a “shadow stock market” that falls outside of regulatory protections.

“We are particularly troubled by the order’s sweeping exemption of TSVs from fundamental registration requirements,” they said.

They also argued the order places an “unreasonable burden” on publicly traded companies by allowing third parties to tokenize their securities unless the issuer objects within 30 days.

“This effectively establishes an opt-out regime in which companies must continuously monitor tokenization activity and respond to potentially numerous requests from different platforms to prevent unauthorized trading of tokenized versions of their shares.”

Related: Trump’s SEC nominee Johnson backs formal rulemaking for tokenized stock trading

The senators also argued venues are exempt from rules requiring them to display their best prices to traders. “These protections are foundational to the fairness and efficiency of our equity markets,” they said.

Atkins previously described the five-year exemption as an interim measure to allow onchain trading of certain US stocks while the agency considers longer-term rules.

In a separate letter, Warren, Van Hollen and Senator Tina Smith urged the SEC to withdraw its Regulation Crypto Assets Proposal, arguing that the proposal only serves to help crypto assets “escape” from the SEC’s regulatory regime.

The proposal would allow eligible crypto projects to raise up to $5 million over four years under a startup exemption or up to $75 million in a 12-month period under a separate fundraising exemption, without registering the offerings with the SEC.

The senators argued these exemptions would let crypto issuers raise money without prior SEC review, particularly the $5 million startup exemption, which would allow early-stage projects to raise money without providing financial statements.

“The proposal rationalizes the startup exemption with the idea that these offerings are from startups, and its only limitation is a $5 million offering cap. However, the exemption does nothing to actually protect investors – it simply means that the SEC is willing for investors to be defrauded in $5 million increments,” they said.

The SEC has previously said that the exemptions would require issuers to give investors written disclosures, and federal anti-fraud and anti-manipulation rules would still apply.

Cointelegraph reached out to the SEC for comment.
