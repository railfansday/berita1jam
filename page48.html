X-ELIO selects e-STORAGE to supply five BESS projects, four in Texas, U.S., and one in New South Wales, Australia, each backed by a 22-year long-term service agreement

KITCHENER, ON, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Canadian Solar Inc. (the "Company" or "Canadian Solar") (NASDAQ: CSIQ) announced today at RE+ 2026, one of North America's largest clean energy events, that e-STORAGE, its energy storage solutions business, has signed agreements with X-ELIO, a global renewable energy developer, to supply five battery energy storage projects totaling 590 MW / 1.43 GWh DC. Four of the projects are in Texas, U.S., and one is in New South Wales, Australia.

Each agreement includes a 22-year long-term service agreement (LTSA). Shipments are underway, with projects expected to reach commercial operation in 2027 and 2028. All five will use e-STORAGE's SolBank 3.0 product, built with battery cells manufactured at Canadian Solar's facilities, together with power conversion systems and plant controls.

In both markets, the systems will shift midday solar output into the evening peakâaddressing Texas's tightest supply hours after sunset and supporting coal retirement goals in New South Wales. The result is dispatchable power that strengthens grid reliability, helping operators balance supply and demand and reducing reliance on fossil-fuel peaking plants.

Jeff Roy, President of e-STORAGE, said: "We are excited to announce the start of our global partnership with X-ELIO. They are truly a leader in renewable energy development with a strong track record over the last twenty years. Having them place their trust in e-STORAGE's products and team for the next 22 years is a positive reflection of our ability to successfully deliver projects across multiple regions with varying complexities."

Where To Play Every Game Of The Year Nominee For 2026

📅 Nov 18, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The votes have been cast and the nominees for 2026's Game of the Year have been revealed. Six of the past year's biggest and best games, and as always, the games that have and haven't made the cut are being hotly debated. There's also a remake in the mix this year along with a game that hadn't been released when the nominations were announced, so a lot for those who think the awards should have clearer criteria to discuss.

Whether you agree with all six nominees or not, it's hard to argue that the games in the running this year aren't six of the six biggest games released this calendar year. The games that, if you haven't played anything else this year, or if there are some you missed, you should probably try your best to play before we get to 2027 and another year of new games begins. Whether you're wondering whether you have the right hardware before buying the nominees, want to know if they have a physical release, or you just want to know how much they cost, you'll find everything you need to know to fill in your GOTY gaps below.

GTA 6 is finally here, and its Game of the Year nomination has caused a stir. The game will be here by the time the awards are handed out, but it was nominated two days before release. That doesn't matter to you right now, though. The launch of GTA 6 is mere hours away as I type this, and if you're not one of the 15 million people who have placed a pre-order, here's what you need to know.

As long as you ignore the state of rent, wages, and world economics, GTA 6's price tag isn't that bad, you guys!

GTA 6 will only be available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S when released. Its PC launch will follow at a later, still unrevealed, time. There are two editions to choose from - standard and ultimate. The standard edition will provide tens, if not hundreds, of hours of entertainment, but there will be various stores, barbers, and tattoo parlors that are off-limits if you don't own the ultimate edition.

The standard edition of GTA 6 is $79.99 on both PS5 and Series X|S, while the ultimate edition is $99.99 on both platforms.

GTA 6 isn't the only game that has caused a stir by picking up a GOTY nomination this year. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has poked the beehive that is remakes being eligible to win the prestigious award. If there was ever a remake that was going to qualify, it's a universally loved one like this that somehow reimagines one of the greatest games of all time without upsetting any of the original's fans.

There is only one place you can play the Ocarina of Time remake, and that's on Switch 2. There are no different editions to choose from, but there is a physical release, and since this is a Switch 2 exclusive, there's a price difference between its digital and physical versions. A digital copy of Ocarina of Time costs $59.99, but if you want a physical copy, it'll cost you $69.99.

Robert Pattinson might be the next James Bond on the big screen, but Patrick Gibson became the video game Bond this year in the critically acclaimed 007 First Light. A performance so good that not only were fans suggesting Gibson could potentially make the jump to Bond movies, but the odds of that happening spiked significantly after the game was released.

First Light is the first nominee on the list that's available to play on Steam. It's also on PS5 and Series X|S, and while a Switch 2 version was promised, it has been delayed until March 18, 2027. I have played a little bit of the game on Switch 2, and for what it's worth, I didn't encounter any issues and was a little surprised that its release was moved into 2027.

It's amazing to see just how far this series has come since the low point that was Resident Evil 6. The trilogy that has followed, alongside the remakes of games from its heyday, has put Resident Evil firmly back on top of the video game mountain. That's why Resident Evil Requiem, a game many would describe as the best new Resi game in decades, is in the running to be crowned Game of the Year.

If you're trying to play all of these games before a winner is crowned, or you want to get one under your belt quickly, Requiem is the place to start. By far the shortest nominee on this year's GOTY list, with a story that takes around 11 hours to play through according to How Long To Beat. It's also the only one of all six nominees that's available on all major platforms, with those who played on Switch 2 awarding it, and Capcom's other day-one Switch 2 releases this year, high praise.

Phantom Blade Zero is a game people have been waiting a long time for. Shown off for the first time well over three years ago, S-Game's action-packed epic finally arrived in October, and it did not disappoint. Rave reviews and a player base that wholeheartedly agrees with what the critics had to say about it have landed it among 2026's Game of the Year nominees.

Your platform options are limited for this one - just PS5 for console players, but it is on PC too if you prefer to play there. There's no physical release either, which is unfortunate, although a physical edition of the PS5 version has been promised for 2027. Hopefully there aren't any issues getting that out on time, with PlayStation pushing forward with its promise to stop putting games on discs in 2028.

Control Resonant might be the most surprising inclusion among this year's Game of the Year nominees. Not because it doesn't deserve to be on this list, but because it's the only game of the six where the combined reaction to its release wasn't overwhelmingly positive, unlike the other five. Not that many people had anything negative to say about it, just that it didn't seem to blow everyone away quite as much as its GOTY competitors.

Now that said, if you want to play the best games 2026 has to offer, Control Resonant should unquestionably be on your list, particularly if you liked the first game and you're an Alan Wake fan. It's available on PS5, Xbox, and PC, there's a physical version if you're into that kind of thing, and it's slightly more expensive and includes an extra outfit, resources, and a digital artbook and soundtrack.

Steam has incredible sales all year round, but nothing quite compares to the deals you can get during the holiday season!

XFollowing a backlash from casters and esports fans, Embark Studios has taken down a recap video from a recent esports tournament for The Finals that replaced the original casters' voices with AI.

The commentators for the APAC Closed Qualifiers for The Grand Major, the annual championship tournament for Embark's team-based FPS, were human. It did not go over well. "After 4 cycles, open/closed quals, the time, effort, passion and emotion myself @TheeHyperactive and @bluepanda_casts put in," The Finals caster ProtoANZ wrote on X. translucent Y2K electronics inspire iridescent skin of cadillac’s F1 race car

📅 Nov 18, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

cadillac wraps its MAC-26 in iridescent circuitry graphics inspired by translucent electronics for the 2026 las vegas grand prix.

The Cadillac Formula 1 Team has unveiled a translucent-look livery for its MAC-26 race car ahead of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix. Inspired by the illuminated streets of the Las Vegas Strip, the design covers sections of the car’s familiar black-and-white bodywork with a web of iridescent circuitry. Pink and violet lines spread across the nose and sidepods to give the impression that the car’s electronic components are glowing beneath a transparent shell.

The effect is created through layered graphics applied to the car’s bodywork. From certain angles, the colored lines appear suspended beneath the surface, while soft washes of blue and purple give the finish a sense of depth. The Cadillac team will debut the design during its final American race of the season, when the cars take to the Strip Circuit under the city’s lights.

The livery of the Cadillac F1 race car brings to mind the translucent consumer electronics of the late 1990s and early 2000s, when computers and gaming accessories arrived with colorful plastic casings that revealed their internal workings. The team at Cadillac takes a similar visual approach, with fine lines resembling a circuit board running across a glassy, iridescent surface. The familiar mechanical silhouette of the Formula 1 car becomes the canvas for a graphic treatment that suggests something happening beneath its outer skin.

The design becomes especially intricate around the cockpit and engine cover, where branching lines overlap in shades of electric pink and blue. The front wing carries a softer version of the same pattern. Against the surrounding black carbon-fiber elements and white bodywork, the colorful detailing gives the MAC-26 an almost computer-generated appearance.

Beyond its Las Vegas inspiration, the circuitry serves as a visual reference to the work involved in establishing Cadillac’s Formula 1 operation. The team describes the livery as a tribute to the people and engineering efforts behind its debut season, which has seen an entirely new American team enter the championship. The intersecting lines suggest the complex network of systems required to bring a Formula 1 car to the grid.

This idea comes through most clearly in the close-up renderings, where the colorful pathways appear to run through the body of the car itself. The graphics play with transparency while preserving the streamlined form and aerodynamic components of the MAC-26. The effect is particularly striking across the broad white surfaces, where the circuitry stands out against the darker mechanical elements.

The Las Vegas design completes a series of special liveries introduced throughout Cadillac’s inaugural Formula 1 season. The team first revealed its asymmetrical black-and-white identity during a Super Bowl commercial in February 2026, before introducing an American-inspired design in Miami. A red, white, and blue edition followed at Silverstone for the Fourth of July, while Austin brought another variation on the team’s American identity.

For its fourth and final special livery of the year, Cadillac turns to the lights of Las Vegas and the technology behind its new racing operation. Drivers Sergio Pérez and Valtteri Bottas will bring the design to the Las Vegas Grand Prix on November 21, where the circuitry will be seen in motion against the illuminated city skyline. After the race, the cars will return to their regular black-and-white livery for the remaining rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

Renovation work continues on the Marriner S. Eccles Federal Reserve Board Building, the main offices of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System on December 9, 2025 in Washington, DC. (Andrew Harnik/Getty Images)

Federal Reserve officials concluded in October that additional rate hikes would be warranted to bring inflation back to 2%, according to minutes the central bank released Wednesday.

Nearly all participants at the Oct. 27-28 meeting concluded that, based on their baseline forecasts for prices and employment, lifting the federal funds target range "would likely be appropriate." A couple of participants said an increase of more than 25 basis points could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were unfavorable.

The Federal Open Market Committee voted 10-2 to hold the benchmark rate at 3.75% to 4%. Fed Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented, preferring a quarter-point increase. The next meeting is Dec. 8-9.

Many participants viewed the AI buildout as "a material source of inflation risk." Some said the investment surge could raise aggregate demand relative to supply if capacity expansion proved slower than expected. Many said demand for AI-related products was straining supply and raising prices of technology-related consumer goods.

The minutes also show concern about AI financing. Fed staff highlighted rapid growth in AI-related debt and said that leasing obligations kept off balance sheets, along with circular funding deals among AI-linked companies, could magnify losses if AI investments failed to deliver the expected returns.

Most participants noted a rapid rise in AI-related borrowing, much of it arranged through intricate, hard-to-see financing structures. A few said risk tied to AI was increasingly clustered in a handful of companies. Almost all participants commented on the risks of a scenario in which AI returns disappointed investors.

Staff estimated that total inflation, measured by the 12-month change in the personal consumption expenditures price index, rose to 3.6% in September. Core inflation edged up to 3.1%.

Fed officials have voiced differing views on how fast to raise interest rates. Fed Governor Christopher Waller told CNBC on Wednesday that the Fed should consider a half-point increase in December and may have to "shock" people.

New York Fed President John Williams said Tuesday, “I don’t think the situation calls for dramatic action,” according to CNBC. St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem said Tuesday the economy “will likely require several interest rate increases before the spring of 2027.”

<a href="https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/unitedhealthcare-unveils-new-care-model-leans-community-pharmacies" hreflang="en">UnitedHealthcare's new care model leans on community pharmacies</a>

📅 2026-11-18  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →
UnitedHealthcare's new care model leans on community pharmacies" style="max-width:100%; height:auto; border-radius:8px;" loading="lazy">

UnitedHealthcare is teaming up with CPSEN for a new program that will lean on community pharmacists to reach Medicaid patients.

The insurance giant said Wednesday that its through the UHC Pharmacy Care Hub model, pharmacists will work alongside the payer's care managers and providers to help close gaps in care and support management for chronic conditions and other needs.

Participating pharmacies will offer a range of services, including telehealth, medication management, point-of-care testing and personalized supports. The program is built on a hub-and-spoke model, where pharmacists can then help lead the patient to the right site of care or through any follow-up if needed.

These interactions can also help track patterns in emergency care use, and help patients find a more appropriate setting if their needs aren't emergent.

The program is currently available to UnitedHealthcare Medicaid members in seven states: Florida, Kansas, Michigan, Missouri, North Carolina, Tennessee and Virginia. UHC expects to add additional states throughout the year.

Part of the goal, the company said, is to tap into the trust that local pharmacists have in the communities they serve.

“Community pharmacies are trusted, local access points for care,” said Arethusa Kirk, M.D., chief medical officer for UnitedHealthcare Community & State, in the announcement. “This new model expands the services independent pharmacists can help deliver, making it easier for people—especially those facing barriers to care—to get timely support closer to home.”

UHC said that most (89%) of Americans live within five miles of a pharmacy, making these locations an easier option to access for many. This is especially true for individuals who may struggle to find an appointment with a doctor or find transportation to a visit.

For example, a participating pharmacist in North Carolina, Madeline Clark, said in the announcement that a patient came into the pharmacy for a urine test, as she had a potential urinary tract infection. The pharmacy conducted the test and sent the results virtually to a provider through UnitedHealthcare's Doctor Chat tool.

A prescription was then filled for the patient in the same visit, and she was out the door within about 30 minutes.

Clark said the patient was thankful not just for the ease of that trip to the pharmacy but for avoiding a visit to the ER as well.

"Not only was this a lower cost of care in the healthcare system space, but it also was just incredibly helpful for the patient," she said.
