10 Deadliest Weapons in Transformers History

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Health  |  Read Source →

Across its many continuities, the Transformers franchise features an extensive and varied arsenal of weaponry shaped by the conflicts between its two major warring factions. These weapons serve purposes ranging from strengthening individual warriors in battle to threatening entire civilizations, while some carry historical significance through their origins and connections to Cybertronian history.

Their reputations across the series’ many interpretations are shaped not only by destructive force, but also by the circumstances surrounding their creation, ownership, and use in battle. Some are tied to ancient Transformers lore, while others emerge from later technological advancements and the demands of continuing warfare across competing forces.

The Underbase is an immense repository containing the accumulated knowledge of the entire Cybertronian race, preserved as patterns of light within Marvel’s Generation One continuity. Its contents also possess considerable destructive power, and as a result, long before the main events, Optimus Prime sent it into space to deny Megatron access to its dangerous capabilities.

That threat, however, resurfaced millions of years later when the Underbase approached Earth, drawing rival Decepticon groups into a race to secure it. Starscream secretly pursued the archive for himself, absorbing part of its power and massacring most of the unbonded Autobots and Decepticons before its full force ultimately obliterated him.

Among Megatron’s most recognizable pieces of equipment is the Fusion Cannon, a powerful arm-mounted weapon that has accompanied the Decepticon leader across numerous Transformers continuities. In Generation One, its technical profile describes a particle-beam system capable of converting small quantities of matter into enormous amounts of explosive nuclear energy to power its attacks.

The weapon can discharge energy across distances reaching twelve miles, with enough force to level a small town in a single blast. His specifications also describe an interdimensional link to a black hole, allowing the cannon to draw antimatter at considerable cost to Megatron’s own systems during even one discharge.

The Spark Extractor is a fearsome Decepticon weapon featured in Transformers Prime, designed to remove a Cybertronian’s spark from its mechanical body. It can attack every Transformer within its operational radius simultaneously, draining the life force that sustains them and leaving their mechanical bodies lifeless within moments, without even requiring direct contact.

After the Autobots recovered it, Smokescreen carried the relic into their battle for the Omega Lock on Cybertron. He dropped it among Megatron’s Vehicons before phasing away, killing the surrounding troops and forcing Megatron to destroy the weapon with his Dark Star Saber to prevent its field from reaching him.

The Cybonic Plague is a Decepticon-engineered virus from Transformers Prime, developed under Megatron’s biological warfare program and responsible for millions of Cybertronian deaths during the war. Spread through contact with infected Energon, it gradually contaminates a Cybertronian’s fuel supply and can cause fatal system failure if a cure is not administered.

Its danger resurfaced when Optimus Prime contracted the dormant infection while investigating a centuries-old Autobot vessel that had crashed on Earth. With Ratchet unable to find a remedy, Bumblebee infiltrated Megatron’s subconscious through a Cortical Psychic Patch and recovered the formula from the Decepticon leader’s memories before Optimus’s condition became irreversible.

The Staff of Merlin is an ancient Cybertronian artifact that originated with Quintessa, who intended to use its power to restore her homeworld. Its ability to drain Unicron’s life energy made it central to her plan for restoring Cybertron, placing Earth in danger once its identity as Unicron became known.

After Quintessa brainwashed Optimus Prime, she ordered him to recover the artifact and bring it to Cybertron, where its power could extract Earth’s life energy. Megatron eventually seized the staff and delivered it to her, allowing the process to begin through the Knights’ submerged ship while the Autobots fought to stop her.

The Requiem Blaster is one of the formidable Mini-Con weapons in Transformers Armada, assembled from three members of the Space Mini-Con Team. Astroscope, Payload, and Sky Blast combine to form its cannon configuration, with Astroscope’s transdimensional research enabling it to draw energy from cosmic phenomena, including supernovas, quasars, and black holes.

Sky Blast summoned Astroscope and Payload with a song, awakening the trio’s combined form as the Autobots struggled against Tidal Wave. Optimus Prime then wielded the completed weapon, using its immense output to drive the Decepticon giant back and protect his allies during the desperate coastal battle for the Autobots’ survival.

The Star Saber is another powerful Mini-Con weapon featured in Transformers Armada, formed when Jetstorm, Runway, and Sonar combine their bodies into a single sword. Its power enhances whoever wields it with immense strength and destructive energy, making its three components coveted prizes throughout the war between the Autobots and Decepticons on Earth.

The sword’s reputation predates the modern conflict, as an ancient civilization collapsed after its people fought over possession of the assembled weapon. On Earth, Hot Shot became its first prominent wielder, using its enhanced power to overwhelm Decepticon opponents before the weapon passed into enemy hands through Sideways’s calculated betrayal.

Also in the Armada continuity, the Hydra Cannon is a colossal Mini-Con superweapon developed by the Decepticons during their campaign to leave Earth. Its firing system combines the Star Saber, Skyboom Shield, and Requiem Blaster, three legendary weapons formed by nine individual Mini-Cons, channeling their collective energy through one devastating discharge.

The Decepticons installed the weapon aboard a lunar warship, constructed by enslaved Mini-Cons under Megatron’s command and intended to transport his forces toward Cybertron after their time on Earth. During its first test, the cannon obliterated an approaching comet in seconds, prompting Megatron to order Starscream to target the Autobot base.

The Planet Buster is a moon-sized heat-ray cannon deployed by the Vok in Beast Wars, concealed within Earth’s second moon until the aliens judged their experiment compromised. Designed to ignite the Energon deposits scattered beneath the surface, its beam would trigger a chain reaction capable of tearing the entire planet apart.

The Vok channelled power from an alien installation on Earth, and the firing beam began disturbing the orbiting stasis pods as it charged. Optimus Primal commandeered a modified stasis pod, rigged its transwarp cell with explosives, and piloted it into the Planet Buster's core, sacrificing himself when Megatron’s sabotage prevented his escape.

The Star Harvester is an enormous machine featured in Revenge of the Fallen. It was built by the original Primes to convert a star’s energy into Energon for the AllSpark and their people. Its use was forbidden near inhabited worlds, since draining their suns would also endanger every civilization dependent upon those solar systems and nearby planets.

After the machine was constructed beneath ancient Egypt, the Fallen attempted to violate the Primes’ agreement by activating it to harvest Earth’s sun. His six fellow Primes confronted him, reclaimed the Matrix of Leadership, and sacrificed themselves to hide it inside a tomb built from their own bodies for millennia.

8 DC Heroes Who Would Be Better Green Lanterns Than Fans Expect

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The Green Lantern Ring is one of the universe’s most powerful weapons, and the Guardians choose their champions based on their ability to overcome great fear. While iconic Green Lanterns such as Hal Jordan and John Stewart are worthy wielders of the ring, the allure of wondering how other heroes would fare is far too great.

The DC universe is full of heroes with the right psychological profiles and traits to make fantastic Green Lanterns. Willpower isn’t in short supply in DC, but some heroes are so fearless, mentally resilient, and willful that they could be even better Green Lanterns than fans realise right away.

This article spotlights eight heroes who would make better Green Lanterns than fans might expect. From street-level heroes to some of DC’s heaviest hitters, these unsung figures have exactly the traits needed to master the ring. They are already formidable, but a Green Lantern ring would unlock an entirely new layer of their potential.

Batman uses fear to keep criminals in check, which makes him a strong candidate for a Yellow Lantern ring, but fans may also not expect the Dark Knight to become a powerful Green Lantern. The animated series The Batman (2004) shows how effective he would be with the ring.

In the series, after Sinestro attacks him, Hal Jordan sends his ring to Gotham City to find Batman. Although the ring initially falls into the Penguin’s hands, it eventually reaches Batman. With the ring on a low charge, Batman fights and defeats Sinestro, showing he is adept at using the Green Lantern ring.

Overall, Batman is one of DC’s best strategists, a master of all kinds of combat, and an extremely disciplined crime fighter. With a lantern ring, Batman can put his strategic mind to good use, turning the ring into the ultimate tactical weapon. When it comes to sheer willpower, very few heroes can match the Caped Crusader.

The Green Lantern Corps has never lacked power, but it has often lacked wisdom at the top. The Guardians have repeatedly made poor decisions with potentially devastating consequences, including creating the Manhunters. As a result, Diana of Themyscira would not only be a powerful addition to the Corps, but also a great leader.

Diana has commanded Amazon armies, anchored the Justice League through its darkest crises, and served as an ambassador between gods and mortals. She is as wise as she is powerful, and her skills would be best used leading the Green Lantern Corps as a member.

Beyond her outstanding leadership and strategic mind, she is also a fierce warrior and a compassionate hero. Diana is a proud Amazon warrior, and bravery and willpower abound on Themyscira. With a Green Lantern ring, she transforms from a hero who can hold her own against Superman into one of the most powerful Green Lanterns.

John Constantine is nothing if not creative. His unprecedented ability to think on his feet and battle powerful otherworldly threats makes him a top-tier Green Lantern. Constantine’s entire career is defined by staring down literal demons, hellish overlords, and cosmic horrors while armed with nothing but raw nerve. It’s not that he’s fearless; he’s just immensely courageous.

His occult expertise could become twice as dangerous when combined with a Lantern ring. His mind could generate ethereal binding runes, hard-light sigils, and metaphysical cages. He could also create devastating constructs of powerful demons and otherworldly beings, crafting intricate esoteric constructs designed specifically to exploit his opponent’s psychological and mystical weaknesses.

Giving a Speedster a Green Lantern ring basically makes them unstoppable. That is even more true when the Speedster is the Flash. Whether it is Barry Allen or Wally West, the Speed Force demands incredible mental fortitude to wield properly. The Flash sees most things in slow motion, giving him plenty of time to create ideal constructs.

Pairing the Green Lantern ring’s ability to operate at the speed of thought with the Flash’s faster-than-light speed thinking means a Flash with a ring could generate millions of unique hard-light constructs instantly. The Flash would have ample time to create constructs that defy the imagination before his enemies can process what is happening.

A Green Lantern ring is limited only by its wielder’s imagination, which makes Jaime Reyes a perfect candidate. The Blue Beetle scarab works much like the Green Lantern ring: it reads intent and creates weapons the user can imagine. Giving Jaime a ring would not only be a power-up, but it’s right in his wheelhouse.

Furthermore, Jaime’s life is defined by constantly resisting the scarab’s lethal programming and standing his ground against cosmic threats. Overcoming the fear of losing control of his body shows immense willpower, but his real advantage is his bond with extraterrestrial technology, which would give his constructs an unimaginable edge.

Instead of generating static shields or basic blades, his mind would produce dynamic, evolving weaponry that mirrors Reach tech. His constructs would instantly adapt to target vulnerabilities, morphing mid-battle, making him a deadly combination of human creativity and machine logic.

When fans think of Raven, they immediately picture shadow magic and the dark, demonic influence of her father, Trigon. However, her entire life has been a constant exercise in mental discipline and emotional control. Holding back absolute evil from consuming her soul takes astonishing willpower, proving her mind is stronger than most people imagine.

Her unique psychological training would give her constructs an unprecedented edge. Coupled with her mastery of magic, Raven would make a uniquely terrifying Green Lantern, able to turn nightmares into reality through powerful constructs. Her intense focus, sharpened by years of keeping inner turmoil at bay, would produce constructs of rare density and absolute stability.

Mister Miracle’s whole existence is defined by resisting the overwhelming, soul-crushing terror of Darkseid and the pits of Apokolips. With impeccable escape artistry and unshakable willpower, he did the unthinkable and escaped Darkseid’s prison and grasp. For Scott Free, surrendering to fear was never an option; his unbreakable determination to break every chain makes him a natural fit for willpower.

Moreover, a Green Lantern ring lets the user turn raw imagination into hard-light constructs, and Scott’s mind is uniquely suited to construct creation. Having mastered every escape technique in the cosmos, Mister Miracle has a creative mind that would lend itself to devastating, intricate constructs.

While Superman is a Symbol of Hope in the DC universe, he also has incredible willpower and is an ideal candidate for a Green Lantern ring. The core requirement of a Green Lantern is not just possessing willpower, but the ability to overcome great fear, and Superman has this in abundance.

The Man of Steel may have unimaginable power; he routinely faces beings strong enough to kill him, such as Darkseid and Doomsday. He never backs away from a confrontation when the world is in danger. Moreover, with a super-genius-level intellect and super-speed cognition, he can create highly detailed and creative constructs.

Furthermore, willpower powers the ring and determines how solid the constructs are. Superman’s willpower and mental discipline, sharpened through years of controlling his immense powers, would make him an incredibly powerful Green Lantern. Earth Green Lanterns usually protect Sector 2814, but Superman already does that and more every day.

‘The Invisible Guest’ Remake Director Danial Rifki on Suharto-Era Fear and Why His New Thriller Is About ‘How to Fight Back’

Indonesian director Danial Rifki’s remake of Spanish thriller “The Invisible Guest,” which he says was completed in 2023 before exclusivity clauses with HBO held up its release, finally reached the big screen at Jakarta World Cinema.

It is one of three Rifki films at the festival.

The Falcon Pictures production, which reworks Oriol Paulo’s 2016 hit “Contratiempo” for an Indonesian setting, stars Reza Rahadian, Mawar de Jongh and Arifin Putra. It debuted on HBO Max on Aug. 7. Rifki tells Variety that the matter has only recently been resolved.

He also has two new features at the festival: romantic comedy “Tua Tua Keladi” and thriller “Sandal Jepit No. 10.”

Set among the older residents of a small village, “Tua Tua Keladi” follows the chaos that breaks out when a chemical leak into the village water supply stirs up desire. Unspoken feelings, long-held confessions and passion come out overnight.

“I was thinking, if they have young energy to live the second life, [it is] the second chance to love,” Rifki says.

He wanted the film to challenge a deep-rooted assumption that older people no longer live with energy. For all the awkwardness of its premise, the film keeps away from eroticism, even with its younger characters.

In the digital era, Rifki says, people can record their sexual behavior openly – “and sometimes that kind of freaks me out because I have a daughter. I’m concerned if my daughter [is exposed to] wild media.”

“So with the young characters, I try to approach them with something pure, not physical,” he says.

“Sandal Jepit No. 10” is a confined-space thriller about the brutal police interrogation of Akbar, a grieving father detained on suspicion of murder.

“Indonesians have trauma in politics. Life back in the days of Orde Baru – the New Order in the Suharto era – for around 32 years, we could not criticize the government,” Rifki says. “Now we have the Reformation Era, but it might be happening underground. On social media, if you criticize the government, they will shadowban [your comment].”

“We are the low class of society with this kind of fear that, if we do something, someone with power will oppress us. So the movie is about how to fight back,” he says.

“If you have social media, netizens are here to help you out. People power now is turning [to] a digital era so we can gain that power again. I’m still sure that [there are] many good people,” he adds.

Rifki says the film grew out of his concern over abuse of power in Indonesia today.

“[When I was] a film student, I really wanted to try how to speak in rom-com, thriller, drama, and other genres. [I’m] an evolving and growing filmmaker still in the making,” he says.

The backbone of all his work, he says, is human moral conflict, whatever the genre. He is also keen to try action and horror.

His next film, “Before Thirty,” is set for release in January 2027. Rifki calls it a “survival drama” about a 29-year-old man whose life is failing.

“In Indonesia, people always ask: what is your achievement before 30? Young people are punished by that value that you have to be successful before the age of 30,” Rifki says.

The film, he says, is his “commentary on that issue.”
