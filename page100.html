Are Young Men the New Kings of the Swing Vote?

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Two years ago, it seemed that the young men of Gen Z were shaping up to be hardcore conservatives. More than half of them voted for Donald Trump’s second presidency, refuting the idea that young people tend to lean progressive. Liberal media panicked about finding “a Joe Rogan of the left” who could reach them, and conservatives took a victory lap. “Gen Z Has Turned Right!” Fox News’s Greg Gutfeld announced, citing a report from the Harvard Kennedy School that highlighted, among other insights, the ways social media has led to an increase in conservatism among young folks. A Turning Point USA Poll from around the same time declared Generation Z “the most conservative generation in decades.”

On Tuesday night, though, as a blue tidal wave flipped states from Iowa to Texas, it was young men who comprised a critical segment of the new Democratic coalition. As reported by CIRCLE, a non-partisan research organization at Tufts University, white men between the ages of 18 to 29 voted blue by a 10-point margin; in 2024, exit polling indicated that 56% of all young men voted for Donald Trump. Even more remarkably, young people across the board voted for Democratic congressional candidates by a 40% margin, according to data from NBC.

“In the wake of 2024, there was this whole catastrophizing panic,” says Aidan Kohn-Murphy, the founder of Gen Z for Change (formerly known as TikTok for Biden) and a prominent political influencer. “Oh my God, everything we thought about Gen Z is wrong. It's actually a conservative generation. And no, that's not what was happening. I mean, a huge part of Gen Z just didn't show up to vote because they didn't feel like they had a reason.”

For Democratic strategist Eric Stern, connecting with men on an emotional level was critical in getting them out to vote. Stern has worked on Ruben Gallego and John Fetterman’s campaigns; his political ad agency, FIGHT, has produced spots for Mayor Zohran Mamdani, Senator-Elect Abdul El-Sayed, and Governor-Elect Gina Hinojosa, to name a few. This election cycle, Stern sought to change voters’ perception of what a Democrat can look like.

“Our most notable ad of the general election was Bob [Brooks] standing in front of a literal dumpster fire and then putting it out because he's a firefighter,” he said. Brooks, now the Representative-elect in the Lehigh Valley, flipped his seat on Tuesday. “A ton of Latino men are really into boxing and MMA fighting, and so Ruben [Gallego] held events around boxing,” Stern told me. For Texas’ Hinojosa, who'll become the state’s first Democratic governor in 35 years when she's sworn in in January, that meant running an ad during Mexican boxing champion Canelo Álvarez's match against Christian Mbilli, an event with a massive Latino viewing audience.

Still, Kohn-Murphy and Stern both argued that the main issue on the ballot this cycle was affordability. “If you look at polling, far and away the top issue for voters is cost of living,” Stern said. “And so you can run a million ads saying that ‘Jane Doe is for they/them,’ but it's not going to resonate with people in the same way if they can't afford to keep their lights on.”

When I asked Republican political consultant Kirsten Kukowski about young men voting blue, she brought up affordability too. After all, nearly nine months into the Iran War, a barrel of oil still costs $100. “Men and young men voted with their bank accounts,” she offered. “And for the last several years, that's how they've been voting. And I think that's how Donald Trump won that group two years ago, because he was talking about affordability and inflation.”

Kukowski, the former Communications Director for the Republican National Committee, now runs her own firm, K2 and Company, out of Minneapolis. “At some point here, people expect results,” she continued. “People expected results from Donald Trump, and they didn't see that. So now we're on the clock again for another two years.”

Met Gala 2027: Costume Institute Announces New Theme After John Galliano Controversy

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

The 2027 Met Gala has a new focus. On November 5, Vogue, alongside the Metropolitan Museum of Art Costume Institute, announced its new spring 2027 exhibition and Met Gala theme. And its new focus is on the “extraORDINARY: Fashion and the Poetics of the Everyday,” which will be curated by Andrew Bolton alongside Stephanie Kramer and Emily Mushaben. This pivot comes after designer John Galliano pulled out of the 2027 Met Gala months after political and civic leaders raised concerns over his previous racist and antisemitic comments that led to his termination as Dior’s creative director in 2011.

The exhibition will be “devoted to the undersung beauty and allure of quotidian garments—think the aforementioned t-shirts and jeans, plus raincoats, sweatshirts, puffer jackets, and more. It will ask visitors ‘to see the everyday anew, and come at its subject from two perspectives: First, exploring how designers have reinvented and elevated archetypal garments to runway-worthy creations and then, secondly, highlight how humble, even ‘disposable’ materials—like paper, aluminum, and plastic—are transformed into wearable art.”

“It was really important to come up with an exhibition that wasn’t just a masterwork show or something that we could do a little bit more easily,” Bolton also said. “That was really important to me: to come up with an idea that wasn’t a substitute. And it had to have some sort of contemporary resonance.”

Before this switch, the museum stated that the original exhibit, John Galliano: Horizons, was meant to “consider how artistic achievement should be understood alongside ethical accountability.” So now, this exploration of the extraORDINARY seems to be more focused on design and creation from a more sustainable viewpoint.

So now that fashion’s biggest night is back on track with a new theme in hand, it will be interesting to see how all our favorite celebrities interpret this new theme. The Met Gala 2027 will take place on May 3, and the exhibit will open to the public on May 9, 2027, and run through January 9, 2028.

Google’s first Wyoming data centre will run on gas with carbon capture

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The $17B Cheyenne campus leans on a 702 MW fuel cell plant, with emissions stored underground.

The entrance to Google’s office at Pancras Square in London.

Google will build its first Wyoming data centre, a $17B investment near Cheyenne. Most of its power will come from natural gas, including a 702 MW fuel cell plant from AEP and Bloom Energy, with carbon capture meant to store most emissions underground.

Google will build its first data centre in Wyoming, a long-term $17B investment in Laramie County, the company said on Thursday. Much of its power will come from natural gas, with carbon capture meant to keep most of the emissions out of the air. It follows a $15B plan for Malaysia that Google announced on Wednesday.

The main source is a 702 MW fuel cell plant from American Electric Power, which turns natural gas into electricity without burning it. AEP said in October it would spend about $4B on the Bloom Energy plant near Cheyenne. Google will buy its power under a 20-year deal, POWER Magazine reported, and the plant should be fully running by the end of 2028.

Carbon from the fuel cells will be captured and stored underground at Tallgrass Energy’s nearby Eastern Wyoming Sequestration Hub. Google says this will remove “a large majority” of the plant’s emissions, but has not given a figure. It calls the plant the largest of its kind planned so far.

Google will also buy power from gas plants run by Tallgrass and Black Hills Energy, and is studying whether carbon capture can be fitted to Tallgrass’s turbines. With Wyoming, its contracted power from plants with carbon capture reaches nearly 1 GW. Its first such deal, for a gas plant in Illinois, came in October 2025.

Google says it will pay for all the power it uses and the grid upgrades its growth requires, something US lawmakers want Big Tech to do. The data centre will be air-cooled to save water.

Water is a sore point in Cheyenne. In July, the city halted some data centre water discharges after a Meta contractor released a rare bacterium into its wastewater reuse system.

Google expects the project to bring in hundreds of millions of dollars in property taxes for the county and its schools. It and its partners are also setting up a community council, after more than 200 residents raised worries about how data centre growth is changing Cheyenne. Google announced the project two days after US voters rejected data centres in 40 of 41 local ballot measures.

Karol G Gets Record Room Entries to Her Celebration of ‘No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto’ on TikTok Live

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

The Colombian superstar's massive party featured guests including Greg González, John Summit and more.

Karol G celebrated her album No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto with a massive party in Mexico City, an exclusive event streamed on TikTok on Wednesday night (Nov. 4), where millions of fans tuned in simultaneously. The Colombian superstar performed a variety of songs during the show and featured special guests such as Greg González, the group La Obsesión and DJ John Summit, who announced the remix of her hit “BbY WOW.”

“La Bichota” achieved a historic milestone as the artist with the highest number of simultaneous viewers on a TikTok Live, reaching 4.8 million stream entries on the platform and recording 3.6 million unique viewers, according to a press release.

Held at Quarry Studios with about 2,000 fans in the venue, the performance served as a prelude to the Latin American leg of her current Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour, which kicks off with six shows in two of Mexico’s biggest stadiums: Nov. 6, 7 and 8 at Monterrey’s Estadio BBVA, and Nov. 13, 14 and 15 at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros.

The nearly hour-long celebration began at 8 p.m. local time with the songs “Maybe” and “Bebiendo lágrimas.” It followed with “Eclipse,” a piano rendition of “Final Feliz” and “Matadora.” Joined by Greg González, leader of the rock band Cigarettes After Sex on guitar, she performed “Después de Ti.”

“This is my favorite song from the entire album, so I hope you sing it really loud,” Karol G said as she introduced “Con Quién Andará?” while dancing with her fans.

Released Aug. 7 via Bichota/Interscope/Capitol, No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto debuted at No. 1 on Billboard’s Top Latin Albums chart and spent 11 weeks at the summit. It also entered the Billboard 200 top 10, peaking at No. 8.

Karol performed a total of 11 songs during the event, including “Si Lo Ven,” a mind-blowing remix of “BbY WOW” alongside DJ John Summit. The original track, recorded with Judeline and rusowsky, has spent three weeks at No. 1 on the Latin Airplay chart and six weeks atop the Billboard Global 200 and Billboard Global Excl. U.S. charts. The setlist also included a remix of “AltaFarra,” featuring Mexican band La Obsesión and DJ Maracuya.

Following her North American trek, the Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour now begins its journey in Latin America, with Mexico as one of its key stops before continuing to Europe.

Karol’s arrival in the country comes after a year of significant milestones in her career. At the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards, she took home four honors: Hot Latin Songs Artist of the Year, Female; Top Latin Albums Artist of the Year, Female; Latin Pop Song of the Year for “Coleccionando heridas,” with Marco Antonio Solís; and Fan Favorite Artist, a new category based on public voting.

These achievements add to her seven nominations at the 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards, including in general categories such as album of the year for Tropicoqueta, as well as record of the year and song of the year for “Coleccionando Heridas.”

Ask a Local: Where to Go in Nairobi According to Runner Emily Chepkor

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

"Nairobi is a city best understood in motion," says Emily Chepkor, founder of We Run Nairobi, the city's biggest and most popular run club. She's from a small town in Kenya, Kapsabet, that has produced more world-class runners per square mile than anywhere else on earth. While the country is known worldwide for its running heritage, Nairobi hadn't quite caught up to the hype until Chepkor came along.

She runs as a hobby, and is a 12-time marathoner (and two-time Boston qualifier) who felt that, though Kenya's running reputation was unmatched internationally, the runner lifestyle itself wasn't accessible to everyday Kenyans—elite athletes only. She set out to change that and bring running closer to all Nairobians, starting with a weekly run club that began with just her and three friends. It slowly grew through word of mouth and especially TikTok, into something bigger, built on a simple idea: no registration, no fees, you just show up and run alongside Emily.

That run has become a defining weekly ritual for Nairobians, drawing over 1,200 people every Saturday morning. It's now etched into the city's identity. The same way New York is known for its bagels, Nairobi is known for the We Run Nairobi neighborhood runs, a cultural landmark for locals and tourists alike.

Below, Emily tells us about some of her favorite things to do in the city she calls home.

Karura Forest is “without a doubt” the best place to run in Nairobi, if you ask Emily.

"Karura Forest, without a doubt, is the most scenic route in the city”, she says. “I run the Sigiria side, through Gate F; it's quieter, more canopied. My dog Biscuit and I do the 10-kilometer loop most Sundays, crossing little rivers every kilometer. If you want something gentler, the 5-kilometer route is just as lovely." Her Saturday club runs the same way: "We're completely pace-agnostic. First-timers run next to marathoners".

Karura is the second largest urban forest in the world after the Tijuca Forest in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, and provides the perfect place for an outdoor run.

“Kengele's is always a safe bet whatever's on, whether it's World Cup, rugby, athletics, you'll find a good crowd," says Chepkor. "What I love is how the embassies get involved: around big sporting events, they'll partner with venues across the city to host public watch parties. I'll never forget the British High Commission's watch party in Karura Forest, the day Sebastian Sawe broke the two-hour marathon barrier. The whole country was locked in, and experiencing that moment surrounded by hundreds of people felt incredibly special.”

The McMillan Memorial Library is one of her favorite places for some peace and quiet, and some history.

“The McMillan Memorial Library is a perfect little time capsule of Nairobi, architecturally and literally—it's currently being renovated by Book Bunk, founded by two Kenyan women committed to restoring our public libraries," she says. "I also love the Nairobi Gallery, home to Point Zero, the spot from which all distances in Kenya are measured.”

“Tigoni is a small town only 30 to 40 minutes from Nairobi, but it feels much farther away. The city gives way to rolling tea plantations and narrow country roads. I start at Brackenhurst with coffee from Muna Tree Cafe on the terrace, then rent a bicycle and ride through the plantations, stopping wherever the view is too good to pass.” Back at Brackenhurst, she'll spread a blanket on the lawn to read, maybe doze off, then finish with tennis or a walk through the Indigenous forest. "It's the itinerary I'm proudest to take an out-of-towner on," Chepkor says.

"Sigiria Coffee House is my default—small, intimate, and full of dogs on Sunday mornings. On tennis mornings it's Waitabit, same easy vibe." For souvenirs, she sends visitors to two spots near each other: "Spinners Web has beautiful rugs, a café, and a used-books library. Just up the road, Tribal Gallery has grander pieces—restored Swahili doors, oversized mirrors—alongside smaller suitcase-friendly finds like ceramics and jewelry."

"Fig & Olive in Tigoni is part bodega, part restaurant, with a lawn where no one blinks if you swap your chair for the grass. In the city, it's the Tin Roof Café in Karen—moving from table to grass and back, especially post-run, and the salad bar is iconic. For something dressier that still softens outdoors, Matteo's: white tablecloths, al fresco seating, and little intimate corners I'm still discovering."

The Talisman Restaurant is a Nairobi stalwart.

Scoring a place by the fireplace is a coup.

"The Talisman Restaurant is a Nairobi stalwart. Scoring a corner table by the fireplace on a chilly evening is a date-night dream. For something more stylish, Inti has skyline views in every direction—some Friday nights it turns into an intimate DJ set." For the rest of the evening, she splits the city in two: “If you're after energy, Geco, alive with Nairobi's music scene, especially on Wednesdays. For actual conversation, Cave-à-Manger has an expansive wine list and keeps the music in the background.”

"Toi Market, with one caveat: Nairobi isn't really a vintage city in the curated, boutique sense— we're much more of an open-air, flea market vibe, with thousands of stalls. Toi is sprawling, busy, and a little chaotic. You'll need patience and a willingness to dig, but if you put in the time, you'll almost always come away with something great for a fraction of what you'd pay elsewhere."

“Nai Nami is properly off the beaten track; it's a walking tour of Nairobi's CBD led by former street children. You see downtown Nairobi through the eyes of people who know it intimately, and leave with a completely different understanding of the city. The Alliance Française is an unexpected answer, but it's one of Nairobi's great cultural hubs—plays, concerts, exhibitions, most of which are free, and the original stomping ground of Sauti Sol, our biggest boy band.”

A view of the city from Nairobi National Park

"Nairobi is the only city in the world with a national park so if you’ve only got one day, make it a wildlife day. I’d go for a game drive through Nairobi National Park at sunrise, then the elephant feeding at Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, and the Giraffe Centre to finish. Afterwards, a slow lunch nearby at either Cultiva, Talisman, or Matteo's. "

"I'd highly recommend coming to a We Run Nairobi Saturday. One of my favorite traditions is that we 'brand' every first-timer with a little sticker, so everyone knows it's your first Saturday. I'll encourage people to say hello, ask where you're visiting from, and recommend a coffee spot. You're moving together with actual Nairobians, in neighborhoods where we actually live. You're not being shown the city, you get to experience it alongside the people who call it home.”

Meta now lets you have voice conversations with your Muse AI agent in real time, CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced today.

The feature has been rolled out to me, and I gave it a spin on the iOS app while writing this article. As you chat with your Muse agent, the avatar at the top of your conversation animates in time with the words that it’s saying. You can also change the tone of the voice by just asking your agent about what you want and letting it decide or by browsing through a bunch of options in the avatar settings.

How Jonathan Gannon built a Top-5 Packers defense without Micah Parsons

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

If you're reading this, you obviously know that on Aug. 28, 2025, the Green Bay Packers traded defensive tackle Kenny Clark, and first-round picks in 2026 and 2027, to the Dallas Cowboys for the services of transcendent edge-rusher Micah Parsons. And when the 2025 season began, it appeared to foster a major improvement in Jeff Hafley's defense. In the first half of the 2025 season, Green Bay's defense ranked 10th in DVOA and Parsons was on a tear. Through Week 15 of the 2025 season, he totaled 14 sacks and 79 pressures, looking every bit the force he had been with Dallas.

Then, in Week 15 against the Denver Broncos, Parsons suffered a torn ACL, and things quickly fell apart for the Pack on Parsons' side of the ball — and overall. The previously 9-3-1 Pack lost every game from then on, and their season ended abruptly with a 31-27 Wild Card playoff loss to the Chicago Bears.

There wasn't enough juice without their defensive epicenter to get things done. The Packers fell to 25th in Defensive DVOA in the second half of the season, and a primary reason was pretty clear.

EPA/play allowed: -0.048 (favorable for the defense)

Pass defense: 367 attempts, 6.29 yards per attempt allowed

Run defense: 273 attempts, 4.13 yards per carry allowed

Without Parsons on the field (469 snaps):

EPA/play allowed: +0.09 (unfavorable for the defense)

Pass defense: 230 attempts, 6.82 yards per attempt allowed

Run defense: 231 attempts, 4.18 yards per carry allowed

"We just need to get healthy," Parsons told me on September 16, three days after the Packers lost their season opener to the Minnesota Vikings, 39-22. "I think once we get healthy, and I'm back on that grass or that turf, we're going to take it to another level."

Thing is, new defensive coordinator Jonathan Gannon couldn't wait around for Parsons to get healthy. The hope was that he'd be ready by Green Bay's Week 6 game against the Cowboys, but that didn't happen. Gannon had to do the best he could with what he had. And the extent to which he's done that is one of the NFL's most impressive stories in the 2026 season.

The Packers currently rank fourth in Defensive DVOA, and Gannon's concepts have been profoundly unsettling to their opponents.

So, how is Gannon doing so well in a new environment, with players he didn't know last season? Everything is working at all three levels.

As you would expect, Gannon is blitzing at a pretty high rate (31.9% this season, 12th-highest in the NFL) to maximize pressure, but Green Bay's pressure rate of 38.7% is only 18th-highest, per SumerSports. Lukas Van Ness leads the team with five sacks and 28 total pressures. Rookie Dani Dennis-Sutton is second with four sacks, and fellow edge-rusher Brenton Cox Jr. is second with 22 pressures. This isn't a Murderer's Row in that regard, but the ways in which Gannon has used his guys up front give those guys an edge.

It's also one reason the Packers have been so effective when they don't blitz.

So, how do you generate effective pressure when your pressure rate isn't that high? In Gannon's case, he's been marvelous with his simulated pressure designs. Showing a blitz look and then having supposed blitzers drop into coverage can create mental pressure for a quarterback when you do it right, and the Packers have done it right more often than not.

Now, imagine Parsons as part of all this skullduggery.

Gannon thought that he'd have linebackers Edgerrin Cooper (season-ending ruptured Achilles tendon) and Zaire Franklin (hairline shin fracture) full-go, but things happen. Now, Isaiah McDuffie and Ty'Ron Hopper are the primary 'backers, and Hopper has been a revelation. He made NFC Secret Superstars for Week 8 after a 27-19 win over the Carolina Panthers in which he totaled one sack, four pressures, six solo tackles, three pass breakups, and an interception. Hopper became the first Packers player since Hall of Famer Charles Woodson in 2009 to record three pass breakups, a sack, and an interception in the same game.

Anytime you can put yourself in the same conversation as Charles Woodson, that's obviously a good thing.

When Gannon runs all of those sim pressures, he needs linebackers who can both threaten pressure and drop into credible coverage. Hopper certainly has it on the ball, and McDuffie has been an effective starter of late.

"Ty'Ron Hopper. I can't say enough great things about him," Matt LaFleur said after the Panthers game. "The night he had. I think, what? Sack, PBU, interception? What a great performance. I think that's something that we did see coming more so in the preseason. I can't say enough about the job that obviously [linebackers coach] Sam Siefkes does with our inside linebackers to get them ready, and I just see a lot of growth from that group. They're playing really well."

They are, and they jibe with the secondary very well.

Safety Evan Williams made Secret Superstars following Green Bay's 23-13 Week 6 win over the Cowboys — the one Micah Parsons wasn't ready for, unfortunately — and that was a game in which Williams, the 2024 fourth-rounder out of Oregon, really put his name out there nationally as one of the NFL's better players at his position(s). This season, Williams has a sack, nine pressures, 49 solo tackles, 13 stops, and two tackles for loss. In coverage, he's allowed 11 catches on 20 targets for 79 yards, one touchdown, two interceptions, four pass breakups, and an opponent passer rating of 41.5.

That's all great, but where Williams really shows up in Gannon's defense is how well he plays to all three levels of the defense. Whether he's starting at the line of scrimmage and backing up to the deep half in coverage, or running the opposite by coming down for a pressure or a run fit, or maintaining the LOS integrity from the box, there isn't much Williams can't do right now.

And when you have one of those players, it allows you to be as multiple as you'd like without the breakdowns you see on defenses where the personnel doesn't quite measure up.

"He's intelligent… I love how Matt says it, 'if you wanna play like a G, you have to prepare like a G', and he studies and when you study the way he does, he knows what's going on," Gannon said of Williams in late October. "Nothing is catching him by surprise… He's playing very well right now."

And now, with all that said… here comes No. 1.

We all know how potentially explosive LaFleur's offense can be, but it's past time to recognize that as the 5-3 Packers progress through the rest of the season, it's the defense that has an equivalent (at least) amount to say regarding how far the team will go.

That's very good news, because Jonathan Gannon has proven to be quite the "G" himself.

Will Manchester United sack Michael Carrick and who could replace him?

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Michael Carrick’s position as Manchester United head coach remains under threat, as a sack decision looms with the focus shifting to this weekend’s Premier League clash against Aston Villa.

The pressure has been mounting on Carrick following a disappointing start to the season, with his side only out of the relegation zone by virtue of a superior goal difference to Crystal Palace.

While, Tuesday night’s comeback win over Roma in the Champions League allowed Carrick to survive another day in the Old Trafford hot-seat, the former Man United midfielder will be aware that he is not out of the woods just yet.

As reported by The Athletic last week, United hierarchy held talks about Carrick’s future as head coach following the 3-0 defeat at home to Bournemouth on October 25.

Carrick had been widely expected to be sacked at that point and then again after Man Utd were beaten 2-0 by Chelsea in the Premier League, a result that sent them crashing down the table.

However, it was agreed that Carrick would oversee the Roma and Aston Villa games before his situation would be reassessed prior to the international break.

Now, even though Carrick ended a nine-game run without a victory in thrilling fashion against Roma, pressure still remains as the first half performance on Tuesday night was a disaster and United could have easily found themselves three or four goals down at half time.

Of course, the second half did prove that the players are playing for Carrick, so much so that Matheus Cunha went overboard in getting himself sent-off after turning the game on its head with two quickfire goals.

There’s an argument that Man Utd now have some much-needed momentum, but you get the feeling only a win over Villa on Sunday will be enough to keep Carrick in the job.

After all, would a draw against Villa would be enough? That question may only be answered come full-time as there is a scenario where United go into the international break in the relegation zone and therefore Carrick’s position becomes untenable.

As per reports, Manchester United's football operations leadership, chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox hold all the power with regards to Carrick’s future.

The pair were both in attendance at Stamford Bridge last weekend and at Old Trafford on Tuesday, amid reports of being involved in discussions over potential replacements.

Taking into account that Carrick held crisis talks with Berrada and Wilcox after the Bournemouth game, they would all - along with minority shareholder Sir Jim Ratcliffe - be expected to meet at Carrington following the Villa fixture.

Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann are leading contenders to replace Carrick if he was to be sacked as Man United head coach.
