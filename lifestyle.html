Verdict split in Watford vs Middlesbrough predictions as hosts show much-needed ‘grit’

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

Watford have endured a tough start to the Championship season under head coach Alessio Dionisi. They are positioned in 20th place in the table after 14 fixtures and are four points above the bottom three.

The Hornets won 1-0 at home against Lincoln City last time out which was a much needed victory. Summer signing Stephy Mavididi got their winner against the Imps in Hertfordshire.

Middlesbrough lost 2-0 away at Wrexham on Wednesday night and were without key striker Will Lankshear in North Wales due to injury. Boro missed his firepower in front of goal and slumped to a disappointing defeat at the Racecourse Ground.

Nevertheless, Boro remain top of the division as they eye promotion to the Premier League under manager Kim Hellberg in this campaign. They are one point above Wolves in 2nd and two ahead of Birmingham City, Stoke City and Swansea City.

“Watford’s win over Lincoln City last time out and eased some pressure off head coach Alessio Dionisi. They have now won their last two home games which should give them confidence ahead of this weekend.

“Despite their struggles, the Hornets do have some good individual players at their disposal like Stephy Mavididi, Iker Bravo and Othmane Maamma. They can cause problems for any opponents on their day when they are at it.

“Middlesbrough will be desperate to bounce back from their loss at Wrexham. They are a different team without Will Lankshear and if he is unavailable again on Saturday, they could be in trouble.

“Nevertheless, Boro are top for a reason and their defeat in Wales was their first one in the league since August in a run of spanning back 11 games. I think they may have to settle for a point in Hertfordshire though.”

“Watford have found a couple of important results in their last two home games. They haven’t been pretty but they’ve shown grit and got points on the board, which will matter in a battle against relegation to the Championship.

“This is a big task though, and Middlesbrough will be out for vengeance. Boro were really poor at Wrexham in the week, as they were far less efficient with their 62.2% possession than we have seen this campaign to date.

“When Kim Hellberg’s side last lost, they responded with a comprehensive 3-1 win over West Brom and went on an 11-game unbeaten run. They’ll be eager to bounce back in similar fashion, and I think they’ll be able to despite the Hornets’ improvements at home.

“I’m going for a comfortable away win as the visitors produce a strong response to their Wrexham disappointment.”

‘Catalyst’ victory can inspire Wrexham to a result in Wolves clash: Predictions

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

Wrexham beat league leaders Middlesbrough at home on Wednesday night. George Thomason and Ollie Rathbone scored two late goals for the Welsh outfit as they returned to winning ways in impressive fashion.

The Red Dragons are sat in 16th place in the Championship table after their first 14 fixtures and they have made a slow start to the season. They are only six points off the play-offs now behind West Ham in 8th position.

As for Wolves, they came from behind away at Bristol City last time out to win 2-1. Newcastle United loan man Sean Neave scored two second-half goals to earn WWFC an important three points at Ashton Gate against the Robins.

Cesar Peixoto‘s men have now risen into 2nd in the division behind Middlesbrough ahead of Birmingham City. The Midlands club were relegated from the Premier League earlier this year after they finished in the bottom three along with West Ham and Burnley.

“Wrexham’s win over Middlesbrough was much needed and has eased some pressure off Phil Parkinson. It has been a tough season for the Red Dragons so far and they will be eager to hit some form now.

“The Welsh side haven’t scored enough goals in this campaign and are lacking a cutting edge. In fact, only six teams have scored less than them in the Championship so far.

“Wolves found a way to win at Bristol City which is a good sign for a team eyeing automatic promotion. WWFC are now unbeaten in their last five games since their home defeat to Bolton Wanderers last month.

“Cesar Peixoto’s squad is littered with Premier League quality and they will fancy their chances in North Wales. However, I think it may end a high scoring draw with the hosts seemingly back on track now.”

“Wrexham produced arguably their best performance of the season to beat Middlesbrough on Wednesday night. That has to be a catalyst for Phil Parkinson if he’s to hold onto his job and if the Red Dragons are to push up the Championship table.

“Another tricky game awaits here with Wolves unbeaten in five, but another home match marks a chance for the North Welsh side to build some momentum and head into the break on a high.

“Wolves are in high spirits themselves though. They’ve responded to poor form with a five-game unbeaten run, the most recent being an impressive comeback win thanks to a Sean Neave brace. That will be a real confidence boost for the young striker as well, as he looks to step up to the senior game and deliver while Wanderers star Raul Jimenez is out.

“This is a tough one to call, as Wrexham are capable of picking up another win if they play like they did on Wednesday. However, I can’t see Wolves losing, so I’ll say this ends level.”

Why Everyone Is Wearing Suede This Fall—And the Chicest Ways to Style It

When the temperatures drop and fall dressing takes center stage, one fabric always finds its way back into our wardrobes: suede. Suede has been that fall fashion staple ever since it has been around. It brings that cozy, elevated feel to even the simplest outfits, making it the perfect texture to embrace when the seasons change.

While it’s common to see suede generally in the fall, lately it’s absolutely everywhere, and I’m here to remind you that there are so many ways you can wear this texture. From suede mini skirts paired with oversized sweaters, knee-high suede boots, and of course purses.

Suede is such a perfect fall trend because it brings a subtle touch of texture to any outfit. Shades like chocolate brown and deep burgundy make the material feel especially autumnal.

Consider this your sign to give your fall wardrobe a suede refresh. Whether you go all in with a statement skirt or keep things simple with a chic suede bag, this is one trend that makes every outfit feel a little more luxurious.
