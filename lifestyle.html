Balmain Resort 2027

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

“For me, the men’s and the women’s, they are kind of like one entity. I really want the men and women to be able to talk to each other.” Antonin Tron was riffing as we riffled the rails of his first pre-spring collections for Balmain, including his first-ever menswear collection for the house. “I don’t necessarily think of them as a couple,” he qualified: “but I think at Balmain the women are quite sexy and out there. And if I was to do something very different for men, it would be weird.”

Looking at his rails simultaneously, that relationship was manifest in substance as well as spirit: “We have a lot of overcrossing material,” Tron confirmed. The same embroidered surfaces, treated denim, and snakeskin jacquards traveled between the two wardrobes, but in womenswear their expression was differently calibrated. This was a more relaxed continuation of the luxuriously louche and wanton glamour of his last runway collection, which itself had been informed by the Californian desert. “With pre-collection I can explore a bit more the casual side of Balmain,” he said.

That meant injecting a dose of dishevelment into Balmain’s appetite for decoration. Sensuously draped dresses in snakeskin and beautiful, borderline-hallucinogenic florals came with cropped flight jackets, while satin tailoring, sheer lurex blouses, and embellished animalier were countered by washed denim and biker leathers. There were micro cardigans over exposed bras, skirts that succumbed into flowerings of asymmetric ruffle, and lavishly embellished pieces whose surfaces seemed weathered by weeks of sun exposure. What blurred in the haze of Tron’s gaze was much distinction between dressing up and down.

This dissolved tension was more than a styling proposition. Tron has little patience for the idea that luxury dressing must conform either to bourgeois standards of propriety, or fashion’s customary cringe of faux-intellectual snobbishness. “For me, this thing with sexiness is also a class thing,” he said of the physical ideals through which notions of elegance are policed. Balmain, in his view, offers a space to articulate sexuality rather than apologize for it.

He recalled meeting a client in Los Angeles while shooting the collection in Joshua Tree. When he mentioned his last show’s desert-and-hallucinogenics theme, she cheerfully volunteered that she had a house there and used to do exactly that. She was accompanied by her muscular husband. “They both wanted to be hot, and that’s it,” Tron observed. This seemed a pretty good expression of the collection’s carefully direct hedonism. Super-long leather boots and Lucite-heeled wedges provided the strut, while the tasseled Zabriskie bag offered easygoing swing.

His instinct is that luxury should enable freedom rather than impose rules. The filament that connects this new Balmain woman and the new Balmain man, in spirit as much as substance, is Tron himself. “I think slowly, I’m finding that line: I’m finding the woman and the man, basically.”

It only took 25 years but David Schwimmer has finally reunited with Ross’ iconic red sweater from Friends.

In a new campaign, titled “The Red Sweater,” for the clothing and lifestyle brand Quince, the actor finds himself chasing different characters through New York City who are all wearing his “dream sweater” — a Quince cashmere sweater in the new shade Coulis Red.

Once he’s finally reunited with the garment, another guy approaches to say, “Hey, that’s my dream sweater.” Schwimmer once again takes off running. Google Messages homepage redesign: Pinned chat avatars, bottom search, corner Gemini

📅 Nov 07, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

In addition to the new app icon, Google Messages for Android is testing a big homepage redesign that has three core components.

Update 11/7: Instead of pinned chats just appearing at the top of the list, Google Messages is trying a redesign that significantly elevates them above all conversations.

This redesign takes advantage of the existing background layer that’s currently just home to the logo/wordmark, search, and account menu.

You’ll see large contact photos appear above all chats, which pushes the list of other conversations down the page. Like iMessage on iOS, this layout has the downside of not showing any history or last sent information.

Meanwhile, Google is testing another change where search becomes a centered floating action button (FAB) alongside new chat. When you’re scrolling down the page, you’ll see “Search” in a pill-shaped button, with the other button minimized.

Otherwise, you’ll see a circular magnifying glass icon and “Start chat” in the pill. The animation that accompanies this change looks nice and feels alive, while boosting one-handed usability.

Google has replaced the Gemini FAB with a button in the top-right corner next to your profile image. This matches the design leveraged by Workspace apps on desktop and mobile.

These updates are currently in the beta channel and not yet widely rolled out to all testers. Some users have all aspects of the redesign, but it appears that Google is rolling out the various components in a piecemeal fashion.

Rashee Rice: Lamborghini Rental Company Attorney Blasts NFL Star For Filing For Bankruptcy

The attorney representing a Lamborghini rental company that claims Rashee Rice never paid them after his 2024 crash is speaking out after the NFL star filed for bankruptcy ... and he thinks Rice is "incapable" of being accountable for his actions.

Kyle Coker -- who represents The Classic Lifestyle -- tells TMZ ... "I think the only reason Rashee Rice filed for bankruptcy was to avoid the impending jury trial related to injuring others during his drag-racing escapades."

Rice caused a six-car pileup after drag-racing his leased Lamborghini in Dallas in March 2024 ... before pleading guilty to felony racing on a highway and felony collision causing serious bodily injury.

The Kansas City Chiefs wide receiver spent over $2.1 million connected to the civil lawsuits involving his 2024 Dallas car crash. Coker says Rice still hasn't paid The Classic Lifestyle after the wreck, despite not having insurance.

Rice filed for Chapter 7 bankruptcy this week, claiming he's more than $3.45 million in debt with only $677,000 in assets ... and Coker is blasting Rice's legal move.

Coker tells us ... "The Court in that case wouldn't give him the continuance he desired, and the other parties involved were ready to hold Rice accountable. Upon discovering that Rice would finally have to face the consequences of his actions, he instead rushed to inform the Court that he had filed for bankruptcy over $3 million in debt, with $10s of millions in expected earnings off his next contract."

Coker says he plans on appearing in the bankruptcy court case on behalf of TCL to ask the court to dismiss his bankruptcy and to "further award sanctions against Rice for this illogical procedural delay."

The attorney also takes issue with Rice's legal team ... who previously described him as a "young man that made a mistake" after the crash.

Coker tell us ... "I believe Rice's actions from the start, continuing through his attorney's original 'he's just a kid, give him a break' speech through Rice's subsequent legal troubles, have shown that Rice is incapable of and wholly disinterested in taking accountability for his actions."

Rice is in the final year of his rookie contract and will be a free agent after the NFL season. He's been a productive player and looks due for a raise.

It's the first Yeezy runway show since 2022.

Plenty of star power was on hand to support West — who continues to face backlash for his years of antisemitic hate speech, though he took out an ad in the Wall Street Journal to apologize in January — including Big Sean, Kid Cudi, 2 Chainz, Natalie Nunn, Sab Zada, designer Jerry Lorenzo, model India Love, his wife Bianca Censori, along with Ye’s four children with ex-wife Kim Kardashian: North, Saint, Psalm and Chicago.

The 49-year-old has a pair of shows left this year, as he’s set to take over AT&T Stadium in Dallas on Saturday (Nov. 7) before heading to Glendale, Ariz., on Nov. 21.

Met Gala 2027: Costume Institute Announces New Theme After John Galliano Controversy

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

The 2027 Met Gala has a new focus. On November 5, Vogue, alongside the Metropolitan Museum of Art Costume Institute, announced its new spring 2027 exhibition and Met Gala theme. And its new focus is on the “extraORDINARY: Fashion and the Poetics of the Everyday,” which will be curated by Andrew Bolton alongside Stephanie Kramer and Emily Mushaben. This pivot comes after designer John Galliano pulled out of the 2027 Met Gala months after political and civic leaders raised concerns over his previous racist and antisemitic comments that led to his termination as Dior’s creative director in 2011.

The exhibition will be “devoted to the undersung beauty and allure of quotidian garments—think the aforementioned t-shirts and jeans, plus raincoats, sweatshirts, puffer jackets, and more. It will ask visitors ‘to see the everyday anew, and come at its subject from two perspectives: First, exploring how designers have reinvented and elevated archetypal garments to runway-worthy creations and then, secondly, highlight how humble, even ‘disposable’ materials—like paper, aluminum, and plastic—are transformed into wearable art.”

“It was really important to come up with an exhibition that wasn’t just a masterwork show or something that we could do a little bit more easily,” Bolton also said. “That was really important to me: to come up with an idea that wasn’t a substitute. And it had to have some sort of contemporary resonance.”

Before this switch, the museum stated that the original exhibit, John Galliano: Horizons, was meant to “consider how artistic achievement should be understood alongside ethical accountability.” So now, this exploration of the extraORDINARY seems to be more focused on design and creation from a more sustainable viewpoint.

So now that fashion’s biggest night is back on track with a new theme in hand, it will be interesting to see how all our favorite celebrities interpret this new theme. The Met Gala 2027 will take place on May 3, and the exhibit will open to the public on May 9, 2027, and run through January 9, 2028.

After a 16-year long dev period, Faraway, a shooting star arcade game about siphoning the energy of other stars, is out now

📅 Nov 05, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

Lord almighty, video games take a while to make, 'ey? Especially if you're just one person working on it by yourself. Such is the case with Faraway, out today, an arcade game that reminds me of the early days of PS3 indies "about a shooting star that creates constellations to stay alive," all made single-handedly by creator of cult-classic mobile game Eliss, Steph Thirion. Did I mention that Faraway took 16 years to make? Like I said, game development is a lengthy process.

Looking at Faraway, and knowing how long it took to produce, it doesn't surprise me to come from a dev who managed to release a mobile game in the early days of the platform, long before free-to-play got its chokehold on the industry. It also really does feel like it should be sitting amongst the likes of PixelJunk, this thing should be something I'm desperate to play on my PS3 while I wait for my controller to charge.

As mentioned, you are a shooting star, a "spark meant to shine and vanish in a blink." But still you persist, connecting to and wrapping yourself around starts to "harvest their energy," in turn lengthening your own life, and of course, in arcadey fashion, racking up a nice score. It looks like game design as poetry, everything you're looking at procedurally generated to bring freshness to every run. I can fully envision this as a "one more run" kind of arcade game, the best kind, especially in an age long past the need to shove in another of your hard earned coins.

Faraway conceptually charms me because it taps into a style of game we don't get to see much of any more. What I'm saying is, I'm just a teensy bit sick of how many roguelikes there are, but that doesn't mean I don't want a game I can just hop in and out of at my own whims. Sure, arcade games might not be in vogue, I just miss them a bit. In any case, Faraway is available to pick up on Steam right now. Seems like it'll be worth a punt!
