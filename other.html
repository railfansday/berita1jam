Star Trek: Unassimilated Officially Features Jeri Ryan's Return As Seven Of Nine

📅 Nov 14, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Star Trek has one of the most eclectic rosters of sci-fi characters ever, but it's safe to say that Jeri Ryan as Seven of Nine in Voyager ranks near the top of fan-favorite crew members. Introduced as a former Borg drone trying to regain some sense of her humanity, Seven quickly became a standout, with Ryan bringing a unique mix of icy precision and dry humor to the role.

Some of Seven's best moments came in episodes like "The Raven," which looked at her traumatic past, "Someone to Watch Over Me," which showcased her awkward attempts at romance, and "Unimatrix Zero," which revisited her complicated relationship with the Borg Collective. Ryan's performance helped make Seven one of Voyager's most compelling characters, and her long-awaited comeback in Star Trek: Picard was equally exciting, eventually seeing her take command of the Enterprise-G.

Now, Jeri Ryan is making another official return to the franchise in an exciting new release, bringing her beloved character back into the spotlight once again.

Jeri Ryan is officially returning to Star Trek in Magic: The Gathering's massive new crossover, and Seven of Nine is getting one of the release's most exciting chase cards. While the character appears on several cards, the standout is undoubtedly "Seven of Nine, Unassimilated" (#1705), an ultra-rare Headliner card featuring Ryan's actual signature alongside a stunning portrait of her beloved character.

According to Wizards of the Coast, fewer than 250 copies of the Jeri Ryan-signed card exist, making it one of the crossover's most exclusive collectibles. It's one of seven different Headliner cards featuring authentic actor autographs, with each card individually hand-signed (as opposed to a print-copied signature).

For collectors hoping to find one, the signed Seven of Nine is exclusively available in Star Trek Collector Boosters, regardless of language. Individual packs are listed for roughly $40, while Collector Booster boxes contain 12 packs and vary in price. If you've got deep pockets, the box will give you more opportunities than a single pack, but the odds of finding Ryan's autograph specifically are still pretty slim.

Thankfully, there are several other Seven of Nine cards available for fans who aren't quite so lucky:

The larger Magic: The Gathering x Star Trek crossover officially launched today, November 13, celebrating the franchise's 60th anniversary with characters and stories spanning nearly every major series. Beyond the standard card collection, there are four Commander decks, collectible scene cards, special Stardates reprints, and much more.

However, Wizards has warned that certain products are experiencing shipping delays, including some Commander decks in North America and Collector Boosters in select international markets. Despite those issues, the release promises an exciting new way to celebrate Star Trek, especially for longtime Seven of Nine fans.

An error message on Amtrak's website while attempting to book a ticket on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026.
