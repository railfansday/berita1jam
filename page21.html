Cathie Wood's Ark Invest sold $6.1 million worth of Block shares and $1.13 million worth of Bullish shares through its ETFs on Monday.

Cathie Wood-led investment firm Ark Invest sold additional shares of Block Block, Inc. (XYZ), Bullish Bullish (BLSH), and its own bitcoin (BTC) exchange-traded fund on Monday, while adding to its holdings in SoFi Technologies and Nvidia.

According to Ark's latest trading disclosure, it sold 68,997 shares of Jack Dorsey's Block, worth $6.1 million, and 49,767 Bullish shares, worth $1.1 million. Block's stock rose 3.6% to close at $88.56, while Bullish gained 7% to $22.80.

Ark also offloaded $1.6 million worth of shares in its own Ark 21Shares Bitcoin ETF ARK 21Shares Bitcoin ETF (ARKB). The fund, which had $2.15 billion in net assets, recorded $9.9 million in net outflows on Monday, contributing to the $201.1 million in total net outflows across U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs.

Meanwhile, the firm purchased $6.8 million worth of SoFi Technologies shares and $16.2 million worth of Nvidia shares.

SoFi's shares closed up 3.33% at $16.75 on Monday. The fintech company reported 503,000 crypto products at the end of September, according to its latest earnings report. Its gross crypto transaction revenue rose 20% quarter over quarter to $161.6 million.

Last month, SoFi also launched SoFi Earn, an interest-bearing tokenized deposit, which it said was the first time a U.S. national bank has offered tokenized deposits to individual clients.

Nvidia fell 4.27% on Monday to $178.67 after the AI chipmaker said its board of directors is investigating CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with a woman reportedly identified by intelligence officials as a Chinese spy.

The investigation came after The New York Times reported last week that a memo, circulated among top U.S. policymakers last spring, alleged that Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman, was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to gather intelligence on the Nvidia founder.

Ark regularly rebalances its ETF weightings to keep individual stock holdings below 10% of a fund's portfolio. That means it would adjust positions as asset prices fluctuate.

‘Supergirl’ Star Milly Alcock Sets Stage Play ‘Julie’ at London’s Old Vic Theater

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

“Supergirl” star Milly Alcock has set her next project – an adaptation of August Strindberg’s play “Miss Julie” at the Old Vic theater in London.

Adapted and directed by Old Vic associate director Rebecca Frecknall, “Julie” will see Alcock in the titular role. The play is set to debut at the theater April 10 and will run through May.

The original play, which was written in Swedish in 1888, tells the story of aristocrat’s daughter Miss Julie who finds herself drawn to one of the family’s married servants. Related Stories ‘Star Trek’ 60th Anniversary Celebration Hits Rome MIA Market With Iconic Franchise at 'Crossroads’ After Paramount-Warner Bros. Merger

“Stumbling into the kitchen on the night of her 21st birthday party, Julie seeks out her father’s employees John and Christine,” reads the Old Vic logline. “As one room becomes a battlefield of class and sexual politics, a reckless flirtation becomes a merciless contest sending all three people hurtling towards destruction.”

The modern adaptation was originally produced by Internationaal Theater Amsterdam.

Alcock, who has also starred in “House of the Dragon,” has previously tread the boards in London, appearing in a 2023 production of “The Crucible” at the National Theatre.

“Julie” is the first new project Alcock has been attached to since “Supergirl” debuted earlier this year to mixed reviews and a disappointing box office.

On Monday it was announced that DC Studios CEOs and chairmen James Gunn and Peter Safran would be stepping down following Skydance’s acquisition of DC parent company Warner Bros. Discovery. They are set to be replaced by Marvel veteran Jeremy Latcham.

“Julie” was unveiled as part of a new season of programming at the Old Vic, and the first since Rupert Goold took over as artistic director earlier this year.

Other titles announced as part of the lineup are Shakespeare’s “Henry V” starring Damian Lewis in January and an adaptation of Stephen Sondheim and John Weidman’s “Assassins,” about a group of misfits attempting to assassinate the U.S. President, which Goold will direct, in June.

“I am thrilled to be presenting my first season with a collection of plays that speaks to the Old Vic’s rich history of classical theatre, European repertoire and musical revivals,” said Goold. “I hope my Old Vic will be able to draw on this theatre’s storied legacy of sensational acting in groundbreaking and accessible productions.”

William Shatner Returns To Star Trek Today In Heartfelt 60th Anniversary Tribute

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

William Shatner is officially making his Star Trek return today in an absolutely stunning new release, honoring the franchise's 60th anniversary with a heartfelt message to fans. The OG captain of the USS Enterprise helped define what Trek would become through his iconic portrayal of James T. Kirk, and has undoubtedly earned a top spot on the Mt. Rushmore of Star Trek captains.

Although the original Star Trek series lasted just three seasons, its impact on culture write large was enormous, spawning an empire that has continued to expand into television, movies, books, gaming, and beyond. Through it all, Shatner's Captain Kirk has been synonymous with the franchise, representing the adventurous spirit and optimism that made Gene Roddenberry's vision so compelling in the first place.

Now, to honor Star Trek's 60th anniversary since the show first premiered in 1966, Shatner is officially returning to celebrate that incredible legacy in Star Trek: The Original Voyages, a gorgeous new release arriving today, November 10.

WilliamShatner's latest Star Trek return is something longtime fans will appreciate just as much, if not more, than an official reprisal of his role as Kirk. The legendary actor has penned a heartfelt foreword for Star Trek: The Original Voyages: A Visual History, an absolutely gorgeous new book celebrating the franchise's 60th anniversary, officially available today, November 10.

Shatner has a unique place in Star Trek history, serving as more than just the face of The Original Series. After six decades, the actor has witnessed Star Trek grow from what was once thought would be a relatively short-lived television series into one of the most influential science fiction properties ever created. His foreword reflects on Star Trek's enduring appeal, including the deeper "philosophical" questions that have always been central to Gene Roddenberry's original vision.

In one especially poignant and heartfelt passage, Shatner explains what he believes makes Star Trek so special and important:

"The search for meaning, the search for importance, the search for what happens after we die, the continuous curiosity of mankind is reflected in Star Trek, and that, I think, is the magic of the show."

His foreword looks at the current state of society and culture and offers a look into why he believes Star Trek cuts through all of that noise and transcends borders, race, and politics. It's exactly the sort of Shatner-esque message you'd expect from the legend, and is the perfect way to honor the franchise's 60th.

That said, Shatner's foreword is only one highlight of this massive 416-page release. Written by Kyle Newman and Jeff Bond, The Original Voyages is easily one of the most comprehensive visual explorations of the original Star Trek era ever assembled, featuring hundreds of stunning photographs, rare archival materials, and must-see previously unpublished behind-the-scenes images.

Readers can expect everything from production photographs and concept artwork to detailed looks at original costumes, props, filming models, and even the creative work that brought the USS Enterprise to life. The book also explores the franchise's evolution beyond TV, including its animated series, six original-cast films, merch, comics, and gives a huge nod to the community and how it's grown over the years.

There's an extraordinary amount of material here for longtime Trekkies to explore, particularly with the inclusion of original interviews and photographs that even the most dedicated fans have likely never seen before.

You can purchase Star Trek: The Original Voyages via Amazon here.

Published by Ten Speed Press, Star Trek: The Original Voyages is available today in hardcover and digital formats. Between Shatner's personal contribution, the wealth of behind-the-scenes material, and the trove of new images, this is practically essential reading for anyone who grew up with Kirk, Spock, and the original Enterprise crew.

White does not regret actions that led to England exile

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Ben White insists fans do not know the full story of the incident that led to him making himself unavailable for England duty, adding he does not regret his actions.

White walked out of Gareth Southgate's squad due to personal reasons at the 2022 World Cup, amid suggestions he had fallen out with a member of the coaching staff.

The Arsenal defender opted out of England duty for the remainder of Southgate's tenure, only returning to the squad under Thomas Tuchel in March.

White featured in two pre-World Cup friendlies against Uruguay and Japan – being booed by many England supporters in the former game – before an injury kept him out of the final tournament.

Now 29 and with just six senior caps to his name, White may have sacrificed the best years of his international career, but in an interview with BBC Radio 5 Live, he said the details of that 2022 fall-out will remain private.

"People don't know the full story, because I have never said anything and I won't put anyone under the bus or share any of that," White said. "I think that is probably the best way."

Asked if he would act differently if a similar incident occurred now, White replied: "No, I don't think I would. Obviously, I was a bit younger, I am a bit older now.

"I think as a person I am quite real and I think I made the decision that I would probably continue to make.

"So, it's something that I am not happy about, but obviously it happened and it is time to move on. It's been a bit tricky and not straightforward, but it is amazing to be here now."

Five Gunners representing  Ben, Ezri, Ebs, Myles and Declan have received England call-ups

White's England return was an eventful one, as he was jeered after coming off the bench against Uruguay. He silenced those boos by scoring his first international goal, then conceded a stoppage-time penalty as the Three Lions were pegged back in a 1-1 draw.

Reflecting on that game, White said: "I was thinking, 'don't stand out, just take it nice and easy'. Then I came on and obviously got booed, then I scored, and then I gave away a penalty.

"So, it was obviously full of emotions that night, but it was a good night."

White is part of Tuchel's latest Three Lions squad, with games against Czechia and Spain to come in the Nations League this week – England only need one point to be assured of a quarter-final place.
