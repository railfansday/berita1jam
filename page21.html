Last month the actor pleaded not guilty to two charges of sexual assault, three charges of voyeurism and one charge of exposure at Westminster Magistrates Court.

Clarke is known for playing Mickey Smith in “Doctor Who” from 2005 to 2010. Gta 6 Won’t Launch Without This PS5 Anti-Piracy Update Sony Just Released

📅 Nov 18, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Sony drops anti-piracy update that’s necessary to play GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto 6 is the most anticipated release of the past decade, and Sony has been using it as its trump card to encourage players to keep their consoles up to date. If they don't update their console to the latest firmware version, the game simply won't run. GTA 6 is now within our grasp — just a few hours away from release — but gamers won't be able to jump in at launch unless they update their consoles right now.

Sony has been heavily targeted by hackers in the past few months due to the rise in piracy on PlayStation 5 systems. As more and more players jailbreak their consoles, the company has been pushing PS5 updates left and right in an attempt to fight this. However, not long after an update goes live, hackers are usually able to modify their software to work around it and release it to the public again rather quickly.

Sony has released one last firmware update to ensure that it can slow down internet pirates and delay attempts at jailbreaking the PS5 post-launch. The short and vague patch notes make it clear that this is the update's purpose: "We’ve made some security fixes to the system software."

On X, the official PlayStation support account reminded players that they should download the update as soon as possible if they'd like to play at midnight. It's not a threat, really, but gamers won't be able to launch Grand Theft Auto 6 unless they update their PlayStation 5 to system software version 26.07-14.23.00. It requires a download of around 1.267 GB, so it's best to get to it right away. ​​​

Rockstar has already sold millions of copies of Grand Theft Auto 6, but many more sales are almost guaranteed on launch day, so it's important for Sony to ensure that the game is protected from hackers. This is a multimillion-dollar release, and if homebrew developers are able to gain access, they might just be able to put it up on the internet for free. Many would surely be interested, especially considering GTA 6 is not going to be released on PC right away.

Saudi Arabia’s Red Sea Film Festival Forges ‘Strategic’ Partnership With Cairo Film Festival

📅 Nov 18, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Saudi Arabia’s Red Sea Film Foundation and the Cairo International Film Festival have announced what is being called a “strategic partnership” that will see the introduction of a dedicated Red Sea Film Festival award at the Cairo fest and other forms of collaboration dedicated to preserving the Arab region’s film heritage.

News of the agreement comes after the Red Sea fest — which is most prominent film event and market in the Arab world — canceled its sixth edition, which was scheduled to take place from Dec. 3 to 12. The decision was made following turbulence in Saudi Arabia due to an escalation in its conflict with Yemen’s Iran-backed Houthi rebels, who targeted several Saudi cities. Related Stories Here's How to Watch the Troy Trojans vs. South Alabama Jaguars Game Live Online MS NOW Has 2026 Midterm Elections Coverage: Here's How to Watch Election Night Results Live Online Free

The Red Sea fest has said it will return in the fourth quarter of 2027 with a “renewed vision and a program that continues to celebrate cinema and filmmakers from Saudi Arabia, the region and around the world,” according to a press release.

Starting in 2027, there will be a dedicated Red Sea Film Festival Award presented in Cairo to an outstanding film selected from that fest’s program. “The award will further strengthen ties between the two festivals while providing additional recognition for exceptional cinematic storytelling,” according to a statement.

“The agreement will also facilitate greater collaboration in film programming, creating opportunities for both festivals to showcase selected films from each other’s programmes, including productions supported by the Red Sea Fund,” the statement added. “This exchange will help broaden the reach of these films, connect filmmakers with new audiences, and encourage greater collaboration across the regional film industry.”

The Red Sea and Cairo fests partnership will also focus on film preservation and cultural exchange. The two entities will collaborate “on an exchange of restored films, with a dedicated weeklong screening program planned in Cairo following the fest’s 2027 edition,” according to the statement.

The Red Sea Film Foundation has supported the restoration of more than 20 films, including some of Egyptian cinema’s most celebrated works like Youssef Chahine’s “The Choice” (1971), “Watch Out for Zouzou” (1972) and “Love in Karnak” (1967).

“As Cairo International Film Festival marks 50 years since its founding, this partnership opens a new chapter of cooperation between two institutions committed to Arab cinema,” said Cairo fest chief Hussein Fahmy in a statement.

Commented Faisal Baltyuor, CEO of the Red Sea Film Foundation: “Egypt has played a defining role in the history of Arab cinema, and Cairo International Film Festival continues to be one of the region’s most important platforms for filmmakers and audiences alike. We are proud to strengthen our relationship through a partnership that reflects our shared commitment to supporting outstanding films, creating opportunities for filmmakers, and preserving our cinematic heritage.”

Scripts can’t run AI factories: Dell moves cluster management from the server to the rack

📅 Nov 18, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

As artificial intelligence clusters grow to supercomputing scale, the hard part of the AI factory is no longer buying hardware. It is keeping racks, switches, GPUs and cooling running once they are switched on.

That operational burden is reshaping what infrastructure providers sell. Dell Technologies Inc. has spent the past year pushing its AI Factory beyond servers and storage, and customers are now asking for best practices and better tools to stand up and run what they buy, according to Andrew Vaz (pictured, left), vice president of product management for the Telecom Systems Business at Dell.

“What we are seeing is a fundamental shift from procuring supply to actually standing up and running these very complicated AI clusters and data centers,” Vaz said. “One of the interesting things that we’ve seen is it’s actually now impossible to manage these things with just a few scripts, manually, in an old world manner. The toolsets are becoming more important.”

Vaz and Saurabh Kapoor (right), director of product management and strategy for AI compute and networking solutions at Dell, spoke with theCUBE Research’s John Furrier and Gemma Allen at SC26, during an exclusive broadcast on theCUBE, SiliconANGLE Media’s livestreaming studio. They discussed supercomputing, rack-scale management, AI observability and why networking sits on the critical path for AI clusters. (* Disclosure below.)

Dell used the conference to introduce Dell OpenManage Rack Scale and Dell Fabric Manager, two tools for running AI infrastructure from deployment through daily operations. OpenManage Rack Scale manages 100,000 devices in its first release, up from 8,000 servers for its predecessor, OpenManage Enterprise, Kapoor explained. Systems such as Nvidia Corp.’s NVL72 racks bundle compute, power shelves, switches and coolant distribution units into one unit.

“OpenManage Enterprise, the previous product we had, was perfect for servers. Server was the unit of compute,” Kapoor said. “But you have seen in the last 18 months, the world has moved very quickly to larger ecosystems and infrastructures. Rack is the new unit of play.”

Visibility is the other half of that equation. Dell’s iDRAC controller pulls more than 320 metrics from every server, and an AI-assist chatbot can query that telemetry through local large language models, according to Kapoor. A single cooling leak can damage millions of dollars of gear, so every layer has to report in, Vaz noted.

“It could be something as simple as, ‘Hey, this transceiver’s about to die that you can’t diagnose with metrics you have today,'” Vaz said. “You need to use AI to go look at it. You need telemetry off the system to go collect all of that, correlate it, run your algorithms on them, train it and understand what’s happening.”

The network is where those pressures concentrate. Dell Fabric Manager turns validated designs built with Nvidia and Advanced Micro Devices Inc. into deployable blueprints across different network operating systems and silicon. Agentic AI is also reshaping traffic, and enterprise bandwidth is outrunning a projected 3.5-fold increase, Vaz noted.

“We’re getting blown away by it. It’s more like nine to 10x,” Vaz said. “It’s going everywhere: east, west, north, south.”

Venezia, occhi sulla Next Gen: Owusu e Pagnucco nel mirino per il mercato di gennaio

📅 Nov 18, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Doppio obiettivo in casa Juve per il Venezia: allo stadio Moccagatta c’era sugli spalti il direttore dell’area tecnica arancioneroverde Cristian Molinaro per visionare dal vivo i talenti juventini della Next Gen, impegnati nella gara poi vinta 3-0 contro il Treviso. In particolare, il club lagunare ha messo gli occhi su due dei gioiellini bianconeri, ovvero il centrocampista Augusto Owusu e il terzino Filippo Pagnucco, fa sapere oggi Tuttosport.

Il classe 2006 ha colpito i dirigenti veneziani per la capacità di giostrare in più ruoli, visto che può essere impiegato ottimamente sia come laterale a tutta fascia in una linea di centrocampo a cinque sia come terzino in una difesa a quattro. Oltre al fatto di essere in grado di coprire entrambe le fasce con ottimi risultati. Un jolly utile e prezioso per tutti gli allenatori che hanno avuto a disposizione in questi anni Pagnucco. Il terzino in estate era stato sondato da diversi club di Serie B (Avellino e Catanzaro) per un prestito, ma la Juve aveva bloccato tutto.

Intanto domenica Molinaro ha potuto visionare da vicino pure il centrocampista Owusu, che ad agosto la Juve sembrava intenzionata a mandare a giocare in prestito per fargli fare esperienza in un altro club professionistico. L’infortunio di Khephren Thuram ha poi cambiato i piani della Signora, che ha stoppato la partenza del mediano aggregandolo spesso e volentieri alla Prima Squadra di Luciano Spalletti. Proprio quest’ultimo apprezza parecchio le doti del classe 2005, tanto da averlo fatto debuttare in Europa League nella sfida vinta 5-0 col NEC Nijmegen.
